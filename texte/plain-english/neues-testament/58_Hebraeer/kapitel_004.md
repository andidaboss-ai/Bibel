# Hebrews – Chapter 4
### God’s rest

---

## A rest remains for God’s people (Verses 1–11)

[1] So let’s be afraid
that any of you might seem to fall short
of the promise
to enter into God’s rest.
[2] For the Good News was announced to us too,
just as to them.
But the word
that they heard
didn’t help them,
because the hearers didn’t join it with faith.
[3] For we
who have believed
do enter into that rest.
As he has said,
“That is why I swore in my anger,
they will not enter into my rest.”
Yet his works were finished from the creation of the world.
[4] For he has said somewhere about the seventh day,
“God rested on the seventh day from all his works.”
[5] And in this place again,
“They will not enter into my rest.”
[6] So it remains
that some are to enter into the rest.
And those
to whom the Good News was announced first
didn’t enter in because of disobedience.
[7] That is why God again sets a certain day:
“today.”
So he said through David a long time afterward,
as has already been said,
“Today,
if you hear his voice,
don’t harden your hearts.”
[8] For if Joshua had already given them rest,
God would not have spoken afterward of another day.
[9] So there remains
a Sabbath rest for the people of God.
[10] For whoever has entered into his rest
has also rested from his own works,
as God did from his.
[11] So let’s be eager
to enter into that rest,
so that no one falls by following the same example of disobedience.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 4 takes up Genesis 2:2: “And on the seventh day he rested from all the work he had done.” God rested after creation. That is why the Sabbath is a day of rest.
> Verse 8: Joshua led the people of Israel into the promised land. But that was not yet the whole rest that God wants to give.
> Verse 9: “So there remains a Sabbath rest for the people of God”: The Sabbath is a foretaste of the perfect rest with God. For Jews the Sabbath is a great gift of God to this day. Many Christians celebrate Sunday as a day of rest.
> Verse 10: God’s rest means: peace with God, rest from all toil and fear. We don’t have to earn God’s love.
> For today: People need rest today too. Regular breaks and a day off each week are a gift of God.

## God’s word is living (Verses 12–13)

[12] For the word of God is living and active.
It is sharper than any two-edged sword.
It pierces even to the dividing of soul and spirit,
of joints and marrow.
And it is able to judge the thoughts and intentions of the heart.
[13] No creature is hidden from his sight.
But all things are naked and laid open before the eyes of him
to whom we must give account.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 12: God’s word is not just an old text. It speaks to us today and reaches us deep inside.
> The “sword” is a picture. It is not about violence against others, but about God’s word showing us honestly what we are like.
> Verse 13: We can hide nothing from God. That can be frightening. But the next verse brings comfort: God is merciful.

---

## Jesus, our high priest (Verses 14–16)

[14] So we have a great high priest,
who has passed through the heavens:
Jesus,
the Son of God.
So let’s hold tightly to what we confess.
[15] For we don’t have a high priest
who can’t feel with us in our weaknesses.
But he was tempted in all things just like we are,
yet without sin.
[16] So let’s come with confidence to the throne of grace,
so that we may receive mercy
and find grace
that helps us
when we need it.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 15 is a great comfort: Jesus understands us. He knows weakness, temptation, fear and pain from his own experience.
> Verse 16: “So let’s come with confidence to the throne of grace”: God’s throne is not a place we have to be afraid of. We may come to God boldly and with trust, especially when we need help.
