# James – Chapter 3
### The power of words

---

## The tongue is small, but powerful (Verses 1–12)

[1] My brothers,
not many of you should become teachers.
For you know:
We will be judged more strictly.
[2] For we all make many mistakes.
Whoever makes no mistakes in speaking
is a perfect person.
He is able to keep his whole body in check too.
[3] We put bits into the mouths of horses
so that they obey us.
So we guide their whole body.
[4] Look at the ships too:
They are so big
and are driven by strong winds.
Yet they are guided by a very small rudder
wherever the pilot wants.
[5] In the same way the tongue is a small part of the body,
and yet it boasts great things.
See
how a small fire can set a great forest on fire!
[6] And the tongue is a fire.
The tongue is a world of wrongdoing among the parts of our body.
It stains the whole body.
It sets on fire the whole course of life
and is itself set on fire by Gehenna.
[7] For every kind of animal,
bird,
creeping thing
and sea creature
is tamed by mankind
and has been tamed.
[8] But no one can tame the tongue.
It is a restless evil,
full of deadly poison.
[9] With it we bless God,
the Father.
And with it we curse people,
who are made in the image of God.
[10] Out of the same mouth come blessing and cursing.
My brothers,
it should not be so.
[11] Does a spring send out
fresh and bitter water from the same opening?
[12] Can a fig tree,
my brothers,
produce olives,
or a vine figs?
In the same way no spring can give
both salt water and fresh water.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 1: Teachers have a great responsibility, because their words influence many people.
> Verses 3–5: Three pictures: The bit guides a big horse, the small rudder a big ship, a small spark can set a whole forest on fire. In the same way the small tongue has great power.
> Verse 6: The WEB says “Gehenna.” That was a valley near Jerusalem and became a picture for hell. Evil words can destroy a lot.
> Verse 9: Every person is created in the image of God (Genesis 1:27). Whoever praises God and at the same time curses people contradicts himself.
> For today: This also applies to words on the internet and in social media. Insults, lies and hate can hurt people deeply. If you are insulted or bullied online, you can find help. In the US, call or text 988. In the UK and Ireland, call Samaritans at 116 123. Elsewhere, find help at findahelpline.com.

## The wisdom from above (Verses 13–18)

[13] Who is wise and understanding among you?
Let him show by a good life
that his deeds come from gentle wisdom.
[14] But if you have bitter jealousy
and selfish ambition in your heart,
don’t boast
and don’t lie against the truth.
[15] This wisdom doesn’t come from above.
But it is earthly,
human
and demonic.
[16] For where jealousy and selfish ambition are,
there is confusion
and every evil deed.
[17] But the wisdom from above is first pure,
then peaceful,
gentle,
reasonable,
full of mercy and good fruits,
without partiality
and without hypocrisy.
[18] And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace
by those
who make peace.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 13: True wisdom does not show itself in clever speeches, but in a good, gentle life.
> Verses 14–16: Jealousy and selfish ambition destroy every community.
> Verse 17: A beautiful description of God’s wisdom: pure, peaceful, gentle, reasonable, merciful, fair and honest.
> Verse 18: Whoever makes peace sows something good that will grow. Jesus says: “Blessed are those who make peace” (Matthew 5:9).
