# 2 Timothy – Chapter 2
### A good worker for God

---

## Be strong and hold out (Verses 1–7)

[1] You then,
my child,
be strong in the grace
that is in Christ Jesus.
[2] What you have heard from me before many witnesses,
entrust to faithful people
who will be able
to teach others also.
[3] So endure hardship with me
as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.
[4] No soldier on duty
gets tangled up in the affairs of everyday life.
He wants to please the one
who enlisted him.
[5] And whoever competes in athletics
only receives the victor’s wreath
if he competes by the rules.
[6] The farmer
who works hard
should be the first to get a share of the crops.
[7] Think about
what I am saying.
The Lord will give you understanding in all things.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 2: This is how faith is passed on: from Paul to Timothy, from Timothy to other faithful people, and from them on to others. To this day.
> Verses 3–6: Three pictures: the soldier, the athlete and the farmer. All three need commitment, perseverance and patience.
> Important: The picture of the soldier doesn’t mean that Christians should use violence. It is about faithfulness and devotion.

---

## God’s word is not chained (Verses 8–13)

[8] Remember Jesus Christ,
risen from the dead,
of the offspring of David.
That is what my Good News says.
[9] For it I suffer hardship
to the point of chains,
like a criminal.
But God’s word isn’t chained.
[10] That is why I endure all things
for the sake of the chosen ones,
so that they too may obtain the salvation that is in Christ Jesus,
with eternal glory.
[11] This saying is trustworthy:
“For if we died with him,
we will also live with him.
[12] If we endure,
we will also reign with him.
If we deny him,
he also will deny us.
[13] If we are faithless,
he remains faithful.
For he can’t deny himself.”

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 8: “Of the offspring of David”: Jesus is the Messiah whom God promised to Israel.
> Verse 9: Paul is in chains, but no one can chain God’s word. It keeps spreading.
> Verses 11–13 are probably an old song or confession of the first Christians.
> Verse 13 is a great comfort: Even if we are faithless, God remains faithful. His faithfulness does not depend on ours.

---

## A worker who doesn’t need to be ashamed (Verses 14–21)

[14] Remind them of these things.
Warn them before the Lord
not to argue about words.
That does no good,
but harms those
who listen.
[15] Do your best
to present yourself to God as approved,
as a worker
who doesn’t need to be ashamed,
because he handles the word of truth properly.
[16] But avoid empty chatter.
For it leads further and further into ungodliness.
[17] And such words spread like gangrene.
Among these are Hymenaeus and Philetus.
[18] They have wandered from the truth.
They say
that the resurrection has already happened.
And they are upsetting the faith of some people.
[19] However, God’s firm foundation stands.
It has this seal:
“The Lord knows those
who are his.”
And:
“Let every one
who names the name of the Lord
depart from unrighteousness.”
[20] In a large house there are not only vessels of gold and silver,
but also of wood and clay.
Some are for honorable use,
others for ordinary use.
[21] So whoever cleanses himself from these things
will be a vessel for honorable use,
made holy
and useful to the master of the house,
prepared for every good work.

*gangrene* = a disease in which flesh rots and the damage keeps spreading
*vessels* = containers such as dishes, jars and bowls

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 14: Arguing about words helps no one. It only does harm.
> Verse 15: Whoever explains God’s word should do it carefully and honestly, like a good craftsman.
> Verse 18: Hymenaeus and Philetus taught: The resurrection has already happened, but only spiritually. In this way they denied the hope of a future resurrection.
> Verse 19: The first sentence recalls Numbers 16:5. There Moses says: *In the morning the LORD will show who are his.* God knows his own, even when people are wrong.
> Verses 20–21: A picture from the household: There are valuable dishes and simple ones. But every person can become a “vessel” that God can use.

---

## Be gentle toward all (Verses 22–26)

[22] Flee from youthful lusts.
Pursue righteousness,
faith,
love
and peace,
together with all
who call on the Lord out of a pure heart.
[23] Refuse foolish and senseless arguments.
You know
that they only produce quarrels.
[24] The Lord’s servant must not quarrel.
But he must be gentle toward all,
able to teach
and patient.
[25] He must correct those
who oppose him
with gentleness.
Perhaps God will give them repentance,
so that they come to know the truth
[26] and come to their senses again.
Then they can escape from the snare of the devil,
who has taken them captive
to do his will.

*repentance* = turning around, changing your heart and life

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 22: “Youthful lusts” probably doesn’t mean only sexual desires, but also impatience, love of quarreling and ambition.
> Verses 24–25: Important advice for everyone who has responsibility in the church: Don’t quarrel, but be gentle, patient and kind, even toward opponents.
> Verses 25–26: The goal is not to defeat the other person, but to help them find the truth. God gives repentance, not pressure.
