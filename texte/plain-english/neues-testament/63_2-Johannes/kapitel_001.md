# 2 John – Chapter 1
### Remain in truth and in love

---

## Before we begin: The second letter of John

This letter is very short. It has only 13 verses. It probably fit on a single sheet of papyrus.
The sender calls himself “the elder.” According to an old tradition this is the apostle John. Many scholars think it was a well-known leader from the circle around John.
The letter is written to a “chosen lady and her children.” This probably means a church and its members. But it could also be a woman who led a church in her house.
The themes are the same as in the first letter of John: love, truth and the warning against false teachers.
The second letter of John has only 1 chapter.

---

## Greeting (Verses 1–3)

[1] The elder writes
to the chosen lady and her children.
I love them in truth.
And not only I,
but also all
who know the truth.
[2] We love you because of the truth,
which remains in us
and will be with us forever.
[3] Grace, mercy and peace
will be with us
from God the Father
and from the Lord Jesus Christ,
the Son of the Father,
in truth and love.

> **What does this mean?**
> “The chosen lady”: At the end (verse 13) the children of her “chosen sister” send greetings. That is why many think: Two churches are meant, which are like sisters.
> Truth and love belong together. Truth without love becomes hard. Love without truth means that anything goes.

---

## Love one another (Verses 4–6)

[4] I am very glad
that I have found some of your children
living in the truth,
just as we received a commandment from the Father.
[5] And now I ask you,
dear lady:
Let’s love one another.
I am not writing you a new commandment,
but what we had from the beginning.
[6] And this is love:
that we live by his commandments.
And this is the commandment,
just as you heard from the beginning:
that you should live in love.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 5: Again the old commandment of love, as in the first letter of John (1 John 2:7).
> Verse 6: Love and commandments belong together. God’s commandments say what love looks like in practice.

---

## Beware of deceivers (Verses 7–11)

[7] For many deceivers have gone out into the world.
They don’t confess
that Jesus Christ has come as a human being of flesh and blood.
This is the deceiver and the Antichrist.
[8] Watch yourselves,
so that we don’t lose
what we have worked for,
but receive a full reward.
[9] Whoever goes beyond it
and doesn’t remain in the teaching of Christ
doesn’t have God.
Whoever remains in the teaching
has both the Father and the Son.
[10] If anyone comes to you
and doesn’t bring this teaching,
don’t receive him into your house
and don’t welcome him.
[11] For whoever welcomes him
takes part in his evil deeds.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 7: As in the first letter of John, this is about teachers who say: Jesus was not really a human being. On the word “Antichrist” see the note at 1 John 2.
> Verse 9: “Goes beyond it”: The false teachers thought that they were already further along than the simple message of Jesus.

> **Important note**
> Verses 10–11 sound very harsh. At that time teachers traveled from church to church. They stayed with Christians, and the church met in houses. Whoever took in a teacher gave him a stage in the church. So what is meant is: Don’t give false teachers a place to teach in your church.
> These verses do not give permission to treat people unkindly or to shut out people who believe differently. Jesus ate with everyone. Christians are to respect all people and be kind.

---

## Closing (Verses 12–13)

[12] I would have much more to write to you.
But I don’t want to do it with paper and ink.
Instead I hope
to come to you
and speak with you face to face,
so that our joy may be complete.
[13] The children of your chosen sister greet you.
Amen.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 12: A very human sentence: Some things are better said in person than in a letter. Nothing can replace meeting in person.
> Verse 13: The “chosen sister” is probably another church where the elder is at the moment.

---

## Looking back: What have we read in the second letter of John?

> **What does this mean?**
> **Truth and love:** Both belong together (verses 1–3).
> **The old commandment:** Love one another (verses 5–6).
> **Beware of deceivers:** Remain with the message of Jesus, who really became a human being (verses 7–11).
> **Better in person:** Joy becomes complete when people meet (verse 12).
> **For today:** Remain with the truth, but remain loving as you do.
> **What comes next?** Next comes the third letter of John. It is written to a man named Gaius and speaks about hospitality.
