# 1 John – Chapter 1
### God is light

---

## Before we begin: The first letter of John

This letter has no sender and no greeting at the beginning. It is more like a sermon or a circular letter to churches.
According to an old tradition the apostle John wrote it. Many scholars think that it comes from the circle of students from which the Gospel of John also comes. Language and thoughts are very similar to the Gospel.
There had been conflict in the church. A group had left the church. Some of them probably said: Jesus was not really a human being of flesh and blood.
The letter answers: Jesus really became a human being. And whoever loves God must also love his brothers and sisters.
The big themes are light, truth and above all love. Here we find the famous sentence: God is love (4:8 and 4:16).
The first letter of John has 5 chapters.

---

## We have seen it and touched it (Verses 1–4)

[1] We write to you about what was from the beginning.
We have heard it.
We have seen it with our eyes.
We have looked at it,
and our hands have touched it.
It is about the Word of life.
[2] And the life was revealed.
We have seen it
and testify to it.
We announce to you the eternal life,
which was with the Father
and was revealed to us.
[3] What we have seen and heard
we announce to you,
so that you also may have fellowship with us.
And our fellowship is with the Father
and with his Son Jesus Christ.
[4] And we write these things to you,
so that our joy may be complete.

> **What does this mean?**
> This beginning recalls the beginning of the Gospel of John: “In the beginning was the Word” (John 1:1). The “Word of life” means Jesus.
> Verse 1: “Heard, seen, touched”: Jesus was a real human being. People could touch him. That is important against the opinion that Jesus only seemed to be a human being.
> Verse 3: Faith is not only a private matter. It connects people with one another and with God.

---

## God is light (Verses 5–10)

[5] This is the message
that we have heard from him
and announce to you:
God is light,
and in him is no darkness at all.
[6] If we say
that we have fellowship with him
but live in darkness,
we lie
and don’t tell the truth.
[7] But if we live in the light,
as he is in the light,
we have fellowship with one another.
And the blood of Jesus Christ, his Son,
cleanses us from all sin.
[8] If we say
that we have no sin,
we deceive ourselves,
and the truth is not in us.
[9] If we confess our sins,
he is faithful and righteous.
He forgives us our sins
and cleanses us from all unrighteousness.
[10] If we say
that we haven’t sinned,
we make him a liar,
and his word is not in us.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 5: “God is light”: With God there is nothing evil and nothing hidden. He is completely good.
> Verses 6–7: To “live in the light” means: living honestly, hiding nothing, living in a way that fits God.
> Verses 8 and 10: No one is without faults. Whoever claims that is fooling themselves.
> Verse 9 is a great comfort: Whoever honestly admits their guilt is forgiven by God. You don’t have to be perfect. You may be honest.
> “Confessing” can happen in prayer directly before God. In some churches there is also confession to a pastor or priest.
> Verse 7: “The blood of Jesus” means his death on the cross. Christians believe: Through his death God gives forgiveness.
