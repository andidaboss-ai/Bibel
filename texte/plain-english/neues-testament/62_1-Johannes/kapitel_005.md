# 1 John – Chapter 5
### Faith overcomes the world

---

## Faith is the victory (Verses 1–5)

[1] Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Christ
has been born of God.
Everyone who loves the Father
also loves the child who is born of him.
[2] This is how we know that we love God’s children:
We love God and keep his commandments.
[3] Loving God means keeping his commandments.
And his commandments are not hard.
[4] Everyone who is born of God wins the victory over the world.
Our faith is the victory that has won over the world.
[5] Who wins the victory over the world?
Only the one who believes that Jesus is the Son of God.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 1: Whoever loves God also loves the other children of God. You can’t love the Father and despise his children.
> Verse 3: “His commandments are not hard”: God’s commandments are not a burden that crushes. They help us to live. And God gives the strength for them.
> Verse 4: “The victory over the world” doesn’t mean ruling over other people. It means: not letting fear, greed and evil rule you.

---

## Three witnesses (Verses 6–12)

[6] Jesus Christ is the one who came by water and blood.
He did not come with the water only,
but with the water and with the blood.
And the Spirit tells us that this is true,
because the Spirit is the truth.
[7] There are three who tell about it:
[8] the Spirit,
the water
and the blood.
And the three agree.
[9] We accept what people say about something.
But what God says is greater.
And God has spoken about his Son.
[10] Whoever believes in the Son of God
has God’s word about him in his own heart.
Whoever does not believe God makes God a liar,
because he has not believed what God has said about his Son.
[11] And this is what God has said:
God gave us eternal life,
and this life is in his Son.
[12] Whoever has the Son has the life.
Whoever does not have God’s Son does not have the life.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 6: “Water and blood”: The water probably recalls the baptism of Jesus in the Jordan. The blood recalls his death on the cross. Both belong to Jesus. The false teachers perhaps said: Christ was only there at the baptism, but not on the cross. The letter says: Yes, Jesus Christ really died.
> Verses 7–8: In some later Bibles there is a longer text here about the Father, the Word and the Holy Spirit in heaven. This addition is not in the old Greek manuscripts. It was only added much later. That is why it is not here. The churches still believe in Father, Son and Holy Spirit. This shows in other places of the Bible.
> Verse 12: The life that God gives is found in Jesus.

## So that you may know (Verses 13–17)

[13] You believe in the name of the Son of God.
I have written this to you so that you may know
that you have eternal life.
And I want you to keep on believing in the name of the Son of God.
[14] This is why we can be confident when we come to God:
If we ask for anything that agrees with his will,
he listens to us.
[15] We know that he listens to us,
whatever we ask.
So we also know that we already have what we asked him for.
[16] Someone may see his brother commit a sin
that does not lead to death.
Then he should pray for him,
and God will give him life.
This is for those whose sin does not lead to death.
There is a sin that leads to death.
I am not saying that you should pray about that sin.
[17] Everything that is wrong is sin.
But there is a sin that does not lead to death.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 13: The letter wants to give certainty: You may know that you belong to God. You don’t have to live in uncertainty.
> Verses 14–15: God hears prayers. That doesn’t mean that every wish is granted exactly that way. Whoever prays entrusts themselves to God’s will.
> Verse 16: A beautiful picture: Christians pray for one another when someone makes mistakes.

> **Important note**
> We don’t know for sure what “a sin that leads to death” is exactly. Perhaps the letter means that someone turns away from Jesus very deliberately and for good, like the false teachers who left the church.
> If you are afraid that you have committed such a sin: This very fear shows that you have not turned away from God. God forgives everyone who comes to him (1:9). The letter also doesn’t forbid praying for someone. You may pray for all people.
> If you feel weighed down, you can talk to someone. In the US, call or text 988. In the UK and Ireland, call Samaritans at 116 123. Elsewhere, find help at findahelpline.com.

## What we know (Verses 18–21)

[18] We know that whoever is born of God doesn’t sin.
But he who was born of God keeps himself.
And the evil one cannot touch him.
[19] We know that we belong to God.
And we know that the whole world is in the power of the evil one.
[20] We know that the Son of God has come.
He has given us understanding,
so that we can know the one who is true.
And we are in the one who is true,
in his Son Jesus Christ.
He is the true God and eternal life.
[21] Little children,
keep away from idols.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 18: On “doesn’t sin” see the note in chapter 3: It means not living in evil constantly and on purpose.
> Verse 18: Here it says: “But he who was born of God keeps himself.” Some manuscripts say: *He keeps him*. Then Jesus is meant, who protects those who believe.
> Verse 20: Here Jesus is probably called “the true God.” Some also understand the sentence to mean God the Father.
> Verse 21: The last sentence: “Idols” are everything that people put in the place of God, for example money, power or a false picture of Jesus.

## Looking back: What have we read in the first letter of John?

> **What does this mean?**
> **Jesus was really a human being:** The apostles saw him, heard him and touched him (chapters 1 and 4).
> **God is light:** Whoever belongs to God lives honestly. Whoever confesses their guilt is forgiven by God (chapter 1).
> **Loving one another:** The old and new commandment. Love shows itself in deeds, not only in words (chapters 2 and 3).
> **Children of God:** God gives us the gift of being his children (chapter 3).
> **God is greater than our heart:** Even when we accuse ourselves (chapter 3).
> **God is love:** He loved us first. Love drives out fear (chapter 4).
> **Certainty:** Whoever believes in Jesus may know that he has eternal life (chapter 5).
> **For today:** Whoever loves God also loves the people he sees.
> **What comes next?** Next come two very short letters: the second and the third letter of John. They are the shortest books of the Bible.
