# 1 John – Chapter 2
### Staying in the light

---

## Jesus stands up for us (Verses 1–2)

[1] My little children,
I am writing this to you so that you will not sin.
But if anyone does sin,
we have a Counselor who speaks for us with the Father.
He is Jesus Christ, the righteous one.
[2] He is the sacrifice that takes away our sins.
He is the sacrifice not only for our sins, but also for the sins of the whole world.

> **What does this mean?**
> “My little children”: This is how an old teacher speaks lovingly to his church.
> Verse 1: A “Counselor” is someone who stands up for another person, like a good lawyer. Whoever makes mistakes is not alone. Jesus stands up for them before God.
> Verse 2: “Not only for our sins, but also for the sins of the whole world”: What Jesus did is not only for a small group, but for all people.

---

## Whoever knows God lives like Jesus (Verses 3–11)

[3] This is how we know that we really know him:
We keep his commandments.
[4] Someone may say, “I know him.”
But if he does not keep his commandments, he is a liar.
The truth is not in him.
[5] But if someone keeps his word,
God’s love has truly become complete in him.
This is how we know that we are in him:
[6] Whoever says that he remains in him
should live just as Jesus lived.
[7] Brothers, I am not writing a new commandment to you.
It is an old commandment.
You have had it from the beginning.
This old commandment is the word you heard from the beginning.
[8] And yet, I am writing a new commandment to you.
It is true in him and in you.
The darkness is passing away,
and the true light is already shining.
[9] Someone may say that he is in the light,
but he hates his brother.
Then he is still in the darkness.
[10] Whoever loves his brother remains in the light.
There is nothing in him that makes others stumble.
[11] But whoever hates his brother is in the darkness.
He walks around in the darkness.
He does not know where he is going,
because the darkness has made him blind.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 3–6: Knowing God is not just knowledge in the head. It shows itself in life: You live the way Jesus lived.
> Verses 7–8: An old and at the same time new commandment: This means the commandment of love. It is old, because the Torah already says: Love your neighbor (Leviticus 19:18). It is new, because Jesus gave it to his disciples anew and lived it himself (John 13:34).
> Verses 9–11: “Brother” here means all brothers and sisters in faith, men and women. Whoever hates others lives in the dark, even if he thinks he is godly.

## To children, fathers and young men (Verses 12–14)

[12] Little children, I am writing to you,
because your sins are forgiven for the sake of his name.
[13] Fathers, I am writing to you,
because you know him who has been there from the beginning.
Young men, I am writing to you,
because you have won against the evil one.
Little children, I am writing to you,
because you know the Father.
[14] Fathers, I have written to you,
because you know him who has been there from the beginning.
Young men, I have written to you,
because you are strong.
The word of God remains in you,
and you have won against the evil one.

> **What does this mean?**
> Here the letter speaks to different groups. Perhaps these are age groups. Perhaps they are also stages in faith: beginners, experienced and strong. Everyone in the church is meant, the women too.
> Each group is told something good: You are forgiven. You know God. You are strong.

---

## Don’t set your heart on the world (Verses 15–17)

[15] Do not love the world or the things in the world.
If anyone loves the world,
the Father’s love is not in him.
[16] Everything in the world does not come from the Father.
It comes from the world.
This means: wanting what the body desires,
wanting what the eyes see,
and showing off with what you have.
[17] The world is passing away,
and so is everything people desire in it.
But whoever does God’s will remains forever.

> **What does this mean?**
> “World” here doesn’t mean creation and doesn’t mean people. God loves the world (John 3:16). It means a life that revolves only around greed, possessions and status.
> Verse 16 names three traps: wanting things, wanting what you see, and showing off with what you have.
> Verse 17: What you own passes away. What you do out of love remains.

## A warning against deceivers (Verses 18–27)

[18] Little children, these are the end times.
You have heard that the Antichrist is coming.
And even now many antichrists have come.
That is how we know that it is the last hour.
[19] They left us,
but they never really belonged to us.
If they had belonged to us,
they would have stayed with us.
But they left.
This made it clear that none of them belong to us.
[20] But you have an anointing from the Holy One.
And all of you have knowledge.
To anoint means: to pour oil on someone as a sign that God has chosen them.
[21] I am not writing to you because you do not know the truth.
I am writing because you do know it.
And you know that no lie comes from the truth.
[22] Who is the liar?
It is the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ.
Whoever denies the Father and the Son is the Antichrist.
[23] Whoever denies the Son does not have the Father either.
Whoever confesses the Son has the Father too.
[24] So hold on to what you heard from the beginning.
Let it remain in you.
If what you heard from the beginning remains in you,
you will also remain in the Son and in the Father.
[25] And this is what he promised us:
eternal life.
[26] I have written this to you because of the people
who are trying to lead you the wrong way.
[27] But as for you:
The anointing you received from him remains in you.
So you do not need anyone to teach you.
His anointing teaches you about everything.
It is true. It is not a lie.
It has taught you,
and so you will remain in him.

> **Important note**
> The word “Antichrist” appears in the Bible only in the letters of John. It means: someone who is against Christ. Here it means teachers who had left the church and taught wrongly about Jesus.
> In the course of history people often used this word to run others down: other churches, rulers or even whole peoples. It was also misused against Jews. That is wrong and has brought great suffering. The letter speaks of a conflict within the Christian church, not of the Jewish people.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 19: There had been a split in the church. A group had left. That made the church unsure.
> Verses 20 and 27: “Anointing” probably means the Holy Spirit. Every Christian has received him, not only the teachers. That is why verse 27 doesn’t mean that you should never learn anything. It means: You don’t depend on deceivers. God’s Spirit helps you recognize the truth.
> Verses 22–23: For the letter this is important: Whoever rejects Jesus doesn’t understand God rightly either. This means the teachers who had turned away from Jesus.

## Remain in him (Verses 28–29)

[28] And now, little children, remain in him.
Then we can be confident when he appears.
We will not have to hide from him in shame when he comes.
[29] You know that he is righteous.
So you also know this:
Everyone who does what is right has been born of him.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 28: “Remain” is one of John’s favorite words. It means: being firmly connected, like a branch with the vine (John 15).
> Verse 29: “Born of him”: Whoever belongs to God is like his child. This shows itself in doing good.
