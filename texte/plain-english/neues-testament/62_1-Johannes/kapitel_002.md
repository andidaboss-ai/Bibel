# 1 John – Chapter 2
### Staying in the light

---

## Jesus stands up for us (Verses 1–2)

[1] My little children,
I write these things to you
so that you may not sin.
But if anyone sins,
we have a Counselor with the Father:
Jesus Christ,
the righteous.
[2] He is the sacrifice
that atones for our sins.
And not only for our sins,
but also for the sins of the whole world.

> **What does this mean?**
> “My little children”: This is how an old teacher speaks lovingly to his church.
> Verse 1: A “Counselor” is someone who stands up for another person, like a good lawyer. Whoever makes mistakes is not alone. Jesus stands up for them before God.
> Verse 2: “Not only for our sins, but also for the sins of the whole world”: What Jesus did is not only for a small group, but for all people.

---

## Whoever knows God lives like Jesus (Verses 3–11)

[3] This is how we know
that we know him:
if we keep his commandments.
[4] Whoever says, “I know him,”
but doesn’t keep his commandments,
is a liar,
and the truth isn’t in him.
[5] But whoever keeps his word,
in him God’s love has truly been made perfect.
This is how we know
that we are in him:
[6] Whoever says that he remains in him
must also himself live
as he lived.
[7] Brothers,
I write no new commandment to you,
but an old commandment
which you had from the beginning.
The old commandment is the word
which you heard from the beginning.
[8] And yet I write a new commandment to you.
It is true in him and in you.
For the darkness is passing away,
and the true light already shines.
[9] Whoever says that he is in the light
but hates his brother
is still in the darkness even now.
[10] Whoever loves his brother
remains in the light,
and there is nothing in him
that makes others stumble.
[11] But whoever hates his brother
is in the darkness
and lives in the darkness.
He doesn’t know
where he is going.
For the darkness has blinded his eyes.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 3–6: Knowing God is not just knowledge in the head. It shows itself in life: You live the way Jesus lived.
> Verses 7–8: An old and at the same time new commandment: This means the commandment of love. It is old, because the Torah already says: Love your neighbor (Leviticus 19:18). It is new, because Jesus gave it to his disciples anew and lived it himself (John 13:34).
> Verses 9–11: “Brother” here means all brothers and sisters in faith, men and women. Whoever hates others lives in the dark, even if he thinks he is godly.

## To children, fathers and young men (Verses 12–14)

[12] I write to you, little children,
because your sins are forgiven
for his name’s sake.
[13] I write to you, fathers,
because you know him
who is from the beginning.
I write to you, young men,
because you have overcome the evil one.
I write to you, little children,
because you know the Father.
[14] I have written to you, fathers,
because you know him
who is from the beginning.
I have written to you, young men,
because you are strong,
and the word of God remains in you,
and you have overcome the evil one.

> **What does this mean?**
> Here the letter speaks to different groups. Perhaps these are age groups. Perhaps they are also stages in faith: beginners, experienced and strong. Everyone in the church is meant, the women too.
> Each group is told something good: You are forgiven. You know God. You are strong.

---

## Don’t set your heart on the world (Verses 15–17)

[15] Don’t love the world
or the things that are in the world.
If anyone loves the world,
the Father’s love isn’t in him.
[16] For all that is in the world,
the desire of the body,
the desire of the eyes
and showing off with one’s own life,
isn’t from the Father,
but from the world.
[17] And the world is passing away with its desires.
But whoever does the will of God
remains forever.

> **What does this mean?**
> “World” here doesn’t mean creation and doesn’t mean people. God loves the world (John 3:16). It means a life that revolves only around greed, possessions and status.
> Verse 16 names three traps: wanting things, wanting what you see, and showing off with what you have.
> Verse 17: What you own passes away. What you do out of love remains.

## A warning against deceivers (Verses 18–27)

[18] Little children,
it is the last hour.
You have heard
that the Antichrist is coming.
And now many antichrists have already come.
By this we know
that it is the last hour.
[19] They went out from us,
but they didn’t belong to us.
For if they had belonged to us,
they would have stayed with us.
But they left,
so that it might be made clear
that none of them belong to us.
[20] But you have an anointing from the Holy One,
and you all have knowledge.
[21] I don’t write to you
because you don’t know the truth,
but because you know it
and because no lie comes from the truth.
[22] Who is the liar,
if not he
who denies
that Jesus is the Christ?
This is the Antichrist:
he who denies the Father and the Son.
[23] Whoever denies the Son
doesn’t have the Father either.
Whoever confesses the Son
has the Father also.
[24] Let what you heard from the beginning
remain in you.
If what you heard from the beginning
remains in you,
you also will remain in the Son and in the Father.
[25] And this is the promise
that he gave us:
eternal life.
[26] I have written these things to you
because of the people
who want to lead you astray.
[27] And as for you:
The anointing
which you received from him
remains in you.
You don’t need anyone
to teach you.
But his anointing teaches you all things.
It is true and is no lie.
Remain in him,
just as it taught you.

> **Important note**
> The word “Antichrist” appears in the Bible only in the letters of John. It means: someone who is against Christ. Here it means teachers who had left the church and taught wrongly about Jesus.
> In the course of history people often used this word to run others down: other churches, rulers or even whole peoples. It was also misused against Jews. That is wrong and has brought great suffering. The letter speaks of a conflict within the Christian church, not of the Jewish people.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 19: There had been a split in the church. A group had left. That made the church unsure.
> Verses 20 and 27: “Anointing” probably means the Holy Spirit. Every Christian has received him, not only the teachers. That is why verse 27 doesn’t mean that you should never learn anything. It means: You don’t depend on deceivers. God’s Spirit helps you recognize the truth.
> Verses 22–23: For the letter this is important: Whoever rejects Jesus doesn’t understand God rightly either. This means the teachers who had turned away from Jesus.

## Remain in him (Verses 28–29)

[28] And now,
little children,
remain in him.
Then we can be bold
when he appears,
and we won’t have to be ashamed before him
when he comes.
[29] If you know
that he is righteous,
you know also:
Everyone who does what is right
is born of him.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 28: “Remain” is one of John’s favorite words. It means: being firmly connected, like a branch with the vine (John 15).
> Verse 29: “Born of him”: Whoever belongs to God is like his child. This shows itself in doing good.
