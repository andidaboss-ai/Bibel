# 1 Thessalonians – Chapter 3
### Good news from Timothy

---

## Paul sends Timothy (Verses 1–5)

[1] So when we couldn’t stand it any longer,
we decided
to be left behind at Athens alone,
[2] and we sent Timothy to you.
He is our brother
and God’s servant in the Good News of Christ.
He was to strengthen you
and encourage you in your faith,
[3] so that no one would be shaken by these afflictions.
For you yourselves know:
We are appointed to this.
[4] For even when we were with you,
we told you beforehand
that we would suffer affliction.
And so it happened,
and you know it.
[5] That is why I too couldn’t stand it any longer
and sent Timothy,
to find out
how your faith was doing.
For I was afraid
that the tempter had somehow tempted you
and that our labor had been in vain.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 1: After Thessalonica Paul had traveled to Athens by way of Berea (Acts 17:10–15). In Athens he was alone and very worried about the young church.
> Verses 3–4: Paul had honestly warned the Christians beforehand: Whoever believes in Jesus may face trouble and hostility because of it.
> Verse 5: Paul openly admits that he was afraid. An apostle, too, worries about the people he loves.
> “The tempter” is Satan. Paul feared that the Christians would give up their faith under pressure.

---

## Timothy brings good news (Verses 6–10)

[6] But now Timothy has come back to us from you.
He has brought us glad news
of your faith and your love.
You always have good memories of us
and long
to see us,
just as we also long
to see you.
[7] For this reason,
brothers,
we were comforted through your faith,
in all our distress and affliction.
[8] For now we live,
if you stand fast in the Lord.
[9] How can we thank God enough for you,
for all the joy
that we have because of you before our God?
[10] Night and day we pray very earnestly
that we may see you again
and may make complete
what is lacking in your faith.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 6: Timothy probably came back to Corinth, where Paul was by then (Acts 18:5). There Paul wrote this letter.
> Verse 8: “Now we live”: The good news gave Paul new courage. The church comforts the apostle, not only the other way around.
> Verse 10: “What is lacking in your faith”: The church was still young. Paul had only been able to be with it for a short time. There were still many questions, for example about those who had died (chapter 4).

---

## Prayer for the church (Verses 11–13)

[11] May our God and Father himself
and our Lord Jesus Christ
direct our way to you.
[12] And may the Lord make you grow
and overflow in love for one another
and for all people,
just as we also love you.
[13] So may he strengthen your hearts,
so that they are blameless and holy
before our God and Father
at the coming of our Lord Jesus with all his saints.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 12: Love is not to stay only within the church, but is to be for “all people.”
> Verse 13: “With all his saints”: This can mean angels or all who belong to God. The picture recalls Zechariah 14:5.
> Paul hopes for the return of Jesus. Until then the Christians are to grow in love.
