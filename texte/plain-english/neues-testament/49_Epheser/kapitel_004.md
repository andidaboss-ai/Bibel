# Ephesians – Chapter 4
### One body and a new life

---

## Keep the unity (Verses 1–6)

[1] So I,
the prisoner in the Lord,
beg you:
Live in a way
that is worthy of the calling
with which you were called.
[2] Be completely humble and gentle.
Be patient
and bear with one another in love.
[3] Be eager
to keep the unity of the Spirit
in the bond of peace.
[4] There is one body and one Spirit,
just as you were also called in one hope.
[5] There is one Lord,
one faith,
one baptism,
[6] one God and Father of all.
He is over all,
through all
and in all.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 2: “Bear with one another in love”: In every community there are differences and sometimes anger. Love helps people put up with one another.
> Verses 4–6: Seven times the word “one” appears here: one body, one Spirit, one hope, one Lord, one faith, one baptism, one God. These verses are very important for ecumenism, the way the churches live together. All Christians are connected through the one baptism.

---

## Different gifts for the one body (Verses 7–16)

[7] But to each one of us grace was given,
according to the measure that Christ gave.
[8] That is why it says:
“When he ascended on high,
he led captivity captive,
and gave gifts to people.”
[9] But what does “He ascended” mean?
Only
that he also first descended
into the lower parts of the earth.
[10] He who descended
is also the one
who ascended far above all the heavens,
that he might fill all things.
[11] And he gave some to be apostles,
some to be prophets,
some to be evangelists,
some to be shepherds and teachers.
[12] They are to equip the saints for the work of serving,
so that the body of Christ is built up,
[13] until we all attain to the unity of the faith
and of the knowledge of the Son of God,
to a mature,
full-grown person,
to the full measure of the fullness of Christ.
[14] Then we are no longer children
who are tossed back and forth
and carried about by every wind of teaching,
by the trickery of people,
by their craftiness,
with which they lead others astray.
[15] But speaking truth in love,
we want to grow up in all things into him
who is the head:
Christ.
[16] From him the whole body is fitted
and held together
by every joint
that supplies it.
Each individual part works according to its measure.
In this way the body grows
and builds itself up in love.

> **What does this mean?**
> Evangelists means: people who spread the Good News.
> Verse 8 takes up Psalm 68:18 (in the Hebrew Bible and in some Bibles 68:19). The letter applies the psalm to Christ.
> Verse 9: “The lower parts of the earth”: Some understand this as Jesus coming to earth, others as his way into the realm of the dead.
> Verses 11–12: Leaders in the church have the task of equipping all the others for their service. Every Christian has a service.
> Verse 15: “Speaking truth in love”: Truth without love can hurt. Love without truth can deceive. Both belong together.
> Verse 16: Every part of the body is important, even the smallest.

---

## The old and the new person (Verses 17–24)

[17] So this I say
and testify in the Lord:
No longer live
the way the rest of the nations live,
with their empty thinking.
[18] Their understanding is darkened.
They are cut off from the life of God
because they are ignorant
and their hearts are hardened.
[19] They have become callous
and have given themselves up to lust,
to practice every kind of uncleanness with greed.
[20] But you didn’t learn Christ that way.
[21] You have heard of him
and were taught in him,
just as truth is in Jesus:
[22] You are to put away the old man,
who belongs to your former way of life
and is being ruined by deceitful desires.
[23] You are to be renewed in the spirit of your mind
[24] and put on the new man,
who has been created in the likeness of God,
in true righteousness and holiness.
Righteousness means: living right before God.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 17–19: The letter describes the former life of the Christians from the nations without God. This is no judgment on all people of other religions today.
> Verses 22–24: A picture from everyday life: You take off old clothes and put on new ones. In the same way a person can begin a new life with Christ.
> Verse 24: “In the likeness of God” recalls creation (Genesis 1:27).

---

## How a new person lives (Verses 25–32)

[25] So put away falsehood
and speak truth,
each one with his neighbor.
For we are members of one another.
[26] “Be angry,
and don’t sin.”
Don’t let the sun go down on your anger.
[27] And don’t give the devil a place.
[28] Whoever stole
should steal no more.
Rather he should work
and produce something good with his own hands,
so that he has something
to give to those in need.
[29] Let no corrupt speech come out of your mouth,
but only what is good,
what builds others up
where it is needed,
so that it does good
to those
who hear.
[30] And don’t grieve the Holy Spirit of God.
In him you were sealed for the day of redemption.
[31] Let all bitterness,
rage,
anger,
outcry
and slander
be put away from you,
together with all malice.
[32] But be kind to one another
and tenderhearted.
Forgive one another,
just as God also forgave you in Christ.

> **What does this mean?**
> Slander means: saying bad and untrue things about others.
> Verse 25 recalls Zechariah 8:16.
> Verse 26: “Be angry, and don’t sin.” These words take up Psalm 4:4 (in the Hebrew Bible and in some Bibles 4:5). Anger in itself is not sin. But you should not hold on to it. Good advice: Settle a quarrel on the same day if you can.
> Important: Whoever has experienced injustice or violence may be angry. That is a healthy feeling. Forgiveness often needs time, and it does not mean that you have to accept injustice. If you experience violence at home, you can get help. For domestic violence, US: 1-800-799-7233. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the US, 999 in the UK).
> Verse 28: Work is not only for yourself, but also to help others.
> Verse 29: Words can build up or destroy. That is also true on the internet.
> Verse 32: God has forgiven us. That is why we too can forgive one another.
