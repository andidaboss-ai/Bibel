# Ephesians – Chapter 3
### God’s mystery and his great love

---

## The mystery: All nations belong (Verses 1–13)

[1] For this reason I, Paul,
am the prisoner of Christ Jesus
for you people from the nations.
[2] You have surely heard
what task God in his grace has given me for you.
[3] By a revelation the mystery was made known to me,
as I already wrote briefly above.
[4] When you read this,
you can understand
how I understand the mystery of Christ.
[5] In earlier generations
it was not made known to people.
But now God has revealed it to his holy apostles and prophets
through the Spirit:
[6] The people from the nations are fellow heirs.
They are fellow members of the same body.
And they share in the same promise
in Christ Jesus through the Good News.
[7] I serve this Good News.
That is a gift of God’s grace,
which was given to me through the working of his power.
[8] To me,
the very least of all saints,
this grace was given:
I may preach to the nations the unsearchable riches of Christ.
[9] I may show all people
how God carries out his mystery.
For ages it was hidden in God,
who created all things through Jesus Christ.
[10] So now, through the assembly,
the many-sided wisdom of God
is to be made known to the rulers and powers in the heavenly places.
[11] This is according to the eternal purpose
that God carried out in Christ Jesus our Lord.
[12] In him we have boldness
and access to God in confidence
through our faith in him.
[13] So I ask you:
Don’t lose heart because of my troubles for you.
They are your glory.

*mystery* = here: God’s plan that was hidden for a long time and is now revealed

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 1: The letter was written in prison. Paul was often in prison because he preached the Good News.
> Verse 6: The “mystery” is: The people from the nations also belong to God’s people. They are “fellow heirs,” they inherit together with Israel. They stand beside Israel, not in its place.
> Verse 8: “To me, the very least of all saints”: Paul sees himself humbly. He knows that he once persecuted Christians.
> Verse 10: The church, in which people from all nations live together, is a sign of God’s wisdom, even for the invisible powers.
> Verse 12: Through Jesus we may come to God boldly and with confidence.

---

## Prayer for strength and love (Verses 14–21)

[14] For this reason I bow my knees to the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.
[15] From him every family in heaven and on earth is named.
[16] I pray
that, according to the riches of his glory, he will grant you
to be strengthened with power through his Spirit in the inner person,
[17] that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith,
and that you are rooted and grounded in love.
[18] In this way you are to receive strength, together with all the saints,
to understand
how wide and long and high and deep
[19] the love of Christ is.
You are to know it,
even though it surpasses all knowledge.
So may you be filled with all the fullness of God.
[20] God is able to do far more
than all that we ask or think,
through the power
that works in us.
[21] To him be the glory in the assembly
and in Christ Jesus,
to all generations,
forever and ever.
Amen.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 14: At that time people usually prayed standing. Kneeling showed special reverence and urgency.
> Verse 15: In Greek, “Father” (pater) and “family” (patria) sound similar. God is the source of every family and community.
> Verse 17: “Rooted and grounded in love”: like a tree that has deep roots and therefore stands firm.
> Verses 18–19: The love of Christ is so great that you can never fully grasp it. And yet we may experience it.
> Verses 20–21: A song of praise at the end of the first part of the letter: God can do much more than we can imagine.
