# 1 Peter – Chapter 4
### Serve one another with your gifts

---

## A new life (Verses 1–6)

[1] Since Christ suffered for us in the body,
arm yourselves also with the same attitude.
For whoever has suffered in the body
has stopped sinning.
[2] So you are to live the rest of your time
no longer by the desires of people,
but by the will of God.
[3] For it is enough
that in the past you lived
as the Gentiles want:
in wild living,
lusts,
drunkenness,
feasting,
drinking parties
and abominable idol worship.
[4] They are surprised
that you no longer join them
in this wild behavior.
That is why they speak evil of you.
[5] They will have to give account to him
who is ready
to judge the living and the dead.
[6] For this is why the Good News was also preached to the dead:
They were judged like people in the body,
but they are to live with God in the spirit.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 3–4: In the past the Christians had taken part in wild parties and in idol worship. Now they no longer join in. That is why the people around them mock them.
> Verse 6: A difficult verse. It says: The Good News is also for the dead. No one is too far away for God, not even through death (compare 3:19).

---

## Love one another and serve one another (Verses 7–11)

[7] The end of all things is near.
So be sensible and sober,
so that you can pray.
[8] Above all, love one another with all your strength.
For love covers a multitude of sins.
[9] Be hospitable to one another
without grumbling.
[10] Each one has received a gift.
Serve one another with it,
as good managers of the many kinds of God’s grace.
[11] If anyone speaks,
let him do it
as one passing on God’s words.
If anyone serves,
let him do it with the strength
that God gives him.
So in all things God is to be glorified
through Jesus Christ.
To him belong the glory and the power
forever and ever.
Amen.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 8: “For love covers a multitude of sins” recalls Proverbs 10:12. Whoever loves is ready to forgive and not to count up every mistake.
> Important: This doesn’t mean that you should keep violence or abuse secret. Wrongdoing must come to light so that victims are protected.
> Verse 10: Every person has received gifts from God. No gift is unimportant. They are there to help others.

---

## Suffering as a Christian (Verses 12–19)

[12] Beloved,
don’t be surprised at the fiery trial
that has come upon you,
as if something strange were happening to you.
[13] But rejoice,
because you share in the sufferings of Christ.
Then you will also rejoice
with all your heart
when his glory is revealed.
[14] If you are insulted for the name of Christ,
you are blessed.
For the Spirit of glory
and the Spirit of God rests on you.
On their part he is blasphemed,
but on your part he is glorified.
[15] None of you should suffer
because he is a murderer
or a thief
or an evildoer
or because he meddles in other people’s matters.
[16] But if someone suffers
because he is a Christian,
let him not be ashamed.
But let him glorify God for it.
[17] For the time has come
for judgment to begin with the household of God.
But if it begins first with us,
what will happen to those
who don’t obey the Good News of God?
[18] “If the righteous is saved only with difficulty,
what will happen to the ungodly and the sinner?”
[19] So let those also
who suffer according to the will of God
while doing good
entrust their lives to the faithful Creator.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 12: “Fiery trial”: The Christians experienced hostility and persecution.
> Verse 16: Here we find the word “Christian.” It appears only three times in the New Testament (compare Acts 11:26). At that time it was often a name used to mock.
> Verse 17: The time has come “for judgment to begin with the household of God.” That means: The church is first to examine itself honestly, whether it is living rightly.
> Verse 18 quotes Proverbs 11:31 in the form of the old Greek translation.
> Verse 19: A comfort: God is a faithful Creator. We may put our lives into his hands.
> For today: In many countries Christians are still persecuted because of their faith today. Christians are to pray for them and stand up for freedom of religion for all people.
