# 1 Timothy – Chapter 2
### Pray for all people

---

## God wants all people to be saved (Verses 1–7)

[1] So first of all I urge:
Bring requests,
prayers,
intercessions
and thanksgiving before God
for all people,
[2] for kings
and for all
who are in high positions.
In this way we can lead a peaceful and quiet life
in all godliness and dignity.
[3] For this is good
and pleases God our Savior.
[4] He desires
all people to be saved
and to come to full knowledge of the truth.
[5] For there is one God
and one mediator between God and people:
the man Christ Jesus.
[6] He gave himself as a ransom for all.
This is the testimony
that was given at the proper time.
[7] For this I was appointed a preacher and an apostle.
I am telling the truth in Christ,
I am not lying.
I am a teacher of the nations
in faith and truth.

*intercessions* = prayers for other people
*mediator* = someone who brings two sides together
*ransom* = money paid to set a prisoner or slave free

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 1–2: Christians are to pray for all people, including those who govern. At that time that was the Roman emperor, who was not a Christian and sometimes persecuted Christians. Still they were to pray for him.
> For today: Praying for politicians doesn’t mean agreeing with everything. You can pray for someone and still criticize them.
> Verse 4 is very important: God wants all people to be saved. No one is excluded from God’s love.
> Verse 5: “One mediator”: Christ connects God and people. He himself is “the man Christ Jesus”: he knows us.
> Verse 6: “Ransom”: A picture from that time. Prisoners or slaves were bought free with a ransom. Jesus gave his life to set people free.

## How men and women are to pray (Verses 8–15)

[8] So I want
the men in every place to pray,
lifting up holy hands,
without anger and without doubting.
[9] In the same way, the women are to adorn themselves
in decent clothing,
with modesty and good sense,
not with braided hair,
gold,
pearls
or expensive clothing,
[10] but with good works.
That is fitting for women
who profess
to honor God.
[11] Let a woman learn in quietness
with full submission.
[12] But I don’t permit a woman
to teach
or to exercise authority over a man,
but to be in quietness.
[13] For Adam was formed first,
then Eve.
[14] Adam wasn’t deceived,
but the woman was deceived
and has fallen into disobedience.
[15] But she will be saved
through her childbearing,
if they continue
in faith,
love and holiness
with good sense.

> **Important note first**
> Verses 11–15 are among the most disputed verses of the Bible. In history they were often used to keep women down. We render them closely following the WEB and explain them fairly.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 8: At that time people often prayed standing with raised hands. The men are to pray without quarreling and anger. Apparently there was quarreling in the church.
> Verses 9–10: This is not about a ban on jewelry in itself. At that time rich women showed off their wealth with expensive jewelry and elaborate hairstyles. The letter says: In worship the rich are not to show themselves off. Good works are more important.
> Verse 11: For that time it was new that women were to learn at all. That was progress.
> Verse 12: How to understand this verse is very disputed among Christians:
> Some churches still understand it as a general command: Women are not to preach or lead in the church.
> Many other churches say: The letter responds to a specific situation in Ephesus. There some women were probably spreading false teachings (compare 5:13 and 2 Timothy 3:6). That is why the rule does not apply for all times. These churches have women pastors, preachers and bishops.
> They point out that Paul too worked with women who taught and led: Priscilla, together with her husband, taught the preacher Apollos (Acts 18:26). Phoebe was a servant of the church (Romans 16:1). Junia was highly regarded among the apostles (Romans 16:7). Women prayed and prophesied in worship (1 Corinthians 11:5).
> Verses 13–14 refer to the story of creation (Genesis 2 and 3). Important: Elsewhere the Bible says that sin came into the world through Adam (Romans 5:12). Eve is not more guilty than Adam. Women are not easier to deceive than men.
> Verse 15 is hard to understand. It doesn’t mean that women have to earn salvation through children. Salvation is God’s gift through faith. Some interpreters think it is about the birth of Jesus through a woman. Others think: Women are not to look down on their lives as women and mothers, as some false teachers demanded (compare 4:3). Women without children are worth just as much before God.
> Very important: These verses give no one the right to put women down, to silence them or to deny them their dignity. Man and woman are both created in God’s image (Genesis 1:27) and are of equal worth before God. In Christ they are *one* (Galatians 3:28).
