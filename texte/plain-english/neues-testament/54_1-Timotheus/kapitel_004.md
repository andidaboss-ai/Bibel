# 1 Timothy – Chapter 4
### Be an example

---

## Everything God created is good (Verses 1–5)

[1] But the Spirit says clearly:
In later times some will fall away from the faith.
They will listen to deceiving spirits
and to teachings of demons.
[2] This happens through the hypocrisy of people
who tell lies.
Their conscience is branded as with a hot iron.
[3] They forbid marriage.
And they demand
that people stay away from certain foods.
But God created these
so that those who believe
and know the truth
may receive them with thanksgiving.
[4] For everything
that God created
is good.
Nothing is to be rejected
if it is received with thanksgiving.
[5] For it is made holy
through the word of God and prayer.

> **What does this mean?**
> Hypocrisy means: pretending to be good while acting differently.
> Verses 1–3: There were teachers who forbade marriage and banned certain foods. They thought everything physical was bad.
> Verse 4 is very important: Everything God created is good. This recalls the story of creation: *God saw that it was good* (Genesis 1). The body, food and marriage are good gifts from God.
> Verse 5: Giving thanks before eating: Saying grace at meals reminds us that food is a gift from God.
> For today: Whoever does without certain foods for personal, health or religious reasons is doing nothing wrong. Jewish food laws are not meant here either. The letter is directed against teachers who force bans on others and speak badly of God’s creation.

---

## Train yourself in godliness (Verses 6–11)

[6] If you put these things before the brothers,
you will be a good servant of Christ Jesus.
You are nourished by the words of the faith
and of the good teaching
that you have followed.
[7] But reject godless old wives’ fables.
Rather, train yourself in godliness.
[8] For bodily training has some value.
But godliness has value in all things.
It holds promise for the life that is now
and for the life to come.
[9] This saying is trustworthy
and deserves
to be accepted by all.
[10] For this is why we labor
and suffer insults:
because we have put our trust in the living God.
He is the Savior of all people,
especially of those who believe.
[11] Command and teach these things.

> **What does this mean?**
> Godliness means: a life that honors God.
> Verse 7: “Old wives’ fables” was a fixed expression at that time for silly, made-up stories. It is no statement about older women. Today we might say *nonsense* or *fairy tales*.
> Verses 7–8: A picture from sports: Just as you train your body, you are also to *train* your faith, for example through prayer, reading the Bible and good deeds. Sports are good, but faith is even more important.
> Verse 10: God is the “Savior of all people.” His love is for everyone, not only for a small group.

---

## Be an example (Verses 12–16)

[12] Let no one look down on you
because you are young.
But be an example to those who believe:
in speech,
in conduct,
in love,
in spirit,
in faith
and in purity.
[13] Until I come,
give attention to the public reading,
to encouraging
and to teaching.
[14] Don’t neglect the gift
that is in you.
It was given to you through prophecy,
when the elders laid their hands on you.
[15] Take care of these things.
Give yourself wholly to them,
so that all may see
your progress.
[16] Pay attention to yourself and to your teaching.
Continue in these things.
For if you do this,
you will save both yourself
and those
who hear you.

> **What does this mean?**
> Elders means: leaders of the church.
> Verse 12: Timothy was still fairly young. The letter says: Age is not what matters. Young people too can be an example in faith.
> Verse 13: “The public reading”: In worship the holy Scriptures were read aloud, at that time mainly the Scriptures of Israel, our Old Testament.
> Verse 14: The laying on of hands is a sign of blessing and of being sent for a task. To this day many churches bless people for a ministry in this way, for example at ordination.
> Verse 16: Whoever teaches others should also pay attention to their own life. Words and deeds should fit together.
