# Revelation – Chapter 15
### The song of Moses and of the Lamb

---

## The victors sing (Verses 1–4)

[1] I saw another sign in the sky.
It was great and amazing.
I saw seven angels with the seven last plagues.
For with them God’s anger comes to an end.
[2] I saw something like a sea of glass mixed with fire.
I saw the people who had won the victory over the beast,
over his image, and over the number of his name.
They were standing on the sea of glass.
They had harps from God.
[3] They sang the song of Moses, the servant of God,
and the song of the Lamb.
They sang,
“Great and amazing are your works, Lord God, the Almighty!
Your ways are right and true, you King of the nations.
[4] Who would not fear you, Lord, and give glory to your name?
For only you are holy.
All the nations will come and worship before you.
For your right and just acts have been made known.”

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 1: “With them God’s anger comes to an end”: God’s anger has an end. After it comes what is new.
> Verses 2–3: The sea of glass recalls the Red Sea. Israel went through the sea and was saved. Afterward Moses sang a song with the people (Exodus 15). In the same way, those who are saved sing here by the sea.
> “The song of Moses and the song of the Lamb”: The rescue of Israel out of Egypt and the rescue through Jesus belong together. It is the same God who saves.
> Verses 3–4: Many words in this song come from the Psalms and the prophets (for example Psalm 86:9 and Jeremiah 10:7).
> Verse 4: “All the nations will come”: That is a great hope. In the end all nations are to know God and honor him.

---

## The seven angels with the bowls (Verses 5–8)

[5] After these things I looked.
The temple in heaven was opened.
It is the tent of the testimony.
[6] The seven angels who had the seven plagues came out of the temple.
They were dressed in pure, bright linen.
They wore golden sashes around their chests.
[7] One of the four living creatures gave the seven angels seven golden bowls.
The bowls were full of the anger of God, who lives forever and ever.
[8] The temple was filled with smoke from the glory of God and from his power.
No one could go into the temple until the seven plagues of the seven angels were finished.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 5: “The tent of the testimony” means the holy tent in the wilderness. In it was the ark of the covenant with the Ten Commandments (Exodus 40).
> Verse 8: The smoke recalls how God’s glory filled the holy tent and later the temple (Exodus 40:34–35 and 1 Kings 8:10–11). God is very near, but also holy and beyond reach.
> The bowls are poured out in the next chapter. Again it is true: These are pictures for God’s No to evil, not permission for people to punish others.
