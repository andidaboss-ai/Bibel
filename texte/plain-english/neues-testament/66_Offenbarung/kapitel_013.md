# Revelation – Chapter 13
### The two beasts

---

> **Important note first**
> This chapter is about two “beasts” and about the number 666. There are very many wild guesses about this chapter. Again and again people have claimed that the beast is a certain politician, a church, a people, a technology, a barcode, a chip or a vaccine. All these claims have made people afraid, and they were wrong.
> Many conspiracy myths use this chapter. Some of them are directed against Jews. That is dangerous and wrong.
> For the first readers it was clear: The beast is a picture for the Roman Empire and for the emperor who had himself worshiped like a god. But the message is also true today: Christians are not to worship any power that puts itself in the place of God.

---

## The beast from the sea (Verses 1–10)

[1] Then I stood on the sand of the sea.
I saw a beast coming up out of the sea.
It had 10 horns and 7 heads.
On its horns were 10 crowns.
On its heads were names that insulted God.
[2] The beast I saw looked like a leopard.
Its feet were like a bear’s feet.
Its mouth was like a lion’s mouth.
The dragon gave the beast his power, his throne, and great authority.
[3] One of its heads looked as if it had received a deadly wound.
But the deadly wound was healed.
The whole earth was amazed at the beast.
[4] People worshiped the dragon, because he had given his authority to the beast.
And they worshiped the beast.
They said,
“Who is like the beast?
Who is able to fight against him?”
[5] The beast was given a mouth that spoke proud words and insults against God.
It was given authority to make war for 42 months.
[6] It opened its mouth to insult God.
It insulted his name and his dwelling place,
and those who live in heaven.
[7] It was allowed to make war against the saints and to defeat them.
It was given authority over every tribe, people, language, and nation.
[8] All who live on the earth will worship the beast.
These are all the people whose names have not been written in the book of life of the Lamb who was killed.
Their names have not been written there since the creation of the world.
[9] Whoever has ears should listen.
[10] If anyone is to be taken away as a prisoner, he will be taken away as a prisoner.
If anyone is to be killed with the sword, he must be killed.
This is where the saints need to endure and keep their faith.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 1–2: The beast recalls the four beasts in the book of Daniel (Daniel 7). There the beasts stand for cruel world empires. Here all the beasts are united in one. For the first readers this was Rome. For them, Rome came “over the sea.”
> Verse 2: The dragon, that is, the devil, gives the beast its power. When a power oppresses people and has itself worshiped, evil is behind it.
> Verse 3: The healed wound: Perhaps a hint at Emperor Nero. After his death people told each other that he would come back. The beast imitates the Lamb: The Lamb was really dead and lives (5:6). The beast only pretends.
> Verse 4: “Who is like the beast?” is an evil imitation of the praise for God: “Who is like you, LORD” (compare Exodus 15:11).
> Verse 5: The power of the beast is limited: only 42 months. And it is only “given” to it. God stays in control.
> Verse 10 recalls Jeremiah 15:2. Christians are not to strike back with violence. They are to stand firm. Some manuscripts say: Whoever kills with the sword must be killed with the sword.

## The beast from the earth (Verses 11–18)

[11] I saw another beast coming up out of the earth.
It had two horns like a lamb,
but it spoke like a dragon.
[12] It uses all the authority of the first beast in its presence.
It makes the earth and the people who live on it worship the first beast,
the one whose deadly wound was healed.
[13] It does great signs.
It even makes fire come down from the sky to the earth while people watch.
[14] It deceives my own people who live on the earth.
It does this with the signs it was allowed to do in front of the beast.
It tells the people who live on the earth to make an image of the beast.
That is the beast that was wounded by the sword and yet lived.
[15] It was allowed to give breath to the image of the beast.
So the image of the beast could even speak.
And it could have everyone killed who would not worship the image of the beast.
[16] It forces everyone to receive a mark on their right hand or on their forehead:
the small and the great,
the rich and the poor,
the free and the slave.
[17] No one can buy or sell unless he has the mark.
The mark is the name of the beast or the number of its name.
[18] This calls for wisdom.
Whoever has understanding should figure out the number of the beast.
For it is the number of a man.
Its number is 666.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 11: The second beast looks harmless like a lamb, but it speaks like the dragon. It is a picture for false prophets and for propaganda. Later it is called “the false prophet” (16:13).
> At that time there were priests and officials in the cities who made sure that everyone worshiped the emperor. Whoever didn’t take part got into trouble.
> Verse 14: The WEB, our English source text, has “my own people.” Most manuscripts have only: It deceives those who live on the earth.
> Verses 16–17: The “mark” shows whom you belong to. It is the evil imitation of God’s seal on the forehead (7:3). Whoever wanted to be part of the guilds and the market often had to take part in emperor worship. Whoever refused lost his work.
> Verse 18: In Greek and Hebrew, letters also had number values. Many scholars think: If you write “Emperor Nero” in Hebrew letters, it adds up to 666. Some manuscripts have 616. That also fits another way of spelling Nero.
> The number six is one less than seven, the number of perfection. 666 then means: The beast wants to be like God, but it stays imperfect and fails.
