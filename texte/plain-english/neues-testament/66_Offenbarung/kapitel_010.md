# Revelation – Chapter 10
### The angel with the little scroll

---

## A mighty angel and seven thunders (Verses 1–7)

[1] I saw a mighty angel coming down out of the sky.
He was dressed in a cloud.
A rainbow was over his head.
His face was like the sun.
His feet were like pillars of fire.
[2] In his hand he had a little book that was open.
He put his right foot on the sea and his left foot on the land.
[3] He shouted with a loud voice, like a lion roars.
When he shouted, the seven thunders spoke with their voices.
[4] When the seven thunders had spoken, I was about to write.
But I heard a voice from the sky.
It said,
“Keep secret what the seven thunders said.
Don’t write it down.”
[5] The angel I saw standing on the sea and on the land
lifted his right hand up to the sky.
[6] He made a solemn promise.
He swore by the one who lives forever and ever.
He is the one who created heaven and everything in it,
the earth and everything in it,
and the sea and everything in it.
The angel swore: “There will be no more waiting.
[7] The seventh angel will soon blow his trumpet.
In the days when his voice is heard,
God’s secret plan will be completed.
It will happen just as God announced it to his servants, the prophets.”

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 2: The angel stands on the sea and on the land. That means: His message is for the whole world.
> Verse 4: John may not write everything down. There are things that only God knows. We don’t have to know everything about the future, and we can’t.
> Verses 6–7: “There will be no more waiting”: For the persecuted Christians who asked “How long?” (6:10), this is an answer. God will finish his work.
> “God’s secret plan” is his plan to save the world and to make everything good. The prophets already announced it.

## John eats the little scroll (Verses 8–11)

[8] The voice I had heard from heaven spoke with me again.
It said,
“Go and take the book that is open in the hand of the angel.
He is the one who stands on the sea and on the land.”
[9] So I went to the angel.
I told him to give me the little book.
He said to me,
“Take it and eat it.
It will make your stomach bitter.
But in your mouth it will be as sweet as honey.”
[10] I took the little book out of the angel’s hand and ate it.
In my mouth it was as sweet as honey.
But when I had eaten it, my stomach became bitter.
[11] Then they told me,
“You must prophesy again about many peoples, nations, languages, and kings.”

> **What does this mean?**
> This recalls the prophet Ezekiel. He too had to eat a scroll, and it was as sweet as honey (Ezekiel 2:8–3:3).
> “Eating the book” means: taking God’s word completely into yourself before you pass it on.
> Sweet and bitter at the same time: God’s word is beautiful and good. But it can also be hard to pass it on, especially when it is about suffering and judgment.
> Verse 11: John receives a new task. His message is for all peoples.
