# 2 Corinthians – Chapter 9
### God loves a cheerful giver

---

## Be ready (Verses 1–5)

[1] It is really not necessary
for me to write to you about the service to the saints.
[2] For I know your readiness.
I am proud of it,
and I tell the people of Macedonia about it:
Achaia has been ready since last year.
And your zeal has stirred up very many of them.
[3] But I have sent the brothers,
so that our boasting about you in this matter may not be empty,
and so that you may be ready,
just as I said.
[4] Otherwise it could happen
that people from Macedonia come with me
and find you unprepared.
Then we would be put to shame in our confidence,
to say nothing of you.
[5] So I thought it necessary
to ask the brothers
to go ahead to you
and prepare the generous gift
that you promised before.
It should be ready
as a gift given generously
and not out of greed.

> **What does this mean?**
> Paul writes more about the collection for Jerusalem. Achaia is the province in which Corinth lies.
> Verses 2–4: Paul has praised the church in Corinth to others. Now he hopes that they will also keep their promise.

---

## Giving with joy (Verses 6–15)

[6] Remember this:
He who sows sparingly
will also reap sparingly.
And he who sows generously
will also reap generously.
[7] Let each one give
as he has decided in his heart,
not reluctantly
or under pressure.
For God loves a cheerful giver.
[8] And God is able to give you all grace in rich measure,
so that you always have enough in everything
and have plenty left over for every good work.
[9] As it is written:
“He has scattered abroad.
He has given to the poor.
His righteousness remains forever.”
[10] He who gives seed to the sower
and bread for food
will also give you seed for sowing and multiply it.
And he will increase the fruits of your righteousness.
[11] So you will be rich in everything
and can give generously.
And through us this leads
to people giving thanks to God.
[12] For this service of giving
not only supplies what the saints lack,
but also overflows in many thanks to God.
[13] For through this service it becomes clear
what you are like.
And those who receive it glorify God
because you obey your confession of the Good News of Christ,
and because of your generous sharing with them and with all.
[14] And they pray for you
and long for you,
because of the surpassing grace of God
which is in you.
[15] Thanks be to God for his gift,
which is too wonderful for words!

> **What does this mean?**
> Sow means: to put seeds in the ground.
> Reap means: to harvest.
> Verse 6: A picture from farming: Whoever sows a lot can harvest a lot. Generosity bears fruit.
> Verse 7: Giving should be voluntary and joyful. No one should be put under pressure.
> Important: Some preachers promise that God will make people rich if they give a lot of money to their church. This text doesn’t teach that. Paul is concerned with help for the poor, not with wealth for the givers. No one should give more than they can (8:12).
> Verse 9 quotes Psalm 112:9. Verse 10 recalls Isaiah 55:10.
> Verses 12–14: The gift connects Christians from different peoples. The Christians in Jerusalem thank God and pray for the Christians in Corinth.
> Verse 15: “His gift, which is too wonderful for words”: Paul probably means Jesus Christ himself.
