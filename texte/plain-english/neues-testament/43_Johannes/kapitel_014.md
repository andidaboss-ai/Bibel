# John – Chapter 14
### The way, the truth, and the life

---

## “I am the way” (Verses 1–14)

[1] “Don’t let your heart be upset.
Believe in God.
Believe in me too.
[2] In my Father’s house there are many places to live.
If that were not true, I would have told you.
I am going there to get a place ready for you.
[3] I will go and get a place ready for you.
Then I will come back and take you to be with me.
I want you to be where I am.
[4] You know where I am going.
And you know the way.”
[5] Thomas said to him,
“Lord, we don’t know where you are going.
So how can we know the way?”
[6] Jesus said to him,
“I am the way, the truth, and the life.
No one comes to the Father except through me.
[7] If you had really known me, you would have known my Father too.
From now on, you know him.
And you have seen him.”
[8] Philip said to him,
“Lord, show us the Father.
That will be enough for us.”
[9] Jesus said to him,
“Philip, I have been with you all for such a long time.
And you still don’t know me?
Whoever has seen me has seen the Father.
So how can you say, ‘Show us the Father’?
[10] Don’t you believe that I am in the Father and the Father is in me?
The words I say to you are not my own words.
The Father lives in me.
He is the one doing his works.
[11] Believe me when I say that I am in the Father and the Father is in me.
Or else believe because of the works themselves.
[12] I tell you the truth:
Whoever believes in me will do the works that I do.
He will do even greater works than these,
because I am going to my Father.
[13] I will do whatever you ask in my name.
Then the Father will be glorified in the Son.
[14] If you ask for anything in my name, I will do it.

> **What does this mean?**
> John 14–17 are called the “farewell speeches.” Jesus is speaking with his disciples at the last meal. He is preparing them for his death and his return to the Father.
> Verses 1–3: These words are often read at funerals. With God there is room for all who belong to Jesus.
> Verse 6: “I am the way, the truth, and the life” is the sixth “I am” saying. For Christians Jesus is the way to God.
> Important: Christians understand this sentence differently when it comes to other religions. The Catholic Church teaches: The church rejects nothing that is true and holy in other religions (*Nostra Aetate*, 1965). Many Protestant Christians stress something similar: God’s grace is greater than our understanding. Judaism has its own covenant given by God (Romans 11:29). This verse must never be used to look down on people of other religions or to put pressure on them.
> Verse 9: “Whoever has seen me has seen the Father.” In Jesus we see what God is like.
> Verses 13–14: Asking “in my name” means: asking in a way that fits Jesus. It is not a magic formula to get everything you want.

## The promise of the Holy Spirit (Verses 15–31)

[15] If you love me, do what I command.
[16] I will ask the Father.
And he will give you another Counselor, who will be with you forever.
[17] He is the Spirit of truth.
The world can’t accept him,
because the world doesn’t see him and doesn’t know him.
But you know him,
because he lives with you, and he will be in you.
[18] I will not leave you alone like orphans.
I will come to you.
[19] In a little while, the world will not see me anymore.
But you will see me.
Because I live, you will live too.
[20] On that day you will know that I am in my Father.
You will know that you are in me, and I am in you.
[21] Whoever knows my commands and does them is the one who loves me.
My Father will love the one who loves me.
I will love him too, and I will show myself to him.”
[22] Then Judas spoke to him.
This was not Judas Iscariot.
He said,
“Lord, why will you show yourself to us, but not to the world?”
[23] Jesus answered him,
“Anyone who loves me will hold on to my word.
My Father will love him.
We will come to him and live with him.
[24] Whoever doesn’t love me doesn’t hold on to my words.
The word you hear is not mine.
It comes from the Father who sent me.
[25] “I have told you these things while I am still with you.
[26] But the Father will send the Counselor in my name.
The Counselor is the Holy Spirit.
He will teach you everything.
He will help you remember everything I have told you.
[27] I leave peace with you.
I give you my peace.
I don’t give it to you the way the world gives.
Don’t let your heart be upset or afraid.
[28] You heard me tell you, ‘I am going away, and I will come back to you.’
If you loved me, you would be glad that I said, ‘I am going to my Father.’
The Father is greater than I.
[29] I have told you this now, before it happens.
Then, when it happens, you will believe.
[30] I will not talk with you much longer.
The ruler of this world is coming.
He has no power over me.
[31] But the world must know that I love the Father.
So I do just what the Father has commanded me.
Get up, let’s go from here.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 16: “Counselor” (in Greek “Paraclete”) means: someone who stands by your side, comforts, advises and defends. The Holy Spirit is meant.
> Verse 18: “I will not leave you alone like orphans.” Jesus does not abandon his disciples.
> Verse 22: This Judas is a different disciple, not Judas Iscariot. Luke calls him Judas the son of James (Luke 6:16).
> Verse 23: God wants to live with the people who love him.
> Verse 26: The Holy Spirit helps the disciples to remember Jesus’ words and to understand them. The Gospels also rest on this.
> Verse 27: “Peace” is “shalom” in Hebrew. That was (and is) the Jewish farewell greeting. Jesus’ peace is more than quiet: It is God’s wholeness and well-being.
> Verse 28: “The Father is greater than I”: Christians have thought a lot about this. Many understand it this way: As a human being Jesus places himself under the Father, and yet he is one with the Father (10:30).
> Verse 31: “Let’s go from here”: Even so, more speeches follow in chapters 15–17.
> For today: Whoever is afraid may hold on to Jesus’ words: “Don’t let your heart be upset.”
