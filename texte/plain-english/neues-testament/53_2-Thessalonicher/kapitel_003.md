# 2 Thessalonians – Chapter 3
### Pray and work quietly

---

## Pray for us (Verses 1–5)

[1] Finally,
brothers:
Pray for us,
that the word of the Lord may spread rapidly
and be honored,
just as it was with you,
[2] and that we may be rescued from unreasonable and evil people.
For not all have faith.
[3] But the Lord is faithful.
He will strengthen you
and guard you from the evil one.
[4] We have confidence in the Lord concerning you:
You do
what we command you,
and you will keep doing it.
[5] May the Lord direct your hearts
into God’s love
and into the perseverance of Christ.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 1: Paul asks the church for prayer. Apostles, too, need the support of others.
> Verse 3: “The Lord is faithful”: People can disappoint, but God stays faithful.
> Verse 5: The Lord is to direct hearts “into the perseverance of Christ.” Jesus held out even in suffering. He gives strength to hold out yourself.

---

## Work quietly (Verses 6–15)

[6] Brothers,
in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ we command you:
Keep away from every brother
who lives in rebellion
and not according to the tradition
that you received from us.
[7] For you yourselves know
how you ought to imitate us.
We didn’t live in rebellion among you.
[8] We didn’t eat anyone’s bread without paying for it.
But with labor and toil
we worked night and day,
so that we would not be a burden to any of you.
[9] Not
because we don’t have the right.
But we wanted to make ourselves an example to you,
so that you would imitate us.
[10] For even when we were with you,
we told you this:
“If anyone is not willing to work,
don’t let him eat.”
[11] For we hear
that some among you live in rebellion.
They don’t work at all,
but are busybodies.
[12] Such people we command
and urge in the Lord Jesus Christ:
They are to work quietly
and eat their own bread.
[13] But you,
brothers,
don’t be weary in doing
what is right.
[14] But if anyone doesn’t obey our word in this letter,
take note of that person
and have nothing to do with him,
so that he may be ashamed.
[15] Don’t count him as an enemy.
Instead, warn him as a brother.

> **What does this mean?**
> Busybodies means: people who meddle in other people’s affairs.
> Verses 6–11: Some in the church had stopped working. Perhaps they thought Jesus would come soon anyway. They let others provide for them and meddled in everything.
> Verses 8–9: As an apostle Paul would have had the right to be provided for by the church. He gave up this right and worked himself.
> Verse 10: Very important: It says here “If anyone is not willing to work,” not *if anyone is not able to work*. This sentence is not directed against the sick, people with disabilities, the elderly, children or the unemployed. The church is to help them. The Bible calls again and again for caring for the poor and the weak. This verse must not be used to look down on people in need.
> Verse 13: “Don’t be weary in doing what is right”: Even if some take advantage of people’s readiness to help, Christians are to keep helping.
> Verses 14–15: The distance is meant to help the person change their mind. But he remains a brother, not an enemy. The goal is reconciliation, not exclusion.

---

## Peace and greeting (Verses 16–18)

[16] May the Lord of peace himself
give you peace
at all times
and in all ways.
The Lord be with you all.
[17] I,
Paul,
write this greeting with my own hand.
This is the sign in every letter.
This is how I write.
[18] The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ
be with you all.
Amen.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 17: Paul dictated his letters. He wrote the closing greeting himself. That way the church could recognize that the letter was genuine. This was important because there were probably forged letters (compare 2:2).

## Looking back: What have we read in the second letter to the Thessalonians?

> **What does this mean?**
> **God is just:** He sees the suffering of the persecuted. He will comfort them and judge injustice. Revenge belongs to God alone (chapter 1).
> **Don’t let yourselves be confused:** The day of the Lord is not here yet. No one should spread fear or calculate dates (chapter 2).
> **Staying alert:** There are powers that let themselves be worshiped like God and lead people astray with lies. Christians are to love the truth. But no one should put the label *Antichrist* on other people (chapter 2).
> **God has chosen you:** Comfort and hope for the church (chapter 2).
> **Keep working quietly:** Waiting for Jesus is no reason to give up normal life. Whoever can work should work. Whoever can’t should be helped (chapter 3).
> **For today:** God is faithful. That is why Christians don’t need to be afraid of the future. They can live calmly and responsibly and do good.
> **What comes next?** Next comes the first letter to Timothy. It is addressed to a coworker of Paul and gives advice for life in the church.
