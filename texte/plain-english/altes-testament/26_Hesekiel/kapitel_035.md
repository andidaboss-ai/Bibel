# Ezekiel – Chapter 35
### Against Mount Seir

---

## Hatred that never ends (Verses 1–9)

[1] The word of the LORD came to me again. He said,
[2] “Son of man, turn your face against Mount Seir,
and prophesy against it.
[3] Tell it,
‘This is what the Lord GOD says:
“Look, I am against you, Mount Seir.
I will stretch out my hand against you.
I will make you a ruin and a horror.
[4] I will destroy your cities,
and you will be a ruin.
Then you will know that I am the LORD.
[5] “‘“Your hatred never ended.
You handed over the people of Israel to the power of the sword
at the time of their disaster,
at the time when their guilt came to its end.
[6] So as surely as I live,” says the Lord GOD,
“I will hand you over to bloodshed,
and bloodshed will chase you.
Because you did not hate bloodshed,
bloodshed will chase you.
[7] So I will make Mount Seir a horror and a ruin.
I will cut off from it everyone who comes and goes.
[8] I will fill its mountains with its dead.
Those killed by the sword will fall on your hills,
in your valleys, and in all your streams.
[9] I will make you a ruin forever.
No one will live in your cities.
Then you will know that I am the LORD.

> **What does this mean?**
> Seir is the mountain country of Edom, south of the Dead Sea. The Edomites were descendants of Esau, the twin brother of Jacob (Israel). The hatred between the two peoples ran through their whole history, just as it did between the two brothers (Genesis 27).
> Verse 5: When Jerusalem fell, Edom did not protect the refugees. Instead it handed them over to the enemy (see Obadiah 14).
> Verse 6: “Because you did not hate bloodshed” – whoever does not reject violence will be caught up by violence himself. Violence creates new violence.
> Why is this chapter here, in the middle of the words of hope? Because Chapter 36 speaks to the mountains of Israel. Here the mountains of Edom, which wanted to take over Israel’s land, and there the mountains of Israel, which will become green again. The two belong together.

---

## You wanted to take the land (Verses 10–15)

[10] “‘“You have said,
‘These two nations and these two countries will be mine,
and we will take them over.’
But the LORD was there.
[11] So as surely as I live,” says the Lord GOD,
“I will treat you with the same anger
and the same jealousy that you showed in your hatred against them.
And I will make myself known among them
when I judge you.
[12] You will know that I, the LORD, have heard all your insults
that you spoke against the mountains of Israel.
You said,
‘They lie in ruins.
They have been given to us to devour.’
[13] You have boasted against me with your mouth.
You have spoken many words against me.
I have heard it.”
[14] This is what the Lord GOD says:
“While the whole earth is glad,
I will make you a ruin.
[15] You were glad over the land that belonged to the people of Israel,
because it lay in ruins.
I will do the same to you.
You will be a ruin, Mount Seir,
and all of Edom, all of it.
Then they will know that I am the LORD.”’”

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 10: Edom wanted to grab the empty land of Israel and Judah (“these two nations”). But Edom had forgotten: “The LORD was there.” The land belongs to God, not to whoever takes it the fastest.
> Verse 13: “I have heard it.” God also hears mocking and hateful words.
> Verse 15: Again it is about being glad when others suffer. As you have done to others, so it will be done to you.
> Important: Edom no longer exists as a people today. This chapter is no reason for hatred against people or nations in this region today.
