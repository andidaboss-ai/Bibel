# Ezekiel – Chapter 22
### The city of blood

---

## The city that sheds blood (Verses 1–5)

[1] The word of the LORD came to me again. He said,
[2] “You, son of man, will you judge?
Will you judge the city of blood?
Then show her all the disgusting things she does.
[3] You must say,
‘This is what the Lord GOD says:
“You are a city that sheds blood inside her walls,
so that her time of judgment comes.
You make idols for yourself, and they make you unclean.
[4] You have become guilty because of the blood you have shed.
You have become unclean because of the idols you have made.
You have brought your days near.
You have come to the end of your years.
So I have made you something the nations scorn,
and something all the countries mock.
[5] Those who are near you and those who are far from you will mock you.
You have a bad name, and you are full of turmoil.

> **What does this mean?**
> Jerusalem, the “holy city,” is called the “city of blood” here. That is a shocking name. It shows: In this city a lot of injustice and violence was done.
> Two main charges run through the chapter: violence (“shedding blood”) and the worship of idols. The two belong together: Whoever no longer honors God also loses respect for the lives of other people.

---

## The list of sins (Verses 6–16)

[6] “‘“Look, the princes of Israel have been in you.
Each of them used all his power
to shed blood.
[7] In you they have treated father and mother with contempt.
Among you they have oppressed the foreigner.
In you they have wronged the orphan and the widow.
[8] You have despised the things that are holy to me,
and you have treated my Sabbaths as if they were not holy.
[9] In you there have been men who tell lies about others, so that blood is shed.
In you they have eaten on the mountains.
Among you they have done shameful things.
[10] In you men have slept with their fathers’ wives.
In you men have forced themselves on women
during their monthly time when they were unclean.
[11] One man has done a disgusting thing with his neighbor’s wife.
Another has shamefully abused his daughter-in-law.
Another in you has forced himself on his sister,
his father’s daughter.
[12] In you they have taken bribes to shed blood.
You have charged interest and profit on loans.
You have greedily made money from your neighbors by oppressing them.
And you have forgotten me,”
says the Lord GOD.
[13] “So look, I strike my hands together
against the dishonest profit you have made,
and against the blood that has been shed in you.
[14] Will your heart stay strong?
Will your hands stay strong
in the days when I deal with you?
I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it.
[15] I will scatter you among the nations.
I will spread you out through the countries.
I will remove your filth from you.
[16] You will be dishonored in yourself in front of the nations.
Then you will know that I am the LORD.”’”

> **What does this mean?**
> Here there is a long list of wrongs. Almost all of them are about how people treat other people:
> – The powerful use their power to kill.
> – Parents are treated with contempt.
> – Foreigners, orphans and widows, that is, the weakest people, are oppressed. All through the Bible, God takes the side of these people again and again (see Exodus 22:21–24; Deuteronomy 10:18).
> – Lies about others that lead to death.
> – Sexual violence and abuse in families.
> – Bribes, unfair interest, taking advantage of others out of greed.
> Verse 9: “Eaten on the mountains” means: at the places where idols were worshiped.
> Verses 10–11: “Forced himself on” means sexual violence here: Women were abused against their will, even inside the family. The Bible clearly calls this wrong. The guilt is always with the one who does it, never with the victim.
> If you have experienced sexual violence or abuse: It is not your fault, and you are not alone. In the US, call or text 988. In the UK and Ireland, call Samaritans at 116 123. Elsewhere, find help at findahelpline.com. In the US: Childhelp 1-800-422-4453, RAINN 1-800-656-4673. For domestic violence, US: 1-800-799-7233. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the US, 999 in the UK).
> Verse 12: At the end of the list it says: “And you have forgotten me.” That is the root of everything.

## In the furnace (Verses 17–22)

[17] The word of the LORD came to me. He said,
[18] “Son of man, the people of Israel have become slag to me.
All of them are bronze, tin, iron, and lead in the middle of the furnace.
They are the slag left over from silver.
[19] So this is what the Lord GOD says:
‘All of you have become slag.
So look, I will gather you into the middle of Jerusalem.
[20] People gather silver, bronze, iron, lead, and tin
into the middle of the furnace.
They blow on the fire to melt the metal.
In the same way I will gather you in my anger and in my fury.
I will put you there and melt you.
[21] Yes, I will gather you
and blow on you with the fire of my fury.
And you will be melted inside the city.
[22] As silver is melted in the middle of the furnace,
so you will be melted inside the city.
And you will know that I, the LORD,
have poured out my fury on you.’”

> **What does this mean?**
> A picture from working with metal: To make silver pure, it is melted in a furnace. The less valuable metals separate from it as “slag.” Slag is the worthless waste that is left when metal is melted.
> Israel was supposed to be valuable silver. But it is only slag now. Jerusalem becomes a furnace: During the siege, everyone is crowded together in the city, and the “fire” of judgment comes over them.
> In other texts, the furnace is also a picture of making pure. That means God cleans his people so that the good is left over (Isaiah 1:25; Malachi 3:2–3).

## No one stands in the gap (Verses 23–31)

[23] The word of the LORD came to me. He said,
[24] “Son of man, say to her,
‘You are a land that has not been made clean.
No rain falls on you on the day of anger.’
[25] Her prophets inside her have made a secret plan together.
They are like a roaring lion tearing its prey.
They have devoured people.
They take treasure and valuable things.
They have made many women into widows inside her.
[26] Her priests have done violence to my law.
They have treated my holy things as if they were not holy.
They have made no difference between what is holy and what is ordinary.
They have not taught people
to tell the difference between what is unclean and what is clean.
They have closed their eyes to my Sabbaths.
So people treat me as if I were not holy.
[27] Her princes inside her are like wolves tearing their prey.
They shed blood and destroy people
to get dishonest profit.
[28] Her prophets have covered over these things for them, like whitewash on a wall.
They see false visions
and tell them lies about the future.
They say, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says,’
when the LORD has not spoken.
[29] The people of the land have oppressed others and robbed them.
Yes, they have troubled the poor and the needy.
They have oppressed the foreigner unjustly.
[30] “I looked for a man among them
who would build up the wall
and stand in the gap in front of me for the land,
so that I would not destroy it.
But I found no one.
[31] So I have poured out my anger on them.
I have destroyed them with the fire of my fury.
I have brought on their own heads what they have done,”
says the Lord GOD.

> **What does this mean?**
> Here all the groups in society are named, and all of them have failed:
> – the prophets (verses 25 and 28): They lie and make themselves rich.
> – the priests (verse 26): They no longer teach God’s commandments.
> – the princes (verse 27): They are like wolves, greedy and violent.
> – the people of the land (verse 29): Even ordinary people oppress the poor and foreigners.
> Verse 30: A moving verse: God looks for one single person who will “stand in the gap.” That means someone who stands in the hole of the broken wall, stands up for his people, and prays. Moses did this once (Psalm 106:23). But God finds no one.
> This verse is a question for us today: Where are people needed who stand up for others, who pray, help, and work for justice? God is looking for people like this.
