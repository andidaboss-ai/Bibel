# Ezekiel – Chapter 25
### Words against the neighboring nations

---

## Against Ammon (Verses 1–7)

[1] The word of the LORD came to me. He said,
[2] “Son of man, turn your face toward the people of Ammon,
and prophesy against them.
[3] Tell the Ammonites,
‘Hear the word of the Lord GOD!
This is what the Lord GOD says:
“You said, ‘Aha!’ about my sanctuary when it was made unholy,
and about the land of Israel when it was ruined,
and about the people of Judah when they were taken away as prisoners.
[4] So look, I will hand you over to the peoples of the east.
They will own you.
They will set up their camps in you
and make their homes in you.
They will eat your fruit,
and they will drink your milk.
[5] I will make Rabbah a pasture for camels,
and the land of the people of Ammon a resting place for flocks.
Then you will know that I am the LORD.”
[6] For this is what the Lord GOD says:
“You have clapped your hands
and stamped your feet.
You have been glad about the land of Israel
with all the contempt in your heart.
[7] So look, I have stretched out my hand against you.
I will hand you over to the nations, and they will rob you.
I will cut you off from the peoples.
I will make you disappear from the countries.
I will destroy you.
Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

> **What does this mean?**
> Here the second part of the book begins: words about the neighboring nations (Chapters 25–32).
> The Ammonites lived east of the Jordan River (today in Jordan; their capital city Rabbah is today’s Amman). When Jerusalem was destroyed, they were glad and called out “Aha!” They clapped their hands.
> The message: Being glad about the suffering of others is wrong. God sees it. (See Proverbs 24:17: Don’t be glad when your enemy falls.)
> The “peoples of the east” are tribes of nomads from the desert. Nomads are people who move from place to place with their animals.

## Against Moab (Verses 8–11)

[8] “‘This is what the Lord GOD says:
“Moab and Seir say,
‘Look, the people of Judah are just like all the other nations.’
[9] So look, I will open up the side of Moab.
I will start with the cities,
with its cities on its borders,
the pride of the country:
Beth Jeshimoth, Baal Meon, and Kiriathaim.
[10] I will open it to the peoples of the east,
who will go against the people of Ammon.
I will give these lands to them to own,
so that no one among the nations will remember the people of Ammon anymore.
[11] I will carry out judgment on Moab.
Then they will know that I am the LORD.”

> **What does this mean?**
> Moab was east of the Dead Sea. Seir is another name for the mountains of Edom.
> Verse 8: Moab says: “The people of Judah are just like all the other nations.” This means: Judah is nothing special, and neither is its God. With this they mock not only the people, but also God and his promises.

---

## Against Edom (Verses 12–14)

[12] “‘This is what the Lord GOD says:
“Edom took revenge on the people of Judah.
It did great wrong
when it took revenge on them.”
[13] So this is what the Lord GOD says:
“I will stretch out my hand against Edom.
I will cut off people and animals from it.
I will make it a wasteland, starting from Teman.
All the way to Dedan, they will die by the sword.
[14] I will take my revenge on Edom
through my people Israel.
They will deal with Edom as my anger and my fury demand.
Then they will know my revenge,”
says the Lord GOD.

> **What does this mean?**
> Edom was south of the Dead Sea. The Edomites were descendants of Esau, Jacob’s brother. So they were really relatives of Israel. That made it even worse that they took revenge on Judah and helped when Jerusalem was destroyed (see Obadiah 10–14; Psalm 137:7).
> Verse 14: Here God says: “I will take my revenge on Edom through my people Israel.” This is a difficult verse. But it gives no one today the right to take revenge in God’s name or to justify violence against a nation. The Bible says clearly: “Revenge belongs to me, and paying back” (Deuteronomy 32:35; see Romans 12:19). Revenge is God’s business, not ours.

---

## Against the Philistines (Verses 15–17)

[15] “‘This is what the Lord GOD says:
“The Philistines took revenge.
They took revenge with contempt in their hearts.
They wanted to destroy, because their hatred never ends.”
[16] So this is what the Lord GOD says:
“Look, I will stretch out my hand against the Philistines.
I will cut off the Cherethites,
and I will destroy the people who are left on the seacoast.
[17] I will take great revenge on them
with angry punishments.
Then they will know that I am the LORD,
when I take my revenge on them.”’”

> **What does this mean?**
> The Philistines lived on the coast in the west. They had been enemies of Israel for hundreds of years (“their hatred never ends”).
> Verse 16: The Cherethites were a group of the Philistines. They probably came from the island of Crete in the beginning.
> All four nations in this chapter have something in common: They were glad about Judah’s misfortune, or they took advantage of it. The message: Whoever mocks and kicks the weak when they are down is guilty.
> These old nations do not exist as living peoples today. These texts must not be used to support hatred against people living in this region today.
