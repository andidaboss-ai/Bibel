# Genesis – Chapter 13
### Abram and Lot go separate ways

---

## Back to the beginning (Verses 1–4)

[1] Abram went up out of Egypt into the South,
he and his wife and everything he had.
And Lot was with him.
[2] Abram was very rich in livestock, in silver and in gold.
[3] He traveled from camp to camp, from the South as far as Bethel,
to the place where his tent had been at the beginning, between Bethel and Ai.
[4] That was the place where he had built the altar before.
There Abram called on the name of the LORD.

> **What does this mean?**
> After his mistake in Egypt, Abram goes back to the place where he first prayed. That is like a new beginning with God.
> Whoever has gone astray may always come back again.

---

## A quarrel between the herdsmen (Verses 5–9)

[5] Lot, who traveled with Abram, also had flocks and herds and tents.
[6] The land was not big enough for both of them to live together.
For they owned so much that they could not stay together anymore.
[7] There was a quarrel between the herdsmen of Abram’s livestock and the herdsmen of Lot’s livestock.
At that time, the Canaanites and the Perizzites also lived in the land.

[8] So Abram said to Lot,
“Please, let there be no quarrel between you and me,
and between your herdsmen and my herdsmen.
For we are relatives!
[9] Is not the whole land in front of you?
Please separate from me.
If you go to the left, I will go to the right.
And if you go to the right, I will go to the left.”

> **What does this mean?**
> Owning a lot can become a problem. Here it leads to a quarrel in the family.
> Other peoples also live in the land, and that makes it even more crowded.
> “We are relatives” means here: We are one family. Lot is actually Abram’s nephew.
> Abram is the older one. He would have the right to choose first. But he lets Lot go first. Peace matters more to him than the best place.
> Abram can be generous because he trusts God: God will take care of me, no matter which land I get.

---

## Lot chooses with his eyes (Verses 10–13)

[10] Lot looked up and saw the whole plain of the Jordan.
It had plenty of water everywhere,
like the garden of the LORD, like the land of Egypt, as far as Zoar.
That was before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah.
[11] So Lot chose the whole plain of the Jordan for himself.
Lot traveled east.
So they separated from each other.

[12] Abram lived in the land of Canaan.
Lot lived in the cities of the plain.
He moved his tents as far as Sodom.
[13] But the men of Sodom were very wicked.
They sinned greatly against the LORD.

> **What does this mean?**
> Lot chooses by what he sees: the greenest, best land. It looks like paradise. He only thinks about his own advantage.
> But step by step, he comes closer and closer to Sodom, a city full of wickedness.
> What looks beautiful is not always good. The Bible is already hinting: This will not end well (see Chapter 19).

---

## God renews his promise (Verses 14–18)

[14] After Lot had separated from Abram, the LORD said to Abram,
“Look up, and look from the place where you are standing,
to the north and to the south, to the east and to the west.
[15] For all the land that you see
I will give to you and to your descendants forever.
[16] I will make your descendants as many as the dust of the earth.
If anyone can count the dust of the earth,
then your descendants can be counted too.
[17] Get up and walk through the land, through its length and through its width.
For I will give it to you.”

[18] So Abram moved his tents.
He came to the oaks of Mamre, which are in Hebron, and lived there.
And he built an altar there to the LORD.

> **What does this mean?**
> Lot looked up and chose for himself. Now God tells Abram to look up too – in **all** directions. And God will give **him** all of this land.
> Whoever is generous does not lose out with God.
> Abram does not even have a single child yet. And God promises him descendants as many as the specks of dust. That sounds impossible. But God means it.
> Hebron is still an important city today. According to tradition, the tomb of Abraham and Sarah is there.
