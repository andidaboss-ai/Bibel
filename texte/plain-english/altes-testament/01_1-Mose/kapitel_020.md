# Genesis – Chapter 20
### Abraham and King Abimelech

---

## The same lie again (Verses 1–2)

[1] Abraham traveled from there toward the land of the South.
He lived between Kadesh and Shur.
For a time, he lived as a foreigner in Gerar.
[2] Abraham said about his wife Sarah, “She is my sister.”
Then Abimelech, king of Gerar, sent men and took Sarah.

> **What does this mean?**
> Unbelievable: Abraham makes the same mistake as in Egypt (Chapter 12). Again he says his wife is his sister, to protect himself.
> And this happens right now – shortly before Sarah is to have the promised son. God’s whole promise is at stake.
> Gerar is in the south of the land, near what is now the Gaza Strip.

---

## God warns the king in a dream (Verses 3–8)

[3] But God came to Abimelech in a dream at night.
He said to him,
“Look, you are a dead man because of the woman you have taken.
For she is married to a man.”

[4] But Abimelech had not come near her.
He said,
“Lord, will you kill even a nation that is innocent?
[5] Did he not tell me himself, ‘She is my sister’?
And she herself also said, ‘He is my brother.’
I did this with an honest heart and with clean hands.”

[6] God said to him in the dream,
“Yes, I know that you did this with an honest heart.
And I also kept you from sinning against me.
That is why I did not let you touch her.
[7] So now give the man his wife back.
For he is a prophet.
He will pray for you, and you will live.
But if you do not give her back, then know for sure:
You will die, you and everyone who belongs to you.”

[8] Abimelech got up early in the morning.
He called all his servants together and told them all these things.
Then the men became very afraid.

> **What does this mean?**
> God steps in again to protect Sarah. He even speaks to a foreign king who does not belong to Abraham’s family.
> Abimelech is honest and decent. He did not know he was doing anything wrong. God recognizes this.
> A “prophet” is a person through whom God speaks and who is especially close to God. Here, for the first time in the Bible, someone is called a prophet – of all people Abraham, who has just lied. God holds on to Abraham, in spite of his mistakes.

---

## The king confronts Abraham (Verses 9–13)

[9] Then Abimelech called Abraham.
He said to him,
“What have you done to us?
How have I wronged you,
that you have brought such great guilt on me and my kingdom?
You have done things to me that should not be done!”
[10] And Abimelech said to Abraham,
“What were you thinking when you did this?”

[11] Abraham answered,
“I thought: Surely there is no fear of God in this place.
They will kill me to get my wife.
[12] Besides, she really is my sister.
She is the daughter of my father, but not the daughter of my mother.
And she became my wife.
[13] When God made me wander away from my father’s house, I said to her,
‘Do me this kindness:
Everywhere we go, say about me, “He is my brother.”’”

> **What does this mean?**
> Abraham thought: There is no fear of God here. But he was wrong. The foreign king showed more reverence for God than Abraham himself.
> We should not judge others too quickly just because they are foreign.
> Abraham excuses himself with a half-truth: Sarah is his half-sister. Back then, such a marriage was still allowed. Later, God forbade it (Leviticus 18:9).

---

## Abimelech makes everything right (Verses 14–18)

[14] Then Abimelech took sheep and cattle, male and female servants.
He gave them to Abraham.
And he gave his wife Sarah back to him.
[15] Abimelech said,
“Look, my land is open to you.
Live wherever you like.”
[16] And to Sarah he said,
“Look, I have given your brother 1,000 pieces of silver.
It is a covering of the eyes for all who are with you.
So in front of everyone, your honor is restored.”

[17] Abraham prayed to God.
Then God healed Abimelech, and his wife and his female servants too.
They were able to have children again.
[18] For the LORD had made every woman in Abimelech’s household unable to have children,
because of Sarah, Abraham’s wife.

> **What does this mean?**
> Abimelech is generous. He gives Sarah back, gives Abraham gifts and pays a lot of silver so that no one will speak badly about Sarah. Word for word it says: It is to be “a covering of the eyes” – that means, everyone is to look away and forget the matter.
> Abraham, the guilty one, prays for the king, who was innocent. And God answers this prayer.
> Strange and beautiful: Abimelech’s household has children again. And in the next chapter, Sarah finally has her child too.
