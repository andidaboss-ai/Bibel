# Genesis – Chapter 9
### The rainbow

---

## A new blessing (Verses 1–7)

[1] God blessed Noah and his sons.
He said to them,
“Be fruitful and become many! Fill the earth!
[2] All the animals of the earth will fear you and be afraid of you,
and all the birds of the sky,
everything that moves along the ground,
and all the fish of the sea.
I put them into your hand.

[3] “Every moving thing that lives will be food for you.
Just as I gave you the green plants, I now give you everything.
[4] But you must not eat meat that still has its life in it – that is, its blood.

[5] “And for your own blood, for your life, I will surely demand an account.
I will demand it from every animal.
And I will demand it from every human being,
even from every person’s brother.
I will demand an account for the life of a human being.
[6] Whoever sheds human blood,
his blood will be shed by people.
For God made man in his own image.

[7] “And you: Be fruitful and become many!
Spread out on the earth and become many on it.”

> **What does this mean?**
> After the flood, the world is different from the Garden of Eden. People and animals no longer live together in complete peace. The animals are afraid of people.
> Now people may also eat meat. But with reverence: The life is in the blood. And life belongs to God. That is why, to this day, Jews only eat meat from which the blood has been removed.
> “To demand an account” means: God asks about it. No one may simply take a human life.
> The reason is in verse 6: Every person is an image of God. Whoever kills a person attacks God’s image.
> “His blood will be shed by people” – in ancient times, this was the basis for courts. Not everyone should take revenge for himself. The community should see to justice. This prevents endless blood feuds.

## God’s covenant and the sign (Verses 8–17)

[8] God spoke to Noah and to his sons who were with him.
He said,
[9] “Look: I am making my covenant with you
and with your descendants after you,
[10] and with every living creature that is with you:
with the birds, with the livestock and with every wild animal with you,
with all that came out of the ship,
with every animal of the earth.
[11] I am making my covenant with you:
Never again will all living beings be wiped out by the waters of a flood.
Never again will there be a flood to destroy the earth.”

[12] And God said,
“This is the sign of the covenant
that I am making between me and you and every living creature with you,
for all generations, forever:
[13] I set my rainbow in the clouds.
It will be the sign of the covenant between me and the earth.
[14] When I bring clouds over the earth,
and the rainbow is seen in the clouds,
[15] then I will remember my covenant
between me and you and every living creature of all living beings.
Never again will the water become a flood to destroy all life.
[16] The rainbow will be in the clouds.
I will look at it.
Then I will remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living creature on the earth.”

[17] God said to Noah,
“This is the sign of the covenant that I have made between me and all living beings on the earth.”

> **What does this mean?**
> God’s covenant with Noah is a promise without conditions. People do not have to do anything to earn it.
> It is for **all** people in the whole world – and even for the animals.
> The Hebrew word for “bow” also means the bow for war, which shoots arrows. In a way, God hangs his weapon in the sky – with the open side pointing up, away from the earth. With this he says: I want peace with the world.
> The rainbow is meant to remind not only us, but God himself. When you see a rainbow, you may think: God keeps his word.

## The sons of Noah (Verses 18–19)

[18] The sons of Noah who went out of the ship were Shem, Ham and Japheth.
Ham is the father of Canaan.
[19] These three were the sons of Noah.
From them came all the people who spread out over the whole earth.

> **What does this mean?**
> All the people in the world are one big family. We have the same ancestors.
> Canaan is already mentioned here because he comes into the story right away. Later, the land where Israel lives is called “Canaan.”

---

## Noah and the wine (Verses 20–29)

[20] Noah became a farmer.
He planted a vineyard.
[21] He drank some of the wine and got drunk.
Then he lay uncovered in his tent.

[22] Ham, the father of Canaan, saw his father naked.
He went out and told his two brothers.
[23] Then Shem and Japheth took a piece of clothing.
They both laid it on their shoulders.
Then they walked backward into the tent
and covered their father’s naked body.
They kept their faces turned away.
So they did not see their father naked.

[24] Noah woke up from his wine.
He found out what his youngest son had done to him.
[25] Then he said,
“Canaan is cursed!
He will be the lowest servant of his brothers.”
[26] And he said,
“Praise be to the LORD, the God of Shem!
Let Canaan be his servant.
[27] May God enlarge Japheth.”
That means: May God give him much room.
“Let him live in the tents of Shem.
And let Canaan be his servant.”

[28] After the flood, Noah lived another 350 years.
[29] Noah lived 950 years in all.
Then he died.

> **What does this mean?**
> Even Noah, the righteous man, makes mistakes. The Bible tells the truth about its heroes – with all their weaknesses.
> Ham sees his father in an embarrassing situation. Instead of protecting him, he tells others about it. Shem and Japheth act differently: They protect their father’s dignity and cover him without looking. This is how we should deal with the weaknesses of others.
> In Hebrew, “Japheth” sounds like “to make room.” That is why the blessing says: “May God enlarge Japheth.”
> **Important:** This curse was terribly misused in history. Some people used it to justify the slavery of people from Africa. That is **wrong**. The text does not curse Ham, and it certainly does not curse people with a certain skin color. It speaks about Canaan, the people who later lived in the land of Canaan. And the whole Bible says: Every person is an image of God.
