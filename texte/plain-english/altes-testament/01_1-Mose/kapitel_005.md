# Genesis – Chapter 5
### The family from Adam to Noah

---

## God’s image (Verses 1–2)

[1] This is the book of the family line of Adam.
When God created man, he made him like God.
[2] He created them male and female.
He blessed them.
And on the day they were created, he gave them the name Adam.
Adam means: human being.

> **What does this mean?**
> Before the long family list begins, the Bible reminds us once more of Chapter 1: The human being is like God. Man and woman are blessed.
> This is still true after the Garden of Eden, after Cain and Abel. Human dignity is not lost.

---

## Adam, Seth and Enosh (Verses 3–11)

[3] Adam was 130 years old when he had a son.
The son was like him, in his own image.
He named him Seth.
[4] After Seth was born, Adam lived another 800 years.
He had other sons and daughters.
[5] Adam lived 930 years in all.
Then he died.

[6] Seth was 105 years old when he had Enosh.
[7] After Enosh was born, Seth lived another 807 years.
He had other sons and daughters.
[8] Seth lived 912 years in all.
Then he died.

[9] Enosh was 90 years old when he had Kenan.
[10] After Kenan was born, Enosh lived another 815 years.
He had other sons and daughters.
[11] Enosh lived 905 years in all.
Then he died.

> **What does this mean?**
> God made the human being as his image. In the same way, Adam has a son as his image. Life and dignity are passed on – from generation to generation.

---

## Kenan, Mahalalel and Jared (Verses 12–20)

[12] Kenan was 70 years old when he had Mahalalel.
[13] After Mahalalel was born, Kenan lived another 840 years.
He had other sons and daughters.
[14] Kenan lived 910 years in all.
Then he died.

[15] Mahalalel was 65 years old when he had Jared.
[16] After Jared was born, Mahalalel lived another 830 years.
He had other sons and daughters.
[17] Mahalalel lived 895 years in all.
Then he died.

[18] Jared was 162 years old when he had Enoch.
[19] After Enoch was born, Jared lived another 800 years.
He had other sons and daughters.
[20] Jared lived 962 years in all.
Then he died.

---

## Enoch walks with God (Verses 21–24)

[21] Enoch was 65 years old when he had Methuselah.
[22] After Methuselah was born, Enoch walked with God for 300 years.
He had more sons and daughters.
[23] Enoch lived 365 years in all.
[24] Enoch walked with God.
Then he could not be found anymore.
For God had taken him.

> **What does this mean?**
> For everyone else in this list, it says at the end: “Then he died.” For Enoch, it does **not** say that.
> Enoch lived as close to God as two friends who go for a walk together every day. And one day, God simply took him along.
> In the middle of this list of the dead, this is a ray of light: Death does not have the last word.
> 365 years – as many years as a year has days. Some people see this as a sign of a “full” life.

---

## Methuselah, Lamech and Noah (Verses 25–32)

[25] Methuselah was 187 years old when he had Lamech.
[26] After Lamech was born, Methuselah lived another 782 years.
He had other sons and daughters.
[27] Methuselah lived 969 years in all.
Then he died.

[28] Lamech was 182 years old when he had a son.
[29] He named him Noah.
He said, “This one will comfort us in our work
and in the hard labor of our hands,
because of the ground that the LORD has cursed.”
[30] After Noah was born, Lamech lived another 595 years.
He had other sons and daughters.
[31] Lamech lived 777 years in all.
Then he died.

[32] Noah was 500 years old.
Then Noah had three sons: Shem, Ham and Japheth.

> **What does this mean?**
> The people in this list become unbelievably old. You can understand this in different ways. Some readers take the numbers literally. Others think: The numbers show that these people lived in a distant ancient time that was different from ours.
> The sentence “Then he died” comes again and again. What God said in Chapter 3 has come true: Human beings must die.
> Methuselah lives to be 969 years old – older than any other person in the Bible. In English, people say someone is “as old as Methuselah.”
> This Lamech is not the violent Lamech from Chapter 4. He is a different man. He sighs about the hard work and hopes for comfort.
> In Hebrew, the name “Noah” sounds like “rest” and “comfort.” Noah brings hope.
