# Genesis – Chapter 17
### New names and the sign of the covenant

---

## God Almighty (Verses 1–2)

[1] Abram was 99 years old.
Then the LORD appeared to Abram and said to him,
“I am God Almighty.
Walk before me and be blameless.
[2] I will make my covenant between me and you.
And I will make you very, very many.”

> **What does this mean?**
> 13 years have passed since Ishmael was born. During this time, God was silent. Now he speaks again.
> In Hebrew, “God Almighty” is “El Shaddai.” With this, God says: Nothing is impossible for me – not even a child for a 99-year-old man.
> “Walk before me” means: live as if God is always watching – not out of fear, but out of trust.

---

## Abram becomes Abraham (Verses 3–8)

[3] Then Abram bowed down with his face to the ground.
And God talked with him.
He said,
[4] “Look, this is my covenant with you:
You will be the father of many nations.
[5] So your name will no longer be Abram.
Your name will be Abraham.
For I have made you the father of many nations.
[6] I will make you very, very fruitful.
I will make nations from you.
And kings will come from you.

[7] “I will make my covenant between me and you and your descendants after you,
from generation to generation, as an everlasting covenant.
I will be your God and the God of your descendants after you.
[8] I will give to you and to your descendants after you the land where you now live as a foreigner:
the whole land of Canaan, to own forever.
And I will be their God.”

> **What does this mean?**
> To bow down with your face to the ground is a sign of deep reverence.
> “Abram” means “great father.” “Abraham” sounds like “father of many.” God gives him a new name that fits his promise – even before it has come true.
> In the Bible, a new name often means: a new stage of life, a new task.
> The most important sentence is: “I will be your God.” That is the heart of the covenant. God binds himself to people – forever.

---

## The sign of circumcision (Verses 9–14)

[9] And God said to Abraham,
“As for you, you are to keep my covenant,
you and your descendants after you, from generation to generation.
[10] This is my covenant between me and you and your descendants after you, which you are to keep:
Every male among you is to be circumcised.
[11] You are to be circumcised in the foreskin of your penis.
That will be the sign of the covenant between me and you.

[12] “Every boy among you is to be circumcised when he is eight days old, from generation to generation.
This is true for the boys who are born in your household,
and for those you have bought with money from foreigners and who are not your descendants.
[13] Whoever is born in your household and whoever is bought with your money
must be circumcised.
So my covenant will be in your body, as an everlasting covenant.
[14] A male who is not circumcised,
who has not been circumcised in the foreskin of his penis,
is to be cut off from his people.
For he has broken my covenant.”

> **What does this mean?**
> Circumcision is a small operation: The foreskin of the male organ is removed. It is carried as a sign on one’s own body – for a lifetime.
> Just as the rainbow is the sign of the covenant with Noah, circumcision is the sign of the covenant with Abraham and his descendants.
> To this day, Jews celebrate circumcision on the eighth day of a boy’s life. The celebration is called “Brit Milah” – “covenant of circumcision.” Muslims also circumcise boys.
> Servants and slaves who do not belong to the family are also part of the covenant. God’s covenant is not only for blood relatives.
> In the New Testament, there is a lot of discussion about whether Christians who are not Jews must be circumcised. The apostles decided: No (Acts 15). Paul speaks of a circumcision of the heart (Romans 2:29).

## Sarai becomes Sarah (Verses 15–22)

[15] God said to Abraham,
“As for your wife Sarai, you are no longer to call her Sarai.
Her name will be Sarah.
[16] I will bless her.
And I will also give you a son by her.
Yes, I will bless her.
She will be the mother of nations.
Kings of peoples will come from her.”

[17] Then Abraham bowed down with his face to the ground, and he laughed.
He said in his heart,
“Will a child be born to a man who is 100 years old?
And will Sarah, who is 90 years old, give birth?”
[18] And Abraham said to God,
“Oh, if only Ishmael could live before you!”

[19] God said,
“No, but your wife Sarah will give birth to a son for you.
You are to name him Isaac.
I will make my covenant with him, as an everlasting covenant for his descendants after him.
[20] And about Ishmael, I have heard you.
Look: I have blessed him.
I will make him fruitful and very, very many.
He will become the father of 12 princes.
And I will make him into a great nation.
[21] But I will make my covenant with Isaac.
Sarah will give birth to him for you at this time next year.”

[22] When God had finished talking with him, God went up from Abraham.

> **What does this mean?**
> Sarah also gets a new name. “Sarai” and “Sarah” both mean “princess.” The new name shows: She is not just a side character. God’s promise is clearly for her too.
> Abraham laughs. It just sounds too unbelievable: A man of 100 and a woman of 90 are going to have a baby?
> God is not angry about the laughter. He even turns it into the child’s name: “Isaac” means “he laughs.”
> Abraham loves Ishmael and prays for him. God hears this prayer too: Ishmael will be blessed and become a great nation. But the covenant is to go on through Isaac.

---

## Abraham obeys at once (Verses 23–27)

[23] Abraham took his son Ishmael,
and all who were born in his household,
and all whom he had bought with his money –
every male among the men of Abraham’s household.
And he circumcised them in their foreskin on that very same day, just as God had told him.
[24] Abraham was 99 years old when he was circumcised in his foreskin.
[25] His son Ishmael was 13 years old when he was circumcised in his foreskin.
[26] On the same day, Abraham and his son Ishmael were circumcised.
[27] And all the men of his household,
those born in the household and those bought with money from foreigners,
were circumcised with him.

> **What does this mean?**
> Abraham does not hesitate. “On that very same day” he does what God said.
> Ishmael is also circumcised. Even though the covenant goes on through Isaac, Ishmael belongs to Abraham’s family and is not left out.
