# Genesis – Chapter 7
### The flood comes

---

## Go into the ark (Verses 1–5)

[1] The LORD said to Noah,
“Go into the ship, you and your whole household.
For I have seen: Among the people of this time, you are righteous before me.
[2] Take with you seven pairs of every clean animal, the male and his female.
And of the animals that are not clean, take two, the male and his female.
[3] Also of the birds of the sky, take seven pairs, male and female.
That way, their young will stay alive on the whole earth.
[4] For in 7 days, I will make it rain on the earth
for 40 days and 40 nights.
Then I will wipe out from the face of the ground every living thing that I have made.”

[5] And Noah did everything that the LORD commanded him.

> **What does this mean?**
> “Clean” and “not clean”: Later, the people of Israel were given rules about which animals they could eat and offer to God. These animals are called “clean,” for example cattle, sheep, goats and doves. Other animals, for example pigs, are counted as “not clean.”
> Noah takes more of the clean animals with him. After the flood, he will offer some of them to God as a thank offering.
> God gives seven more days. Even now, he is in no hurry to judge.
> In the Bible, the number 40 often stands for a time of testing: Israel was in the wilderness for 40 years, and Jesus fasted for 40 days.

---

## Noah goes into the ark (Verses 6–10)

[6] Noah was 600 years old when the flood came, the water over the earth.
[7] Noah went into the ship to escape the waters of the flood.
His sons, his wife and his sons’ wives went in with him.
[8] Clean animals and animals that are not clean,
birds and everything that creeps on the ground
[9] came to Noah into the ship in pairs, a male and a female,
just as God had commanded Noah.
[10] After the seven days, the waters of the flood came over the earth.

> **What does this mean?**
> Noah does not have to catch the animals. They “come” to him. God takes care of it.
> God said: in seven days. And that is exactly what happens. You can rely on God’s word.

---

## The sky opens (Verses 11–16)

[11] It was in the 600th year of Noah’s life, in the second month, on the 17th day of the month.
On that day, all the springs of the great deep burst open.
And the windows of the sky opened.
[12] The rain fell on the earth for 40 days and 40 nights.

[13] On that very day, Noah went into the ship.
With him went Shem, Ham and Japheth, the sons of Noah,
and also Noah’s wife and the three wives of his sons.
[14] With them went every wild animal, each according to its kind,
all the livestock, each according to its kind,
every creeping thing that creeps on the earth, each according to its kind,
and every bird, each according to its kind – birds of every sort.
[15] They came to Noah into the ship, in pairs from all living beings that have the breath of life.
[16] Those that went in were male and female of all living beings,
just as God had commanded Noah.
Then the LORD shut him in.

> **What does this mean?**
> The Bible gives the exact date, like in a diary. That shows: This was a day that should never be forgotten.
> The water comes from below (from the springs of the deep) and from above (from the windows of the sky). In Chapter 1, God separated the water above from the water below, to make room for life. Now everything comes crashing back together. It is as if creation were running backward.
> A beautiful little sentence: “Then the LORD shut him in.” God himself closes the door. Noah is safe in God’s hand.

---

## The water rises (Verses 17–24)

[17] The flood came over the earth for 40 days.
The waters rose and lifted up the ship.
So it was lifted high above the earth.
[18] The waters became mighty and rose higher and higher on the earth.
And the ship floated on the surface of the waters.
[19] The waters rose very high on the earth.
All the high mountains under the whole sky were covered.
[20] The water rose about 22 feet higher than the mountains.
So the mountains were covered.

[21] Then all living beings that moved on the earth died:
the birds, the livestock, the wild animals,
every creeping thing that creeps on the earth,
and every human being.
[22] Everything on the dry land that had the breath of life in its nostrils died.
[23] So every living thing on the face of the ground was wiped out:
people, livestock, creeping things and birds of the sky.
They were wiped out from the earth.
Only Noah was left, and those who were with him in the ship.

[24] The waters flooded the earth for 150 days.

> **What does this mean?**
> The Bible says: 15 cubits above the mountains. That is about 22 feet.
> This is a very serious, sad passage. The Bible does not make anything sound nicer than it is.
> The message: Evil and violence have consequences. In the end, they destroy everything.
> But in the middle of the destruction, the ark floats. It is a place of rescue. Later, Christians often saw the ark as a picture: With God, you are safe, even when everything around you is going under.
