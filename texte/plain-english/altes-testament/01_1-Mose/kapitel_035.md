# Genesis – Chapter 35
### Back to Bethel – Rachel and Isaac die

---

## Away with the foreign gods (Verses 1–5)

[1] God said to Jacob,
“Get up, go up to Bethel and live there.
Build an altar there to the God who appeared to you
when you ran away from your brother Esau.”

[2] Then Jacob said to his household and to all who were with him,
“Get rid of the foreign gods that you have with you.
Make yourselves clean and change your clothes.
[3] Let us get up and go up to Bethel.
There I will build an altar to the God who answered me when I was in trouble.
He was with me on the way that I went.”

[4] So they gave Jacob all the foreign gods that they had,
and the rings that were in their ears.
Jacob hid them under the oak tree near Shechem.

[5] Then they traveled on.
A terror from God came over the cities all around them.
So they did not chase the sons of Jacob.

> **What does this mean?**
> After the terrible events in Shechem, God calls Jacob back to the beginning: to Bethel, where God met him for the first time.
> Jacob notices: His family still has foreign gods with them, for example the household gods that Rachel stole. Now he cleans up. Whoever truly wants to meet God must let go of what separates them from God.
> Back then, people often wore earrings as lucky charms, connected with foreign gods.
> Putting on fresh clothes is a sign of a new beginning.
> Jacob was afraid of revenge because of Shechem. But God protects him.

---

## Back at Bethel (Verses 6–8)

[6] So Jacob came to Luz (that is, Bethel) in the land of Canaan,
he and all the people who were with him.
[7] He built an altar there
and named the place El Beth El.
For there God had shown himself to him when he ran away from his brother.

[8] Deborah, Rebekah’s nurse, died there.
She was buried below Bethel under the oak tree.
That is why it was named Allon Bacuth.

> **What does this mean?**
> “El Beth El” means “God of Bethel.” Now Jacob no longer honors only the place, but the God who met him there.
> Deborah was Rebekah’s nurse. She had come with Rebekah from Haran (Genesis 24:59). She probably moved to Jacob later. Her death is mentioned on purpose – she was a loved person.
> “Allon Bacuth” means “oak of weeping.”

---

## God blesses Jacob once more (Verses 9–15)

[9] When Jacob had come from Paddan Aram, God appeared to him again and blessed him.
[10] God said to him,
“Your name is Jacob.
But your name will not be Jacob any more. Your name will be Israel.”
So he named him Israel.

[11] God said to him,
“I am God Almighty.
Have many children and become many.
A nation, and a whole community of nations, will come from you.
And kings will come from your own body.
[12] The land that I gave to Abraham and Isaac, I will give to you.
And I will give the land to your descendants after you.”

[13] Then God went up from him, at the place where he had spoken with him.
[14] Jacob set up a pillar at the place where God had spoken with him, a pillar of stone.
He poured out a drink offering on it, and he poured oil on it.
[15] Jacob named the place where God had spoken with him “Bethel.”

> **What does this mean?**
> God confirms the new name Israel and the blessing. They are the same promises that Abraham and Isaac received.
> A “drink offering” is a drink, mostly wine, that is poured out for God. It is a sign of thankfulness.
> Jacob’s path in life is like a circle: It began at Bethel, when he was on the run. Now he comes back to Bethel as a changed man.

---

## Benjamin is born – Rachel dies (Verses 16–20)

[16] They traveled on from Bethel.
There was still some distance to go to Ephrath.
Then Rachel began to give birth.
The birth was very hard.
[17] When the birth was so hard, the midwife said to her,
“Don’t be afraid!
Now you will have another son.”

[18] Rachel was dying.
As her life was leaving her, she named her son Benoni.
But his father named him Benjamin.
[19] So Rachel died.
She was buried on the way to Ephrath. Ephrath is also called Bethlehem.
[20] Jacob set up a pillar on her grave.
It is the pillar of Rachel’s grave to this day.

> **What does this mean?**
> At Joseph’s birth, Rachel had wished for another son (Genesis 30:24). Her wish comes true – but she dies giving birth.
> “Benoni” means “son of my pain.” Jacob does not want his son to carry this sad name all his life. He names him “Benjamin.” That means “son of the right hand” or “son of good fortune.”
> Benjamin is the only son of Jacob who is born in the land of Canaan.
> This is the first time Bethlehem is mentioned in the Bible. Later, King David is born there – and Jesus.
> Rachel’s grave near Bethlehem is still a place today where many people pray.

---

## Reuben and the 12 sons of Jacob (Verses 21–26)

[21] Israel traveled on.
He set up his tent beyond the tower of Eder.
[22] While Israel was living in that land, Reuben went and slept with Bilhah, his father’s concubine.
A concubine is a wife of lower rank.
And Israel heard about it.

Jacob had 12 sons.
[23] The sons of Leah were:
Reuben, Jacob’s firstborn, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar and Zebulun.
[24] The sons of Rachel were:
Joseph and Benjamin.
[25] The sons of Bilhah, Rachel’s servant, were:
Dan and Naphtali.
[26] The sons of Zilpah, Leah’s servant, were:
Gad and Asher.
These are the sons of Jacob who were born to him in Paddan Aram.

> **What does this mean?**
> “Tower of Eder” means “tower of the flock.”
> Reuben sleeps with Bilhah, his father’s concubine. That is a serious wrong. Back then, such a thing also meant: I want to take over my father’s power. Because of this, Reuben later loses his rights as the firstborn (Genesis 49:3–4).
> Jacob hears about it – and keeps silent again. Just as he kept silent about Dinah.
> The list of the 12 sons is very important: The 12 tribes of Israel come from them.
> Actually, Benjamin was not born in Paddan Aram, but near Bethlehem. The list simply counts them all together.

---

## Isaac dies (Verses 27–29)

[27] Jacob came to his father Isaac at Mamre, near Kiriath Arba (that is, Hebron),
where Abraham and Isaac had lived as foreigners.
[28] Isaac lived to be 180 years old.
[29] Then Isaac breathed his last and died.
He was gathered to his people, old and full of days.
That means: He had lived a long and full life.
His sons Esau and Jacob buried him.

> **What does this mean?**
> Jacob sees his father again. His mother Rebekah is not mentioned. She has probably already died, without seeing her favorite son one more time.
> Just as with Abraham, when Isaac and Ishmael buried their father together, Esau and Jacob also bury their father together. At their father’s grave, the brothers who have made peace stand side by side.
> Isaac lives to be 180 years old – older than Abraham.
