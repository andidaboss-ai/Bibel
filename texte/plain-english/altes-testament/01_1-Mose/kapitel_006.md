# Genesis – Chapter 6
### Evil grows – God saves Noah

---

## God’s sons and the giants (Verses 1–4)

[1] People began to become many on the earth.
And daughters were born to them.
[2] Then God’s sons saw that the daughters of men were beautiful.
And they took any of them they wanted as their wives.

[3] Then the LORD said,
“My Spirit will not struggle with man forever.
For he too is only flesh.
His days will be 120 years.”

[4] In those days, the Nephilim were on the earth.
They were giants.
They were also there later,
when God’s sons came to the daughters of men and had children with them.
Those were the mighty men of long ago, the famous men.

> **What does this mean?**
> This short passage is one of the most puzzling in the whole Bible. No one knows for sure who is meant by “God’s sons.”
> There are three main explanations: Some say they are angels who crossed their limits. Others say they are powerful kings who acted like gods. Still others say they are the descendants of Seth.
> But the message is clear: Limits that God has set are crossed. The strong take what they want.
> “Flesh” here means: weak, easily hurt, and mortal.
> God sets a limit: A human being is to live 120 years at the most. A human being is not God.

---

## God is grieved in his heart (Verses 5–8)

[5] The LORD saw that the wickedness of people on the earth was great.
Everything their hearts thought and planned was only evil, all the time.
[6] The LORD was sorry that he had made people on the earth.
And it hurt him in his heart.

[7] The LORD said,
“I will wipe out the people I have created from the face of the ground.
The people, and also the animals, the creeping things and the birds of the sky.
For I am sorry that I have made them.”

[8] But Noah found favor in the eyes of the LORD.

> **What does this mean?**
> In the beginning, God looked at the world and saw that it was very good. Now he looks at the world, and it hurts him in his heart.
> God is not cold and without feelings. Evil makes him sad – just as parents are sad when their child is on a bad path.
> But then comes a big **but**: Noah. “To find favor” means: God looks kindly at him. In the middle of judgment, there is grace.

---

## Noah, a righteous man (Verses 9–13)

[9] This is the story of Noah and his family.
Noah was a righteous man.
Among the people of his time, he was blameless.
Blameless means: No one could accuse him of anything wrong.
Noah walked with God.
[10] Noah had three sons: Shem, Ham and Japheth.

[11] But the earth was corrupt in God’s eyes.
Corrupt means: rotten and ruined.
The earth was full of violence.
[12] God looked at the earth.
And he saw: It was corrupt.
For all living beings on the earth had corrupted their way.

[13] Then God said to Noah,
“I will bring an end to all living beings.
For because of them, the earth is full of violence.
Look: I will destroy them, together with the earth.”

> **What does this mean?**
> In the Bible, “righteous” means: to live in a way that pleases God, and to treat others fairly.
> Noah “walked with God” – just like Enoch in Chapter 5. He lived differently from everyone around him. That takes courage.
> In Hebrew, the same word appears here four times. It means “corrupt” and also “destroy.” People have corrupted the good world. Now God will “destroy” it. That means: People reap what they have sown.
> The word “violence” comes up twice. Since Cain and Lamech, violence has spread everywhere.

## Build a ship! (Verses 14–16)

[14] “Make yourself a ship out of gopher wood.”
This ship is often called the ark.
It is a huge ship shaped like a box.
“Make rooms in the ship.
And cover it with pitch inside and outside.
[15] This is how you are to make it:
about 450 feet long, about 75 feet wide and about 45 feet high.
[16] Make a roof for the ship.
Finish it so that there is an opening about 18 inches high under the roof.
Put the door of the ship in its side.
And build it with three levels: a lower, a middle and an upper level.”

> **What does this mean?**
> In the Bible, the measurements are in cubits: 300 cubits long, 50 cubits wide, 30 cubits high, plus one cubit for the opening under the roof. A cubit is the length from the elbow to the tip of the middle finger, about 18 inches.
> The ark is huge: as long as one and a half football fields. It is not a sailboat. It is a floating box.
> Today no one knows exactly what “gopher wood” is. Maybe it was cypress wood.
> Pitch is a black, sticky substance. It makes wood waterproof.

## God’s covenant with Noah (Verses 17–22)

[17] “For look: I will bring a flood, water over the earth.
I will destroy all life under the sky, everything that has the breath of life.
Everything that is on the earth will die.

[18] “But I will make my covenant with you.”
A covenant is a firm promise.
“You are to go into the ship:
you, your sons, your wife and your sons’ wives with you.

[19] “And of every living thing, of all living beings,
you are to bring two of every kind into the ship,
to keep them alive with you.
They are to be a male and a female.
[20] Of the birds, each according to its kind,
of the livestock, each according to its kind,
of every creeping thing on the ground, each according to its kind –
two of every kind will come to you, to keep them alive.
[21] And you: Take some of every kind of food that people eat.
Gather it with you.
It will be food for you and for the animals.”

[22] And Noah did it.
He did everything exactly as God commanded him.

> **What does this mean?**
> Here the word “covenant” appears for the first time in the Bible. God binds himself. He promises Noah: I will save you.
> God does not want to end all life. He wants to start again with Noah, his family and the animals.
> Noah does not ask questions. He trusts and does what God says. To the neighbors, it must have looked very strange: a huge ship in the middle of dry land.
> Many ancient peoples knew stories about a great flood. The Bible tells it in its own way: God does not look away when the world is full of violence. But he saves those who trust him.
