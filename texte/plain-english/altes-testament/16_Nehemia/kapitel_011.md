# Nehemiah – Chapter 11
### Who lives in Jerusalem

---

## Every tenth person moves to Jerusalem (Verses 1–3)

[1] The leaders of the people lived in Jerusalem.
The rest of the people cast lots.
In this way they chose one tenth of the people to live in Jerusalem, the holy city.
The other nine tenths stayed in the other cities.
[2] Some men offered on their own to live in Jerusalem.
The people blessed all of them.
[3] Here are the leaders of the province who lived in Jerusalem.
In the cities of Judah, everyone lived on his own land in their cities.
These were the Israelites, the priests, the Levites, the temple servants,
and the descendants of Solomon’s servants.

> **What does this mean?**
> Here the problem from Nehemiah 7:4 is solved: Jerusalem had a wall, but too few people living in it.
> One tenth of the people moves to Jerusalem, like a “tithe” for God.
> Why didn’t everyone want to live in Jerusalem? The city was still in ruins and dangerous. The people in the country had their fields and houses. Moving to Jerusalem meant making sacrifices.
> That is why the volunteers are blessed. The people honor those who put themselves out for the community.

---

## From Judah and Benjamin (Verses 4–9)

[4] Some of the descendants of Judah and of Benjamin lived in Jerusalem.
Of the descendants of Judah:
Athaiah the son of Uzziah, the son of Zechariah, the son of Amariah,
the son of Shephatiah, the son of Mahalalel, of the descendants of Perez,
[5] and Maaseiah the son of Baruch, the son of Colhozeh, the son of Hazaiah,
the son of Adaiah, the son of Joiarib, the son of Zechariah, the son of the Shilonite.
[6] All the descendants of Perez who lived in Jerusalem were 468 brave men.
[7] These are the descendants of Benjamin:
Sallu the son of Meshullam, the son of Joed, the son of Pedaiah,
the son of Kolaiah, the son of Maaseiah, the son of Ithiel, the son of Jeshaiah.
[8] After him came Gabbai and Sallai.
There were 928 men.
[9] Joel the son of Zichri was in charge of them.
Judah the son of Hassenuah was second in command over the city.

> **What does this mean?**
> Perez was a son of Judah (Genesis 38:29). David also descended from him (Ruth 4:18–22).
> “The Shilonite” here probably means a descendant of Shelah, another son of Judah.
> This list is similar to 1 Chronicles 9, but it is not quite the same.

---

## The priests (Verses 10–14)

[10] Of the priests:
Jedaiah the son of Joiarib, Jachin,
[11] Seraiah the son of Hilkiah, the son of Meshullam, the son of Zadok,
the son of Meraioth, the son of Ahitub, the leader of God’s house.
[12] Their brothers did the work in the temple.
There were 822 of them.
Then there was Adaiah the son of Jeroham, the son of Pelaliah, the son of Amzi,
the son of Zechariah, the son of Pashhur, the son of Malchijah,
[13] and his brothers, who were heads of families.
There were 242 of them.
Then there was Amashsai the son of Azarel, the son of Ahzai,
the son of Meshillemoth, the son of Immer,
[14] and their brothers, who were brave, strong men.
There were 128 of them.
Zabdiel, the son of Haggedolim, was in charge of them.

> **What does this mean?**
> More than 1,100 priests lived in Jerusalem, near the temple.
> “Haggedolim” means “the great ones”. Maybe it is not a name, but a title: Zabdiel, from one of the great families.

---

## The Levites, singers and gatekeepers (Verses 15–21)

[15] Of the Levites:
Shemaiah the son of Hasshub, the son of Azrikam, the son of Hashabiah, the son of Bunni.
[16] There were also Shabbethai and Jozabad, two of the leaders of the Levites.
They were in charge of the outside work of God’s house.
[17] There was Mattaniah the son of Mica, the son of Zabdi, the son of Asaph.
He was the leader who started the prayer of thanks.
There was Bakbukiah, who was second among his brothers.
And there was Abda the son of Shammua, the son of Galal, the son of Jeduthun.
[18] There were 284 Levites in all in the holy city.
[19] The gatekeepers were Akkub, Talmon, and their brothers.
They kept watch at the gates.
There were 172 of them.
[20] The rest of the Israelites, the priests, and the Levites lived in all the cities of Judah.
Each one lived on the land his family had received.
[21] The temple servants lived on the Ophel hill.
Ziha and Gishpa were in charge of the temple servants.

> **What does this mean?**
> Mattaniah began the song of praise at prayer. He was a leading singer, like a cantor today.
> The “outside work” was probably tasks outside the actual temple service, for example managing the gifts.

---

## Other people in charge (Verses 22–24)

[22] Uzzi was in charge of the Levites in Jerusalem.
He was the son of Bani, the son of Hashabiah, the son of Mattaniah, the son of Mica.
He was one of the descendants of Asaph.
They were the singers who were responsible for the service in God’s house.
[23] The king had given an order about them.
The singers received a fixed supply of what they needed each day.
[24] Pethahiah the son of Meshezabel was a descendant of Zerah, the son of Judah.
He was the king’s helper in all matters that had to do with the people.

> **What does this mean?**
> The Persian king even provided for the singers in the temple. He supported worship in Jerusalem (compare Ezra 7:24).
> Pethahiah was probably a representative of the Jews at the Persian court, a kind of ambassador.

## In the villages of Judah and Benjamin (Verses 25–36)

[25] Now about the villages and their fields:
Some of the descendants of Judah lived in Kiriath Arba and its towns,
in Dibon and its towns, in Jekabzeel and its villages,
[26] in Jeshua, in Moladah, in Beth Pelet,
[27] in Hazar Shual, in Beersheba and its towns,
[28] in Ziklag, in Meconah and its towns,
[29] in En Rimmon, in Zorah, in Jarmuth,
[30] in Zanoah, Adullam, and their villages,
in Lachish and its fields, and in Azekah and its towns.
So they lived everywhere from Beersheba to the valley of Hinnom.
[31] The descendants of Benjamin lived from Geba onward,
at Michmash and Aija, and at Bethel and its towns,
[32] at Anathoth, Nob, Ananiah,
[33] Hazor, Ramah, Gittaim,
[34] Hadid, Zeboim, Neballat,
[35] Lod, and Ono, the valley of craftsmen.
[36] Some groups of the Levites from Judah settled in the land of Benjamin.

> **What does this mean?**
> Kiriath Arba is the old name of Hebron (Joshua 14:15).
> “Its towns”: the Hebrew says “its daughters”. These are smaller places that belonged to a larger city.
> “From Beersheba to the valley of Hinnom”: from the south right up to Jerusalem.
> We know many of these places from earlier books of the Bible. Those who returned live again in the places of their ancestors. God’s promise to bring them back has been fulfilled.
