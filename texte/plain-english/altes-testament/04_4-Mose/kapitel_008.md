# Numbers – Chapter 8
### The lampstand and the dedication of the Levites

---

## The seven lamps (Verses 1–4)

[1] The LORD spoke to Moses.
He said:
[2] “Speak to Aaron and tell him:
‘When you light the lamps, the seven lamps shall give light to the area in front of the lampstand.’”
[3] Aaron did this.
He lit its lamps so that they gave light to the area in front of the lampstand,
as the LORD had commanded Moses.
[4] This is how the lampstand was made: of hammered gold.
From its base to its flowers it was hammered work.
He made the lampstand by the pattern that the LORD had shown Moses.

> **What does this mean?**
> After the gifts of the tribal leaders (Chapter 7), the lampstand is lit. Now there is light in the sanctuary.
> The lamps shall shine forward, toward the table with the 12 loaves of bread across from it. The light of God shines on the 12 tribes.

---

## The Levites are made clean (Verses 5–14)

[5] The LORD spoke to Moses.
He said:
[6] “Take the Levites from among the people of Israel and make them clean.
[7] This is what you shall do to them to make them clean:
Sprinkle the water of cleansing on them.
They shall shave their whole bodies with a razor,
wash their clothes and make themselves clean.
[8] Then they shall take a young bull and its grain offering, fine flour mixed with oil.
And you shall take another young bull as a sin offering.
[9] You shall bring the Levites in front of the Tent of Meeting.
And you shall gather the whole community of the people of Israel.
[10] You shall bring the Levites before the LORD.
The people of Israel shall lay their hands on the Levites.
[11] And Aaron shall offer the Levites before the LORD as a wave offering on behalf of the people of Israel.
So they shall do the work of the LORD.

[12] “The Levites shall lay their hands on the heads of the bulls.
You shall offer one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering to the LORD,
to make atonement for the Levites.
[13] You shall set the Levites before Aaron and before his sons,
and offer them to the LORD as a wave offering.
[14] So you shall set the Levites apart from among the people of Israel.
And the Levites shall be mine.

> **What does this mean?**
> The Levites are not anointed like the priests. They are made clean: with water, by shaving and by washing. That is a sign of a new beginning.
> Verse 10 is special: All the people lay their hands on the Levites. The Levites stand before God in the place of the whole people. They serve in the place of the firstborn of all the families.
> The Levites are “offered as a wave offering.” With animals and bread, the gift was waved back and forth in front of the altar. With the Levites, it is probably a solemn gesture by which they are handed over to God. They are like a living gift of the people to God.
> Later Paul writes: Give your bodies to God as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God (Romans 12:1). Acceptable means: God is pleased with it.

---

## The Levites belong to God (Verses 15–19)

[15] “After that, the Levites shall go in to do the work at the Tent of Meeting.
You shall make them clean and offer them as a wave offering.
[16] For they are given to me completely from among the people of Israel.
I have taken them for myself instead of all who are the first to come from the womb,
instead of all the firstborn of the people of Israel.
[17] For all the firstborn among the people of Israel are mine, both people and animals.
On the day when I struck down all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, I made them holy for myself.
[18] I have taken the Levites instead of all the firstborn among the people of Israel.
[19] I have given the Levites as a gift to Aaron and to his sons, from among the people of Israel.
They shall do the work of the people of Israel at the Tent of Meeting
and make atonement for the people of Israel,
so that no plague comes on the people of Israel when the people of Israel come near the sanctuary.”

> **What does this mean?**
> The Levites are a “gift” for the priests. They help them with all the work.
> And they protect the people. Because they do the work at the sanctuary, nobody else has to come too close to the holy things and put himself in danger.

---

## The dedication is carried out (Verses 20–22)

[20] Moses, Aaron and the whole community of the people of Israel did this to the Levites.
The people of Israel did to them everything the LORD had commanded Moses about the Levites.
[21] The Levites made themselves clean from sin and washed their clothes.
And Aaron offered them before the LORD as a wave offering.
And Aaron made atonement for them to make them clean.
[22] After that, the Levites went in to do their work at the Tent of Meeting, before Aaron and before his sons.
As the LORD had commanded Moses about the Levites, so they did to them.

---

## The age of service for the Levites (Verses 23–26)

[23] The LORD spoke to Moses.
He said:
[24] “This is the rule for the Levites:
From 25 years old and up they shall come to serve and work at the Tent of Meeting.
[25] And from the age of 50 they shall stop doing the work
and shall serve no more.
[26] But they shall help their brothers at the Tent of Meeting to carry out their duties.
They shall no longer do the work themselves.
This is how you shall arrange the duties of the Levites.”

> **What does this mean?**
> In Chapter 4 it said: The Levites work from 30 to 50 years old. Here it says: from 25. Maybe the first five years were a time of training. Jewish teachers have explained it this way.
> At 50, the Levites go into a kind of retirement. They no longer have to carry heavy loads. But they are not useless. They help the younger ones and pass on their experience. That is a beautiful picture of old age: You no longer do everything, but you are still needed.
