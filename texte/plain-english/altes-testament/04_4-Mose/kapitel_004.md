# Numbers – Chapter 4
### How the sanctuary is carried

---

## The Kohathites and the most holy things (Verses 1–4)

[1] The LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron.
He said:
[2] “Take a census of the descendants of Kohath among the Levites, by their families and by their fathers’ houses,
[3] from 30 years old up to 50 years old,
all who come to serve, to do the work at the Tent of Meeting.
[4] This is the service of the descendants of Kohath at the Tent of Meeting: They take care of the most holy things.

> **What does this mean?**
> In Chapter 3, all the Levites from one month old were counted. Now only those who really work are counted: the men between 30 and 50 years old. At this age a man is strong enough and has enough experience.

---

## The priests cover the holy things (Verses 5–16)

[5] “When the camp sets out, Aaron and his sons shall go in.
They shall take down the curtain that screens off the Most Holy Place.
And they shall cover the ark of the Testimony with it.
[6] They shall put a covering of sealskin over it.
And over that they shall spread a cloth that is all blue.
Then they shall put in its poles.

[7] “Over the table of the showbread they shall spread a blue cloth.
On the cloth they shall put the dishes, the spoons, the bowls and the cups for pouring out drink offerings.
And the bread that is always there shall stay on it.
[8] Over them they shall spread a scarlet cloth.
And they shall cover it with a covering of sealskin.
Then they shall put in its poles.

[9] “They shall take a blue cloth
and cover the lampstand for the light with it,
its lamps, its wick trimmers, its trays
and all its oil jars that are used for the work on it.
[10] They shall put it and all its equipment into a covering of sealskin.
And they shall put it on the carrying frame.

[11] “Over the golden altar they shall spread a blue cloth.
And they shall cover it with a covering of sealskin.
Then they shall put in its poles.
[12] They shall take all the equipment that is used for the service in the sanctuary.
They shall put it in a blue cloth,
cover it with a covering of sealskin,
and put it on the carrying frame.

[13] “They shall take away the ashes from the altar.
And they shall spread a purple cloth over it.
[14] On it they shall put all the equipment that is used for the work on it:
the fire pans, the meat forks, the shovels and the basins, all the equipment of the altar.
Over it they shall spread a covering of sealskin.
Then they shall put in its poles.

[15] “When the camp sets out, Aaron and his sons shall first finish covering the sanctuary and all the furniture of the sanctuary.
After that, the descendants of Kohath shall come to carry it.
But they must not touch the holy things, or they will die.
These are the things of the Tent of Meeting that the descendants of Kohath shall carry.

[16] “Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, shall be responsible
for the oil for the light, the sweet-smelling incense, the daily grain offering and the anointing oil.
He shall be in charge of the whole Tabernacle and of everything in it,
of the sanctuary and its equipment.”

> **What does this mean?**
> The holy objects may not be carried openly through the wilderness. First the priests cover them carefully with cloths and leather. Only then may the Kohathites carry them.
> Here the WEB, our English source text, says “sealskin.” In Exodus it said “sea cow hides.” It is the same Hebrew word. Nobody knows exactly which animal is meant.
> The ark of the covenant is covered with a blue cloth last of all. With all the other objects, the leather is on the outside. Only the ark shines blue. So everyone on the journey could see where the ark was.
> Later the ark was once carried on a cart instead of on people’s shoulders. Uzzah touched it and died (2 Samuel 6:6–7). So these rules were taken very seriously.
> Verse 7: The showbread is the holy bread that always lies before God.
> Verse 8: Scarlet is a bright red color.

---

## The Kohathites shall not die (Verses 17–20)

[17] The LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron.
He said:
[18] “Do not let the tribe of the families of the Kohathites be wiped out from among the Levites.
[19] But do this for them, so that they will live and not die when they come near the most holy things:
Aaron and his sons shall go in
and give every man his work and his load.
[20] But the Kohathites themselves shall not go in to look at the holy things, not even for a moment.
Otherwise they will die.”

> **What does this mean?**
> God cares about the Kohathites. Their work is dangerous because it is so close to the holy things. That is why they get clear instructions that protect them.
> The rules are not there to punish people, but to keep them alive.

---

## The Gershonites (Verses 21–28)

[21] The LORD spoke to Moses.
He said:
[22] “Take a census of the descendants of Gershon too, by their fathers’ houses and by their families.
[23] You shall count them from 30 years old up to 50 years old,
all who come to serve, to do the work at the Tent of Meeting.
[24] This is the service of the families of the Gershonites, in serving and in carrying loads:
[25] They shall carry the curtains of the Tabernacle and the Tent of Meeting,
its covering and the covering of sealskin that is on top of it,
the curtain for the door of the Tent of Meeting,
[26] the hangings of the courtyard,
the curtain for the door at the gate of the courtyard, which is around the Tabernacle and the altar,
their ropes and all the equipment for their service,
and everything that has to be done with them. That is their service.
[27] All the service of the Gershonites shall be done at the command of Aaron and his sons,
in everything they carry and in everything they do.
You shall tell them exactly what they are responsible for.
[28] This is the service of the families of the Gershonites at the Tent of Meeting.
Their duties shall be under the direction of Ithamar, the son of Aaron the priest.

---

## The Merarites (Verses 29–33)

[29] “You shall count the descendants of Merari by their families and by their fathers’ houses.
[30] You shall count them from 30 years old up to 50 years old,
everyone who comes to serve, to do the work at the Tent of Meeting.
[31] This is the load they shall carry, in all their service at the Tent of Meeting:
the boards of the Tabernacle, its crossbars, its posts and its bases,
[32] the posts of the courtyard all around, their bases, their tent pegs and their ropes,
with all their equipment and with everything that belongs to their service.
You shall give them by name the equipment that they shall carry.
[33] This is the service of the families of the descendants of Merari, all their service at the Tent of Meeting,
under the direction of Ithamar, the son of Aaron the priest.”

> **What does this mean?**
> “Give them by name” means: Everyone knows exactly which part he shall carry. Nothing gets lost. Nothing is forgotten.
> Everyone has his task, and every task is important. In the New Testament, Paul compares the church to a body. Every part has its own task, and no part is unimportant (1 Corinthians 12).

---

## The numbers of the working Levites (Verses 34–49)

[34] Moses, Aaron and the leaders of the community counted the descendants of the Kohathites, by their families and by their fathers’ houses,
[35] from 30 years old up to 50 years old,
everyone who came to serve, to work at the Tent of Meeting.
[36] Those of them who were counted by their families were 2,750.
[37] These were the ones counted from the families of the Kohathites, all who served at the Tent of Meeting.
Moses and Aaron counted them at the command of the LORD through Moses.

[38] Those counted from the descendants of Gershon, by their families and by their fathers’ houses,
[39] from 30 years old up to 50 years old,
everyone who came to serve, to work at the Tent of Meeting –
[40] those of them who were counted by their families and by their fathers’ houses were 2,630.
[41] These were the ones counted from the families of the descendants of Gershon, all who served at the Tent of Meeting.
Moses and Aaron counted them at the command of the LORD.

[42] Those counted from the families of the descendants of Merari, by their families and by their fathers’ houses,
[43] from 30 years old up to 50 years old,
everyone who came to serve, to work at the Tent of Meeting –
[44] those of them who were counted by their families were 3,200.
[45] These were the ones counted from the families of the descendants of Merari.
Moses and Aaron counted them at the command of the LORD through Moses.

[46] All the Levites who were counted, whom Moses, Aaron and the leaders of Israel counted,
by their families and by their fathers’ houses,
[47] from 30 years old up to 50 years old,
everyone who came to do the work of service and the work of carrying loads at the Tent of Meeting –
[48] those of them who were counted were 8,580.
[49] They were counted through Moses at the command of the LORD,
each one for his service and for his load.
So they were counted by him, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

> **What does this mean?**
> 2,750 + 2,630 + 3,200 = 8,580. The number is right.
> It is interesting: The Merarites have the most workers. After all, they carry the heaviest parts.
