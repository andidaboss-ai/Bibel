# Numbers – Chapter 2
### The order of the camp

---

## Everyone by his flag (Verses 1–2)

[1] The LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron.
He said:
[2] “The people of Israel shall set up their tents, every man by his own flag,
with the banners of their fathers’ houses.
They shall camp around the Tent of Meeting, at some distance from it.

> **What does this mean?**
> Now it is decided what the camp shall look like. The sanctuary stands in the middle. The Levites camp around it. And outside of them, the twelve tribes camp, three on each side.
> So there is a big square with God in the middle. Every tribe has its fixed place.
> A “banner” is a flag or a sign for an army. By it, everyone could see where his tribe was camping.

---

## In the east: the camp of Judah (Verses 3–9)

[3] “Those who camp on the east side, toward the sunrise, shall be under the flag of the camp of Judah, by their army divisions.
The leader of the descendants of Judah shall be Nahshon, the son of Amminadab.
[4] His division, those who were counted, were 74,600.
[5] Next to him the tribe of Issachar shall camp.
The leader of the descendants of Issachar shall be Nethanel, the son of Zuar.
[6] His division, those who were counted, were 54,400.
[7] Then the tribe of Zebulun.
The leader of the descendants of Zebulun shall be Eliab, the son of Helon.
[8] His division, those who were counted, were 57,400.
[9] All who were counted in the camp of Judah were 186,400, by their army divisions.
They shall set out first.

> **What does this mean?**
> The east is the most important side. The entrance of the sanctuary is there too. Judah, the largest tribe, camps here. On the journey, Judah goes first.
> Later Judah becomes the tribe of the kings. Jacob’s blessing already announced this (Genesis 49:10).

---

## In the south: the camp of Reuben (Verses 10–16)

[10] “On the south side shall be the flag of the camp of Reuben, by their army divisions.
The leader of the descendants of Reuben shall be Elizur, the son of Shedeur.
[11] His division, those who were counted, were 46,500.
[12] Next to him the tribe of Simeon shall camp.
The leader of the descendants of Simeon shall be Shelumiel, the son of Zurishaddai.
[13] His division, those who were counted, were 59,300.
[14] Then the tribe of Gad.
The leader of the descendants of Gad shall be Eliasaph, the son of Reuel.
[15] His division, those who were counted, were 45,650.
[16] All who were counted in the camp of Reuben were 151,450, by their armies.
They shall set out second.

> **What does this mean?**
> In Chapter 1:14, the father of Eliasaph is called “Deuel.” Here he is called “Reuel.” In Hebrew, the letters D and R look very similar. It is probably the same man, and a letter was mixed up when the text was copied.

---

## In the middle: the sanctuary and the Levites (Verse 17)

[17] “Then the Tent of Meeting shall set out, with the camp of the Levites, in the middle of the camps.
As they camp, so they shall also set out, every man in his place, by their flags.

> **What does this mean?**
> On the journey, too, the sanctuary is in the middle. Two camps go in front, two camps go behind. God is always at the center of his people, when they camp and when they travel.

---

## In the west: the camp of Ephraim (Verses 18–24)

[18] “On the west side shall be the flag of the camp of Ephraim, by their army divisions.
The leader of the descendants of Ephraim shall be Elishama, the son of Ammihud.
[19] His division, those who were counted, were 40,500.
[20] Next to him shall be the tribe of Manasseh.
The leader of the descendants of Manasseh shall be Gamaliel, the son of Pedahzur.
[21] His division, those who were counted, were 32,200.
[22] Then the tribe of Benjamin.
The leader of the descendants of Benjamin shall be Abidan, the son of Gideoni.
[23] His army, those who were counted, were 35,400.
[24] All who were counted in the camp of Ephraim were 108,100, by their army divisions.
They shall set out third.

> **What does this mean?**
> In the west camp the descendants of Rachel: Ephraim and Manasseh (the sons of Joseph) and Benjamin. Rachel was the wife Jacob loved most.

---

## In the north: the camp of Dan (Verses 25–31)

[25] “On the north side shall be the flag of the camp of Dan, by their army divisions.
The leader of the descendants of Dan shall be Ahiezer, the son of Ammishaddai.
[26] His division, those who were counted, were 62,700.
[27] Next to him the tribe of Asher shall camp.
The leader of the descendants of Asher shall be Pagiel, the son of Ochran.
[28] His division, those who were counted, were 41,500.
[29] Then the tribe of Naphtali.
The leader of the descendants of Naphtali shall be Ahira, the son of Enan.
[30] His division, those who were counted, were 53,400.
[31] All who were counted in the camp of Dan were 157,600.
They shall set out last, by their flags.”

> **What does this mean?**
> The camp of Dan is the rear guard. It goes at the end of the line and protects the people from behind.

---

## Summary (Verses 32–34)

[32] These were the people of Israel who were counted by their fathers’ houses.
All who were counted in the camps, by their armies, were 603,550.
[33] But the Levites were not counted among the people of Israel,
just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
[34] The people of Israel did this.
They did everything just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
So they camped by their flags,
and so they set out, everyone with his family, by his fathers’ house.

> **What does this mean?**
> The four camps together: 186,400 + 151,450 + 108,100 + 157,600 = 603,550. The number matches Chapter 1 exactly.
> A crowd of refugees has become an orderly people. Order helps everyone to live together well.
