# Numbers – Chapter 26
### The second census – the new generation

---

## God orders a new count (Verses 1–4)

[1] After the plague, the LORD spoke to Moses and to Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest.
He said,
[2] “Count the whole community of the people of Israel,
everyone 20 years old and older, by their families.
Count everyone in Israel who is able to go to war.”
[3] Moses and Eleazar the priest spoke with them in the plains of Moab,
by the Jordan, at Jericho.
They said,
[4] “Count everyone 20 years old and older,
as the LORD commanded Moses and the people of Israel.”
These are the people of Israel who came out of the land of Egypt:

> **What does this mean?**
> 38 years after the first count (Chapter 1), the people are counted a second time. The old generation has almost completely died. Now the new generation is ready to take the land.
> “Who came out of the land of Egypt” here means the whole people of Israel, the descendants of those who came out.
> The count is also the basis for dividing the land. Each tribe is to get as much land as it has people.

## Reuben (Verses 5–11)

[5] Reuben was the firstborn of Israel.
The descendants of Reuben:
from Hanoch, the family of the Hanochites;
from Pallu, the family of the Palluites;
[6] from Hezron, the family of the Hezronites;
from Carmi, the family of the Carmites.
[7] These are the families of the Reubenites.
The number of those counted was 43,730.
[8] The son of Pallu was Eliab.
[9] The sons of Eliab were Nemuel, Dathan and Abiram.
These are the same Dathan and Abiram who were chosen by the community.
They rebelled against Moses and against Aaron, together with Korah’s group,
when they rebelled against the LORD.
[10] Then the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them up together with Korah,
when that group died.
At that time the fire burned up 250 men.
So they became a warning sign.
[11] But the sons of Korah did not die.

> **What does this mean?**
> With Reuben, the Bible reminds us of the rebellion of Dathan and Abiram (Chapter 16).
> Verse 11 is important: “But the sons of Korah did not die.” Their descendants later became singers in the temple and wrote psalms.

## The total number and the dividing of the land (Verses 51–56)

[51] These are those of the people of Israel who were counted: 601,730.

[52] The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
[53] “The land is to be divided among these as their inheritance, by the number of names.
An inheritance is land that a family receives and keeps for its children.
[54] To a large tribe you must give a large inheritance.
To a small tribe you must give a small inheritance.
Each tribe is to receive its inheritance by the number of its people who were counted.
[55] But the land is to be divided by lot.
They will inherit by the names of the tribes of their fathers.
[56] By the lot, their inheritance is to be divided between the large and the small tribes.”
Casting lots was a way to decide something, a little like drawing straws.

> **What does this mean?**
> At the first count, there were 603,550 men. Now there are 601,730. After 40 years in the wilderness, the people are almost the same size. God gave them children, even though the old generation died.
> If you add up the numbers of the twelve tribes, you get exactly 601,730.
> The land is divided in two ways: The size depends on the number of people. The lot decides which area a tribe gets. That way it is fair, and nobody can grab the best piece.

## The Levites (Verses 57–62)

[57] These are the Levites who were counted by their families:
from Gershon, the family of the Gershonites;
from Kohath, the family of the Kohathites;
from Merari, the family of the Merarites.
[58] These are the families of Levi:
the family of the Libnites, the family of the Hebronites, the family of the Mahlites,
the family of the Mushites and the family of the Korahites.
Kohath became the father of Amram.
[59] The name of Amram’s wife was Jochebed.
She was a daughter of Levi, born to Levi in Egypt.
She gave birth to Aaron and Moses for Amram, and to their sister Miriam.
[60] Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar were born to Aaron.
[61] Nadab and Abihu died when they offered strange fire before the LORD.
Strange fire means: fire that God had not commanded.
[62] The number of those counted was 23,000, every male one month old and older.
They were not counted among the people of Israel,
because they were not given an inheritance among the people of Israel.

> **What does this mean?**
> Here Moses’ family is named too: his parents Amram and Jochebed, his brother Aaron and his sister Miriam.
> The Levites do not get any land. That is why they are counted separately again.

## None of the old generation is left (Verses 63–65)

[63] These are the ones counted by Moses and Eleazar the priest.
They counted the people of Israel in the plains of Moab, by the Jordan, at Jericho.
[64] But among them there was not a single man of those
whom Moses and Aaron the priest had counted
when they counted the people of Israel in the wilderness of Sinai.
[65] For the LORD had said about them, “They will surely die in the wilderness.”
Not one of them was left,
except Caleb, the son of Jephunneh, and Joshua, the son of Nun.

> **What does this mean?**
> God’s word has come true. The whole generation that had refused to go into the land has died in the wilderness (Numbers 14:29–30). Only Caleb and Joshua, who had trusted God, are still alive.
> And Moses too. But he will not enter the land either (Numbers 20:12).
> A new generation has grown up. It did not see the miracles in Egypt for itself. But it is ready to trust God and to go into the land.
