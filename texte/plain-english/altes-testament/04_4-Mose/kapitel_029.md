# Numbers – Chapter 29
### The offerings for the festivals in the fall

---

## The Feast of Trumpets (Verses 1–6)

[1] “‘In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, you must have a holy assembly.
You must not do any regular work.
It is to be a day of blowing trumpets for you.
[2] You must bring a burnt offering as a pleasant smell to the LORD:
one young bull, one ram and seven male lambs one year old without any defect.
[3] With them, bring their grain offering, fine flour mixed with oil:
about 7 quarts for the bull, about 4½ quarts for the ram,
[4] and about 2 quarts for each of the seven lambs.
[5] And bring one male goat as a sin offering, to make atonement for you.
[6] All this is in addition to the burnt offering of the new moon with its grain offering,
and the regular burnt offering with its grain offering, and their drink offerings,
as the rule says, as a pleasant smell, an offering made by fire to the LORD.

> **What does this mean?**
> In the Bible the measures are given like this: three tenths, two tenths and one tenth of an ephah of flour. An ephah is about 6 gallons.
> The seventh month, in the fall, is the most festive month of the year. On its first day, the horn is blown. Today this is the Jewish New Year festival, “Rosh Hashanah.”

---

## The Day of Atonement (Verses 7–11)

[7] “‘On the tenth day of this seventh month, you must have a holy assembly.
You must humble yourselves.
You must not do any kind of work.
[8] Instead, you must bring a burnt offering to the LORD as a pleasant smell:
one young bull, one ram and seven male lambs one year old, all without any defect.
[9] With them, bring their grain offering, fine flour mixed with oil:
about 7 quarts for the bull, about 4½ quarts for the one ram,
[10] about 2 quarts for each of the seven lambs.
[11] And bring one male goat as a sin offering.
This is in addition to the sin offering of atonement, and the regular burnt offering
with its grain offering, and their drink offerings.

> **What does this mean?**
> The Day of Atonement, “Yom Kippur,” was described in detail in Leviticus 16. “Humble yourselves” means above all: to fast. To fast means to go without food.

---

## The Feast of Booths: the first seven days (Verses 12–34)

[12] “‘On the fifteenth day of the seventh month, you must have a holy assembly.
You must not do any regular work.
For seven days you must celebrate a festival to the LORD.
[13] You must bring a burnt offering, an offering made by fire, as a pleasant smell to the LORD:
13 young bulls, 2 rams and 14 male lambs 1 year old, all without any defect.
[14] With them, bring their grain offering, fine flour mixed with oil:
about 7 quarts for each of the 13 bulls, about 4½ quarts for each of the 2 rams,
[15] and about 2 quarts for each of the 14 lambs.
[16] And bring one male goat as a sin offering.
This is in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

[17] “‘On the second day, you must offer 12 young bulls, 2 rams and 14 male lambs 1 year old without any defect.
[18] With them, bring their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams and for the lambs,
by their number, as the rule says.
[19] And bring one male goat as a sin offering.
This is in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and their drink offerings.

[20] “‘On the third day: 11 bulls, 2 rams and 14 male lambs 1 year old without any defect.
[21] With them, bring their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams and for the lambs,
by their number, as the rule says.
[22] And bring one male goat as a sin offering.
This is in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

[23] “‘On the fourth day: 10 bulls, 2 rams and 14 male lambs 1 year old without any defect.
[24] With them, bring their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams and for the lambs,
by their number, as the rule says.
[25] And bring one male goat as a sin offering.
This is in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

[26] “‘On the fifth day: 9 bulls, 2 rams and 14 male lambs 1 year old without any defect.
[27] With them, bring their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams and for the lambs,
by their number, as the rule says.
[28] And bring one male goat as a sin offering.
This is in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

[29] “‘On the sixth day: 8 bulls, 2 rams and 14 male lambs 1 year old without any defect.
[30] With them, bring their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams and for the lambs,
by their number, as the rule says.
[31] And bring one male goat as a sin offering.
This is in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offerings.

[32] “‘On the seventh day: 7 bulls, 2 rams and 14 male lambs 1 year old without any defect.
[33] With them, bring their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams and for the lambs,
by their number, as the rule says.
[34] And bring one male goat as a sin offering.
This is in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

> **What does this mean?**
> The Feast of Booths is the biggest festival of the year. It is the festival of joy after the harvest. People live in simple huts made of branches during this festival.
> The number of bulls gets smaller by 1 every day: 13, 12, 11, 10, 9, 8, 7. Together that makes 70 bulls.
> Jewish teachers have said: The 70 bulls stand for the 70 nations of the world that are listed in Genesis 10. So at this festival, Israel also prays for all the other nations. The prophet Zechariah foretells that one day all nations will come to Jerusalem for the Feast of Booths (Zechariah 14:16).
> Every day, 14 lambs and 2 rams are offered too. In the 7 days, that makes 98 lambs.

## The eighth day (Verses 35–38)

[35] “‘On the eighth day, you must have a solemn assembly.
You must not do any regular work.
[36] Instead, you must bring a burnt offering, an offering made by fire, as a pleasant smell to the LORD:
one bull, one ram and seven male lambs one year old without any defect.
[37] Their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bull, for the ram and for the lambs
are to be by their number, as the rule says.
[38] And bring one male goat as a sin offering.
This is in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and its drink offering.

> **What does this mean?**
> On the eighth day there is only 1 bull. After the 70 bulls for all nations, there is 1 bull for Israel alone.
> To this day, Jews celebrate this day as “Shemini Atzeret,” the closing of the festival. After it comes the festival of joy in the Torah, “Simchat Torah.” On that day, people dance with the Torah scrolls.

---

## Summary (Verses 39–40)

[39] “‘You must offer these to the LORD at your appointed festivals,
in addition to your vows and your freewill offerings.
They are for your burnt offerings, your grain offerings, your drink offerings and your peace offerings.’”
[40] Moses told the people of Israel everything that the LORD had commanded Moses.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 39: A vow is a solemn promise to God.
> In some Bibles, verse 40 is already the first verse of Chapter 30 (30:1). Here we follow the verse numbers of the WEB, our English source text.
> If you add everything up, more than 1,000 lambs and more than 100 bulls were offered every year as shared offerings of the people. On top of that came the many offerings of individual people.
> The whole year is centered on God: every day, every week, every month, every festival.
