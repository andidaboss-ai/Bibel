# Numbers – Chapter 18
### The duties and the pay of the priests and Levites

---

## The responsibility of the priests and Levites (Verses 1–7)

[1] The LORD said to Aaron,
“You and your sons and your family with you
shall carry the guilt that has to do with the holy place.
And you and your sons with you shall carry the guilt that has to do with your priesthood.
[2] Also bring your brothers with you, the tribe of Levi, the tribe of your father.
They shall join you and serve you.
But you and your sons with you shall serve in front of the Tent of the Testimony.
[3] They shall follow your instructions and do the duties for the whole tent.
But they shall not come near the objects of the holy place or the altar,
so that they do not die, neither they nor you.
[4] They shall join you
and take care of the Tent of Meeting, for all the work at the tent.
No outsider shall come near you.

[5] “You shall do the duties of the holy place and the duties of the altar,
so that there is no more anger against the people of Israel.
[6] Look, I myself have taken your brothers, the Levites, from among the people of Israel.
They are a gift to you, given to the LORD,
to do the work at the Tent of Meeting.
[7] You and your sons with you shall do your work as priests
in everything that has to do with the altar and with what is behind the curtain.
You shall serve.
I give you the work of the priesthood as a gift.
Any outsider who comes near shall be put to death.”

> **What does this mean?**
> This is God’s answer to the fear of the people (17:12–13). The priests and Levites stand like a protective layer between the holy God and the people.
> “Carry the guilt” means: They carry the responsibility. If something goes wrong in the service at the holy tent, they are responsible, not the whole people.
> The priesthood is a “gift.” It is not a right that someone can take for himself, as Korah wanted. God gives it.

## What belongs to the priests (Verses 8–19)

[8] The LORD spoke to Aaron,
“Look, I myself have put you in charge of my special offerings,
all the holy gifts of the people of Israel.
I have given them to you and your sons because you were anointed, as your share forever.
[9] Of the most holy gifts, the parts that are not burned shall be yours:
every offering they give to me, every grain offering, every sin offering, and every guilt offering.
They shall be most holy for you and for your sons.
[10] You shall eat it as something most holy.
Every male may eat it. It shall be holy to you.

[11] “This is yours too: the gifts they give as wave offerings,
all the wave offerings of the people of Israel.
I have given them to you, to your sons, and to your daughters with you, as your share forever.
Everyone in your house who is clean may eat it.

[12] “I have given you all the best of the oil,
all the best of the new wine and of the grain,
the first part of the harvest that they give to the LORD.
[13] The first ripe fruit of everything in their land that they bring to the LORD
shall be yours.
Everyone in your house who is clean may eat it.
[14] “Everything in Israel that is completely given over to God shall be yours.

[15] “Every firstborn,
of all living beings that they bring to the LORD, both people and animals, shall be yours.
But you must surely let the firstborn sons of people be bought back.
And you shall also let the firstborn of unclean animals be bought back.
[16] Those who must be bought back you shall buy back from the age of one month,
at the price you set: five shekels of silver, by the weight of the holy place. A shekel is 20 gerahs.

[17] “But you shall not let the firstborn of a cow, a sheep, or a goat be bought back.
They are holy.
You shall sprinkle their blood on the altar
and burn their fat as an offering by fire, a pleasant smell for the LORD.
[18] Their meat shall be yours, just as the breast of the wave offering and the right thigh are yours.
[19] All the special offerings from the holy gifts that the people of Israel bring to the LORD,
I have given to you, to your sons, and to your daughters with you, as your share forever.
It is a covenant of salt forever before the LORD, for you and for your descendants with you.”

> **What does this mean?**
> The priests live from the gifts of the people. They receive parts of the offerings, the best of the oil, wine, and grain, and the first fruits.
> Some things only the priests themselves may eat (“most holy”). Other things the whole family may eat, including the daughters.
> A “covenant of salt” is a covenant that lasts forever. Salt does not spoil. Back then, people who ate bread and salt together were firmly bound to each other.
> Five shekels are about 2 ounces of silver.

## God is their inheritance (Verses 20–24)

[20] The LORD said to Aaron,
“You shall have no inheritance in their land,
and you shall have no share among them.
I am your share and your inheritance among the people of Israel.

[21] “Look, I have given the descendants of Levi the whole tenth part of everything in Israel as their inheritance.
This tenth part is called the tithe.
It is their pay for the work they do, the work at the Tent of Meeting.
[22] From now on, the people of Israel shall not come near the Tent of Meeting,
so that they do not carry sin and die.
[23] But the Levites shall do the work at the Tent of Meeting.
And they shall carry the guilt for it.
This is a rule forever, for all your generations.
They shall have no inheritance among the people of Israel.
[24] For I have given the Levites the tithe of the people of Israel as their inheritance,
which the people give to the LORD as a special offering.
That is why I have said to them, ‘They shall have no inheritance among the people of Israel.’”

> **What does this mean?**
> All the other tribes receive their own area in the promised land. The priests and Levites receive no land.
> God says: “I am your share and your inheritance.” That is a beautiful sentence. They have no land, but they have God himself. And God provides for them through the gifts of the people.
> Psalm 16:5 says something similar: The LORD has given me my share and my cup. He keeps my future safe.

## The Levites also give a tithe (Verses 25–32)

[25] The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
[26] “Also speak to the Levites and tell them,
‘You will receive from the people of Israel the tithe that I have given you from them as your inheritance.
Then you shall give a special offering from it to the LORD, a tenth of the tithe.
[27] Your special offering will count for you
as if it were grain from the threshing floor and wine from the full winepress.
[28] So you too shall give a special offering to the LORD from all your tithes
that you receive from the people of Israel.
And from it you shall give the LORD’s special offering to Aaron the priest.
[29] From all your gifts you shall give every special offering to the LORD,
from the best of everything, the holy part of it.’

[30] “So you shall tell them,
‘When you have given the best of it,
it will count for the Levites like the harvest from the threshing floor and the harvest from the winepress.
[31] You may eat it anywhere, you and your families.
For it is your pay for your work at the Tent of Meeting.
[32] You will not carry any sin because of it when you have given the best of it.
You shall not dishonor the holy gifts of the people of Israel, so that you do not die.’”

> **What does this mean?**
> Even the Levites, who live from the tithe, are to give a tithe themselves. No one is excused from giving something back to God.
> For the Levites, the tithe is like the harvest for a farmer. And just as the farmer gives some of it away, they give some of it away too.
> What they keep is their rightful pay. They may eat it anywhere with their families. Whoever serves others may also live from it (1 Corinthians 9:13–14).
