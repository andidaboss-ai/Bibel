# Numbers – Chapter 17
### Aaron’s staff blossoms

---

## 12 staffs before God (Verses 1–7)

[1] The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
[2] “Speak to the people of Israel and get staffs from them,
a staff for each family, from all their leaders, family by family: 12 staffs.
Write each man’s name on his staff.
[3] You shall write Aaron’s name on the staff of Levi.
For there shall be one staff for each head of their families.
[4] Put them in the Tent of Meeting in front of the covenant, where I meet with you.
[5] The staff of the man I choose will sprout.
That way I will put an end to the complaints of the people of Israel, which they make against you.
Then I will not have to hear them anymore.”
[6] Moses spoke to the people of Israel.
And all their leaders gave him staffs, a staff for each leader, family by family, 12 staffs in all.
Aaron’s staff was among their staffs.
[7] Moses put the staffs before the LORD in the Tent of the Testimony.

> **What does this mean?**
> In some Bibles this is 17:16–28, because there Chapter 17 already begins at 16:36.
> After Korah’s rebellion, it is to become clear once and for all whom God has chosen as priest. That is why there is a test in which no one has to die.
> A staff was the sign of a leader. It was made of dead, dry wood. Each staff has the name of a tribal leader on it.

---

## The staff blossoms (Verses 8–11)

[8] On the next day, Moses went into the Tent of the Testimony.
Look! Aaron’s staff for the house of Levi had sprouted.
It had grown buds, produced blossoms, and borne ripe almonds.
[9] Moses brought all the staffs out from before the LORD to all the people of Israel.
They looked, and each man took his staff.
[10] The LORD said to Moses,
“Put Aaron’s staff back in front of the covenant.
It shall be kept there as a sign for the people who rebel.
That way you will put an end to their complaints against me, so that they do not die.”
[11] Moses did it.
He did just as the LORD had commanded him.

> **What does this mean?**
> Overnight a miracle happens: A dead stick produces buds, blossoms, and ripe almonds, all at the same time.
> In Israel, the almond tree is the first tree to blossom in the spring. In Hebrew it is called “shaked,” the “one that keeps watch.” The lampstand in the holy tent also had blossoms like almond blossoms (Exodus 25:33).
> God shows: Where he chooses, life grows out of something dead.
> The staff was kept in front of the ark of the covenant as a reminder. According to the Letter to the Hebrews, it was even inside the ark (Hebrews 9:4).

---

## The people are afraid (Verses 12–13)

[12] The people of Israel said to Moses,
“Look, we are dying! We are lost! We are all lost!
[13] Everyone who comes near the LORD’s tabernacle dies!
Are we all going to die?”

> **What does this mean?**
> After everything that has happened, the people are very afraid now. They ask: How can we live near God at all without dying?
> The next chapter answers this. God appointed the priests and Levites for exactly this purpose: so that the people are protected and can still live near God.
