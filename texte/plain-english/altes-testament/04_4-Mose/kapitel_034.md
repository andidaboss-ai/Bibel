# Numbers – Chapter 34
### The borders of the land

---

## The southern border (Verses 1–5)

[1] The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
[2] “Command the people of Israel, and tell them,
‘You will come into the land of Canaan.
This is the land that will be given to you as an inheritance, the land of Canaan with its borders.
[3] Your south side will run from the wilderness of Zin along the side of Edom.
Your southern border will start in the east, at the end of the Salt Sea.
[4] Your border will turn south of the ascent of Akrabbim and go on to Zin.
It will pass south of Kadesh Barnea.
From there it will go to Hazar Addar and go on to Azmon.
[5] From Azmon the border will turn to the brook of Egypt,
and it will end at the sea.

> **What does this mean?**
> God describes the borders of the land that Israel is to receive.
> The “Salt Sea” is the Dead Sea. It is so salty that no fish live in it.
> “Akrabbim” is Hebrew and means “scorpions.” The ascent of Akrabbim, the “Scorpion Pass,” is a steep road in the south.
> The “brook of Egypt” is probably a river valley in the north of the Sinai Peninsula. It only has water when it rains. The “sea” means the Mediterranean Sea.

---

## The western border and the northern border (Verses 6–9)

[6] “‘For the western border, you will have the great sea and its coast.
This will be your western border.
[7] “‘This will be your northern border:
From the great sea you must mark out your border to Mount Hor.
[8] From Mount Hor you must mark it out to the entrance of Hamath.
And the border will pass by Zedad.
[9] Then the border will go to Ziphron, and it will end at Hazar Enan.
This will be your northern border.

> **What does this mean?**
> The “great sea” is the Mediterranean Sea.
> Mount Hor here in the north is a different mountain from the Mount Hor where Aaron died. That one was in the south, near Edom.
> The WEB, our English source text, says “the entrance of Hamath.” Many translate it as a place name: “Lebo Hamath.” Hamath was a city in what is today Syria.

---

## The eastern border (Verses 10–12)

[10] “‘You must mark out your eastern border from Hazar Enan to Shepham.
[11] The border will go down from Shepham to Riblah, on the east side of Ain.
The border will go on down and reach the shore of the sea of Chinnereth on the east.
[12] The border will go down to the Jordan and end at the Salt Sea.
This will be your land, with its borders all around.’”

> **What does this mean?**
> The “sea of Chinnereth” is the Sea of Galilee. Later Jesus lived and preached by this lake.
> The eastern border is the Jordan. So the land of Canaan in the narrow sense lies west of the Jordan.
> In its history, Israel only rarely owned all of the land with these borders. It came closest under King David and King Solomon.
> These old borders are not a map for politics today. Here the Bible describes what God promised his people back then.

---

## The land for nine and a half tribes (Verses 13–15)

[13] Moses commanded the people of Israel. He said,
“This is the land that you will inherit by lot.
The LORD has commanded that it be given to the nine tribes and to the half-tribe.
[14] For the tribe of the people of Reuben, by their families,
the tribe of the people of Gad, by their families,
and the half-tribe of Manasseh have already received their inheritance.
[15] The two tribes and the half-tribe have received their inheritance on the other side of the Jordan,
at Jericho, to the east, toward the sunrise.”

> **What does this mean?**
> This connects to Chapter 32: Reuben, Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh have already received their land east of the Jordan.

---

## The men who divide the land (Verses 16–29)

[16] The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
[17] “These are the names of the men who will divide the land among you as an inheritance:
Eleazar the priest, and Joshua, the son of Nun.
[18] You must take one leader from every tribe to divide the land as an inheritance.
[19] These are the names of the men:
From the tribe of Judah: Caleb, the son of Jephunneh.
[20] From the tribe of the people of Simeon: Shemuel, the son of Ammihud.
[21] From the tribe of Benjamin: Elidad, the son of Chislon.
[22] From the tribe of the people of Dan, a leader: Bukki, the son of Jogli.
[23] From the people of Joseph:
from the tribe of the people of Manasseh, a leader: Hanniel, the son of Ephod.
[24] From the tribe of the people of Ephraim, a leader: Kemuel, the son of Shiphtan.
[25] From the tribe of the people of Zebulun, a leader: Elizaphan, the son of Parnach.
[26] From the tribe of the people of Issachar, a leader: Paltiel, the son of Azzan.
[27] From the tribe of the people of Asher, a leader: Ahihud, the son of Shelomi.
[28] From the tribe of the people of Naphtali, a leader: Pedahel, the son of Ammihud.”
[29] These are the men whom the LORD commanded
to divide the inheritance among the people of Israel in the land of Canaan.

> **What does this mean?**
> The land is to be divided fairly. That is why every tribe gets a representative. No tribe is to be treated unfairly.
> Only ten tribes are named. Reuben and Gad are missing, because they already have their land. Manasseh still gets a representative, because the other half of the tribe will still receive land west of the Jordan.
> Caleb is the only well-known name. He was one of the two faithful spies. Now, after 40 years, he may help divide the land that he explored back then.
