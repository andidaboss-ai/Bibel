# Leviticus – Chapter 18
### Rules for sexuality and family

---

## Not like in Egypt and not like in Canaan (Verses 1–5)

[1] The LORD said to Moses,
[2] “Speak to the people of Israel and tell them,
‘I am the LORD your God.
[3] You shall not do what people do in the land of Egypt, where you lived.
And you shall not do what people do in the land of Canaan, where I am bringing you.
You shall not live by their rules.
[4] You shall do what my laws say.
You shall keep my rules and live by them.
I am the LORD your God.
[5] So you shall keep my rules and my laws.
Whoever does them will live by them.
I am the LORD.

> **What does this mean?**
> Israel is to live differently from the nations around it. God’s commandments are not a burden. They are a way to life: “Whoever does them will live by them.”
> This chapter is about sexuality and family. The Bible speaks openly about these things. The goal is to protect the family, especially the weaker people in the family.

---

## No sex with close relatives (Verses 6–18)

[6] “‘None of you shall come near any close relative to have sex with them.
I am the LORD.

[7] “‘You shall not have sex with your father.
And you shall not have sex with your mother.
She is your mother. You shall not have sex with her.
[8] “‘You shall not have sex with your father’s wife.
She belongs to your father.
[9] “‘You shall not have sex with your sister,
the daughter of your father or the daughter of your mother,
whether she was born at home or somewhere else.
[10] “‘You shall not have sex with your son’s daughter or your daughter’s daughter.
For their body is your own body.
[11] “‘You shall not have sex with the daughter of your father’s wife, whom your father fathered.
She is your sister.
[12] “‘You shall not have sex with your father’s sister.
She is a close relative of your father.
[13] “‘You shall not have sex with your mother’s sister.
For she is a close relative of your mother.
[14] “‘You shall not have sex with your father’s brother.
You shall not come near his wife. She is your aunt.
[15] “‘You shall not have sex with your daughter-in-law.
She is your son’s wife. You shall not have sex with her.
[16] “‘You shall not have sex with your brother’s wife.
She belongs to your brother.
[17] “‘You shall not have sex with a woman and her daughter.
You shall not take her son’s daughter or her daughter’s daughter to have sex with her.
They are close relatives. It is wicked.
[18] “‘You shall not marry your wife’s sister as well,
so that they become rivals, and have sex with her while your wife is still alive.

> **What does this mean?**
> Our English source text says “uncover the nakedness.” That is an old, discreet expression for sexual intercourse. It means: to sleep with someone.
> These rules forbid sex between close relatives. This is called incest. To this day it is forbidden in almost all countries. It destroys families and hurts children and young people very badly.
> Back then, many generations often lived together in one large family. These rules protected everyone, especially the women and girls, from abuse within the family.
> Verse 18 is a reminder of Jacob. He married two sisters, Leah and Rachel. That led to a lot of jealousy and fighting (Genesis 29–30).
> Verse 16 has an exception: If a brother died without children, his brother was to marry the widow (Deuteronomy 25:5–6).

## Other forbidden acts (Verses 19–23)

[19] “‘You shall not come near a woman to have sex with her while she is unclean because of her monthly period.
[20] “‘You shall not sleep with your neighbor’s wife and make yourself unclean with her.
[21] “‘You shall not give any of your children as a sacrifice to Molech.
You shall not dishonor the name of your God. I am the LORD.
[22] “‘You shall not sleep with a man the way one sleeps with a woman.
That is disgusting.
[23] “‘You shall not sleep with any animal and make yourself unclean with it.
And no woman may give herself to an animal to have sex with it.
That is a perversion.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 20 forbids adultery. That is also in the Ten Commandments (Exodus 20:14).
> Verse 21: Molech was a god of the neighboring nations. Children were sacrificed and burned for him. For Israel this is one of the worst crimes of all. The Bible condemns it sharply again and again (for example Jeremiah 7:31).
> Verse 22 is about sex between men. Today people understand this passage in very different ways:
> Many Jews and Christians, especially in traditional congregations, see it as a lasting commandment of God.
> Other Jews and Christians say: This rule must be understood from its own time. Back then it may have been about certain practices, for example the worship of other nations, or violence and exploitation. Loving, faithful partnerships as we know them today were not in view back then.
> What matters: This passage must never be used to justify hatred, mockery, or violence against people. The Bible also says: “You shall love your neighbor as yourself” (Leviticus 19:18). Every human being is created in God’s image (Genesis 1:27).

## The land vomits out its people (Verses 24–30)

[24] “‘Do not make yourselves unclean by any of these things.
For the nations that I am driving out ahead of you made themselves unclean by all these things.
[25] The land became unclean.
So I punished it for its guilt.
And the land vomited out the people who lived in it.
[26] So you shall keep my rules and my laws.
You shall not do any of these disgusting things.
This is true for those born in Israel and for the foreigners who live among you.
[27] For the people of the land who were there before you did all these disgusting things,
and the land became unclean.
[28] Otherwise the land will vomit you out too when you make it unclean,
just as it vomited out the nation that was there before you.

[29] “‘For whoever does any of these disgusting things
shall be cut off from his people.
[30] So you shall do what I require.
Do not live by any of these disgusting customs that people practiced before you.
And do not make yourselves unclean by them.
I am the LORD your God.’”

> **What does this mean?**
> Here the land is almost like a living being. When the people in it do wrong, it feels “sick” and vomits them out.
> This is a serious warning to Israel: You are not safe either if you live like this. The land is a gift. It is not something you simply own forever.
> Later in Israel’s history, the people really were taken away into exile. The prophets saw this as the result of Israel not keeping God’s commandments.
