# Leviticus – Chapter 9
### Aaron’s first offering – God’s glory appears

---

## Today the LORD appears to you (Verses 1–7)

[1] On the eighth day, Moses called Aaron and his sons and the elders of Israel.
[2] He said to Aaron,
“Take a calf from the cattle as a sin offering and a ram as a burnt offering, both without any fault,
and bring them before the LORD.
[3] And speak to the people of Israel and say,
‘Take a male goat as a sin offering,
and a calf and a lamb, both one year old and without any fault, as a burnt offering,
[4] and a bull and a ram as peace offerings, to sacrifice before the LORD,
and a meal offering mixed with oil.
For today the LORD appears to you.’”

[5] They brought what Moses had commanded in front of the Tent of Meeting.
The whole community came near and stood before the LORD.
[6] Moses said,
“This is what the LORD commanded you to do.
Then the LORD’s glory will appear to you.”
Glory means: the bright light of God’s greatness.
[7] Moses said to Aaron,
“Come near to the altar.
Bring your sin offering and your burnt offering.
Make atonement for yourself and for the people.
Then bring the offering of the people and make atonement for them,
as the LORD commanded.”

> **What does this mean?**
> After the seven days of the ceremony comes the eighth day. It is the first day of a new beginning. Now Aaron does his work as a priest himself for the first time.
> First Aaron must bring an offering for himself. The high priest, too, is a person with faults. Only then can he come before God for the people.
> Aaron has to offer a calf, of all things. Maybe this is meant to remind him of the golden calf he had made (Exodus 32). Now this guilt is forgiven.

---

## Aaron’s offering for himself (Verses 8–14)

[8] So Aaron came near to the altar and killed the calf for the sin offering, which was for himself.
[9] Aaron’s sons brought the blood to him.
He dipped his finger in the blood and put it on the horns of the altar.
He poured out the rest of the blood at the bottom of the altar.
[10] But he burned on the altar the fat, the kidneys and the covering from the liver of the sin offering,
as the LORD commanded Moses.
[11] He burned the meat and the skin with fire outside the camp.

[12] He killed the burnt offering.
Aaron’s sons handed him the blood, and he sprinkled it all around on the altar.
[13] They handed him the burnt offering, piece by piece, and the head.
He burned them on the altar.
[14] He washed the inner parts and the legs, and burned them on top of the burnt offering on the altar.

---

## Aaron’s offering for the people (Verses 15–21)

[15] Then he brought the people’s offering.
He took the goat for the sin offering, which was for the people, and killed it,
and offered it for sin, like the first one.
[16] He brought the burnt offering and offered it as the rule says.
[17] He brought the meal offering.
He filled his hand with some of it and burned it on the altar,
in addition to the burnt offering of the morning.

[18] He also killed the bull and the ram, the sacrifice of peace offerings for the people.
Aaron’s sons handed him the blood, and he sprinkled it all around on the altar.
[19] And they handed him the fat of the bull and of the ram:
the fat tail, the fat that covers the inner parts, the kidneys and the covering of the liver.
[20] They put the fat on the breasts.
And he burned the fat on the altar.
[21] Aaron waved the breasts and the right thigh before the LORD as a wave offering,
as Moses commanded.

> **What does this mean?**
> Now all the kinds of offerings come together: the sin offering, the burnt offering, the meal offering and the peace offering.
> The order has a meaning: First the guilt is forgiven (sin offering). Then you give yourself completely to God (burnt offering). Then you give thanks (meal offering). And last, you celebrate fellowship with God (peace offering).

---

## The blessing and the fire from the LORD (Verses 22–24)

[22] Aaron lifted up his hands toward the people and blessed them.
Then he came down, after he had brought the sin offering, the burnt offering and the peace offerings.
[23] Moses and Aaron went into the Tent of Meeting.
Then they came out and blessed the people.
And the LORD’s glory appeared to all the people.
[24] Fire came out from before the LORD and burned up the burnt offering and the fat on the altar.
When all the people saw it, they shouted for joy and fell down on their faces.

> **What does this mean?**
> This is a great high point. God accepts the offerings. Fire comes from God and burns up the offering. This is the sign: God is here, and he is at peace with his people.
> Aaron lifts his hands and blesses the people. Numbers 6:24–26 shows how this blessing later sounds. It begins: May the LORD bless you and keep you … These words are still spoken today in synagogues and churches.
> The people shout for joy and fall down. Joy and deep respect belong together.
> But in the very next chapter, something terrible happens.
