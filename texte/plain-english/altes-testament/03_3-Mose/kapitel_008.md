# Leviticus – Chapter 8
### Aaron and his sons are made priests

---

## The people gather (Verses 1–5)

[1] The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
[2] “Take Aaron and his sons with him,
and the clothes, the anointing oil, the bull for the sin offering, the two rams and the basket of bread without yeast.
Anointing oil is special holy oil. It is poured on people or things to set them apart for God.
[3] And gather the whole community at the door of the Tent of Meeting.”
[4] Moses did as the LORD had commanded him.
And the community gathered at the door of the Tent of Meeting.
[5] Moses said to the community,
“This is what the LORD has commanded to be done.”

> **What does this mean?**
> Now what God commanded in Exodus 29 happens: Aaron and his sons are made priests.
> The whole people is there. After all, the priests serve the whole people. That is why everyone should see how they are put into their office.
> Again and again this chapter says: “as the LORD commanded Moses.” Everything happens exactly according to God’s word.

---

## Washing, dressing and anointing (Verses 6–13)

[6] Moses brought Aaron and his sons and washed them with water.
[7] He put the tunic on Aaron and tied the sash around him.
A tunic is a long shirt.
He dressed him in the robe and put the ephod on him.
The ephod is a special apron of the high priest.
He tied the skillfully woven band of the ephod around him and fastened the ephod to him with it.
[8] He put the breastplate on him.
In the breastplate he put the Urim and Thummim.
The Urim and Thummim were holy objects. The priest used them to ask God for decisions.
[9] He set the turban on his head.
And on the front of the turban he set the golden plate, the holy crown,
as the LORD commanded Moses.

[10] Moses took the anointing oil.
He anointed the tabernacle and everything in it, and made them holy.
The tabernacle is the holy tent.
[11] He sprinkled some of it on the altar seven times.
He anointed the altar and all its tools, and the basin and its stand, to make them holy.
[12] He poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron’s head and anointed him, to make him holy.
[13] Moses brought Aaron’s sons.
He dressed them in tunics, tied sashes around them and put headbands on them,
as the LORD commanded Moses.

> **What does this mean?**
> The clothes and objects were described in Exodus 28.
> Oil is poured over Aaron. Psalm 133:2 talks about this: It is like the precious oil on the head that runs down on the beard, on Aaron’s beard. There it is a picture of the joy when people live together in peace.

---

## The sin offering (Verses 14–17)

[14] He brought the bull for the sin offering.
Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the bull for the sin offering.
[15] He killed it.
Moses took the blood and put it with his finger all around on the horns of the altar.
So he made the altar clean.
He poured out the rest of the blood at the bottom of the altar.
So he made it holy, to make atonement for it.
[16] He took all the fat that was on the inner parts, the covering of the liver, and the two kidneys with their fat.
And Moses burned it on the altar.
[17] But the bull, its skin, its meat and its dung he burned with fire outside the camp,
as the LORD commanded Moses.

---

## The burnt offering (Verses 18–21)

[18] He brought the ram for the burnt offering.
Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram.
[19] He killed it.
And Moses sprinkled the blood all around on the altar.
[20] He cut the ram into its pieces.
And Moses burned the head, the pieces and the fat.
[21] He washed the inner parts and the legs with water.
And Moses burned the whole ram on the altar.
It was a burnt offering, a pleasant smell, an offering made by fire to the LORD,
as the LORD commanded Moses.

> **What does this mean?**
> At this ceremony, Moses acts as the priest. After all, Aaron and his sons have not been set apart yet. Only after the ceremony may they bring offerings themselves.

---

## The consecration offering (Verses 22–30)

[22] He brought the other ram, the ram of consecration.
Consecration means: setting someone apart for God’s service.
Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram.
[23] He killed it.
Moses took some of its blood and put it
on the bottom of Aaron’s right ear,
on the thumb of his right hand
and on the big toe of his right foot.
[24] He brought Aaron’s sons.
Moses put some of the blood on the bottom of their right ear,
on the thumb of their right hand and on the big toe of their right foot.
And Moses sprinkled the rest of the blood all around on the altar.

[25] He took the fat:
the fat tail, all the fat that was on the inner parts, the covering of the liver,
the two kidneys and their fat, and the right thigh.
[26] And from the basket of bread without yeast that stood before the LORD,
he took one cake without yeast, one cake of bread made with oil, and one thin wafer.
He put them on the fat and on the right thigh.
[27] He put all this in Aaron’s hands and in his sons’ hands.
And he waved it before the LORD as a wave offering.
[28] Moses took it from their hands and burned it on the altar, on top of the burnt offering.
It was a consecration offering, a pleasant smell, an offering made by fire to the LORD.
[29] Moses took the breast and waved it before the LORD as a wave offering.
It was Moses’ share of the ram of consecration,
as the LORD commanded Moses.

[30] Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood that was on the altar.
He sprinkled it on Aaron and on his clothes,
and on his sons and on his sons’ clothes with him.
So he made Aaron and his clothes holy, and his sons and his sons’ clothes with him.

> **What does this mean?**
> The blood on the ear, the hand and the foot means: With his ear, the priest should listen to God. With his hand, he should do God’s work. And with his foot, he should walk on God’s paths. The whole person belongs to God.
> Oil and blood are sprinkled on the priests together. The oil is a sign of God’s Spirit and of God’s choice. The blood is a sign of atonement and of being made clean.

---

## Seven days at the door of the tent (Verses 31–36)

[31] Moses said to Aaron and his sons,
“Boil the meat at the door of the Tent of Meeting.
Eat it there, together with the bread that is in the basket of consecration.
That is what I commanded when I said, ‘Aaron and his sons shall eat it.’
[32] Whatever is left of the meat and of the bread you shall burn with fire.
[33] For seven days you shall not go out from the door of the Tent of Meeting,
until the days of your consecration are over.
For he will consecrate you for seven days.
[34] What has been done today is what the LORD has commanded to be done,
to make atonement for you.
[35] You shall stay at the door of the Tent of Meeting day and night for seven days,
and keep the LORD’s command, so that you do not die.
For that is what I have been commanded.”
[36] Aaron and his sons did all the things that the LORD commanded through Moses.

> **What does this mean?**
> The ceremony lasts seven days. Seven days – like the seven days of creation. A new life begins for the priests.
> During these seven days they stay at the holy tent day and night. They should be completely with God before they begin their service.
