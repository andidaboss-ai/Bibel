# Proverbs – Chapter 4
### Guard your heart

---

## What my father taught me (Verses 1–9)

[1] Listen, sons, to a father’s instruction!
Pay attention, so that you know understanding!
[2] For I give you good teaching.
Don’t leave my law!
[3] For I was a son to my father too,
tender and the only child in the eyes of my mother.
[4] He taught me and said to me:
“Let your heart hold on to my words.
Keep my commandments, and you will live.
[5] Get wisdom! Get understanding!
Don’t forget, and don’t turn away from the words of my mouth!
[6] Don’t leave her, and she will keep you safe.
Love her, and she will protect you.
[7] Wisdom is the most important thing. Get wisdom!
Yes, even if it costs everything you own, get understanding!
[8] Value her highly, and she will lift you up.
She will bring you to honor when you embrace her.
[9] She will give your head a beautiful wreath.
She will hand you a splendid crown.”

> **What does this mean?**
> Here a father speaks who learned from his own father. Wisdom is passed on from generation to generation: from grandfather to father to son.
> Verse 3: A loving look back: The father remembers how he himself was a small, tender child, loved by his mother.
> Verse 7: Wisdom is the most important thing. It is worth giving everything for it. Jesus tells a similar parable about a man who sells everything to buy a precious pearl (Matthew 13:45–46).

---

## Two ways: light and darkness (Verses 10–19)

[10] Listen, my son, and receive my words!
Then the years of your life will be many.
[11] I have taught you in the way of wisdom.
I have led you on straight paths.
[12] When you walk, your steps will not be held back.
When you run, you will not stumble.
[13] Hold on tight to instruction! Don’t let her go!
Keep her, for she is your life.
[14] Don’t step onto the path of the wicked!
Don’t walk in the way of evil men!
[15] Avoid it, and don’t walk on it!
Turn away from it, and go on!
[16] For they don’t sleep unless they have done evil.
Their sleep is taken away unless they have made someone fall.
[17] For they eat the bread of wickedness
and drink the wine of violence.
[18] But the path of the righteous is like the light of dawn
that shines brighter and brighter until the full day.
[19] The way of the wicked is like darkness.
They don’t know what they stumble over.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 14–15: Wise advice: Don’t even start on the wrong path. Stay well away from it.
> Verses 16–17: Evil people are addicted to evil. They cannot sleep until they have harmed someone.
> Verses 18–19: A beautiful picture: The way of the righteous is like the sunrise. It keeps getting brighter. The way of the wicked is dark. They stumble and don’t even know why.

---

## Guard your heart (Verses 20–27)

[20] My son, pay attention to my words!
Turn your ear to what I say!
[21] Don’t let them out of your sight!
Keep them in the center of your heart!
[22] For they are life to those who find them,
and health to their whole body.
[23] Guard your heart with all care,
for out of it is the spring of life.
[24] Keep a twisted mouth away from you!
Keep lying lips far away from you!
[25] Let your eyes look straight ahead!
Fix your gaze directly in front of you!
[26] Make the path of your feet level!
Let all your ways be firm.
[27] Don’t turn to the right or to the left!
Keep your foot away from evil!

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 23 is one of the most important sentences of the book. In the Bible, the heart is not only feelings. It is the center of a person: thinking, wanting, feeling. Everything we do comes out of the heart. So: Take good care of your heart! What do you let in? What do you think? What do you want?
> Verses 24–27: The whole person is needed: the mouth (what I say), the eyes (where I look), the feet (where I go).
> Verse 25: Look ahead to your goal, and don’t let yourself be distracted.
