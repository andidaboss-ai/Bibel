# Proverbs – Chapter 2
### Search for wisdom like a treasure

---

## If you search for wisdom (Verses 1–11)

[1] My son, if you receive my words
and store up my commandments within you,
[2] so that you turn your ear to wisdom
and set your heart on understanding,
[3] yes, if you call out for good sense
and raise your voice for understanding,
[4] if you look for her like silver
and search for her as for hidden treasures,
[5] then you will understand the fear of the LORD
and find the knowledge of God.
[6] For the LORD gives wisdom.
Out of his mouth come knowledge and understanding.
[7] He stores up sound wisdom for the upright.
He is a shield for those who live with integrity,
[8] so that he may guard the paths of justice
and protect the way of his holy ones.
[9] Then you will understand righteousness and justice,
fairness and every good path.
[10] For wisdom will come into your heart.
Knowledge will be pleasant to your soul.
[11] Good judgment will watch over you.
Understanding will protect you.

> **What does this mean?**
> Wisdom does not fall from the sky. You have to search for it, like a treasure hunter who digs for silver. You have to listen, call out, search.
> But at the same time, wisdom is a gift: “The LORD gives wisdom” (verse 6). Both belong together: our searching and God’s giving.
> Verse 11: Whoever is wise has a guard inside. He notices in time when something becomes dangerous.

---

## Wisdom protects you (Verses 12–22)

[12] She will save you from the way of evil,
from the men who speak twisted things,
[13] who leave the paths of uprightness
to walk in the ways of darkness,
[14] who are happy to do evil
and enjoy the twisted ways of evil,
[15] who are crooked in their ways
and wrong in their paths.
[16] She will save you from the strange woman,
from the foreign woman who flatters with her words,
[17] who leaves the friend of her youth
and forgets the covenant of her God.
[18] For her house leads down to death,
her paths to the spirits of the dead.
[19] No one who goes to her comes back again.
They do not reach the paths of life.
[20] So walk in the way of good men,
and keep to the paths of the righteous!
[21] For the upright will live in the land.
The blameless will stay in it.
[22] But the wicked will be cut off from the land.
The unfaithful will be torn out of it.

> **What does this mean?**
> Wisdom protects from two dangers: from evil men (verses 12–15) and from the “strange woman” (verses 16–19).
> The “strange woman” is a woman who leaves her husband (“the friend of her youth”) and seduces another man. This is about adultery. “Strange” here means: she does not belong to you, she is married to someone else. It is not a statement against foreign women or against women in general.
> Because the book was written for young men, it speaks of the seductive woman. But the warning is just as true the other way around: for women facing seductive men. Unfaithfulness destroys relationships and lives.
> Verse 17: Marriage is a covenant before God. Whoever breaks it breaks more than a promise to a person.
