# Song of Songs – Chapter 3
### I looked for the one my soul loves

---

## Searching at night (Verses 1–5)

[1] At night on my bed,
I looked for the one my soul loves.
I looked for him, but I did not find him.
[2] I will get up now and go around the city.
In the streets and in the squares I will look for
the one my soul loves.
I looked for him, but I did not find him.
[3] The watchmen who go around the city found me.
“Have you seen the one my soul loves?”
[4] I had hardly passed them
when I found the one my soul loves.
I held him and would not let him go
until I had brought him into my mother’s house,
into the room of the woman who conceived me.
[5] I make you promise, daughters of Jerusalem,
by the gazelles or by the female deer of the field,
that you do not stir up or awaken love
until it is ready.

> **What does this mean?**
> The young woman cannot sleep. She longs for the one she loves. In the middle of the night, she looks for him all over the city.
> Four times she says: “the one my soul loves.” Her love is very deep.
> Verse 4: When she finds him, she holds on to him and does not let him go.
> Many people have also read these verses as a picture of the search for God: You search and search, and then you find him and don’t want to let him go.

---

## Solomon’s wedding procession (Verses 6–11)

[6] Who is this coming up from the wilderness
like columns of smoke,
scented with myrrh and frankincense,
with all the spices of the merchant?
[7] Look, it is Solomon’s carriage!
60 mighty men are around it,
from the mighty men of Israel.
[8] They all carry the sword
and are experienced in war.
Each one has his sword at his side
because of the terrors of the night.
[9] King Solomon made himself a carriage
from the wood of Lebanon.
[10] He made its posts of silver,
its floor of gold,
its seat of purple cloth.
Its inside is lovingly decorated
by the daughters of Jerusalem.
[11] Go out, you daughters of Zion,
and see King Solomon
with the crown with which his mother crowned him
on the day of his wedding,
on the day of the joy of his heart.

> **What does this mean?**
> A splendid wedding procession is coming: Solomon’s carriage, a seat carried by people, made of precious wood, silver, gold and purple, surrounded by 60 bodyguards. A cloud of scent from frankincense and myrrh goes ahead of it.
> Verse 10: “Lovingly decorated”: The women of Jerusalem have decorated the inside with love.
> Verse 11: On his wedding day, the groom wears a crown. This is still a custom at some weddings in the Middle East today. The wedding day is “the day of the joy of his heart.”
