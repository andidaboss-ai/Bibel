# Job – Chapter 25
### Bildad’s last speech: a human being is a worm

---

## How can a human being be righteous? (Verses 1–6)

[1] Then Bildad the Shuhite answered,
[2] “Power to rule and fear belong to him.
He makes peace in his high places.
[3] Can anyone count his armies?
On whom does his light not rise?
[4] So how can a human being be righteous before God?
How can someone born of a woman be clean?
[5] Look, even the moon has no brightness,
and the stars are not pure in his eyes.
[6] How much less a human being, who is a worm,
a son of man, who is a worm!”

> **What does this mean?**
> This is the shortest speech in the whole book, only five verses. The friends are running out of arguments.
> Bildad praises God’s greatness: God rules over everything. He makes peace in heaven. His armies of angels cannot be counted.
> Then he repeats what Eliphaz already said (Job 4:17; 15:14): No human being is clean before God.
> His last word is harsh: A human being is “a worm.” In Hebrew there are two different words here, something like “maggot” and “worm.” That is Bildad’s picture of a human being: small, dirty, worthless.
> In other places, the Bible sees this differently: A human being is small before God, but is made in God’s image (Genesis 1:27). God made human beings only a little lower than the heavenly beings (Psalm 8:5; in some Bibles 8:6). God loves people.
> Zophar does not speak at all in this third round. The friends have nothing more to say.
