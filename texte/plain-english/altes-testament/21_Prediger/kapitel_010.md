# Ecclesiastes – Chapter 10
### A little foolishness

---

## Dead flies in the perfume (Verses 1–7)

[1] Dead flies make the perfumer’s oil stink.
In the same way, a little foolishness outweighs wisdom and honor.
[2] The heart of the wise is at his right hand,
but the heart of the fool is at his left.
[3] Yes, even when the fool walks along the road,
his understanding fails him,
and he tells everyone that he is a fool.
[4] If the anger of the ruler rises against you,
don’t leave your place,
for calmness puts great wrongs to rest.
[5] There is an evil that I have seen under the sun,
a kind of mistake that comes from the ruler:
[6] Foolishness is set in high places of honor,
and the rich sit in low places.
[7] I have seen servants on horses,
and princes walking on the ground like servants.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 1: A well-known picture: Even one dead fly can spoil an expensive perfume. In the same way, even a small foolish act can destroy a good reputation.
> Verse 2: Back then, “right” stood for what is good and skillful, “left” for what is clumsy. This is a picture from the language, not a judgment about left-handed people.
> Verse 3: A fool gives himself away as soon as he goes out into the street.
> Verse 4: When your boss is angry with you, don’t run away. Stay calm. Calmness can settle a lot of trouble.
> Verses 6–7: Sometimes the world is upside down: People who are not able get high positions, and able people are passed over.

---

## Every job has its danger (Verses 8–11)

[8] Whoever digs a pit may fall into it,
and whoever breaks through a wall may be bitten by a snake.
[9] Whoever cuts stones may be hurt by them.
Whoever splits wood may be put in danger by it.
[10] If the ax is dull and you don’t sharpen the edge,
then you must use more strength,
but skill brings success.
[11] If the snake bites before it is charmed,
the snake charmer’s tongue is of no use.

> **What does this mean?**
> Every job has its risks. You have to be careful.
> Verse 10: Practical advice: First sharpen the ax! Whoever prepares wisely saves a lot of strength. Wisdom makes work easier.
> Verse 11: Whoever comes too late gets no use from his skill. The right moment matters.

---

## The words of the fool (Verses 12–15)

[12] The words from the mouth of the wise are gracious,
but the fool is swallowed up by his own lips.
[13] The beginning of the words of his mouth is foolishness,
and the end of his talk is wicked madness.
[14] The fool also makes many words.
People do not know what will be.
And what will be after him, who can tell him?
[15] The hard work of fools makes every one of them tired,
for he does not even know how to get to the city.

> **What does this mean?**
> The fool talks a lot, but nothing good comes of it. He talks about the future as if he knew it.
> Verse 15: A funny sentence: The fool is so unable that he cannot even find the way to the city. He works hard and gets nowhere.

---

## Good and bad rulers (Verses 16–20)

[16] How terrible for you, land, when your king is a child
and your princes feast in the morning!
[17] Happy are you, land, when your king is the son of nobles
and your princes eat at the right time,
to gain strength and not to get drunk!
[18] Through laziness the roof sinks in,
and through idle hands the house leaks.
[19] A feast is made for laughter,
and wine makes life glad,
and money is the answer for everything.
[20] Don’t curse the king, not even in your thoughts,
and don’t curse the rich in your bedroom,
for a bird of the sky may carry your voice,
and something with wings may tell the matter.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 16–17: A land suffers when its leaders are immature and only party. A land is happy when its leaders act responsibly.
> Verse 18: Whoever does not take care of his house will soon have rain coming through the roof. That is true for a state too.
> Verse 19: An ironic sentence: Some people think money solves all problems. The Preacher describes this attitude. He does not praise it.
> Verse 20: People say: A little bird told me. This saying reminds us of this verse. Be careful about what you say about the powerful. It can come out. Today this is especially true for messages and posts on the internet.
