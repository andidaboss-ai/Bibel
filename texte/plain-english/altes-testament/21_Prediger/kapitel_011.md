# Ecclesiastes – Chapter 11
### Throw your bread on the water

---

## Dare to do something! (Verses 1–6)

[1] Throw your bread on the waters,
for after many days you will find it again.
[2] Give a share to seven, yes, even to eight,
for you don’t know what disaster will come on the earth.
[3] When the clouds are full of rain,
they empty themselves on the earth.
And when a tree falls toward the south or toward the north,
it stays in the place where the tree fell.
[4] Whoever watches the wind will not sow,
and whoever looks at the clouds will not harvest.
[5] Just as you don’t know which way the wind goes,
or how the bones grow in the womb of a pregnant woman,
so you also don’t know the work of God, who does everything.
[6] In the morning, sow your seed,
and in the evening, don’t let your hand rest,
for you don’t know which will succeed, this or that,
or whether both will be equally good.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 1: “Throw your bread on the waters”: A puzzling picture. Many understand it as a call to be generous: Give without expecting something back right away. It will come back to you later. Others think of trade by sea: Dare to do something, send your goods across the sea.
> Verse 2: Share with many, for you don’t know when you yourself will need help. Or: Spread out your risk.
> Verse 4: Whoever always waits for perfect weather never sows and never harvests. Whoever wants to make everything safe does nothing in the end.
> Verse 5: We don’t even understand how a child grows in its mother’s womb. How could we understand everything God does?
> Verse 6: So: Do what you can, morning and evening. What comes of it is in God’s hand.

---

## Enjoy your youth (Verses 7–10)

[7] Truly, the light is sweet,
and it is pleasant for the eyes to see the sun.
[8] Yes, if a person lives many years,
let him be glad in all of them,
but let him remember the days of darkness,
for there will be many.
All that comes is vanity.
[9] Be glad, young man, in your youth,
and let your heart make you cheerful in the days of your youth.
Walk in the ways of your heart
and after what your eyes see.
But know that for all these things God will bring you to judgment.
[10] So remove sorrow from your heart,
and keep evil away from your body,
for youth and the dawn of life are vanity.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 7: A beautiful, simple sentence: The light is sweet. It is good to see the sun. Life is a gift.
> Verse 8: Be glad in every year. But don’t forget: Dark days will come too, old age and death.
> Verse 9: An encouragement to young people: Enjoy your youth! Follow your heart! But remember: You are responsible to God for what you do. Freedom and responsibility belong together.
> Verse 10: Youth passes quickly, like the dawn. So: Don’t let worries steal your joy.
