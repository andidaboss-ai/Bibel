# Ecclesiastes – Chapter 6
### Who knows what is good for people?

---

## Rich but not happy (Verses 1–6)

[1] There is an evil that I have seen under the sun,
and it lies heavily on people:
[2] a person to whom God gives riches, wealth, and honor,
so that he lacks nothing of all that his soul desires,
and yet God does not give him the ability to eat of it,
but a stranger eats it.
This is vanity, and it is an evil sickness.
[3] If a man has 100 children
and lives many years, so that the days of his years are many,
but his soul is not filled with good things,
and he does not even get a burial,
then I say: A stillborn child is better off than he is.
[4] For it comes in vanity
and goes away in darkness,
and its name is covered with darkness.
[5] Also, it has not seen the sun or known it.
It has more rest than the other.
[6] Yes, even if he lived 1,000 years twice over
and yet could not enjoy good things,
don’t all go to one place?

> **What does this mean?**
> A sad picture: Someone has everything: money, honor, many children, a long life. But he cannot enjoy any of it. He dies unhappy and is not even properly buried.
> Verses 3–5: The Preacher says: A child that is born dead is better off. At least it has rest. This is a very hard statement that is meant to show how terrible a life without joy is. It is no judgment about children who die before or at birth.
> Parents who have lost a child can find help, for example, in support groups for bereaved parents. In the US, call or text 988. In the UK and Ireland, call Samaritans at 116 123. Elsewhere, find help at findahelpline.com.

## People are like a shadow (Verses 7–12)

[7] All of a person’s hard work is for his mouth,
and yet his hunger is not satisfied.
[8] For what advantage does the wise person have over the fool?
What does the poor person gain by knowing
how to behave before the living?
[9] Better is what the eyes see
than the wandering of desire.
This too is vanity and a chasing after wind.
[10] Whatever has been, its name was given long ago,
and it is known what a person is.
He cannot argue with the one who is stronger than he is.
[11] For there are many words that increase vanity.
What good is that to a person?
[12] For who knows what is good for a person in life,
all the days of his empty life, which he spends like a shadow?
For who can tell a person
what will be after him under the sun?

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 7: We work in order to eat. But hunger comes back again and again. And the longing for more never stops.
> Verse 9: “Better is what the eyes see”: Enjoy what you have instead of always dreaming of more. That is contentment.
> Verse 10: “The one who is stronger” is God. People cannot argue with him.
> Verse 12: Our life is as short as a shadow. No one can tell us what comes afterward. These questions remain open. The book of Ecclesiastes lives with this tension.
