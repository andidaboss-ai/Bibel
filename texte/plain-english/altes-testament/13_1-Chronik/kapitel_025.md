# 1 Chronicles – Chapter 25
### The singers in the temple

---

## The families of the singers (Verses 1–7)

[1] David and the captains of the army set apart for the service some of the sons of Asaph, of Heman, and of Jeduthun.
They were to prophesy with harps, with stringed instruments, and with cymbals.
The number of those who did the work in their service was:
[2] of the sons of Asaph:
Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asharelah.
The sons of Asaph were under the leadership of Asaph,
who prophesied at the order of the king.
[3] Of Jeduthun, the sons of Jeduthun:
Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Shimei, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah, six,
under the leadership of their father Jeduthun,
who prophesied with the harp, giving thanks and praising the LORD.
[4] Of Heman, the sons of Heman:
Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth,
Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-Ezer,
Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth.
[5] All these were the sons of Heman,
the king’s seer in the words of God, to lift up the horn.
God gave Heman fourteen sons and three daughters.
[6] All these were under the leadership of their father for song in the LORD’s house,
with cymbals, stringed instruments, and harps, for the service of God’s house.
Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman were under the order of the king.
[7] Their number, with their brothers
who were trained in singing to the LORD,
all who were skillful,
was 288.

> **What does this mean?**
> Music and singing in the temple were called “prophesying”. That means: through the songs God spoke to the people, and the people spoke to God. Music can touch people deeply and lead them to God.
> Heman is called “the king’s seer”. He was a musician and a prophet at the same time.
> “To lift up the horn” is a picture for strength and honor. God gave Heman a large family.
> Heman also had three daughters. According to verse 6, probably all his children sang along, the daughters too.
> Interesting: the last names of Heman’s sons (from Hananiah on) almost form a little prayer in Hebrew when put together: “Be gracious to me, LORD, be gracious to me, you are my God. I have made great and lifted up the help …” Some scholars think that here the first lines of a psalm became names.
> 288 is 24 times 12. So there are 24 groups of 12 singers each, like the 24 divisions of priests.

## The 24 groups of singers (Verses 8–31)

[8] They cast lots for their duties,
all alike, the small as well as the great,
the teacher as well as the student.
[9] Now the first lot came out for Asaph to Joseph.
The second to Gedaliah; he and his brothers and sons were twelve.
[10] The third to Zaccur, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[11] The fourth to Izri, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[12] The fifth to Nethaniah, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[13] The sixth to Bukkiah, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[14] The seventh to Jesharelah, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[15] The eighth to Jeshaiah, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[16] The ninth to Mattaniah, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[17] The tenth to Shimei, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[18] The eleventh to Azarel, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[19] The twelfth to Hashabiah, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[20] The thirteenth to Shubael, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[21] The fourteenth to Mattithiah, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[22] The fifteenth to Jeremoth, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[23] The sixteenth to Hananiah, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[24] The seventeenth to Joshbekashah, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[25] The eighteenth to Hanani, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[26] The nineteenth to Mallothi, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[27] The twentieth to Eliathah, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[28] The twenty-first to Hothir, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[29] The twenty-second to Giddalti, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[30] The twenty-third to Mahazioth, his sons and his brothers, twelve.
[31] The twenty-fourth to Romamti-Ezer, his sons and his brothers, twelve.

> **What does this mean?**
> Again the lot decides, as with the priests. “The teacher as well as the student”: all are equal, whether experienced or new.
> Each group had twelve singers. So there were always enough musicians for worship.
> Some names are spelled a little differently than in verses 2–4: Izri is Zeri, Jesharelah is Asharelah, Azarel is Uzziel, Shubael is Shebuel, Jeremoth is Jerimoth.
> For the first group (Joseph) the word “twelve” is missing. It was probably lost when the text was copied.
