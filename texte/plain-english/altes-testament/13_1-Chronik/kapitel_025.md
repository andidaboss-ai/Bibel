# 1 Chronicles – Chapter 25
### The singers in the temple

---

## The families of the singers (Verses 1–7)

[1] David and the commanders of the army chose some of the sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun for a special service.
They were to prophesy with harps, lyres, and cymbals.
Here is the list of the men who did this work:
[2] From the sons of Asaph:
Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asharelah.
The sons of Asaph were led by Asaph.
He prophesied when the king ordered it.
[3] From Jeduthun, the sons of Jeduthun:
Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Shimei, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah.
These were six.
They were led by their father Jeduthun.
He prophesied with the harp.
He gave thanks and praised the LORD.
[4] From Heman, the sons of Heman:
Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth,
Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-Ezer,
Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth.
[5] All these were the sons of Heman.
Heman was the king’s seer.
He told the king the words of God.
He was to lift up the horn.
God gave Heman 14 sons and 3 daughters.
[6] All these were led by their father.
They made music in the LORD’s house
with cymbals, lyres, and harps.
That was their service in God’s house.
Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman took their orders from the king.
[7] They and their relatives were trained in singing to the LORD.
All of them were skilled.
There were 288 of them.

> **What does this mean?**
> Music and singing in the temple were called “prophesying.” That means: through the songs God spoke to the people, and the people spoke to God. Music can touch people deeply and lead them to God.
> Heman is called “the king’s seer.” He was a musician and a prophet at the same time.
> “To lift up the horn” is a picture for strength and honor. God gave Heman a large family.
> Heman also had three daughters. According to verse 6, probably all his children sang along, the daughters too.
> Interesting: the last names of Heman’s sons (from Hananiah on) almost form a little prayer in Hebrew when put together: “Be gracious to me, LORD, be gracious to me, you are my God. I have made great and lifted up the help …” Some scholars think that here the first lines of a psalm became names.
> 288 is 24 times 12. So there are 24 groups of 12 singers each, like the 24 divisions of priests.

## The 24 groups of singers (Verses 8–31)

[8] They drew lots for their duties.
Everyone was treated the same:
the young and the old,
the teacher as well as the student.
[9] The first lot for Asaph fell to Joseph.
The second lot fell to Gedaliah.
He, his relatives, and his sons were 12.
[10] The third lot fell to Zaccur.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[11] The fourth lot fell to Izri.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[12] The fifth lot fell to Nethaniah.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[13] The sixth lot fell to Bukkiah.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[14] The seventh lot fell to Jesharelah.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[15] The eighth lot fell to Jeshaiah.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[16] The ninth lot fell to Mattaniah.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[17] The tenth lot fell to Shimei.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[18] The eleventh lot fell to Azarel.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[19] The twelfth lot fell to Hashabiah.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[20] The thirteenth lot fell to Shubael.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[21] The fourteenth lot fell to Mattithiah.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[22] The fifteenth lot fell to Jeremoth.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[23] The sixteenth lot fell to Hananiah.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[24] The seventeenth lot fell to Joshbekashah.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[25] The eighteenth lot fell to Hanani.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[26] The nineteenth lot fell to Mallothi.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[27] The twentieth lot fell to Eliathah.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[28] The twenty-first lot fell to Hothir.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[29] The twenty-second lot fell to Giddalti.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[30] The twenty-third lot fell to Mahazioth.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.
[31] The twenty-fourth lot fell to Romamti-Ezer.
He, his sons, and his relatives were 12.

> **What does this mean?**
> Again the lot decides, as with the priests. “The teacher as well as the student”: all are equal, whether experienced or new.
> Each group had 12 singers. So there were always enough musicians for worship.
> Some names are spelled a little differently than in verses 2–4: Izri is Zeri, Jesharelah is Asharelah, Azarel is Uzziel, Shubael is Shebuel, Jeremoth is Jerimoth.
> For the first group (Joseph) the number 12 is missing. It was probably lost when the text was copied.
