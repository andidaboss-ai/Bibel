# 1 Chronicles – Chapter 22
### David prepares for building the temple

---

## The place for the temple (Verses 1–5)

[1] Then David said,
“This is the house of the LORD God,
and this is the altar of burnt offering for Israel.”
[2] David gave orders to gather together the foreigners who were in the land of Israel.
And he set stonecutters to cut squared stones to build God’s house.
[3] David prepared plenty of iron
for the nails for the doors of the gates and for the clamps,
and so much bronze that it could not be weighed,
[4] and cedar trees without number.
For the Sidonians and the people of Tyre brought plenty of cedar trees to David.
[5] David said,
“Solomon my son is young and tender.
And the house that is to be built for the LORD
must be extremely magnificent,
famous and glorious throughout all countries.
So I will make preparations for it.”
So David prepared a great deal before his death.

> **What does this mean?**
> After the story of the angel and the altar (Chapter 21), David knows: here, on Ornan’s threshing floor, the temple is to stand.
> David is not allowed to build the temple himself. But he prepares everything: stones, iron, bronze and wood. He does what he can so that his son has it easier. Sometimes it is our task to prepare for others what we cannot finish ourselves.
> “The foreigners”: people from other nations who lived in Israel. They had to work on the building (compare 2 Chronicles 2:17–18).

---

## David gives Solomon the task (Verses 6–16)

[6] Then he called for Solomon his son
and commanded him to build a house for the LORD, the God of Israel.
[7] David said to Solomon his son,
“As for me, it was in my heart
to build a house for the name of the LORD my God.
[8] But the LORD’s word came to me, saying,
‘You have shed a lot of blood and have fought great wars.
You shall not build a house for my name,
because you have shed much blood on the earth in my sight.
[9] Look, a son shall be born to you,
who shall be a man of peace.
I will give him rest from all his enemies all around.
For his name shall be Solomon,
and I will give peace and quietness to Israel in his days.
[10] He shall build a house for my name.
He will be my son, and I will be his father.
And I will make the throne of his kingdom over Israel firm forever.’
[11] Now, my son,
may the LORD be with you and give you success,
so that you build the house of the LORD your God,
as he has spoken about you.
[12] May the LORD give you good sense and understanding,
and put you in charge of Israel,
so that you may keep the law of the LORD your God.
[13] Then you will have success,
if you are careful to do the laws and the rules
which the LORD gave Moses for Israel.
Be strong and courageous.
Don’t be afraid and don’t lose heart.
[14] Now look, in my hardship I have prepared for the LORD’s house
one hundred thousand talents of gold,
one million talents of silver,
and so much bronze and iron that it cannot be weighed, for there is plenty of it.
I have also prepared timber and stone.
And you may add to them.
[15] There are also plenty of workmen with you:
cutters and workers of stone and timber,
and all kinds of men who are skillful in every kind of work.
[16] There is no number to the gold, the silver, the bronze, and the iron.
Get up and get to work,
and may the LORD be with you.”

> **What does this mean?**
> Here is the reason why David was not allowed to build the temple: he had shed much blood. God’s house is to be a house of peace. That fits Solomon, whose name means “the peaceful one” (the Hebrew word “shalom” means “peace”).
> David gives his son advice for life: keep God’s law. “Be strong and courageous. Don’t be afraid.” Moses had also spoken like this to Joshua (Joshua 1:6–9).
> “In my hardship”: David gathered the material in spite of many difficulties.
> The Bible says “one hundred thousand talents of gold” and “one million talents of silver”. A talent is about 75 pounds. Those would be unimaginably large amounts, much more than all the gold there was in the whole region at that time. The numbers probably want to say: it was beyond measure. Perhaps they were also changed when the text was copied.

## David calls on the princes (Verses 17–19)

[17] David also commanded all the princes of Israel to help Solomon his son:
[18] “Isn’t the LORD your God with you?
Hasn’t he given you rest on every side?
For he has handed over the people of the land to me,
and the land is subdued before the LORD and before his people.
[19] Now set your heart and your soul to follow the LORD your God.
Get up, then, and build the sanctuary of the LORD God,
so that the ark of the LORD’s covenant and the holy vessels of God may be brought
into the house that is to be built for the LORD’s name.”

> **What does this mean?**
> The temple is not only Solomon’s task. Everyone is to help.
> The most important thing is not the building, but the heart: “Set your heart and your soul to follow the LORD your God.” A house for God without a heart for God would be empty.
