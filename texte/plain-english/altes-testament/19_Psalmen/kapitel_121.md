# Psalm 121
### The one who protects Israel does not sleep

---

## Where does my help come from? (Verses 1–8)

[1] A song of ascents.
I lift up my eyes to the hills.
Where does my help come from?
[2] My help comes from the LORD,
who made heaven and earth.
[3] He will not let your foot slip.
The one who protects you will not fall asleep.
[4] Look, the one who protects Israel
will not doze and will not sleep.
[5] The LORD is the one who protects you.
The LORD is your shade at your right side.
[6] The sun will not harm you during the day,
and the moon will not harm you at night.
[7] The LORD will protect you from all evil.
He will protect your life.
[8] The LORD will protect you when you go out and when you come home,
from now on and forever.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 121 is one of the best-loved psalms. People often pray it when they say goodbye, go on a trip, or at baptisms and funerals.
> The pilgrim sees the hills in front of him. The way there is dangerous: steep paths, robbers, heat. He asks: Who will help me? The answer: not the hills, but the God who made the hills.
> The word “protect” appears six times. God watches over us, like a guard who never falls asleep.
> Verse 6: The sun could cause heatstroke. Back then, people believed that the moon could make them sick. God protects us by day and by night.
> Verse 8: “When you go out and when you come home”: This means the whole of life, every way, every day.
