# Psalm 124
### If the LORD had not been on our side

---

## The trap is broken (Verses 1–8)

[1] A song of ascents. By David.
If the LORD had not been on our side,
let Israel now say it,
[2] if the LORD had not been on our side
when people rose up against us,
[3] then they would have swallowed us alive
when their anger burned against us.
[4] Then the waters would have flooded over us.
The river would have gone over our soul.
[5] Then the proud waters
would have gone over our soul.
[6] Praise be to the LORD,
who has not given us to their teeth to tear apart.
[7] Our soul has escaped like a bird
out of the trap of the bird catcher.
The trap is broken, and we have escaped.
[8] Our help is in the name of the LORD,
who made heaven and earth.

> **What does this mean?**
> The people look back: Without God, we would have been lost. The enemies were like wild animals, like a flood.
> Verse 7: A beautiful picture: A bird is caught in a trap. Suddenly the trap breaks, and the bird flies away free. That is how God set his people free.
> Verse 8 is still spoken at the beginning of many church services today: “Our help is in the name of the LORD, who made heaven and earth.”
