# Psalm 87
### Zion, the mother of all nations

---

## Glorious things are spoken about you (Verses 1–3)

[1] A psalm by the sons of Korah. A song.
His foundation is on the holy mountains.
[2] The LORD loves the gates of Zion
more than all the homes of Jacob.
[3] Glorious things are spoken about you,
city of God.
Selah.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 87 is a short song about Jerusalem (Zion). God founded this city, and he loves it in a special way.
> Verse 3 is famous: “Glorious things are spoken about you, city of God.” A well-known English hymn (“Glorious Things of Thee Are Spoken”) is based on it. It is sung to a melody by Joseph Haydn.

---

## This one was born there (Verses 4–7)

[4] I will list Rahab and Babylon among those who know me.
Look, Philistia, Tyre, and also Ethiopia:
“This one was born there.”
[5] Yes, about Zion people will say,
“This one and that one were born in her.”
The Most High himself will make her strong.
[6] When the LORD writes down the peoples, he will count them:
“This one was born there.”
Selah.
[7] Those who sing and those who dance will say,
“All my springs are in you.”

> **What does this mean?**
> This is an amazing psalm! The enemies of Israel are listed: “Rahab” (a poetic name for Egypt), Babylon, Philistia, Tyre, and Ethiopia (in Hebrew, “Cush”: the land south of Egypt).
> And God says about them: “This one was born there,” in Zion. God writes people from all nations into his book, as if they were citizens of Jerusalem.
> In this way, Zion becomes the mother of all nations. That is a great vision: All people can belong to God, no matter where they come from (compare Isaiah 2:2–4; Galatians 4:26).
> Verse 7: Everyone sings and dances: “All my springs are in you.” God is the source of life for everyone.
