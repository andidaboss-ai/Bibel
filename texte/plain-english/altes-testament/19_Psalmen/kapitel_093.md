# Psalm 93
### The LORD is King

---

## The LORD is King (Verses 1–2)

[1] The LORD reigns!
That means: The LORD is King!
He is dressed in majesty!
The LORD is armed with strength.
The world also stands firm.
It cannot be moved.
[2] Your throne has stood firm from long ago.
You have always been there, from everlasting.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 93 is the first of the so-called “kingship psalms” (Psalm 93; 95–99). They call out: “The LORD reigns!” That means: The LORD is King!
> After the end of the kings in Israel (Psalm 89), hope turns to God himself as the true King.
> God is dressed like a king: with majesty and strength instead of purple and gold.

---

## Mightier than the roar of the sea (Verses 3–5)

[3] The floods have risen up, LORD.
The floods have raised their voice.
The floods lift up their waves.
[4] Mightier than the roar of many waters,
mightier than the powerful breaking waves of the sea,
is the LORD on high.
[5] Your laws stand firm.
Holiness makes your house beautiful, LORD, forever.

> **What does this mean?**
> For people back then, the sea with its roaring waves was a picture of chaos and danger. You can hear the waves roll in three times: “The floods …”
> But God is mightier than the raging sea. Even the most threatening powers in the world are smaller than he is.
> Verse 5: God’s laws can be trusted, and his house is holy.
