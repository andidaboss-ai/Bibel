# Psalm 43
### Send out your light and your truth

---

## God, take my side (Verses 1–2)

[1] God, prove that I am in the right.
Argue my case against an ungodly nation.
Oh, rescue me from lying and wicked people.
[2] For you are the God who gives me strength.
Why have you rejected me?
Why do I go around mourning,
because the enemy oppresses me?

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 43 continues Psalm 42. It has no heading of its own. In many old manuscripts, both psalms are one single psalm.
> The one who prays is being oppressed by an “ungodly nation,” maybe in a foreign land.
> Verse 2 repeats Psalm 42:9 almost exactly.

---

## Send out your light (Verses 3–4)

[3] Oh, send out your light and your truth.
Let them lead me.
Let them bring me to your holy hill,
to the places where you live.
[4] Then I will go to the altar of God,
to God, my overflowing joy.
I will praise you on the harp, God, my God.

> **What does this mean?**
> Now comes a new request: God should send his light and his truth, like two companions who take the one who prays by the hand and lead him home to the temple.
> Verse 4: “To God, my overflowing joy”: This is the goal of his longing. Not only the temple, but God himself is the greatest joy.

---

## Hope in God (Verse 5)

[5] Why are you in such despair, my soul?
Why are you so troubled inside me?
Hope in God!
For I will still praise him:
my Savior, my helper and my God.

> **What does this mean?**
> The refrain comes for the third time. Psalms 42 and 43 do not end with a solution to the trouble, but with hope.
> The sadness is still there. But the hope has grown stronger. At the beginning, there was only longing. Now there is firm trust: I will still praise him.
