# Psalm 147
### He heals those whose hearts are broken

---

## He counts the stars and heals hearts (Verses 1–11)

[1] Praise Yah, for it is good to sing praises to our God.
It is pleasant, and it is right to praise him.
[2] The LORD builds up Jerusalem.
He gathers together the people of Israel who were driven out.
[3] He heals those whose hearts are broken,
and he bandages their wounds.
[4] He counts the number of the stars.
He calls them all by their names.
[5] Our Lord is great and very powerful.
His understanding has no end.
[6] The LORD lifts up the humble.
He brings the wicked down to the ground.
[7] Sing to the LORD with thanks!
Sing praises on the harp to our God,
[8] who covers the sky with clouds,
who gets rain ready for the earth,
who makes grass grow on the mountains.
[9] He gives food to the farm animals,
and to the young ravens when they call.
[10] He does not take delight in the strength of a horse.
He takes no pleasure in the strong legs of a man.
[11] The LORD takes pleasure in those who have deep respect for him,
in those who hope in his loving kindness.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 2–3: After the exile, God builds Jerusalem up again and brings the scattered people home. And he heals the hearts that are broken, like a doctor who bandages wounds.
> Verses 3–4: A wonderful contrast: The God who knows the billions of stars and gives them names cares about one single broken heart.
> Verse 9: God even hears the young ravens that cry out for food. Jesus says: “Consider the ravens … God feeds them” (Luke 12:24).
> Verses 10–11: God is not impressed by war horses or strong muscles, but by people who trust him.

---

## He sends out his word (Verses 12–20)

[12] Praise the LORD, Jerusalem!
Praise your God, Zion!
[13] For he has made the bars of your gates strong.
He has blessed your children inside you.
[14] He brings peace inside your borders.
He fills you with the finest wheat.
[15] He sends out his command to the earth.
His word runs very fast.
[16] He gives snow like wool.
He scatters frost like ashes.
[17] He throws down his hail like little stones.
Who can stand up against his cold?
[18] He sends out his word and melts them.
He makes his wind blow, and the waters flow.
[19] He shows his word to Jacob,
his rules and his rulings to Israel.
[20] He has not done this for any other nation.
They do not know his rulings.
Praise Yah!

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 15–18: God’s word is like a fast messenger. It brings winter with snow, frost and hail. And it also brings the thaw, when the ice melts and the streams flow again.
> Verses 19–20: God has given Israel something special: his word, his teaching. That is a privilege and a responsibility. Christians believe that God’s word later came to all nations through Israel.
