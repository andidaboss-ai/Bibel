# Psalm 65
### You crown the year with your blessing

---

## God hears prayer and forgives (Verses 1–4)

[1] For the music director. A psalm by David. A song.
Praise waits for you, God, in Zion.
People will keep the vows they made to you.
[2] You who hear prayer,
all people will come to you.
[3] My sins were too much for me,
but you made atonement for our wrongdoings.
That means: You took away our guilt.
[4] Happy is the one whom you choose and let come near,
so that he may live in your courts.
We will be filled with the good things of your house,
your holy temple.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 65 is a song of thanks, perhaps for the harvest festival.
> “Praise waits for you”: In the temple, praise is already waiting, ready for God. Others translate: “Silence and praise are fitting for you.”
> “You who hear prayer”: That is a beautiful name for God.
> Verse 3: God forgives sins that are too much for a person. That is the foundation for everything else.

---

## The hope of all the ends of the earth (Verses 5–8)

[5] With awesome deeds of righteousness you answer us,
God who saves us.
You are the hope of all the ends of the earth
and of those who are far away on the sea.
[6] By your power you form the mountains.
You have put on strength like a belt.
[7] You calm the roaring of the seas,
the roaring of their waves,
and the uproar of the nations.
[8] Even those who live in faraway places are afraid of your wonders.
You make the dawn of the morning and the evening sing with joy.

> **What does this mean?**
> God is not only the God of Israel. He is “the hope of all the ends of the earth.”
> He made the mountains, and he calms the raging sea. He also brings the raging nations to rest. Later, Jesus calmed the storm on the lake (Mark 4:39).
> Verse 8: From the sunrise in the east to the sunset in the west, the whole world shouts for joy.

---

## You crown the year (Verses 9–13)

[9] You take care of the earth and water it.
You make it very rich.
The river of God is full of water.
You give people grain, for that is what you have decided.
[10] You soak its furrows.
You level its ridges.
You soften it with rain showers.
You bless its crops.
[11] You crown the year with your good gifts.
Your carts overflow with plenty.
[12] The grasslands of the wilderness overflow.
The hills are dressed in gladness.
[13] The pastures are covered with flocks.
The valleys are dressed in grain.
They shout for joy! They also sing.

> **What does this mean?**
> A wonderful picture of spring and the harvest. God is like a farmer who takes care of his fields: He waters the ground, levels it, softens it, and blesses the seed.
> Verse 11: “You crown the year with your good gifts”: The harvest is like a crown for the whole year.
> “Your carts overflow with plenty”: Wherever God drives along with his cart, everything grows. In Hebrew, it literally says “your tracks” or “your cart tracks drip with fat.”
> At the end, all of nature sings: The hills, pastures, and valleys shout for joy. Creation praises its Creator.
