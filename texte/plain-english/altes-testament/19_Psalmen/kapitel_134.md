# Psalm 134
### Praise the LORD in the night

---

## The blessing at the end (Verses 1–3)

[1] A song of ascents.
Look! Praise the LORD, all you servants of the LORD,
who stand in the LORD’s house at night!
[2] Lift up your hands toward the holy place.
Praise the LORD!
[3] May the LORD bless you from Zion,
he who made heaven and earth!

> **What does this mean?**
> This is the last of the 15 songs of ascents.
> Verses 1–2: The pilgrims call out to the priests and Levites who serve in the temple at night: Praise God in the night, too!
> Verse 3: The priests answer with a blessing for the pilgrims, before they go back home.
> This is how the journey ends: People do not go home with empty hands, but with God’s blessing.
