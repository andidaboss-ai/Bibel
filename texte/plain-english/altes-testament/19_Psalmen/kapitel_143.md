# Psalm 143
### Teach me to do your will

---

## No one is righteous before you (Verses 1–6)

[1] A psalm by David.
Hear my prayer, LORD!
Listen to my requests!
Help me, because you are faithful and righteous!
[2] Do not take your servant to court,
because in your sight no person alive is righteous.
[3] For the enemy is chasing after my soul.
He has crushed my life down to the ground.
He has made me live in dark places,
like people who have been dead for a long time.
[4] That is why my spirit grows weak inside me.
My heart inside me is frozen with horror.
[5] I remember the days of long ago.
I think deeply about all that you have done.
I think about the work of your hands.
[6] I spread out my hands to you.
My soul is thirsty for you, like a dry land.
Selah.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 143 is the last of the seven psalms of repentance.
> Verse 2: An important sentence: “In your sight no person alive is righteous.” No one can say before God: I am completely righteous. That is why the person praying does not ask to be judged by what he has done, but asks for grace. Paul takes up this thought (Romans 3:20; Galatians 2:16).
> Verses 3–4: The person praying feels as if he is buried alive. Many people with severe depression know this feeling.
> Verses 5–6: What helps? Remembering what God has done before. And reaching out for him, like dry ground reaches out for rain.

---

## Let me hear of your loving kindness in the morning (Verses 7–12)

[7] Answer me quickly, LORD! My spirit is failing.
Do not hide your face from me,
or I will become like those who go down into the pit.
[8] Let me hear of your loving kindness in the morning,
because I trust in you.
Show me the way I should walk,
because I lift up my soul to you.
[9] Rescue me from my enemies, LORD!
I run to you so that you can hide me.
[10] Teach me to do your will,
because you are my God.
Your Spirit is good.
Lead me in a land where things are right.
[11] Give me new life, LORD, for the sake of your name!
Because you are righteous, bring my soul out of trouble!
[12] Because of your loving kindness, cut off my enemies.
Destroy all those who trouble my soul,
because I am your servant.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 8: After a dark night, the person praying hopes for the morning. He wants to hear about God’s loving kindness like good news in the morning.
> Verse 10: “Teach me to do your will”: one of the most beautiful short prayers in the Bible. “Lead me in a land where things are right”: God leads us on a straight, honest way.
> Verse 12: The person praying asks God to set him free from his enemies. He does not use violence himself, but leaves it to God. For us today, the way of Jesus is: love our enemies and pray for them (Matthew 5:44).
