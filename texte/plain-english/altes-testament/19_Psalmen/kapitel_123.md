# Psalm 123
### Our eyes look to the LORD

---

## Like the eyes of servants (Verses 1–4)

[1] A song of ascents.
I look up to you,
you who sit on your throne in heaven.
[2] Servants watch the hand of their master.
A maid watches the hand of the woman she works for.
In the same way, our eyes look to the LORD our God,
until he is merciful to us.
[3] Be merciful to us, LORD, be merciful to us,
because we have put up with a lot of scorn.
[4] Our soul has had more than enough
of the mocking of those who live without worries,
of the scorn of the proud.

> **What does this mean?**
> A short prayer of people who are looked down on.
> Verse 2: Back then, servants watched the hand of their master closely. A small sign was enough. In the same way, the people look to God: paying close attention and full of hope.
> Verse 4: “Those who live without worries”: people who are doing well and who look down on others. Whoever is mocked may tell God about it.
