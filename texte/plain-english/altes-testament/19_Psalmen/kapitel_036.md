# Psalm 36
### With you is the spring of life

---

## The wicked person (Verses 1–4)

[1] For the Chief Musician. By David, the servant of the LORD.
God has shown my heart something
about the wrongdoing of the wicked:
He does not fear God at all.
[2] For he flatters himself in his own eyes.
He thinks so highly of himself
that he cannot find his sin and hate it.
[3] The words of his mouth are evil and full of lies.
He has stopped being wise and doing good.
[4] He makes evil plans on his bed.
He sets out on a way that is not good.
He does not hate evil.

> **What does this mean?**
> The psalm describes how a wicked person thinks: He has no respect for God. He thinks he is so great that he does not see his own faults.
> Paul quotes verse 1 in Romans 3:18.
> Verse 4: Even at night in bed, he thinks up evil things. He no longer holds back from evil.
> Verse 1 is hard to translate from the Hebrew. Word for word, it says something like: A saying of wrongdoing to the wicked in my heart.

---

## Your loving kindness reaches to the heavens (Verses 5–9)

[5] LORD, your loving kindness reaches to the heavens.
Your faithfulness reaches to the skies.
[6] Your righteousness is like the mountains of God.
Your judgments are like the great deep sea.
LORD, you protect both people and animals.
[7] How precious is your loving kindness, God!
People come for safety
under the shadow of your wings.
[8] They will eat their fill of the rich food in your house.
You will let them drink from the river of your delights.
[9] For with you is the spring of life.
In your light we will see light.

> **What does this mean?**
> Now comes a strong contrast: After the dark picture of the wicked person comes shining praise of God’s goodness.
> God’s goodness is as great as the heavens. His righteousness is as firm as the highest mountains. His judgments are as deep as the sea.
> “You protect both people and animals”: God cares for all of creation.
> Verse 9 is one of the most beautiful verses in the Bible: “For with you is the spring of life. In your light we will see light.” All life comes from God. And only in his light can we really see and understand.
> Jesus says, “I am the light of the world” (John 8:12), and he speaks of “living water” (John 4:10–14).

---

## Keep me safe (Verses 10–12)

[10] Oh, keep showing your loving kindness to those who know you,
and your righteousness to those whose hearts are honest.
[11] Do not let the foot of proud people step on me.
Do not let the hand of the wicked drive me away.
[12] There the evildoers have fallen.
They are pushed down and will not be able to get up.

> **What does this mean?**
> The one who prays asks: Let me stay in your goodness. Keep me safe from proud and evil people.
> Verse 12: The one who prays already sees the end of the evildoers: They fall and do not get up again.
