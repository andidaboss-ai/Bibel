# Psalm 12
### God’s words are pure

---

## Everyone lies (Verses 1–4)

[1] For the music director. On an eight-stringed lyre. A psalm by David.
Help, LORD, for there are no more people who are faithful to you.
Those who can be trusted are disappearing from among people.
[2] Everyone lies to his neighbor.
They speak with flattering lips
and with a double heart.
[3] May the LORD cut off all flattering lips
and the tongue that boasts.
[4] They have said, “With our tongue we will win.
Our lips belong to us. Who is lord over us?”

> **What does this mean?**
> The person praying feels alone. There seem to be no more faithful, honest people.
> “With a double heart”: They say one thing and mean something else.
> Verse 4: The liars are proud of the power of their words. They think: We can say whatever we want. No one can touch us.
> Today, too, we see how much harm lies, rumors and hate speech can do, especially on the internet.

---

## God gets up (Verses 5–8)

[5] “Because the weak are oppressed,
and because the needy groan,
I will now get up,” says the LORD.
“I will bring him to safety, away from those who tell lies about him.”
[6] The LORD’s words are pure words,
like silver refined in a clay furnace,
purified seven times.
[7] You will keep them, LORD.
You will protect them from this generation forever.
[8] The wicked walk around freely on every side,
when what is shameful is praised among people.

> **What does this mean?**
> In verse 5, God himself speaks. He has heard the groaning of the poor, and he gets up to help them.
> Verse 6: People’s words are full of lies. But God’s words are pure like silver that has been melted and purified seven times. Seven is the number of perfection. You can fully rely on God’s word.
> Verse 8: An honest ending: The wicked are still everywhere. When what is shameful is celebrated, they have an easy time. But God’s promise in verse 5 still stands.
