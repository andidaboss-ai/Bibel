# Psalm 148
### All creation praises God

---

## Praise him from the heavens (Verses 1–6)

[1] Praise Yah!
Praise the LORD from the heavens!
Praise him in the heights!
[2] Praise him, all his angels!
Praise him, all his army!
[3] Praise him, sun and moon!
Praise him, all you shining stars!
[4] Praise him, you highest heavens,
and you waters above the heavens!
[5] Let them praise the name of the LORD,
for he gave the command, and they were created.
[6] He has also set them in their place forever and ever.
He has made a law that will not pass away.

> **What does this mean?**
> A huge choir: Everything that is in heaven is to praise God: the angels, the sun, the moon, the stars.
> Verse 4: “Waters above the heavens”: That is how people pictured the world back then (compare Genesis 1:7). The rain came from up there.
> Verses 5–6: Everything exists because God called it. And he keeps it in its order.

---

## Praise him from the earth (Verses 7–14)

[7] Praise the LORD from the earth,
you great sea creatures and all the deep places,
[8] lightning and hail, snow and clouds,
stormy wind that does what he says,
[9] mountains and all hills,
fruit trees and all cedars,
[10] wild animals and all farm animals,
small creatures and flying birds,
[11] kings of the earth and all peoples,
princes and all judges of the earth,
[12] young men and young women,
old men and children!
[13] Let them praise the name of the LORD,
for his name alone is lifted up high.
His glory is above the earth and the heavens.
[14] He has lifted up the horn of his people,
a reason for praise for all his holy people,
for the people of Israel, a people who are close to him.
Praise Yah!

> **What does this mean?**
> Now it is the earth’s turn: sea creatures, weather, mountains, trees, animals, and last of all people, all people: kings and ordinary people, young and old, men and women, children.
> All of creation forms one choir. Each creature praises God in its own way, simply by being there.
> Francis of Assisi was inspired by this psalm when he wrote his “Canticle of the Sun.”
> This psalm also reminds us: Creation belongs to God. We humans are to treat it well.
> Verse 14: “He has lifted up the horn of his people”: God has given his people new strength and dignity.
