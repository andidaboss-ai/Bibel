# Psalm 138
### You have given me strength

---

## I thank you with my whole heart (Verses 1–8)

[1] By David.
I will thank you with my whole heart.
In front of the gods I will sing praises to you.
[2] I will bow down toward your holy temple.
I will thank your name for your loving kindness and for your faithfulness.
For you have lifted up your name and your word above everything.
[3] On the day I called, you answered me.
You gave me courage and put strength in my soul.
[4] All the kings of the earth will thank you, LORD,
because they have heard the words you have spoken.
[5] Yes, they will sing about the ways of the LORD,
because the glory of the LORD is great!
[6] For even though the LORD is high above,
he cares for those who are low.
But he knows the proud from far away.
[7] Even when I walk through the middle of trouble, you will give me new life.
You will stretch out your hand against the anger of my enemies.
Your right hand will save me.
[8] The LORD will finish what he has started for me.
Your loving kindness, LORD, lasts forever.
Do not leave the works of your own hands!

> **What does this mean?**
> A psalm of thanks. God has answered a prayer.
> Verse 1: “In front of the gods”: Other peoples worshiped many gods. Right in the middle of them, David bravely sings for the one God.
> Verse 6: God is at the very top, but he looks all the way down, at the small and the weak. But he stays far away from the proud.
> Verse 8: “The LORD will finish what he has started for me”: What God has begun, he also brings to an end. I am a work of his hands. He does not leave me half-finished.
