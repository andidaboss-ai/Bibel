# Psalm 125
### Like the mountains around Jerusalem

---

## Whoever trusts the LORD stands firm (Verses 1–5)

[1] A song of ascents.
Those who trust in the LORD are like Mount Zion.
It cannot be moved, but it stays forever.
[2] As the mountains surround Jerusalem,
so the LORD surrounds his people,
from now on and forever.
[3] For the rule of the wicked will not stay
over the land given to the righteous.
Then the righteous will not use their hands to do evil.
[4] LORD, do good to those who are good,
to those whose hearts are honest!
[5] But those who turn away to their crooked ways,
the LORD will lead them away together with those who do evil.
May there be peace on Israel!

> **What does this mean?**
> Jerusalem is surrounded by mountains on all sides. The pilgrims see this with their own eyes. In the same way, the psalm says, God surrounds his people: on all sides.
> Verse 3: The rule of evil people will not last forever. Otherwise, good people would also be tempted to do evil.
> The psalm ends with a blessing: “May there be peace on Israel!”
