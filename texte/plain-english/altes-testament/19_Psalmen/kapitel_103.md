# Psalm 103
### Praise the LORD, my soul

---

## Do not forget the good he has done for you (Verses 1–5)

[1] By David.
Praise the LORD, my soul!
Everything inside me, praise his holy name!
[2] Praise the LORD, my soul,
and do not forget all the good he has done for you.
[3] He forgives all your sins.
He heals all your diseases.
[4] He saves your life from the grave.
He crowns you with loving kindness and tender mercy.
[5] He fills your life with good things,
so that you become young again, like the eagle.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 103 is one of the most beautiful psalms of praise in the Bible. The person praying speaks to his own soul and tells it to praise God.
> “Do not forget”: It is easy to forget how much good we have received. The psalm reminds us.
> Five good gifts: forgiveness, healing, rescue, a crown of love, and being filled with good things.
> Verse 5: The eagle sheds its old feathers and gets new ones. Then it looks young and strong again. In the same way, God renews our strength (compare Isaiah 40:31).
> The well-known hymn “Praise to the Lord, the Almighty, the King of creation” was inspired by this psalm.

---

## The LORD is merciful and gracious (Verses 6–12)

[6] The LORD does what is right.
He brings justice for all who are oppressed.
[7] He showed Moses his ways.
He showed the people of Israel what he does.
[8] The LORD is merciful and gracious.
He is slow to become angry, and he is full of loving kindness.
[9] He will not always accuse us.
He will not stay angry forever.
[10] He has not treated us as our sins deserve.
He has not paid us back for our wrongs.
[11] For as high as the heavens are above the earth,
so great is his loving kindness toward those who fear him.
[12] As far as the east is from the west,
so far has he removed our sins from us.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 8 again quotes how God describes himself in Exodus 34:6.
> Verse 10: God does not give us what we deserve. He is more gracious than we deserve.
> Verses 11–12: Two wonderful pictures for God’s love and forgiveness:
> – His loving kindness is as high as the heavens.
> – As far as the east is from the west, that is how far God throws away our sins. East and west never meet. Sin that is forgiven never comes back.

---

## As a father has compassion on his children (Verses 13–18)

[13] As a father has compassion on his children,
so the LORD has compassion on those who fear him.
[14] For he knows how we are made.
He remembers that we are dust.
[15] As for people, their days are like grass.
They bloom like a flower in the field.
[16] Then the wind blows over it, and it is gone.
Its place does not remember it anymore.
[17] But the LORD’s loving kindness lasts from everlasting to everlasting
for those who fear him.
His righteousness reaches to their grandchildren,
[18] to those who keep his covenant,
and to those who remember to obey his commands.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 13: God is like a loving father. He feels for his children.
> Verse 14: God knows how weak we are. He knows that we are made of dust. That is why he does not ask the impossible of us.
> Verses 15–16: Human life is short, like the life of a flower.
> Verse 17: But God’s love lasts forever. It reaches to the grandchildren and great-grandchildren.

---

## Praise the LORD, you angels! (Verses 19–22)

[19] The LORD has set up his throne in heaven.
His kingdom rules over everything.
[20] Praise the LORD, you angels of his,
you mighty and strong ones who carry out his word
and obey the voice of his word!
[21] Praise the LORD, all his armies,
you servants of his who do what he wants!
[22] Praise the LORD, all his works,
in all the places where he rules!
Praise the LORD, my soul!

> **What does this mean?**
> At the end, the praise grows wider and wider: from one’s own soul to the angels, to all the armies of heaven, to all creatures everywhere.
> And then the psalm returns to the beginning: “Praise the LORD, my soul!” The great praise of the whole universe begins and ends in one’s own heart.
