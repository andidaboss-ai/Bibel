# Psalm 56
### When I am afraid, I trust in you

---

## When I am afraid (Verses 1–4)

[1] For the Chief Musician. To the tune of “Silent Dove in Distant Lands.”
A poem by David, when the Philistines caught him in Gath.
Be merciful to me, God, for people want to swallow me up.
All day long they attack me and oppress me.
[2] My enemies want to swallow me up all day long,
for there are many who proudly fight against me.
[3] When I am afraid,
I will put my trust in you.
[4] In God, I praise his word.
In God, I put my trust. I will not be afraid.
What can mere human beings do to me?

> **What does this mean?**
> The superscription reminds us of David’s flight to the Philistines in Gath (1 Samuel 21:10–15; compare Psalm 34).
> “Silent Dove in Distant Lands”: the name of a tune. It fits David: far away from home, without protection, like a dove.
> Verse 3 is very simple and honest: “When I am afraid, I will put my trust in you.” Faith does not mean never being afraid. Faith means going to God with your fear.
> Verse 4: “What can mere human beings do to me?” People can do a lot of harm, but they do not have the last word.

---

## You collect my tears (Verses 5–9)

[5] All day long they twist my words.
All their thoughts are against me, to do me harm.
[6] They plot together and lie in wait.
They watch my steps.
They are eager to take my life.
[7] Will they get away with their evil?
God, in your anger, bring down the peoples.
[8] You count the places where I have wandered.
You put my tears into your bottle.
Aren’t they written in your book?
[9] Then my enemies will turn back
on the day that I call.
I know this: God is for me.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 8 is one of the most tender pictures in the Bible: God collects every tear in a bottle. He writes them in his book. No tear is forgotten. God knows exactly how often and how much we have suffered.
> Verse 9: “God is for me.” Later Paul says, “If God is for us, who can be against us?” (Romans 8:31).

---

## What can people do to me? (Verses 10–13)

[10] In God, I will praise his word.
In the LORD, I will praise his word.
[11] I have put my trust in God. I will not be afraid.
What can people do to me?
[12] God, I am bound by the promises I made to you.
I will bring you offerings of thanks.
[13] For you have rescued my soul from death.
You have kept my feet from falling,
so that I may walk before God
in the light of the living.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 10–11 repeat the refrain of verse 4.
> Verse 12: David had made a promise to God when he was in trouble. Now he wants to keep his promise and thank God.
> “In the light of the living”: God has kept David from death, so that he can live and serve God.
