# Psalm 96
### Sing a new song to the LORD

---

## All the earth, sing to the LORD! (Verses 1–6)

[1] Sing a new song to the LORD!
Sing to the LORD, all the earth!
[2] Sing to the LORD! Praise his name!
Tell about how he saves, day after day!
[3] Tell the nations about his glory.
Tell all the peoples about his wonderful works!
[4] For the LORD is great, and he deserves great praise!
He is to be feared more than all gods.
[5] For all the gods of the peoples are idols,
but the LORD made the heavens.
[6] Honor and majesty are before him.
Strength and beauty are in his holy place.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 96 is a mission song: All nations should learn about God and praise him.
> Verse 5: The gods of the other nations are “idols”. In Hebrew, the word means “nothings”. They cannot do anything. But the LORD made the heavens.
> This psalm also appears almost word for word in 1 Chronicles 16:23–33. There it is sung when the ark of the covenant is brought to Jerusalem.

---

## Give honor to the LORD (Verses 7–10)

[7] You families of the nations, give to the LORD,
give to the LORD glory and strength!
[8] Give to the LORD the glory his name deserves!
Bring an offering, and come into his courtyards!
[9] Worship the LORD in holy clothing!
Tremble before him, all the earth!
[10] Say among the nations: “The LORD reigns!”
That means: The LORD is King!
The world also stands firm. It cannot be moved.
He will judge the peoples fairly.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 7–9 remind us of Psalm 29:1–2. But there it was the heavenly beings. Here it is the families of the nations, that is, all people.
> Verse 10: The good news for the world: “The LORD reigns!” That means: The LORD is King! He will judge fairly.

---

## All creation shouts for joy (Verses 11–13)

[11] Let the heavens be glad, and let the earth rejoice!
Let the sea roar, and everything that fills it!
[12] Let the field and everything in it celebrate!
Then all the trees of the forest will sing for joy
[13] before the LORD, for he comes,
for he comes to judge the earth.
He will judge the world with righteousness,
and the peoples with his truth.

> **What does this mean?**
> Not only people, but all creation shouts for joy: heaven, earth, sea, fields and trees.
> Why? Because God comes to judge the world fairly. For people who are oppressed, this is good news: At last, justice is coming!
> “He comes”: Christians read this psalm in Advent and at Christmas.
