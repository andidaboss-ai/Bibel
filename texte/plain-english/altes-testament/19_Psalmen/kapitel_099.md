# Psalm 99
### He is holy!

---

## The LORD is King in Zion (Verses 1–5)

[1] The LORD reigns! Let the peoples tremble.
He sits on his throne above the cherubim.
Cherubim are heavenly beings.
Let the earth shake.
[2] The LORD is great in Zion.
He is high above all the peoples.
[3] Let them praise your great and awesome name.
He is holy!
[4] The King is strong, and he loves justice.
You have set up what is fair.
You do what is just and right in Jacob.
[5] Lift up the LORD our God with praise!
Worship at his footstool!
He is holy!

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 99 is the last of the kingship psalms (93–99). Three times we hear the call: “He is holy!” (verses 3, 5 and 9). This reminds us of the angels in Isaiah 6:3: “Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of Armies!”
> “Holy” means: completely different, pure, high above everything.
> “His footstool”: This means the ark of the covenant in the temple. People imagined that God sat invisibly on his throne above the cherubim and rested his feet on the ark.
> Verse 4: God’s strength is not random or cruel. It loves justice.

---

## Moses, Aaron and Samuel (Verses 6–9)

[6] Moses and Aaron were among his priests.
Samuel was among those who called on his name.
They called to the LORD, and he answered them.
[7] He spoke to them from the pillar of cloud.
They kept his rules and the law that he gave them.
[8] You answered them, LORD our God.
You were a God who forgave them,
even though you punished them for what they did.
[9] Lift up the LORD our God with praise!
Worship at his holy hill,
for the LORD our God is holy!

> **What does this mean?**
> Three great people of prayer are named: Moses, Aaron and Samuel. They prayed for the people, and God answered (compare Exodus 32:11–14; 1 Samuel 7:9).
> Verse 8: God is holy. That is why he takes sin seriously and punishes it. But he also forgives. Both belong together: justice and grace.
