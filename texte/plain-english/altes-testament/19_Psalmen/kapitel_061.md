# Psalm 61
### Lead me to the rock

---

## I call from the end of the earth (Verses 1–4)

[1] For the music director. For a stringed instrument. By David.
Hear my cry, God!
Listen to my prayer!
[2] From the end of the earth I call to you
when my heart is overwhelmed.
Lead me to the rock that is higher than I am.
[3] For you have been a safe place for me,
a strong tower against the enemy.
[4] I will live in your tent forever.
I will find safety in the shelter of your wings.
Selah.

> **What does this mean?**
> The one praying is far away, “at the end of the earth.” He feels far from God and worn out.
> “Lead me to the rock that is higher than I am”: He cannot climb up to the safe place by himself. God must lead him.
> “Live in your tent”: to be close to God, like a guest who finds protection with God.

---

## For the king (Verses 5–8)

[5] For you, God, have heard my vows.
You have given me the inheritance of those who fear your name.
[6] You will make the king’s life long.
His years will last for generations.
[7] He will sit on his throne in God’s presence forever.
Appoint your loving kindness and truth to guard him.
[8] So I will sing praise to your name forever,
so that I keep my vows every day.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 6–7: A prayer for the king. God should give him a long life.
> “For generations” and “forever”: This goes beyond a single human life. Jews and Christians have understood this as being about the Messiah, the eternal king from David’s family.
> “Loving kindness and truth” are like two bodyguards who protect the king.
