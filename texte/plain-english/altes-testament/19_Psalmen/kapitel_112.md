# Psalm 112
### Happy is the one who fears the LORD

---

## A light in the darkness (Verses 1–4)

[1] Praise Yah!
Yah is a short form of the name of God, the LORD.
Happy is the person who fears the LORD,
who finds great joy in his commands.
[2] His descendants will be mighty in the land.
The generation of upright people will be blessed.
[3] Wealth and riches are in his house.
His righteousness lasts forever.
[4] For upright people, light rises in the darkness.
They are gracious, merciful and righteous.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 112 is the twin of Psalm 111. What is said there about God is said here about the person who fears God. For example: “His righteousness lasts forever” (111:3 and 112:3), and “gracious and merciful” (111:4 and 112:4).
> That means: Whoever honors God becomes like him.
> Verse 4: Righteous people also go through dark times. But in the darkness, a light rises for them.

---

## He gives to the poor (Verses 5–9)

[5] It goes well for the person who is generous and lends to others.
He will defend his case in court.
[6] For he will never be shaken.
The righteous person will be remembered forever.
[7] He is not afraid of bad news.
His heart is steady, and he trusts in the LORD.
[8] His heart stands firm.
In the end, he will not be afraid when he looks at his enemies.
[9] He has spread his gifts around. He has given to the poor.
His righteousness lasts forever.
His horn will be lifted up with honor.
That means: He will be strong and honored.

> **What does this mean?**
> The righteous person is generous. He lends to others. He gives to the poor.
> Verse 7: “He is not afraid of bad news”: Whoever trusts in God has something firm to hold on to, even when terrible news comes.
> Paul quotes verse 9 when he encourages the church in Corinth to give money for the poor (2 Corinthians 9:9).

---

## The wicked person sees it (Verse 10)

[10] The wicked person will see it and be angry.
He will grind his teeth and melt away.
What the wicked want will come to nothing.

> **What does this mean?**
> The psalm ends with the opposite picture: The wicked person is jealous and angry. His wishes do not come true.
