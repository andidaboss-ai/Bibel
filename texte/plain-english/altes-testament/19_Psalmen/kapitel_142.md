# Psalm 142
### A prayer in the cave

---

## No one cares about me (Verses 1–7)

[1] A thoughtful poem by David, when he was in the cave. A prayer.
I cry out loud to the LORD.
With my voice I ask the LORD to be merciful.
[2] I pour out my complaint before him.
I tell him about my troubles.
[3] When my spirit grew weak inside me,
you knew my way.
On the path where I walk,
they have hidden a trap for me.
[4] Look to my right and see:
There is no one who cares about me.
I have no place left where I am safe.
No one cares about my soul.
[5] I cried out to you, LORD.
I said, “You are my safe place,
my share in the land of the living.”
[6] Listen to my cry, for I am in great need!
Rescue me from those who are chasing me,
because they are too strong for me.
[7] Bring my soul out of prison,
so that I can thank your name!
The righteous will gather around me,
because you will be good to me.

> **What does this mean?**
> David hid from King Saul in a cave (1 Samuel 22:1; 24:1–4). He was a refugee, hunted and alone.
> Verse 4: “Look to my right”: In court, the person who defended someone stood on the right. But no one is there. No one helps.
> Verse 5: But one remains: God is my safe place.
> Verse 7: The cave feels like a prison. But in the end, David will not be alone anymore. The righteous will be all around him.
