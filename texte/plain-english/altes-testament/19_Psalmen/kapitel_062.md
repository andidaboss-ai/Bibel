# Psalm 62
### My soul is quiet before God

---

## God alone (Verses 1–4)

[1] For the music director. For Jeduthun. A psalm by David.
My soul finds rest in God alone.
My rescue comes from him.
[2] He alone is my rock, my rescue and my fortress.
I will never be badly shaken.
[3] How long will you attack a man?
Do all of you want to throw him down
like a leaning wall, like a shaky fence?
[4] They fully plan to push him down from his high place.
They love lies.
With their mouths they bless,
but inside they curse.
Selah.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 62 is a psalm of deep trust. In Hebrew, the word “alone” (or “only”) appears six times. Only with God does the one praying find rest.
> “My soul finds rest”: Literally, it says “is silence.” The restlessness inside stops when a person relies on God completely.
> Verse 3: The enemies want to knock him over like a wall that is already leaning. But he stands firm, because God is his rock.
> Verse 4: Hypocrites: friendly words, evil thoughts.

---

## Pour out your heart before him (Verses 5–8)

[5] My soul, wait quietly for God alone,
for my hope comes from him.
[6] He alone is my rock and my rescue, my fortress.
I will not be shaken.
[7] My rescue and my honor are with God.
The rock of my strength and my refuge are in God.
[8] Trust in him at all times, you people!
Pour out your heart before him!
God is a refuge for us.
Selah.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 5–6 repeat verses 1–2, but with a small difference: In verse 2 it said “never be badly shaken,” now it says “not be shaken.” The trust has grown.
> Verse 8 is a beautiful invitation to everyone: “Pour out your heart before him!” You may tell God everything that moves you, just as you empty a jug completely.

---

## Power belongs to God (Verses 9–12)

[9] Surely ordinary people are only a breath,
and important people are a lie.
On the scales they go up.
All together they are lighter than a breath.
[10] Don’t trust in oppressing others!
Don’t put empty hope in robbery!
If your riches grow, don’t set your heart on them!
[11] God has spoken once.
Twice I have heard this:
that power belongs to God.
[12] Lord, loving kindness also belongs to you,
for you pay back each person according to what he has done.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 9: All people, poor or rich, are only a “breath” before God. On a scale, they would be lighter than air.
> Verse 10: A warning: Do not trust in violence and riches. “If your riches grow, don’t set your heart on them!” That is still true today.
> Verses 11–12: Two things belong to God: power and loving kindness. God is strong, but also loving. Both together make him worthy of trust.
> Paul quotes verse 12 in Romans 2:6.
