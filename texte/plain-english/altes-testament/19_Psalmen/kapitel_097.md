# Psalm 97
### The LORD is King, let the earth rejoice

---

## Clouds and darkness around him (Verses 1–6)

[1] The LORD reigns! Let the earth rejoice!
Let the many islands be glad!
[2] Clouds and darkness are all around him.
Righteousness and justice are the foundation of his throne.
[3] A fire goes ahead of him.
It burns up his enemies all around.
[4] His lightning lights up the world.
The earth sees it and trembles.
[5] The mountains melt like wax before the LORD,
before the Lord of the whole earth.
[6] The heavens tell about his righteousness.
All the peoples have seen his glory.

> **What does this mean?**
> God appears like he did at Mount Sinai: with clouds, darkness, fire and lightning (Exodus 19:16–18).
> “The islands”: These are the faraway lands by the sea. It means the whole world.
> Verse 2: God is full of mystery (“clouds and darkness”). But one thing is certain: His rule is built on righteousness.

---

## Let those who serve idols be put to shame (Verses 7–9)

[7] Let all who serve carved images be put to shame,
all who brag about their idols.
Worship him, all you gods!
[8] Zion heard it and was glad.
The daughters of Judah rejoiced because of your judgments, LORD.
[9] For you, LORD, are the Most High over all the earth.
You are lifted high above all gods.

> **What does this mean?**
> Whoever worships images of gods that people have made will be disappointed.
> “Worship him, all you gods!”: All the powers that people honor must bow down before the true God. The letter to the Hebrews applies a similar statement to the angels (Hebrews 1:6).

---

## Light is sown for the righteous (Verses 10–12)

[10] You who love the LORD, hate evil!
He protects the lives of his holy people.
He rescues them from the hand of the wicked.
[11] Light is sown for the righteous,
and joy for those whose hearts are upright.
[12] Be glad in the LORD, you righteous people!
Give thanks to his holy name!

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 10: Whoever loves God turns away from evil. Love for God and hatred of evil belong together.
> Verse 11 is a beautiful picture: Light is “sown” like seed. It grows slowly, but it will come up. This is true even if you cannot see it yet.
