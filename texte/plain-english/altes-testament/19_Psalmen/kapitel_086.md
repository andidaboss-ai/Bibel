# Psalm 86
### Teach me your way

---

## I am poor and needy (Verses 1–7)

[1] A prayer by David.
Hear me, LORD, and answer me,
for I am poor and needy.
[2] Keep my soul safe, for I am faithful to you.
You are my God. Save your servant who trusts in you.
[3] Be merciful to me, Lord,
for I call to you all day long.
[4] Fill the soul of your servant with joy,
for to you, Lord, I lift up my soul.
[5] For you, Lord, are good and ready to forgive.
You are rich in loving kindness to all who call on you.
[6] LORD, hear my prayer!
Listen to my voice when I beg you for help!
[7] On the day of my trouble I will call on you,
for you will answer me.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 86 is the only psalm by David in the third book of Psalms.
> It is put together from many sentences of other psalms and Bible passages, like a prayer woven from well-known words.
> Verse 5: “For you, Lord, are good and ready to forgive”: That is why the one praying trusts God.

---

## No one is like you (Verses 8–10)

[8] Among the gods there is no one like you, Lord.
No deeds are like your deeds.
[9] All the nations you have made
will come and worship before you, Lord.
They will honor your name.
[10] For you are great, and you do wonderful things.
You alone are God.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 9: God made all nations, and one day they will all come to him (compare Revelation 15:4).
> Verse 10: “You alone are God”: a clear statement of faith in the one God.

---

## Give me an undivided heart (Verses 11–13)

[11] Teach me your way, LORD!
I will walk in your truth.
Give me an undivided heart, so that I fear your name.
[12] I will praise you, Lord my God, with my whole heart.
I will honor your name forever.
[13] For your loving kindness to me is great.
You have rescued my soul from the deepest part of Sheol (the world of the dead).

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 11 is a beautiful prayer: “Give me an undivided heart.” A divided heart wants two things at once: God and something else. The one praying asks for a heart that belongs completely to God.
> Verse 13: God has rescued him from deadly danger.

---

## Merciful and gracious (Verses 14–17)

[14] God, proud people have risen up against me.
A gang of violent men is out to take my life.
They do not care about you.
[15] But you, Lord, are a God who is merciful and gracious,
slow to anger, and rich in loving kindness and truth.
[16] Turn to me, and have mercy on me!
Give your strength to your servant!
Save the son of your servant!
[17] Show me a sign of your goodness,
so that those who hate me see it and are put to shame,
because you, LORD, have helped me and comforted me.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 15 quotes one of the most important statements about God in the whole Bible. God himself said it to Moses: “A God who is full of mercy and kindness, slow to become angry, and rich in loving kindness and faithfulness” (Exodus 34:6).
> Verse 16: “The son of your servant”: In Hebrew, it says “the son of your female servant.” It means: I belong to you completely, from birth on, like a child who was born in the house of a master.
> Verse 17: The one praying asks for a visible sign that God is on his side.
