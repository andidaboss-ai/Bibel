# Psalm 46
### A mighty fortress is our God

---

## God is our refuge (Verses 1–3)

[1] For the Chief Musician. By the sons of Korah. According to Alamoth.
God is our refuge and strength.
A refuge is a safe place.
He is always there to help in times of trouble.
[2] So we will not be afraid, even if the earth changes,
even if the mountains are shaken into the middle of the seas,
[3] even if its waters roar and foam,
even if the mountains tremble when the sea rises.
Selah.

> **What does this mean?**
> “According to Alamoth”: probably a note about the music, maybe for high voices (women’s or boys’ voices).
> Psalm 46 inspired Martin Luther to write his famous hymn about the mighty fortress. The hymn says: God is like a mighty fortress. With him we are safe.
> Even if the whole world falls apart, if the mountains fall into the sea, if an earthquake or a flood destroys everything: Whoever looks for safety with God does not need to be afraid.

---

## The city of God (Verses 4–7)

[4] There is a river.
Its streams make the city of God glad,
the holy place where the Most High lives.
[5] God is in the middle of her. She will not be shaken.
God will help her when morning comes.
[6] The nations were in an uproar. The kingdoms were shaken.
He raised his voice, and the earth melted.
[7] The LORD of Armies is with us.
The God of Jacob is our refuge.
Selah.

> **What does this mean?**
> Outside, the sea is roaring (verse 3). But in the city of God, a calm river flows that brings joy. Jerusalem actually had no big river, only a spring. The river is a picture of God’s blessing (compare Ezekiel 47; Revelation 22:1).
> Verse 5: God lives in the middle of the city. That is why it is safe. “When morning comes”: The night of fear passes.
> Verse 7 is a refrain: “The LORD of Armies is with us.” This reminds us of the name “Immanuel,” which means “God with us” (Isaiah 7:14).

---

## Be still and know (Verses 8–11)

[8] Come, see the works of the LORD,
the destruction he has brought on the earth.
[9] He makes wars stop all over the world.
He breaks the bow and smashes the spear.
He burns the chariots in the fire.
[10] “Be still, and know that I am God.
I will be honored among the nations.
I will be honored on the earth.”
[11] The LORD of Armies is with us.
The God of Jacob is our refuge.
Selah.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 9 is a great vision of peace: God ends all wars and destroys all weapons (compare Isaiah 2:4: swords are beaten into plowshares).
> Verse 10: God himself speaks: “Be still.” That means: Stop fighting. Stop your panic and restlessness. Know that I am God.
> For many people, this verse is a place of rest in the middle of the noise and rush of life.
