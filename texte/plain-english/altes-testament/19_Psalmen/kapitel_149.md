# Psalm 149
### Sing a new song to the LORD

---

## Joy and dancing (Verses 1–5)

[1] Praise Yah!
Sing a new song to the LORD!
Sing his praise in the gathering of his holy people!
[2] Let Israel be glad about the one who made them.
Let the people of Zion be joyful about their King.
[3] Let them praise his name with dancing!
Let them sing praises to him with tambourine and harp!
[4] For the LORD takes pleasure in his people.
He crowns the humble with salvation.
[5] Let his holy people be glad about this honor.
Let them sing for joy on their beds.

> **What does this mean?**
> A joyful celebration with music and dancing. God is glad about his people, and the people are glad about God.
> Verse 4: God “crowns the humble”: With God, the small and modest receive a crown.
> Verse 5: “On their beds”: Even at night, while they rest, they still sing for joy.

---

## The sword in their hand (Verses 6–9)

[6] Let the high praises of God be in their mouths,
and a sword with two sharp edges in their hand,
[7] to take revenge on the nations
and to punish the peoples,
[8] to tie up their kings with chains
and their nobles with iron chains,
[9] to carry out on them the judgment that is written.
All his holy people have this honor.
Praise Yah!

> **What does this mean?**
> These verses are difficult. After joy and dancing, there is suddenly talk of a sword and revenge.
> This psalm comes from a time when Israel was oppressed by great empires. It expresses the hope: God will judge the unjust rulers. What is meant is “the judgment that is written”, that is, God’s judgment, not people’s own plans for revenge.
> Sadly, in the course of history, these verses have been misused to justify war and violence in the name of God. That is wrong.
> Many Jews and Christians today understand the sword in a picture sense. The letter to the Hebrews says that God’s word is “sharper than any two-edged sword” (Hebrews 4:12). Paul speaks of “the sword of the Spirit. That is the word of God” (Ephesians 6:17).
> Jesus says: “Put your sword back into its place! For all those who take the sword will die by the sword” (Matthew 26:52). Violence in the name of God is not a way.
