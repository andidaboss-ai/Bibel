# Psalm 64
### Against the arrows of evil words

---

## Their tongue is like a sword (Verses 1–6)

[1] For the music director. A psalm by David.
Hear my voice, God, when I complain to you!
Keep my life safe from fear of the enemy!
[2] Hide me from the secret plans of the wicked,
from the noisy crowd of those who do evil.
[3] They sharpen their tongues like swords.
They aim their arrows, deadly words,
[4] to shoot at innocent people from hiding places.
They shoot at him suddenly and without fear.
[5] They encourage each other in evil plans.
They talk about setting traps in secret.
They say, “Who will see them?”
[6] They plan injustice and say, “We have made a perfect plan!”
Surely the mind and heart of a person are full of tricks.

> **What does this mean?**
> This is about attacks with words: slander, lies, rumors, secret schemes. Words can kill like arrows.
> We know this today too, for example through bullying or hate on the internet.
> The attackers feel safe: “Who will see them?” They think their plan is perfect.

---

## God shoots back (Verses 7–10)

[7] But God will shoot at them.
Suddenly they will be struck down by an arrow.
[8] Their own tongues will ruin them.
All who see them will shake their heads.
[9] All people will be afraid.
They will tell about the work of God
and wisely think about what he has done.
[10] The righteous will be glad in the LORD
and will find safety in him.
All whose hearts are honest will praise him!

> **What does this mean?**
> The turning point: The attackers shot “suddenly” (verse 4), but now they themselves are “suddenly” struck (verse 7). Their own words ruin them.
> God makes sure that evil falls back on the one who does it. The one praying does not have to strike back himself.
> In the end, all people see that God is just.
