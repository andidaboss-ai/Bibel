# Psalm 33
### Sing a new song to the LORD

---

## A new song (Verses 1–5)

[1] Be full of joy in the LORD, you righteous people!
It is fitting for honest people to praise him.
[2] Give thanks to the LORD with the lyre.
Sing praises to him with the harp of 10 strings.
[3] Sing a new song to him.
Play well, with shouts of joy!
[4] For the LORD’s word is right.
All his work is done in faithfulness.
[5] He loves what is right and fair.
The earth is full of the LORD’s loving kindness.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 33 is a great song of praise. It has no heading.
> “A new song”: God’s goodness is new every day, so our praise should also be new and fresh again and again. This phrase appears often in the Bible (compare Psalm 96:1; Revelation 5:9).
> “The earth is full of the LORD’s loving kindness”: You can discover God’s love everywhere in the world.

---

## He spoke, and it was done (Verses 6–9)

[6] The heavens were made by the LORD’s word,
and all their army by the breath of his mouth.
[7] He gathers the waters of the sea together like a heap.
He puts the deep waters in storerooms.
[8] Let all the earth fear the LORD.
Let all the people of the world stand in awe of him.
[9] For he spoke, and it was done.
He gave the command, and it stood firm.

> **What does this mean?**
> God created the world through his word (compare Genesis 1:3: “God said, ‘Let there be light.’”).
> “All their army”: the stars.
> Verse 9 sums it up very briefly: God only needs to speak, and it happens. That is how powerful his word is.

---

## God’s plan stands firm (Verses 10–15)

[10] The LORD brings the plans of the nations to nothing.
He makes the ideas of the peoples useless.
[11] The LORD’s plan stands firm forever,
the thoughts of his heart for all generations.
[12] Blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD,
the people he has chosen as his own.
[13] The LORD looks down from heaven.
He sees all people.
[14] From the place where he lives,
he looks out on everyone who lives on the earth.
[15] He is the one who forms all their hearts,
and he pays attention to everything they do.

> **What does this mean?**
> People and nations make big plans. But in the end, what God plans is what happens.
> God sees all people, not only Israel. He has formed every heart and knows it exactly.

---

## A horse does not help (Verses 16–22)

[16] No king is saved by a large army.
A strong warrior is not rescued by great strength.
[17] A horse is useless for safety.
It does not rescue anyone with its great power.
[18] Look, the LORD’s eye is on those who fear him,
on those who hope in his loving kindness.
[19] He rescues their soul from death
and keeps them alive in times of hunger.
[20] Our soul has waited for the LORD.
He is our help and our shield.
[21] For our heart is full of joy in him,
because we have trusted in his holy name.
[22] LORD, let your loving kindness be on us,
since we have put our hope in you.

> **What does this mean?**
> Neither large armies nor strong war horses can really save. True safety comes from God (compare Psalm 20:7).
> God’s eye rests with love on those who trust him.
> Verse 22 is a beautiful closing prayer. It is also prayed at the end of the ancient song of praise called the “Te Deum.”
