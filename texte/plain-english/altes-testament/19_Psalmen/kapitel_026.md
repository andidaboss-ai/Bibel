# Psalm 26
### I go my way with integrity

---

## Test me, LORD (Verses 1–5)

[1] By David.
Judge me, LORD, for I have lived with integrity.
That means: I have lived honestly, with my whole heart.
I have also trusted in the LORD without doubting.
[2] Examine me, LORD, and test me.
Look closely at my heart and my mind.
[3] For your loving kindness is in front of my eyes.
I have lived by your truth.
[4] I have not sat with liars,
and I will not go along with hypocrites.
[5] I hate the gathering of evil people.
I will not sit with the wicked.

> **What does this mean?**
> The person praying is being accused unfairly. He asks God to test him. He has nothing to hide.
> This is not pride. He does not say: I am without sin. He says: I have honestly tried to live with you.
> Verses 4–5 remind us of Psalm 1: He does not sit with those who plan evil.
> “I hate the gathering of evil people”: He firmly rejects evil. This does not mean hatred of people. It means a clear no to their evil plans.

---

## I wash my hands in innocence (Verses 6–8)

[6] I will wash my hands in innocence.
Then I will go around your altar, LORD,
[7] so that I can let my voice of thanks be heard,
and tell about all the wonderful things you have done.
[8] LORD, I love the house where you live,
the place where your glory lives.

> **What does this mean?**
> “Wash my hands in innocence”: Before the priests went to the altar, they washed their hands (Exodus 30:19–21). The person praying says: I come with a clean conscience.
> We still know the saying “to wash your hands of something” today. Pilate washed his hands when he condemned Jesus (Matthew 27:24). But for him, it was an excuse.
> Verse 8: The person praying loves the temple, because God’s glory lives there.

---

## Do not sweep me away with the sinners (Verses 9–12)

[9] Do not sweep my soul away together with sinners,
or my life together with violent men.
[10] Their hands are full of evil.
Their right hands are full of bribes.
[11] But as for me, I will live with integrity.
Set me free, and be kind to me.
[12] My foot stands on level ground.
In the gatherings I will praise the LORD.

> **What does this mean?**
> The person praying asks: Do not let me share the same fate as the violent people and those who take bribes.
> Verse 11: Even though he lives honestly, he still asks for mercy. He knows: In the end, I depend on God’s mercy.
> Verse 12: “Level ground”: He stands safe and firm. He will not stumble.
