# Psalm 54
### God is my helper

---

## Save me, God (Verses 1–3)

[1] For the Chief Musician. On stringed instruments. A contemplation by David,
when the Ziphites came and said to Saul,
“Isn’t David hiding among us?”
Save me, God, by your name.
Prove that I am in the right by your power.
[2] Hear my prayer, God.
Listen to the words of my mouth.
[3] For strangers have risen up against me.
Violent men want to take my life.
They do not care about God.
Selah.

> **What does this mean?**
> The superscription reminds us how the people of the town of Ziph betrayed David to Saul when he was hiding among them (1 Samuel 23:19; 26:1).
> David is betrayed, surrounded and in danger of his life. He has only one hope left: God.
> “By your name”: God’s name stands for God himself, for his power and faithfulness.

---

## God is my helper (Verses 4–7)

[4] Look, God is my helper.
The Lord is the one who keeps my soul alive.
[5] He will pay back my enemies for their evil.
Destroy them, because you are faithful.
[6] I will bring you a sacrifice
that I give freely.
I will thank your name, LORD, for it is good.
[7] For he has rescued me from all trouble.
My eyes have seen my enemies defeated.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 4 is the turning point: “Look, God is my helper.” In the middle of the danger, David realizes: I am not alone.
> Verse 5: David asks that the evil of his enemies may fall back on them. He does not take revenge himself. In fact, David later spared Saul’s life twice (1 Samuel 24 and 26).
> Verse 6: An offering given freely is a thank offering that people bring out of joy, not out of duty.
