# Psalm 131
### Like a calm child

---

## My soul has become quiet (Verses 1–3)

[1] A song of ascents. By David.
LORD, my heart is not proud,
and my eyes do not look down on others.
I do not busy myself with great matters,
or with things that are too wonderful for me.
[2] Truly, I have calmed my soul and made it quiet,
like a weaned child with his mother.
My soul inside me is like a weaned child.
[3] Israel, hope in the LORD,
from now on and forever.

> **What does this mean?**
> One of the shortest psalms, but very heartfelt.
> The person praying does not have to understand everything or be able to do everything. He is content.
> Verse 2: A “weaned child” no longer drinks milk from his mother’s breast. He no longer comes to his mother because he is hungry, but simply because he wants to be with her. That is how the person praying rests with God: quiet and safe.
> Here God is compared to a mother. The Bible often speaks about God like a mother, too (compare Isaiah 66:13).
