# Psalm 85
### Righteousness and peace kiss each other

---

## You were kind (Verses 1–3)

[1] For the music director. A psalm by the sons of Korah.
LORD, you have been kind to your land.
You have turned things around for Jacob.
[2] You have forgiven the guilt of your people.
You have covered all their sin.
Selah.
[3] You have taken away all your fury.
You have turned away from your burning anger.

> **What does this mean?**
> The psalm begins by looking back: In the past, God forgave and restored his people. This may mean the return from exile in Babylon (around 538 BC).
> “Covered all their sin”: God no longer looks at the guilt. It is forgiven.

---

## Give us new life again (Verses 4–7)

[4] Bring us back, God who saves us,
and stop being angry with us!
[5] Will you be angry with us forever?
Will your anger go on through all generations?
[6] Won’t you give us new life again,
so that your people may rejoice in you?
[7] Show us your loving kindness, LORD.
Give us your saving help.

> **What does this mean?**
> Even after the return, things are difficult. The land is poor, and life is hard. The people ask: Give us new life again!
> “So that your people may rejoice in you”: The goal of the rescue is joy in God.

---

## Righteousness and peace kiss each other (Verses 8–13)

[8] I will listen to what God, the LORD, will say,
for he will promise peace to his people, his faithful ones.
But they must not go back to foolishness.
[9] Surely his saving help is near those who fear him,
so that glory may live in our land.
[10] Mercy and truth meet each other.
Righteousness and peace have kissed each other.
[11] Truth springs up out of the earth.
Righteousness looks down from heaven.
[12] Yes, the LORD will give what is good.
Our land will give its harvest.
[13] Righteousness goes ahead of him
and prepares the way for his steps.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 8: The one praying becomes quiet and listens to God. And God speaks of peace.
> Verses 10–11 are wonderful pictures: God’s qualities are described like people who meet and hug each other. “Righteousness and peace have kissed each other”: There is no real peace without justice, and justice leads to peace.
> Verse 11: From below, truth grows out of the earth. From above, righteousness looks down from heaven. Heaven and earth come together.
> “Truth” (verses 10 and 11): The Hebrew word “emet” means both: truth and faithfulness, being reliable. That is why many Bibles translate it here as “faithfulness.”
> Christians often read this psalm during Advent: God is coming, and righteousness goes ahead of him.
