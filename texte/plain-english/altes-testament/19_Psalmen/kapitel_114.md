# Psalm 114
### When Israel left Egypt

---

## The sea saw it and ran away (Verses 1–4)

[1] When Israel left Egypt,
when the family of Jacob left a people with a foreign language,
[2] Judah became his holy place,
and Israel became the land he ruled.
[3] The sea saw it and ran away.
The Jordan turned back.
[4] The mountains jumped like rams,
the little hills like lambs.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 114 is a short, lively poem about the exodus from Egypt. It belongs to the Hallel, which is sung at Passover.
> Verse 3: Two miracles are named together: The Red Sea divided when the people left Egypt (Exodus 14). The Jordan divided when they entered the land (Joshua 3).
> Verse 4: The mountains jump like young sheep. This means the earthquake at Mount Sinai (Exodus 19:18). Nature reacts with life when God comes.

---

## What is wrong with you, sea? (Verses 5–8)

[5] What is wrong with you, sea, that you run away?
And you, Jordan, that you turn back?
[6] You mountains, that you jump like rams?
You little hills, like lambs?
[7] Tremble, earth, before the Lord,
before the God of Jacob.
[8] He turned the rock into a pool of water,
the hard flint stone into a spring of water.

> **What does this mean?**
> The poet asks the sea and the mountains, almost with humor: What is wrong with you?
> The answer: God has come! The whole earth must tremble before him.
> Verse 8: The God who dries up seas can also make water flow out of hard rock (Exodus 17:6). He can make dead things come alive.
