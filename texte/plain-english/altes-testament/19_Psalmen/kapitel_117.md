# Psalm 117
### Praise the LORD, all you nations!

---

## The shortest psalm (Verses 1–2)

[1] Praise the LORD, all you nations!
Praise him highly, all you peoples!
[2] For his loving kindness toward us is great.
The LORD’s faithfulness lasts forever.
Praise Yah!
Yah is a short form of the name of God, the LORD.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 117 is the shortest psalm, and it is also the shortest chapter in the whole Bible. It is located roughly in the middle of the Bible.
> It is short, but its message is huge: All the nations of the world are called to praise God.
> The reason: God’s loving kindness and faithfulness. These are the two most important qualities of God in the Old Testament.
> Paul quotes verse 1 to show: The good news about God is not only for Israel, but for all nations (Romans 15:11).
