# Psalm 31
### Into your hand I commit my spirit

---

## Be a strong rock for me (Verses 1–5)

[1] For the music director. A psalm by David.
LORD, I come to you for safety.
Never let me be put to shame.
Rescue me, because you are righteous.
[2] Turn your ear to me.
Rescue me quickly.
Be a strong rock for me,
a strong house that keeps me safe.
[3] For you are my rock and my fortress.
So lead me and guide me
for the sake of your name.
[4] Pull me out of the net
that they have secretly set for me,
for you are my stronghold.
[5] Into your hand I commit my spirit.
You set me free, LORD, God of truth.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 5 is very well known: “Into your hand I commit my spirit.” “Commit” here means: to entrust, to hand over.
> Jesus prayed these words on the cross as his last words: “Father, into your hands I commit my spirit!” (Luke 23:46). Stephen prayed something similar when he died (Acts 7:59).
> Jews traditionally pray this verse before going to sleep. Many Christians pray it in the evening and in the hour of death.

---

## You have seen my trouble (Verses 6–8)

[6] I hate those who hold on to worthless idols.
But I trust in the LORD.
[7] I will be glad and full of joy
because of your loving kindness.
For you have seen my suffering.
You have known my soul in times of trouble.
[8] You have not handed me over to my enemy.
You have set my feet in a wide, open place.

> **What does this mean?**
> “Worthless idols”: gods who cannot do anything. The one who prays rejects them firmly.
> Verse 7: “You have known my soul in times of trouble”: God knows exactly how I am doing. He knows my pain.

---

## I am like broken pottery (Verses 9–13)

[9] Have mercy on me, LORD, for I am in trouble.
My eyes, my soul and my body
are wearing away with grief.
[10] For my life is used up in sorrow,
and my years in sighing.
My strength fails because of my guilt.
My bones are wasting away.
[11] Because of all my enemies,
my neighbors look down on me completely.
I have become a horror to the people I know.
Those who saw me on the street ran away from me.
[12] They have forgotten me like a dead man.
I am like broken pottery.
[13] For I have heard many people speaking evil about me.
There is terror on every side.
They plot together against me.
They make plans to take my life.

> **What does this mean?**
> The one who prays is sick, pushed out and forgotten. People avoid him on the street.
> “Like broken pottery”: worthless, thrown away. That is how he feels.
> “Terror on every side”: This phrase often appears in the book of the prophet Jeremiah (for example Jeremiah 20:10).
> Many people know this feeling of being forgotten and written off. God still sees them.

---

## My times are in your hand (Verses 14–18)

[14] But I trust in you, LORD.
I said, “You are my God.”
[15] My times are in your hand.
Rescue me from the hand of my enemies
and from those who hunt me down.
[16] Let your face shine on your servant.
Save me, because of your loving kindness.
[17] LORD, do not let me be put to shame,
for I have called to you.
Let the wicked be put to shame.
Let them be silent in Sheol (the world of the dead).
[18] Let the lying lips be silenced
that speak against the righteous
in a rude way, with pride and scorn.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 14–15: “But I”: Once again the turn toward trust.
> “My times are in your hand”: My whole life, good times and bad times, beginning and end, is with God. Many people hold on to this sentence in hard hours.
> A well-known German Christian song takes up this verse.

---

## How great is your goodness! (Verses 19–24)

[19] Oh, how great is your goodness!
You have stored it up for those who fear you.
You have shown it to those who come to you for safety,
in front of all people.
[20] In the shelter of your presence
you hide them from the plots of people.
You keep them hidden in a shelter,
away from the quarrels of tongues.
[21] Praise the LORD!
For he has shown me his wonderful loving kindness
in a strong city.
[22] As for me, I said in my panic,
“I am cut off from your sight.”
But you heard my voice when I begged you for help,
when I cried out to you.
[23] Oh, love the LORD, all you who belong to him!
The LORD protects those who are faithful.
And he pays back in full
the one who acts proudly.
[24] Be strong, and let your heart take courage,
all you who hope in the LORD.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 19: God has a great store of goodness for those who trust him.
> Verse 20: The “quarrels of tongues” means: rumors, lies about others, evil talk. God protects from this.
> Verse 22 is very honest: In his panic, the one who prayed had thought that God had forgotten him. But God did hear him. Even when we doubt, God stays faithful.
> Verse 24: An encouragement for everyone: Be strong and take courage!
