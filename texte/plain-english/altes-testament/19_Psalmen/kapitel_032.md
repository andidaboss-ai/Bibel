# Psalm 32
### The freedom of forgiveness

---

## Happy is the one who is forgiven (Verses 1–2)

[1] By David. A contemplative psalm.
Blessed is the one whose wrongdoing is forgiven,
whose sin is covered.
Blessed means: truly happy.
[2] Blessed is the man
whose guilt the LORD does not count against him,
and whose spirit has no deceit in it.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 32 is one of the seven psalms of repentance. It is about the happiness of being forgiven.
> “Contemplative psalm”: The Hebrew word is “Maskil.” It may mean “a song that gives insight” or “a skillful song.”
> Three words for wrongdoing: disobedience (crossing a line on purpose), sin (missing the target), guilt (what is wrong and crooked). And three words for forgiveness: forgive (take away), cover, not count against. God forgives completely.
> Paul quotes verses 1–2 in Romans 4:7–8: Forgiveness is a gift, not a reward for good deeds.

---

## When I kept silent (Verses 3–5)

[3] When I kept silent,
my bones wasted away
because of my groaning all day long.
[4] For day and night your hand was heavy on me.
My strength dried up as in the heat of summer.
Selah.
[5] I admitted my sin to you.
I did not hide my guilt.
I said, “I will confess my wrongs to the LORD.”
And you forgave the guilt of my sin.
Selah.

> **What does this mean?**
> David tells about his own experience: As long as he kept quiet about his guilt, he felt bad, even in his body. His bad conscience ate away at him.
> Many people think of the time after David’s adultery with Bathsheba (2 Samuel 11–12).
> Verse 5: When he finally admitted his guilt, he was set free. God forgave him right away.
> This is still true today: Whoever honestly admits their guilt finds forgiveness with God (compare 1 John 1:9).

---

## You are my hiding place (Verses 6–7)

[6] So let everyone who is faithful to God pray to you
at a time when you can be found.
When the great waters flood over,
they will surely not reach him.
[7] You are my hiding place.
You will protect me from trouble.
You will surround me with songs of rescue.
Selah.

> **What does this mean?**
> David invites everyone: Do not wait too long! Pray while God can be found.
> God is a safe hiding place. When the floods of trouble come, you are safe with him.
> “Songs of rescue”: All around, songs of joy are heard, because God has rescued.

---

## I will teach you (Verses 8–11)

[8] I will teach you and show you the way you should go.
I will give you advice, and my eye is on you.
[9] Do not be like the horse or the mule,
which have no understanding.
They must be controlled with bit and bridle,
or else they will not come near to you.
[10] The wicked have many sorrows.
But loving kindness will surround
the one who trusts in the LORD.
[11] Be glad in the LORD, and be full of joy, you righteous people!
Shout for joy, all of you whose hearts are honest!

> **What does this mean?**
> In verse 8, God himself speaks: I will lead you. I keep my eye on you, like a good teacher.
> Verse 9: God does not want to force us like an animal with a bridle. He wants us to come to him freely and with understanding.
> Verses 10–11: Whoever trusts God is surrounded by goodness. The psalm ends with joy.
