# Psalm 52
### Like a green olive tree

---

## Why do you boast about evil? (Verses 1–5)

[1] For the music director. A contemplation by David,
when Doeg the Edomite came and told Saul,
“David has come to Ahimelech’s house.”
Why do you boast about the evil you do, you mighty man?
God’s loving kindness lasts all the time.
[2] Your tongue plans ruin.
It is like a sharp razor, and it works with lies.
[3] You love evil more than good.
You love lying more than speaking the truth.
Selah.
[4] You love all words that destroy,
you lying tongue.
[5] So God will also destroy you forever.
He will grab you and pull you out of your tent.
He will pull you up by the roots
from the land of the living.
Selah.

> **What does this mean?**
> The superscription reminds us of a terrible story: David was fleeing from Saul. The priest Ahimelech gave him bread. Doeg, a man from Edom, told Saul about it. Then Saul had 85 priests killed, and Doeg carried out the murder (1 Samuel 21–22).
> Doeg is an example of a person who causes harm with his tongue. His words are like a sharp razor.
> Verse 1: In the middle of this accusation there is a strong sentence: “God’s loving kindness lasts all the time.” Evil is powerful, but God’s goodness is stronger and lasts longer.

---

## Like a green olive tree (Verses 6–9)

[6] The righteous will also see it and be filled with awe.
They will laugh at him and say,
[7] “Look, this is the man who did not make God his strength.
He trusted in his great riches
and made himself strong by his evil.”
[8] But as for me, I am like a green olive tree in God’s house.
I trust in God’s loving kindness forever and ever.
[9] I will thank you forever, because you have done it.
I will hope in your name, for it is good,
in front of those who are faithful to you.

> **What does this mean?**
> The evil man will be “pulled up by the roots” (verse 5). But the righteous person is like an olive tree that is firmly rooted in the ground and stays green.
> Olive trees grow very old, some more than a thousand years. They are a picture of lasting strength and blessing.
> “In God’s house”: Trees may have grown in the temple area. Whoever lives close to God stays alive and bears fruit (compare Psalm 1:3).
