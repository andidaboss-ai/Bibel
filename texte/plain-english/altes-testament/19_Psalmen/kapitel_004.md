# Psalm 4
### An evening prayer

---

## Answer me when I call (Verses 1–3)

[1] For the music director. On string instruments. A psalm by David.
Answer me when I call, God of my righteousness!
That means: God who makes things right for me.
Give me relief from my trouble.
Be kind to me and hear my prayer.
[2] You people, how long will you turn my honor into shame?
Will you love what is worthless and look for lies?
Selah.
[3] But know this: The LORD has set apart for himself the one who is faithful to him.
The LORD will hear when I call to him.

> **What does this mean?**
> “For the music director”: This note stands above many psalms. It shows that the psalms were sung in worship.
> “God of my righteousness”: God is the one who makes sure I receive justice.
> People are spreading lies about David. They love “what is worthless” and “lies.” Some think of false gods here, others of false rumors.

---

## Be still (Verses 4–5)

[4] Stand in awe, and do not sin.
Search your own heart on your bed, and be still.
Selah.
[5] Bring the right sacrifices.
Put your trust in the LORD.

> **What does this mean?**
> Good advice for the evening: Before you sleep, think about your day. Search your heart. Become still.
> Verse 4 can also be translated in another way. Paul quotes it like this in Ephesians 4:26: Be angry, but do not sin. And Paul adds: Do not let your anger last until the sun goes down.
> “The right sacrifices” (in the WEB: “the sacrifices of righteousness”): sacrifices brought with an honest heart, not only on the outside.

---

## Peace in the night (Verses 6–8)

[6] Many say, “Who will let us see anything good?”
LORD, let the light of your face shine on us.
[7] You have filled my heart with joy,
more than people have when they have lots of grain and new wine.
[8] In peace I will lie down and sleep,
for you alone, LORD, let me live in safety.

> **What does this mean?**
> Many people are unhappy and ask: Where is the good?
> David answers with a prayer from the blessing of Aaron: The LORD make his face shine on you (Numbers 6:25).
> Verse 7: The joy that comes from God is greater than the joy about a rich harvest.
> Verse 8 is one of the most beautiful sentences for the evening: “In peace I will lie down and sleep.” Psalm 3 was a morning prayer. Psalm 4 is an evening prayer.
