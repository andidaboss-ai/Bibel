# Psalm 115
### Not to us, LORD, but to your name give glory

---

## Not to us, but to your name (Verses 1–3)

[1] Not to us, LORD, not to us,
but to your name give glory,
because of your loving kindness and because of your faithfulness!
[2] Why should the nations say:
“Where is their God now?”
[3] But our God is in heaven.
He does whatever he wants.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 1 is a humble prayer: We should not be honored, but God should. Many people have chosen this verse as their motto for life (in Latin: “Non nobis, Domine”).
> The nations mock: Where is your God? You cannot even see him.
> The answer: Our God is in heaven. You cannot see him, but he is mighty.

---

## Idols are dead (Verses 4–8)

[4] Their idols are silver and gold,
made by human hands.
[5] They have mouths, but they do not speak.
They have eyes, but they do not see.
[6] They have ears, but they do not hear.
They have noses, but they do not smell.
[7] They have hands, but they do not feel.
They have feet, but they do not walk.
No sound comes from their throats.
[8] Those who make them will become like them,
yes, everyone who trusts in them.

> **What does this mean?**
> You can see the gods of the other nations. But they are only figures made of metal. They have eyes, ears, hands and feet, but none of them works.
> Verse 8: A deep thought: People become like what they worship. Whoever worships lifeless things becomes lifeless inside. This is also true of modern “idols” like money, power or success.

---

## Trust in the LORD! (Verses 9–15)

[9] Israel, trust in the LORD!
He is their help and their shield.
[10] Family of Aaron, trust in the LORD!
He is their help and their shield.
[11] You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD!
He is their help and their shield.
[12] The LORD remembers us. He will bless us.
He will bless the people of Israel.
He will bless the family of Aaron.
[13] He will bless those who fear the LORD,
both small and great.
[14] May the LORD give you more and more,
you and your children.
[15] You are blessed by the LORD,
who made heaven and earth.

> **What does this mean?**
> Three groups are called: the whole people of Israel, the priests (the family of Aaron), and all who fear God. The third group may also include people from other nations who have joined themselves to the God of Israel.
> Verse 13: God blesses everyone, “both small and great.” No one is too unimportant for God.

---

## We will praise Yah (Verses 16–18)

[16] The heavens belong to the LORD,
but he has given the earth to human beings.
[17] The dead do not praise Yah,
nor does anyone who goes down into silence.
Yah is a short form of the name of God, the LORD.
[18] But we will praise Yah,
from now on and forever.
Praise Yah!

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 16: God has trusted people with the earth. That is a gift and a responsibility (compare Genesis 1:28; 2:15).
> Verses 17–18: As long as we live, we want to praise God, and to do so “forever.”
