# Psalm 27
### The LORD is my light

---

## Whom should I fear? (Verses 1–3)

[1] By David.
The LORD is my light and my salvation.
Whom should I fear?
The LORD is the strength of my life.
Of whom should I be afraid?
[2] When evil people came at me to eat up my flesh,
my enemies and my foes,
they stumbled and fell.
[3] Even if an army sets up camp against me,
my heart will not be afraid.
Even if war breaks out against me,
even then I will be confident.

> **What does this mean?**
> Psalm 27 begins with great trust: God is my light in the dark, my rescue in danger, the strength of my life.
> Whoever has God at his side does not need to be afraid, not even of a whole army.
> “To eat up my flesh”: The enemies are like wild animals hunting their prey.

---

## I ask for only one thing (Verses 4–6)

[4] I have asked the LORD for one thing. That is what I will look for:
that I may live in the LORD’s house all the days of my life,
to see the LORD’s beauty,
and to think and ask in his temple.
[5] For on the day of trouble, he will keep me hidden in his shelter.
In the secret place of his tent, he will hide me.
He will lift me up on a rock.
[6] Now my head will be lifted up above my enemies all around me.
I will bring sacrifices of joy in his tent.
I will sing, yes, I will sing praises to the LORD.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 4 is the heart of the psalm. The person praying has only one wish: to be with God and see his beauty.
> “The LORD’s beauty”: God is not only mighty and just. He is also beautiful, lovable and kind.
> Verse 5: God’s house is like a hiding place in danger. Back then, a person who was being hunted could look for protection in the sanctuary.

---

## Do not abandon me (Verses 7–12)

[7] Hear me, LORD, when I cry out with my voice.
Be kind to me, and answer me.
[8] When you said, “Seek my face,”
my heart said to you, “I will seek your face, LORD.”
[9] Do not hide your face from me.
Do not push your servant away in anger.
You have been my help.
Do not leave me, and do not abandon me,
God who saves me.
[10] Even if my father and my mother abandon me,
the LORD will take me in.
[11] Teach me your way, LORD.
Lead me on a straight path, because of my enemies.
[12] Do not hand me over to what my enemies want,
for false witnesses have risen up against me,
people who breathe out cruelty.

> **What does this mean?**
> Now the tone changes. Trust turns into a request. The person praying is afraid that God might turn away.
> Verse 8: God himself invites: “Seek my face.” The person praying answers: Yes, I want to do that!
> Verse 10 is a great comfort: Even if your own parents let you down, God takes you in. For people who have been rejected by their family, this is a very important sentence.
> Verse 12: False witnesses speak against him. He needs God’s protection in court.

---

## Wait for the LORD (Verses 13–14)

[13] I am still confident of this:
I will see the goodness of the LORD in the land of the living.
[14] Wait for the LORD.
Be strong, and let your heart take courage.
Yes, wait for the LORD.

> **What does this mean?**
> “In the land of the living”: The person praying will experience God’s goodness not only after death, but here and now.
> Verse 14: At the end, the person praying speaks to himself or to others: Wait for God! Be brave!
> “Waiting” here does not mean sitting around and doing nothing. It means holding on full of hope and trusting God.
