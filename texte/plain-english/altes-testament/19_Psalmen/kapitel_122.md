# Psalm 122
### Pray for peace for Jerusalem!

---

## I was glad when they said to me (Verses 1–9)

[1] A song of ascents. By David.
I was glad when they said to me,
“Let’s go to the LORD’s house!”
[2] Jerusalem, our feet are standing inside your gates!
[3] Jerusalem is built as a city
that is closely joined together.
[4] The tribes go up there, the tribes of Yah,
as a rule for Israel says,
to give thanks to the name of the LORD.
Yah is a short form of the name of God.
[5] For there the thrones for judging are set up,
the thrones of David’s family.
[6] Pray for peace for Jerusalem!
May those who love you do well.
[7] May there be peace inside your walls,
and may things go well inside your palaces!
[8] For the sake of my brothers and my friends,
I will now say, “May there be peace inside you!”
[9] For the sake of the house of the LORD our God,
I will look for what is good for you.

> **What does this mean?**
> Now the pilgrims have arrived. They are standing in the gates of Jerusalem. Their joy is great.
> Verse 3: Jerusalem is built closely together, house next to house. This is also a picture of a people that holds together.
> Verse 6: In Hebrew, there is a play on words here: “Yerushalayim” (Jerusalem) and “shalom” (peace). The city carries peace in its name.
> “Pray for peace for Jerusalem”: This request still matters today. Jews, Christians and Muslims have holy places in Jerusalem. To pray for the peace of this city and of all the people there is a good task.
