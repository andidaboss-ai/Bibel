# Psalm 16
### You show me the path to life

---

## You are my Lord (Verses 1–4)

[1] A poem by David.
Keep me safe, God, for I take refuge in you.
[2] My soul, you have said to the LORD,
“You are my Lord.
Apart from you, I have nothing good.”
[3] As for God’s holy people who are on the earth,
they are the wonderful ones. All my joy is in them.
[4] Those who bring gifts to another god will have more and more pain.
I will not pour out their drink offerings of blood.
I will not even speak their names.

> **What does this mean?**
> “Poem”: In Hebrew, it says “Miktam.” No one knows exactly what it means. Some think of “golden song” or “inscription.”
> Verse 2 is a strong confession: God is the greatest good. Without him, everything else is worth nothing.
> Verse 3: The person praying is glad to be together with other people who love God.
> Verse 4: He wants nothing to do with foreign gods. He will not even say their names.

---

## My good inheritance (Verses 5–8)

[5] The LORD has given me my share and my cup.
You have made what I received secure.
[6] The measuring lines have marked out pleasant places for me.
Yes, I have a good inheritance.
[7] I will praise the LORD, who has given me advice.
Yes, my heart teaches me in the night.
[8] I have always kept the LORD in front of me.
Because he is at my right side, nothing will shake me.

> **What does this mean?**
> When Israel came into the land, the land was divided with measuring lines and by drawing lots. Only the priests and Levites received no land. God himself was their inheritance (Numbers 18:20).
> The person praying says: God is my share. That is the best inheritance there is.
> Verse 8: “I have always kept the LORD in front of me.” Whoever thinks of God finds a firm hold. God is at my right side, like a protector.

---

## You will not leave me in death (Verses 9–11)

[9] That is why my heart is glad, and my tongue rejoices.
My body will also live in safety.
[10] For you will not leave my soul in Sheol (the world of the dead).
You will not let your holy one see decay.
[11] You will show me the path of life.
When I am with you, my joy is full.
At your right hand there is delight forever.

> **What does this mean?**
> Here we hear a great trust that reaches beyond death: God will not leave me in the place of the dead. Being close to God is stronger than death.
> Verse 11 is one of the most beautiful verses in the Bible: With God, “my joy is full,” and there is “delight forever.”
> In the New Testament, verses 8–11 are related to the resurrection of Jesus. Peter quotes them in his sermon at Pentecost (Acts 2:25–31). Paul quotes them in Acts 13:35. They say: David died, and his body decayed. But Jesus rose from the dead. He is “your holy one,” who did not see decay.
> Jewish teachers understand the psalm as an expression of trust that God protects the faithful person from death and gives him life.
