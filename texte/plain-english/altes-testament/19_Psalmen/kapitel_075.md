# Psalm 75
### God is the judge

---

## We thank you (Verses 1–3)

[1] For the music director. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A psalm by Asaph. A song.
We give thanks to you, God.
We give thanks, for your Name is near.
People tell about your wonderful deeds.
[2] When I choose the right time,
I will judge fairly.
[3] The earth and all who live on it shake.
I hold its pillars firmly.
Selah.

> **What does this mean?**
> After the lament in Psalm 74, an answer comes here: God will judge, but at his own time.
> In verses 2–3, God himself speaks: I decide when the time has come. Even if everything shakes, I hold the world firm, like a house whose pillars I support.

---

## Don’t brag! (Verses 4–8)

[4] I said to the proud, “Don’t brag!”
I said to the wicked, “Don’t lift up your horn.
[5] Don’t lift up your horn so high.
Don’t speak with a stiff neck.”
[6] For honor does not come from the east,
or from the west, or from the south.
[7] But God is the judge.
He brings one person down, and he lifts another up.
[8] For in the LORD’s hand there is a cup,
full of foaming wine mixed with spices.
He pours it out.
Yes, the wicked of the earth drink and drink it, down to the very last drop.

> **What does this mean?**
> “Lift up your horn”: like a bull that raises its head, proud and ready to attack. A picture for pride and power.
> “Speak with a stiff neck”: to be stubborn and proud.
> Verses 6–7: Success and honor do not come from any direction of the sky, but from God. He removes the powerful and lifts up the lowly (compare 1 Samuel 2:7; Luke 1:52).
> Verse 8: The cup is a picture for God’s judgment. The wicked must drink it down to the very last drop.

---

## I will sing (Verses 9–10)

[9] But as for me, I will tell about this forever.
I will sing praises to the God of Jacob.
[10] I will cut off all the horns of the wicked,
but the horns of the righteous will be lifted up.

> **What does this mean?**
> The one praying promises to praise God forever.
> Verse 10: The one speaking here is probably God himself again, or the king acting for him. The power of the wicked is broken, and the power of the righteous is made strong.
