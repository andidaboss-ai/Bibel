# Joshua – Chapter 23
### Joshua’s farewell speech

---

## God has fought for you (Verses 1–5)

[1] Many days later, when the LORD had given Israel rest from all their enemies around them,
and Joshua was old and well along in years,
[2] Joshua called all Israel together, their elders, their heads, their judges, and their officers.
He said to them,
“I am old and well along in years.
[3] You have seen everything that the LORD your God has done to all these nations because of you.
For it is the LORD your God who has fought for you.
[4] Look, I have given you by lot these nations that are still left, as an inheritance for your tribes,
from the Jordan, with all the nations that I have destroyed, to the great sea in the west, where the sun goes down.
[5] The LORD your God will push them out in front of you and drive them out of your sight.
And you will take their land, as the LORD your God told you.

> **What does this mean?**
> Joshua is old, like Moses at the end of his life. And like Moses, he gives a farewell speech.
> He reminds them: You did not win. God fought for you.

---

## Hold on to the LORD (Verses 6–11)

[6] “So be very courageous. Keep and do everything that is written in the book of the law of Moses.
Do not turn away from it to the right or to the left.
[7] Do not mix with these nations that are still left among you.
Do not mention the names of their gods, and do not swear by them.
Do not serve them, and do not bow down to them.
[8] But hold on to the LORD your God, as you have done to this day.
[9] For the LORD has driven out great and strong nations in front of you.
And no one has been able to stand against you to this day.
[10] One man of you will chase 1,000,
for it is the LORD your God who fights for you, as he told you.
[11] So be very careful with yourselves, that you love the LORD your God.

> **What does this mean?**
> The greatest danger is no longer war but the worship of idols. The nations that still live in the land have other gods. Israel should not even say the names of these gods.
> Verse 11 is the heart of the whole speech: “So be very careful with yourselves, that you love the LORD your God.” Love for God does not happen by itself. You have to take care of it like a fire that should not go out.

---

## A serious warning (Verses 12–16)

[12] “But if you turn back
and hold on to the rest of these nations that are still left among you,
and you marry them, and you go to them and they come to you,
[13] then know for sure:
The LORD your God will no longer drive these nations out of your sight.
Instead, they will become a snare and a trap for you,
a whip on your sides and thorns in your eyes,
until you disappear from this good land that the LORD your God has given you.
[14] Look, today I am going the way of all the earth.
You know with all your heart and with all your soul
that not one of all the good things has failed
that the LORD your God said about you.
All of them have happened to you.
Not one of them has failed.
[15] But just as all the good things that the LORD your God promised you have come to you,
so the LORD will also bring all the bad things on you,
until he has destroyed you from this good land that the LORD your God has given you,
[16] if you break the covenant of the LORD your God, which he commanded you,
and go and serve other gods and bow down to them.
Then the anger of the LORD will burn against you,
and you will quickly disappear from the good land that he has given you.”

> **What does this mean?**
> “Going the way of all the earth” means: dying. Joshua knows that his life is coming to an end.
> Like Moses, Joshua warns them: God’s promises can be relied on, the good ones and the warnings too. If Israel worships other gods, it will lose the land.
> The warning about marriages is not about hatred of foreigners but about faith. The danger was that through marriage people would also take on the foreign gods. That is exactly what happened later with King Solomon (1 Kings 11:1–8).
> The book of Judges then tells how this warning sadly came true.
