# Joshua – Chapter 3
### Crossing the Jordan

---

## Follow the ark of the covenant (Verses 1–6)

[1] Joshua got up early in the morning.
They set out from Shittim and came to the Jordan, he and all the people of Israel.
They camped there before they crossed over.
[2] After three days, the officers went through the middle of the camp
[3] and commanded the people:
“When you see the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God,
and the Levitical priests carrying it,
then leave your place and follow it.
[4] But keep a space of about 3,000 feet between you and it.
Don’t come closer to it.
Then you will know which way to go.
For you have never gone this way before.”
[5] Joshua said to the people,
“Make yourselves holy, for tomorrow the LORD will do wonders among you.”
[6] Joshua said to the priests,
“Pick up the ark of the covenant and cross over in front of the people.”
So they picked up the ark of the covenant and went in front of the people.

> **What does this mean?**
> The Bible says: 2,000 cubits. A cubit is about 18 inches.
> The ark of the covenant was the sign that God was present. It goes first. The people follow it. Faith is like that too: God goes first, and we follow.
> The large space has two reasons: out of deep respect for God’s holiness, and so that everyone can see the ark, even those at the very back.
> “You have never gone this way before”: Before a new stage of life, this sentence can be a real comfort. We do not know the way, but God knows it.
> “Make yourselves holy” means: Get ready to meet God, for example by washing and praying.

---

## God is with Joshua (Verses 7–13)

[7] The LORD said to Joshua,
“Today I will begin to make you great in the eyes of all Israel,
so that they may know that as I was with Moses, so I will be with you.
[8] Command the priests who carry the ark of the covenant:
‘When you come to the edge of the water of the Jordan,
stand still in the Jordan.’”
[9] Joshua said to the people of Israel,
“Come here and listen to the words of the LORD your God.”
[10] Joshua said,
“This is how you will know that the living God is among you,
and that he will surely drive out the Canaanite, the Hittite, the Hivite, the Perizzite,
the Girgashite, the Amorite, and the Jebusite in front of you:
[11] Look, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth is going ahead of you into the Jordan.
[12] So now take 12 men from the tribes of Israel, a man from each tribe.
[13] When the soles of the feet of the priests who carry the ark of the LORD, the Lord of all the earth,
rest in the water of the Jordan,
the water of the Jordan will be cut off.
The water that flows down from above will stand up in one heap.”

> **What does this mean?**
> “The Lord of all the earth”: God is not only the God of Israel. The whole world belongs to him. That is why he can command the river too.
> The priests must put their feet into the water first. Only then does the miracle happen. Sometimes faith means: taking the first step before you see how it will end.

---

## The water stands still (Verses 14–17)

[14] The people set out from their tents to cross the Jordan,
and the priests who carried the ark of the covenant went in front of the people.
[15] The men carrying the ark came to the Jordan,
and the feet of the priests who carried the ark dipped into the edge of the water.
(The Jordan overflows all its banks during the whole time of harvest.)
[16] Then the water that flowed down from above stood still.
It rose up in one heap very far away, at Adam, the city beside Zarethan.
And the water that flowed down toward the sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, was completely cut off.
So the people crossed over near Jericho.
[17] The priests who carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD
stood firm on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan.
And all Israel crossed over on dry ground,
until the whole nation had completely crossed the Jordan.

> **What does this mean?**
> In spring, at harvest time, the Jordan carries a lot of water from the snow of Mount Hermon. It overflows its banks. Exactly then it seems impossible to get across.
> But God holds the water back. This is like the crossing of the sea when the people left Egypt (Exodus 14). The new generation experiences the same miracle as their parents. At the beginning and at the end of the journey through the wilderness there is a way through the water.
> The city of Adam was about 19 miles upstream. There are reports that in later times landslides there blocked the Jordan for hours, for example in the year 1927 during an earthquake. The Bible does not say whether God used such an event here or whether it happened in a completely different way. It says: It happened at exactly the moment the priests stepped into the water, and it was God’s deed.
> Christians think of baptism when they think of this river: Later, Jesus was baptized in this very river, the Jordan (Mark 1:9).
