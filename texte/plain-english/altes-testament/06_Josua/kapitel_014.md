# Joshua – Chapter 14
### Caleb receives Hebron

---

## The land is divided by lot (Verses 1–5)

[1] These are the inheritances that the children of Israel received in the land of Canaan.
Eleazar the priest, Joshua the son of Nun,
and the heads of the families of the tribes of the children of Israel gave them out to them,
[2] by the lot of their inheritance,
as the LORD had commanded through Moses,
for the nine tribes and for the half-tribe.
[3] For Moses had given the inheritance of the two tribes and the half-tribe beyond the Jordan.
But to the Levites he gave no inheritance among them.
[4] For the children of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim.
They gave the Levites no share in the land,
except cities to live in, with their pasture lands for their livestock and for their property.
[5] The children of Israel did as the LORD had commanded Moses, and they divided the land.

> **What does this mean?**
> The land is divided by lot. That way no one could say that they had been treated unfairly. People believed: God himself decides through the lot.
> There are still twelve tribes with land: Levi gets no area of its own, but Joseph counts twice, as Ephraim and Manasseh. So the number works out again.

---

## Caleb reminds Joshua of an old promise (Verses 6–12)

[6] Then the children of Judah came to Joshua in Gilgal.
Caleb, the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite, said to him,
“You know what the LORD said to Moses, the man of God, in Kadesh Barnea,
about me and about you.
[7] I was forty years old when Moses, the servant of the LORD, sent me from Kadesh Barnea
to spy out the land.
I brought him back a report as it was in my heart.
[8] But my brothers who went up with me made the hearts of the people melt with fear.
But I followed the LORD my God completely.
[9] On that day Moses swore,
‘Surely the land where your foot has walked will be an inheritance for you and for your children forever,
because you have followed the LORD my God completely.’
[10] Now, look, the LORD has kept me alive, as he said,
these 45 years, since the time the LORD spoke this word to Moses,
while Israel walked in the wilderness.
And now, look, I am 85 years old today.
[11] I am still as strong today as I was on the day Moses sent me.
As my strength was then, so is my strength now, for war, and for going out and coming in.
[12] So now give me this hill country that the LORD spoke about on that day.
For you heard on that day that the Anakim are there, with great, walled cities.
Maybe the LORD will be with me, and I will drive them out, as the LORD said.”

> **What does this mean?**
> Caleb was one of the two faithful spies (Numbers 13–14). Joshua was the other one. The two old men have known each other for 45 years.
> Caleb is 85 years old. But he says: I am still just as strong as back then. And he does not ask for a comfortable, safe piece of land. He wants the hardest area: the hill country where the “giants” live, the ones everyone was afraid of.
> “Maybe the LORD will be with me”: Caleb is not proud. He trusts God, not himself.
> The numbers help us count: Caleb was 40 when he explored the land. After that, the people were in the wilderness for 38 more years. Now he is 85. So the conquest of the land took about seven years.
> Caleb was a “Kenizzite.” The Kenizzites were originally a people who did not belong to Israel (Genesis 15:19). His family was taken into the tribe of Judah. And it is he who becomes an example of faithfulness.

---

## Joshua gives Hebron to Caleb (Verses 13–15)

[13] Joshua blessed him
and gave Hebron to Caleb, the son of Jephunneh, as his inheritance.
[14] That is why Hebron belongs to Caleb, the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite, as his inheritance to this day,
because he followed the LORD, the God of Israel, completely.
[15] The name of Hebron used to be Kiriath Arba, after the greatest man among the Anakim.
And the land had rest from war.

> **What does this mean?**
> Hebron is a special city. Abraham had lived there, and Abraham, Sarah, Isaac, Rebekah, Jacob, and Leah are buried there (Genesis 23; 49:29–32). This very place becomes the inheritance of faithful Caleb.
> “Kiriath Arba” means “city of Arba.” Arba was the name of a famous man of the Anakim.
> “Followed … completely”: This is said about Caleb again and again. It is the secret of his life.
