# Isaiah – Chapter 12
### A song of thanks

---

## God is my salvation (Verses 1–6)

[1] On that day you will say,
“I thank you, LORD,
for though you were angry with me,
your anger has turned away, and you comfort me.
[2] Look, God is my salvation.
I will trust and not be afraid,
for Yah, the LORD, is my strength and my song,
and he has become my salvation.”
[3] So with joy you will draw water
from the wells of salvation.
[4] On that day you will say,
“Give thanks to the LORD! Call on his name!
Make his deeds known among the peoples!
Announce that his name is lifted up!
[5] Sing to the LORD, for he has done glorious things!
Let this be known in all the earth!
[6] Shout and sing for joy, you who live in Zion,
for great in your midst is the Holy One of Israel!”

> **What does this mean?**
> This short song of thanks closes the first great part of the book (chapters 1–12). After judgment and distress, praise and joy come at the end.
> Verse 1: God’s anger is not the last word. He comforts again.
> Verse 2: “The LORD is my strength and my song”: These words also appear in the song of Moses after crossing the sea (Exodus 15:2).
> Verse 3: “Draw water from the wells of salvation”: A beautiful picture of the joy over God’s help. In earlier times, water was drawn at the Jewish Festival of Booths. To this day, people in Israel sing a cheerful song with this verse: “U-sh’avtem mayim be-sason.”
> Verse 6: The most beautiful thing: The holy God lives in the midst of his people.
