# Isaiah – Chapter 24
### The earth staggers

---

## The whole earth will be ruined (Verses 1–13)

[1] Look, the LORD makes the earth empty and ruins it.
He turns it upside down and scatters its people.
[2] It will be the same for the people as for the priest,
for the servant as for his master,
for the maid as for her mistress,
for the buyer as for the seller,
for the lender as for the borrower,
for the one who takes interest as for the one who pays interest.
[3] The earth will be completely emptied and completely ruined,
for the LORD has spoken this word.
[4] The earth mourns and fades away.
The world withers and fades away.
The high and mighty people of the earth wither.
[5] The earth is also polluted by its people,
for they have broken the laws,
violated the rules,
and broken the everlasting covenant.
[6] So the curse has eaten up the earth,
and those who live on it are found guilty.
So the people of the earth are burned,
and only a few people are left.
[7] The new wine mourns.
The vine withers.
All who were cheerful sigh.
[8] The joy of the tambourines stops.
The noise of those who celebrate ends.
The joy of the harp stops.
[9] They no longer drink wine with a song.
Strong drink is bitter to those who drink it.
[10] The city of confusion is broken down.
Every house is shut, so that no one can go in.
[11] In the streets there is crying because of the wine.
All joy has grown dark.
The happiness of the land is gone.
[12] The city is left in ruins,
and the gate is smashed to pieces.
[13] For it will be like this in the middle of the earth among the peoples,
as when an olive tree is beaten,
as with the leftovers when the grape harvest is over.

> **What does this mean?**
> With chapter 24, a part begins that is often called the “Isaiah Apocalypse” (chapters 24–27). It is no longer about individual peoples, but about the whole world.
> Verse 2: The judgment strikes everyone alike: poor and rich, masters and servants. No one can buy their way out.
> Verse 5: The reason: People have “broken the everlasting covenant.” This reminds us of the covenant with Noah, which is for all people (Genesis 9). When people do wrong, the whole earth suffers with them. Today this can also be applied to the destruction of the environment.
> Verses 7–11: All joy disappears: no more music, no more feasts, no more wine.
> Verse 13: But as at the harvest, a remnant is left.

## Praise from the end of the earth (Verses 14–16)

[14] These will raise their voices.
They will shout for joy over the majesty of the LORD.
They cry out loud from the sea.
[15] So honor the LORD in the east,
the name of the LORD, the God of Israel, in the islands of the sea!
[16] From the farthest end of the earth we have heard songs:
“Glory to the Righteous One!”
But I said, “I waste away! I waste away! How terrible for me!”
The unfaithful act unfaithfully.
Yes, the unfaithful act very unfaithfully.

> **What does this mean?**
> In the middle of judgment, songs of praise are heard, from east to west, from the islands, from the end of the earth. The remnant that survived praises God.
> Verse 16: But the prophet cannot rejoice with them yet. He still sees too much unfaithfulness and injustice.

---

## Terror, pit, and trap (Verses 17–23)

[17] Terror, the pit, and the trap
are on you, people of the earth.
[18] It will happen: Whoever flees from the sound of terror
will fall into the pit,
and whoever climbs up out of the pit
will be caught in the trap,
for the windows on high are opened,
and the foundations of the earth tremble.
[19] The earth is completely broken.
The earth is torn apart.
The earth is shaken violently.
[20] The earth will stagger like a drunk man
and sway back and forth like a hammock.
Its disobedience weighs heavily on it,
and it will fall and not rise again.
[21] On that day it will happen
that the LORD will punish the army of the heights in the heights
and the kings of the earth on the earth.
[22] They will be gathered together,
as prisoners are gathered in a pit,
and they will be locked up in prison,
and after many days they will be punished.
[23] Then the moon will be put to shame and the sun disgraced,
for the LORD of Armies will reign as king
on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem,
and glory will be before his elders.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 17–18: There is no escape: Whoever escapes one disaster falls into the next. In Hebrew, the three words sound alike: “pachad, pachat, pach” (terror, pit, trap).
> Verse 18: “The windows on high are opened”: as in the great flood (Genesis 7:11).
> Verse 20: The earth sways like a drunk man, like a hut in a storm.
> Verse 21: God judges not only the kings on earth, but also the powers “in the heights,” that is, invisible powers that stand against God.
> Verse 23: In the end, God himself rules as king. His light is so bright that the sun and moon fade beside it (compare Revelation 21:23).
