# Isaiah – Chapter 65
### A new heaven and a new earth

---

## I have held out my hands (Verses 1–7)

[1] “I let myself be sought by those who did not ask for me.
I let myself be found by those who did not seek me.
I said, ‘See me, see me!’
to a nation that was not called by my name.
[2] I have held out my hands all day long
to a rebellious people,
who walk on a way that is not good,
following their own thoughts,
[3] a people who constantly provoke me to my face,
who sacrifice in gardens
and burn incense on bricks,
[4] who sit among the graves
and spend the nights in secret places,
who eat pork,
and the broth of disgusting things is in their pots,
[5] who say, ‘Keep to yourself, don’t come near me,
for I am holier than you!’
These are smoke in my nose,
a fire that burns all day long.
[6] Look, it is written before me:
I will not keep silent, but I will repay,
yes, I will repay them in full,
[7] your own wrongdoings and the wrongdoings of your ancestors together,”
says the LORD,
“who burned incense on the mountains
and insulted me on the hills.
So I will first measure out what they have done into their laps.”

> **What does this mean?**
> Verses 1–2: God answers the prayer of chapters 63–64: But I was always there! I called out: “See me, see me!” All day long I held out my hands, like parents who want to take their child in their arms. But the people did not listen.
> Verses 3–4: Some among the people practiced foreign worship: sacrifices in sacred gardens, calling up the dead at graves, forbidden foods.
> Verse 5: And on top of that, they consider themselves holier than others: “Don’t come near me!” God says: This arrogance is like smoke in my nose.
> Paul applies verses 1–2 to people from the nations who found God without seeking him (Romans 10:20–21).

---

## My servants and those who turn away (Verses 8–16)

[8] This is what the LORD says:
“As when new wine is found in a cluster of grapes, and someone says,
‘Don’t destroy it, for there is a blessing in it,’
so I will act for the sake of my servants,
so that I do not destroy them all.
[9] I will bring descendants out of Jacob
and out of Judah an heir to my mountains.
My chosen ones will inherit it,
and my servants will live there.
[10] Sharon will become a pasture for flocks
and the Valley of Achor a resting place for cattle,
for my people who have sought me.
[11] But you who abandon the LORD,
who forget my holy mountain,
who set a table for Fortune
and fill cups of mixed wine for Destiny:
[12] I will destine you for the sword,
and you will all bow down to be slaughtered.
For when I called, you did not answer.
When I spoke, you did not listen,
but you did what is evil in my eyes
and chose what I do not delight in.”
[13] Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says:
“Look, my servants will eat,
but you will be hungry.
Look, my servants will drink,
but you will be thirsty.
Look, my servants will rejoice,
but you will be disappointed.
[14] Look, my servants will sing for joy of heart,
but you will cry out with sorrow of heart
and wail in anguish of spirit.
[15] You will leave your name to my chosen ones as a curse,
and the Lord GOD will kill you.
But he will call his servants by another name,
[16] so that whoever blesses himself on the earth
will bless himself by the God of truth,
and whoever swears an oath on the earth
will swear by the God of truth.
For the troubles of the past are forgotten
and hidden from my eyes.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 8: A picture from winegrowing: If there is still some good juice in a bad cluster of grapes, you don’t throw it away. In the same way, God will not destroy the whole people, for the sake of the faithful.
> Verse 10: Sharon in the west and the Valley of Achor in the east will become fertile pasture again.
> Verse 11: “Fortune” (Gad) and “Destiny” (Meni) were names of gods to whom people offered sacrifices in order to have good luck. Today we might think of superstition or gambling. If you have problems with gambling, you can find help. In the US, call or text 988. In the UK and Ireland, call Samaritans at 116 123. Elsewhere, find help at findahelpline.com. For addiction, US: SAMHSA 1-800-662-4357.
> Verses 12–15: The ways part: The faithful eat, drink and rejoice; those who turn away go empty. This is a serious warning, not a task for people to judge or punish others. The judgment belongs to God.
> Verse 16: God is called the “God of truth,” literally “God of Amen.” What he says holds true.

## Everything will be new (Verses 17–25)

[17] “For look, I am creating new heavens and a new earth,
and the former things will not be remembered
and will not come to mind.
[18] But be glad and rejoice forever
in what I am creating.
For look, I am creating Jerusalem to be a delight
and her people to be a joy.
[19] I will rejoice over Jerusalem
and be glad in my people.
The sound of weeping will no longer be heard in her,
nor the sound of crying.
[20] There will no longer be a baby there who lives only a few days,
nor an old person who does not live out his days.
For a child will die at 100 years old,
and the sinner who is 100 years old will be cursed.
[21] They will build houses and live in them.
They will plant vineyards and eat their fruit.
[22] They will not build for someone else to live in.
They will not plant for someone else to eat.
For the days of my people will be like the days of a tree,
and my chosen ones will long enjoy the work of their hands.
[23] They will not work hard for nothing,
or give birth to children only for disaster.
For they are the descendants of those the LORD has blessed,
and their children with them.
[24] And this is how it will be: Before they call, I will answer.
While they are still speaking, I will hear.
[25] The wolf and the lamb will graze together.
The lion will eat straw like the ox.
And dust will be the serpent’s food.
They will do no harm and cause no destruction
on all my holy mountain,” says the LORD.

> **What does this mean?**
> This is one of the greatest hopes in the Bible: God creates new heavens and a new earth.
> Verse 19: No more weeping and no more crying. The book of Revelation takes this up: “He will wipe away every tear from their eyes” (Revelation 21:4).
> Verse 20: No baby dies young anymore. All people live long lives. Whoever dies at 100 still counts as young. For all parents who have lost a child, this is a comforting hope.
> Verses 21–23: No more exploitation: Whoever builds a house also lives in it. Whoever plants also eats the fruit. No one works for nothing.
> Verse 24: God answers even before people call.
> Verse 25: As in chapter 11:6–9: Wolf and lamb live together in peace. No one does evil anymore. A picture of perfect peace in all creation.
