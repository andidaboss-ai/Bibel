# 2 Chronicles – Chapter 5
### The ark comes into the temple

---

## The temple is finished (Verses 1–6)

[1] So all the work that Solomon did for the LORD’s house was finished.
Solomon brought in the things that David his father had dedicated:
the silver, the gold, and all the objects.
He put them in the treasuries of God’s house.
[2] Then Solomon gathered the elders of Israel
and all the heads of the tribes,
the princes of the families of the children of Israel,
to Jerusalem,
to bring up the ark of the LORD’s covenant out of David’s city, which is Zion.
[3] So all the men of Israel gathered to the king at the feast in the seventh month.
[4] All the elders of Israel came.
And the Levites lifted up the ark.
[5] They brought up the ark,
the Tent of Meeting, and all the holy objects that were in the Tent.
The Levitical priests brought these up.
[6] King Solomon and all the congregation of Israel who had gathered to him
were before the ark.
They sacrificed sheep and cattle, so many that they could not be counted or numbered.

> **What does this mean?**
> The story is told almost the same way in 1 Kings 8.
> The feast in the seventh month is the Feast of Tabernacles (Booths) in the fall.
> This time the Levites carry the ark the right way, on their shoulders (compare 1 Chronicles 15). Chronicles stresses this: here it is “the Levites”, in 1 Kings 8:3 “the priests”.
> “The Levitical priests”: this probably means the priests from the tribe of Levi. Some manuscripts have “the priests and the Levites”.

---

## The ark in the most holy place (Verses 7–10)

[7] The priests brought the ark of the LORD’s covenant to its place,
into the inner room of the house, to the most holy place,
under the wings of the cherubim.
[8] For the cherubim spread out their wings over the place of the ark,
and the cherubim covered the ark and its poles from above.
[9] The poles were so long
that the ends of the poles could be seen from the ark in front of the inner room.
But they could not be seen outside.
And it is there to this day.
[10] There was nothing in the ark except the two tablets
that Moses put there at Horeb,
when the LORD made a covenant with the children of Israel,
when they came out of Egypt.

> **What does this mean?**
> Only the two stone tablets with the Ten Commandments lay in the ark. At the heart of the temple is God’s word.
> “To this day”: this sentence comes from the source that Chronicles used (1 Kings 8:8). At the time of Chronicles the ark had not existed for a long time. It was lost when Jerusalem was destroyed in 587 before Christ.

---

## God’s glory fills the house (Verses 11–14)

[11] The priests came out of the holy place.
All the priests who were there had made themselves holy,
and they didn’t keep to their divisions.
[12] Also the Levites who were the singers, all of them,
Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, and their sons and their brothers,
dressed in fine linen,
with cymbals, stringed instruments, and harps,
stood at the east end of the altar,
and with them 120 priests blowing trumpets.
[13] The trumpeters and singers were as one,
to make one sound to be heard in praising and thanking the LORD.
They lifted up their voice with the trumpets and cymbals and instruments of music,
and praised the LORD:
“For he is good, for his loving kindness endures forever!”
Then the house was filled with a cloud, the LORD’s house,
[14] so that the priests could not stand to do their service because of the cloud.
For the LORD’s glory filled God’s house.

> **What does this mean?**
> This is the high point: God himself comes into his house. The cloud is the sign of his presence.
> Chronicles adds something that is missing in 1 Kings 8: the great music. All the priests, 120 trumpeters, all the singers sing “as one”, with “one sound”. And just at the moment when they sing “For he is good, for his loving kindness endures forever”, God’s glory comes. Praising together opens the space for God’s presence.
> All the priests were there, no matter which division they belonged to. On this special day they all served together.
