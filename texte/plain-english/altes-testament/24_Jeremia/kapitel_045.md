# Jeremiah – Chapter 45
### A word for Baruch

---

## Are you looking for great things? (Verses 1–5)

[1] This is the message that the prophet Jeremiah spoke to Baruch, the son of Neriah.
Baruch was writing these words in a book.
Jeremiah told him what to write.
This was in the fourth year of Jehoiakim, the son of Josiah, king of Judah.
Jeremiah said,
[2] “Baruch, the LORD, the God of Israel, says this to you:
[3] ‘You said, “How terrible for me!
The LORD has added sorrow to my pain!
I am worn out from groaning.
I find no rest.”’
[4] “Tell him: This is what the LORD says:
‘Look, I will tear down what I have built.
I will pull up what I have planted.
I will do this in the whole land.
[5] Are you looking for great things for yourself?
Do not look for them!
Look, I will bring disaster on all people,’ says the LORD.
‘But wherever you go, I will let you escape with your life.’”

> **What does this mean?**
> This short chapter is a personal word to Baruch, Jeremiah’s faithful scribe. It comes from the time when Baruch wrote the first scroll (Chapter 36, in the year 605 BC).
> Verse 3: Baruch is exhausted: “How terrible for me! … I am worn out from groaning. I find no rest.” The prophets’ helpers also suffered under the heavy message. Anyone who helps others can become tired and exhausted themselves.
> Verse 4: God answers: It hurts me too. I have to tear down what I myself have built.
> Verse 5: “Are you looking for great things for yourself? Do not look for them!” Perhaps Baruch had hoped for respect or a career. God says: In this hard time, that is not important. But I promise you: You will survive, wherever you go.
> A comforting word for all who feel exhausted: Great successes are not what counts, but that God protects your life. If you feel burned out, you may get help. In the US, call or text 988. In the UK and Ireland, call Samaritans at 116 123. Elsewhere, find help at findahelpline.com.
