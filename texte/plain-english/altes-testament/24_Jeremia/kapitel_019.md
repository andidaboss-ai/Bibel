# Jeremiah – Chapter 19
### The broken jar

---

## A message in the valley of the son of Hinnom (Verses 1–9)

[1] The LORD said,
“Go and buy a clay jar from a potter.
Take some of the elders of the people
and some of the elders of the priests with you.
[2] Go out to the valley of the son of Hinnom,
which is by the entrance of the Harsith Gate,
and call out there the words that I will tell you.
[3] Say, ‘Hear the LORD’s word, kings of Judah and people of Jerusalem:
The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says,
“Look, I will bring such a disaster on this place
that the ears of everyone who hears about it will ring.
[4] For they have left me,
and have made this place unclean.
They have burned incense in it to other gods that they didn’t know:
they, their ancestors, and the kings of Judah.

They have filled this place with the blood of innocent people.
[5] They have built the high places of Baal,
to burn their children in the fire as burnt offerings to Baal.
I didn’t command it, and I didn’t speak of it,
and it didn’t even come into my mind.
[6] So look, the days are coming,” says the LORD,
“when this place will no longer be called ‘Topheth,’
or ‘The valley of the son of Hinnom,’
but ‘The valley of Slaughter.’
[7] “‘“In this place I will make the plans of Judah and Jerusalem empty.
I will make them fall by the sword in front of their enemies,
and by the hand of those who want to kill them.
I will give their dead bodies as food
to the birds of the sky and to the animals of the earth.
[8] I will make this city a horror,
something people hiss at.
Everyone who passes by it will be horrified,
and hiss because of all its wounds.
[9] I will make them eat the flesh of their sons
and the flesh of their daughters.
Each one will eat the flesh of his friend,
during the siege and in the distress
that their enemies and those who want to kill them will bring on them.”’

> **What does this mean?**
> Jeremiah goes with the elders into the valley of the son of Hinnom. Children were sacrificed there (compare 7:31). God says very clearly again: I never wanted that, “it didn’t even come into my mind.” Killing or tormenting children is a terrible crime.
> Verse 3: The news will be so terrible that people’s “ears will ring.”
> Verse 9: A dreadful picture: In a long siege, hunger becomes so great that people in their despair even eat human flesh. Such things really did happen in sieges (compare Lamentations 4:10). Jeremiah does not describe this because God wants it, but to show where the people’s path leads.

## The jar is broken (Verses 10–15)

[10] “Then break the jar
in front of the men who go with you,
[11] and tell them, ‘The LORD of Armies says:
“In the same way I will break this people and this city,
as someone breaks a potter’s jar,
which can’t be made whole again.
They will bury the dead in Topheth,
until there is no room left to bury.
[12] This is what I will do to this place,” says the LORD,
“and to the people who live in it.
I will make this city like Topheth.
[13] The houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings of Judah
will be unclean like the place of Topheth:
all the houses on whose roofs they have burned incense to all the stars of the sky,
and have poured out drink offerings to other gods.”’”
[14] Then Jeremiah came back from Topheth,
where the LORD had sent him to prophesy.
He stood in the courtyard of the LORD’s house,
and said to all the people:
[15] “The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says,
‘Look, I will bring on this city and on all its towns
all the disaster that I have announced against it,
because they have made their necks stiff,
so that they will not hear my words.’”

> **What does this mean?**
> Again an acted-out sign: Jeremiah breaks a clay jar in front of the elders.
> In the chapter before (Chapter 18), the clay was still soft, and the potter could shape it again. Now the jar has been fired and is hard. When it breaks, it cannot be made whole again. This shows: The people have refused to listen for so long that the judgment is now unavoidable.
> Verse 13: On the flat roofs of the houses, people worshiped the stars as gods.
> Verses 14–15: Jeremiah repeats the message in the temple in front of all the people. This has consequences, as the next chapter shows.
