# Jeremiah – Chapter 35
### The faithful Rechabites

---

## The Rechabites drink no wine (Verses 1–11)

[1] This is the word which came to Jeremiah from the LORD
in the days of Jehoiakim, the son of Josiah, king of Judah:
[2] “Go to the family of the Rechabites, and speak to them.
Bring them into the LORD’s house, into one of the rooms,
and give them wine to drink.”
[3] Then I took Jaazaniah, the son of Jeremiah, the son of Habazziniah,
with his brothers, all his sons,
and the whole family of the Rechabites.
[4] I brought them into the LORD’s house,
into the room of the sons of Hanan, the son of Igdaliah, the man of God.
It was next to the room of the princes,
above the room of Maaseiah, the son of Shallum, the doorkeeper.
[5] I set bowls full of wine and cups in front of the men of the Rechabite family,
and I said to them, “Drink wine!”
[6] But they said, “We will drink no wine.
For Jonadab, the son of Rechab, our ancestor, commanded us:
‘You must never drink wine,
neither you nor your children, forever.
[7] You must not build a house, sow seed, or plant a vineyard,
or own any of these.
Instead, you must live in tents all your life,
so that you may live many days in the land where you live as nomads.’
[8] We have obeyed the voice of Jonadab, the son of Rechab, our ancestor,
in everything he commanded us.
We drink no wine all our lives,
we, our wives, our sons, and our daughters.
[9] We don’t build houses to live in.
We have no vineyard, field, or seed.
[10] We have lived in tents,
and have obeyed, and done everything that our ancestor Jonadab commanded us.
[11] But when Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, came up into the land, we said,
‘Come! Let’s go to Jerusalem,
because we are afraid of the army of the Chaldeans
and of the army of the Syrians.’
That is why we are living in Jerusalem.”

> **What does this mean?**
> The Rechabites were a group who lived by the rules of their ancestor Jonadab (he is mentioned in 2 Kings 10:15–23, about 250 years earlier). They drank no wine, built no houses and lived in tents like nomads. That was how they wanted to live simply and faithfully to God, without tying themselves to the wealth of the land.
> Verses 5–6: Jeremiah puts them to the test: He offers them wine in the temple. But they refuse. They stay faithful to their promise, even after so many generations.
> Verse 11: They moved into the city only because of the war, out of need, not out of unfaithfulness.

## An example for the people (Verses 12–19)

[12] Then the LORD’s word came to Jeremiah. He said,
[13] “The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says:
‘Go and tell the men of Judah and the people of Jerusalem,
“Won’t you accept correction and listen to my words?” says the LORD.
[14] “The words of Jonadab, the son of Rechab, have been kept.
He commanded his sons not to drink wine,
and to this day they drink none,
for they obey their ancestor’s command.
But I have spoken to you early and tirelessly,
and you have not listened to me.
[15] I have also sent you all my servants the prophets,
early and tirelessly.
They said, ‘Each of you, turn back now from your evil way,
change your deeds,
and don’t follow other gods to serve them.
Then you will live in the land which I have given to you and to your fathers.’
But you have not paid attention or listened to me.
[16] The sons of Jonadab, the son of Rechab, have kept the command
that their ancestor gave them,
but this people has not listened to me.”’
[17] “That is why the LORD, the God of Armies, the God of Israel, says:
‘Look, I will bring on Judah and on all the people of Jerusalem
all the disaster that I have announced against them,
because I have spoken to them, but they have not listened,
and I have called to them, but they have not answered.’”
[18] Jeremiah said to the family of the Rechabites,
“The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says:
‘You have obeyed the command of your ancestor Jonadab,
and kept all his rules,
and done everything that he commanded you.’
[19] That is why the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says:
‘Jonadab, the son of Rechab, will never be without a man
to stand in front of me.’”

> **What does this mean?**
> God uses the Rechabites as an example: For generations they have faithfully kept the command of a human being, their ancestor. But God’s people do not even listen to God himself, although he has called them again and again.
> Verse 19: The Rechabites receive a promise: Their family will never die out and will always stand before God. Faithfulness is rewarded.
> The message is not that all people should drink no wine or live in tents. It is about being faithful to what you have promised.
