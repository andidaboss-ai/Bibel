# Exodus – Chapter 37
### The ark, the table, the lamp stand and the altar of incense

---

## The ark of the covenant (Verses 1–5)

[1] Bezalel made the ark of acacia wood.
It was about 3 feet 9 inches long, about 2 feet 3 inches wide and about 2 feet 3 inches high.
[2] He covered it with pure gold, inside and outside.
And he made a gold molding all around it.
[3] He cast four gold rings for it and put them on its four feet:
two rings on one side and two rings on the other side.
[4] He made poles of acacia wood and covered them with gold.
[5] He put the poles through the rings on the sides of the ark, so that the ark could be carried.

> **What does this mean?**
> In cubits: two and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide and high. A cubit is about 18 inches.
> Bezalel makes the ark of the covenant himself. It is the most important piece in the whole sanctuary. You can read more about it in the explanation for Exodus 25.

---

## The cover with the cherubim (Verses 6–9)

[6] He made a mercy seat of pure gold.
It is the cover of the ark.
It was about 3 feet 9 inches long and about 2 feet 3 inches wide.
[7] He made two cherubim of gold.
He made them of hammered gold, at the two ends of the mercy seat:
[8] one cherub at one end and one cherub at the other end.
He made the cherubim one piece with the mercy seat, at its two ends.
[9] The cherubim spread out their wings upward
and covered the mercy seat with their wings.
Their faces were turned toward each other.
The faces of the cherubim looked toward the mercy seat.

> **What does this mean?**
> Cherubim are heavenly beings with wings. They guard the place where God is present.
> “Hammered gold” means: The gold is not poured into a mold. It is beaten into shape with a hammer.

---

## The table (Verses 10–16)

[10] He made the table of acacia wood.
It was about 3 feet long, about 18 inches wide and about 2 feet 3 inches high.
[11] He covered it with pure gold and made a gold molding all around it.
[12] He made a border all around it, about 3 inches wide.
And he made a gold molding all around this border.
[13] He cast four gold rings for the table.
He put the rings at the four corners, on its four legs.
[14] The rings were close to the border.
The poles for carrying the table went through them.
[15] He made the poles of acacia wood and covered them with gold, to carry the table.
[16] He also made the things that belonged on the table:
its dishes, its spoons, its bowls and its pitchers for pouring.
He made them of pure gold.

> **What does this mean?**
> In cubits: two cubits long, one cubit wide, one and a half cubits high. A hand’s width is about 3 inches.
> The bread of the presence lay on this table: 12 loaves for the 12 tribes.

---

## The golden lamp stand (Verses 17–24)

[17] He made the lamp stand of pure gold.
He made the lamp stand of hammered gold.
Its base, its shaft, its cups, its buds and its flowers were one piece with it.
[18] Six branches went out from its sides:
three branches on one side and three branches on the other side.
[19] On one branch there were three cups shaped like almond blossoms, each with a bud and a flower.
And on the next branch there were also three cups shaped like almond blossoms, each with a bud and a flower.
It was like this for all six branches that went out from the lamp stand.
[20] On the lamp stand itself there were four cups shaped like almond blossoms, with their buds and their flowers.
[21] There was a bud under each pair of branches, one piece with the lamp stand:
a bud under the first two branches,
a bud under the next two branches
and a bud under the last two branches.
It was like this for the six branches that went out from it.
[22] The buds and the branches were one piece with it.
The whole thing was one piece of hammered, pure gold.
[23] He made its seven lamps,
and its snuffers and its snuff dishes, of pure gold.
[24] He made the lamp stand and all its tools from a talent of pure gold (about 75 pounds).

> **What does this mean?**
> A talent is about 75 pounds. That much gold went into the lamp stand and its tools.
> The lamp stand with seven branches is called the “menorah” in Hebrew. To this day, it is an important sign of Judaism. It is on the coat of arms of the State of Israel.

---

## The altar of incense, the anointing oil and the incense (Verses 25–29)

[25] He made the altar of incense of acacia wood.
It was square: about 18 inches long and about 18 inches wide.
And it was about 3 feet high.
Its horns were one piece with it.
[26] He covered it with pure gold:
its top, its sides all around and its horns.
And he made a gold molding all around it.
[27] He made two gold rings for it under its molding,
on its two sides, across from each other.
They held the poles for carrying it.
[28] He made the poles of acacia wood and covered them with gold.

[29] He also made the holy anointing oil
and the pure, sweet-smelling incense,
with the skill of a perfume maker.

> **What does this mean?**
> In cubits: one cubit long and wide, two cubits high.
> Now all the objects for the inside of the sanctuary are finished: the ark of the covenant in the Most Holy Place, and the table, the lamp stand and the altar of incense in the Holy Place.
> What was commanded in Chapters 25 and 30 is now done, piece by piece.
