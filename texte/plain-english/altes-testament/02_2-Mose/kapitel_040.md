# Exodus – Chapter 40
### The sanctuary is set up – God’s glory fills it

---

## God’s instructions for setting it up (Verses 1–15)

[1] The LORD spoke to Moses.
He said,
[2] “On the first day of the first month, set up the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting.
[3] Put the ark of the covenant in it.
And hide the ark behind the veil.
[4] Bring in the table and set out in order the things that belong on it.
Bring in the lamp stand and light its lamps.
[5] Put the golden altar for incense in front of the ark of the covenant.
And hang the screen at the door of the tabernacle.

[6] “Put the altar of burnt offering in front of the door of the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting.
[7] Put the basin between the Tent of Meeting and the altar.
And put water in it.
[8] Set up the courtyard all around it.
And hang the screen at the gate of the courtyard.

[9] “Take the anointing oil.
Anoint the tabernacle and everything in it.
Make it holy, with all its furniture. Then it will be holy.
[10] Anoint the altar of burnt offering with all its tools.
Make the altar holy. Then the altar will be most holy.
[11] Anoint the basin and its base, and make it holy.

[12] “Bring Aaron and his sons to the door of the Tent of Meeting.
And wash them with water.
[13] Put the holy clothes on Aaron.
Anoint him and make him holy, so that he can serve me as a priest.
[14] Bring his sons and put tunics on them.
[15] Anoint them, just as you anointed their father, so that they can serve me as priests.
Their anointing shall make them priests forever, through all their generations.”

> **What does this mean?**
> The “first day of the first month” is New Year’s Day, almost exactly one year after leaving Egypt. With the sanctuary, a new year and a new part of the story begin.
> The consecration of the priests that is commanded here is told in detail in Leviticus 8.

---

## Moses sets up the sanctuary (Verses 16–33)

[16] Moses did this.
He did everything exactly as the LORD had commanded him.
[17] In the first month of the second year, on the first day of the month, the tabernacle was set up.
[18] Moses set up the tabernacle.
He laid its sockets, set up its boards, put in its bars and set up its pillars.
[19] He spread the covering over the tent.
And he put the roof of the tabernacle on top of it,
as the LORD commanded Moses.

[20] He took the covenant and put it into the ark.
He put the poles on the ark.
And he put the mercy seat on top of the ark.
[21] He brought the ark into the tabernacle.
He hung up the veil as a screen, and so he screened off the ark of the covenant,
as the LORD commanded Moses.

[22] He put the table in the Tent of Meeting, on the north side of the tabernacle, outside the veil.
[23] He set out the bread on it in order, before the LORD,
as the LORD commanded Moses.
[24] He put the lamp stand in the Tent of Meeting, across from the table, on the south side of the tabernacle.
[25] He lit the lamps before the LORD,
as the LORD commanded Moses.
[26] He put the golden altar in the Tent of Meeting, in front of the veil.
[27] And he burned sweet-smelling incense on it,
as the LORD commanded Moses.
[28] He hung up the screen at the door of the tabernacle.

[29] He put the altar of burnt offering at the door of the tabernacle, the Tent of Meeting.
And he offered the burnt offering and the meal offering on it,
as the LORD commanded Moses.
[30] He put the basin between the Tent of Meeting and the altar.
And he put water in it for washing.
[31] Moses, Aaron and his sons washed their hands and their feet in it.
[32] Whenever they went into the Tent of Meeting, and whenever they came near the altar, they washed,
as the LORD commanded Moses.
[33] He set up the courtyard around the tabernacle and the altar.
And he hung up the screen at the gate of the courtyard.
So Moses finished the work.

> **What does this mean?**
> Moses sets everything up from the inside to the outside: first the tabernacle and the ark of the covenant, then the table, the lamp stand and the altar of incense, then the altar and the basin outside, and last of all the courtyard.
> Again it says seven times: “as the LORD commanded Moses.”
> “So Moses finished the work” – this is also a reminder of creation. There it says: “On the seventh day, God had finished the work he had done” (Genesis 2:2).

## God’s glory fills the sanctuary (Verses 34–38)

[34] Then the cloud covered the Tent of Meeting.
And the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.
[35] Moses was not able to go into the Tent of Meeting,
because the cloud stayed on it,
and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.

[36] Whenever the cloud was lifted up from over the tabernacle, the people of Israel moved on.
It was like this on all their journeys.
[37] But if the cloud was not lifted up, then they did not travel,
until the day when it was lifted up.
[38] For the cloud of the LORD was over the tabernacle by day.
And at night there was fire in the cloud.
All the house of Israel could see it, on all their journeys.

> **What does this mean?**
> This is the great goal of the whole book of Exodus. At the beginning, Israel was in Egypt, slaves of Pharaoh, far away from God. At the end, God lives in the middle of his people.
> God has kept his promise: “I will live among the people of Israel” (Exodus 29:45).
> And this after the golden calf! The people had broken the covenant. But God has forgiven them and comes anyway, to live with them.
> God’s glory is so great that not even Moses can go in. The next book, Leviticus, tells how people can still come near to the holy God.
> The cloud shows the people the way through the desert. When it moves on, they move on. When it stays, they stay. God himself leads his people.
> Here Christians also think of Pentecost: Then God’s Spirit came on the people like fire (Acts 2:3). Paul writes: You yourselves are now the temple where God’s Spirit lives (1 Corinthians 3:16).

## Looking back: What have we read in the book of Exodus?

> **What does this mean?**
> The book of Exodus tells three large parts:
> 1. God frees his people from slavery in Egypt (Chapters 1–18).
> 2. At Mount Sinai, God makes a covenant with his people and gives them his commandments (Chapters 19–24).
> 3. God comes to live in the middle of his people. For this, the sanctuary is built (Chapters 25–40). In the middle of it stand the golden calf and God’s forgiveness (Chapters 32–34).
> The message of the book: God sees the suffering of people who are oppressed. He frees them. He gives them good rules for a life in freedom. And he does not want to be far away. He wants to live among people.
