# Exodus – Chapter 24
### The covenant is made

---

## The invitation up the mountain (Verses 1–2)

[1] God said to Moses,
“Come up to the LORD, you and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu,
and 70 of the elders of Israel.
Worship from a distance.
[2] Only Moses is to come near to the LORD.
The others are not to come near.
And the people are not to go up with him.”

> **What does this mean?**
> Nadab and Abihu are Aaron’s oldest sons.
> There are different levels of nearness to God: the people stay below. The leaders come part of the way up. Only Moses is allowed to come very close to God.

---

## The people say yes (Verses 3–8)

[3] Moses came and told the people all the LORD’s words and all the laws.
All the people answered with one voice,
“We will do everything the LORD has said.”
[4] Moses wrote down all the LORD’s words.
Early the next morning he got up.
At the foot of the mountain he built an altar.
He also set up 12 pillars, for the 12 tribes of Israel.
[5] Then he sent young men of the people of Israel.
They offered burnt offerings
and sacrificed cattle as peace offerings to the LORD.

[6] Moses took half of the blood and put it in bowls.
The other half of the blood he sprinkled on the altar.
[7] Then he took the Book of the Covenant and read it aloud to the people.
They said,
“We will do everything the LORD has said, and we will obey.”
[8] Then Moses took the blood and sprinkled it on the people.
He said,
“Look, this is the blood of the covenant
that the LORD has made with you, based on all these words.”

> **What does this mean?**
> Now the covenant between God and Israel is solemnly made.
> First the people hear what God says. Then they say yes – even twice.
> Moses writes everything down. This is how the “Book of the Covenant” comes about (Chapters 20–23).
> The 12 pillars stand for the 12 tribes. The whole people takes part.
> The blood connects both sides: one part goes on the altar (for God), one part on the people. Back then, a covenant was often sealed with blood. That meant: this covenant is a matter of life and death.
> Jesus takes up these words at the Last Supper. He takes the cup and says: “This is my blood of the new covenant” (Mark 14:24). Christians believe: with Jesus a new covenant begins.

---

## They see God (Verses 9–11)

[9] Then Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu and 70 of the elders of Israel went up.
[10] They saw the God of Israel.
Under his feet was something like a floor made of sapphire stone,
as clear as the sky itself.
[11] But God did not lay a hand on the leaders of the people of Israel.
They saw God, and they ate and drank.

> **What does this mean?**
> This is one of the most mysterious passages in the Bible. The leaders see God – and stay alive.
> The Bible does not describe exactly what they see. It only describes what is under his feet: something blue and clear, like a floor of precious stones. A sapphire is a blue precious stone.
> Then they eat and drink. A feast with God. Back then, people celebrated an agreement like this: with a meal together.
> Later it says: “No one can see me and live” (Exodus 33:20). Many understand it like this: they saw only a reflection of God’s glory, not God himself.

---

## Moses goes up into the cloud (Verses 12–18)

[12] The LORD said to Moses,
“Come up to me on the mountain and stay here.
I will give you the stone tablets
with the law and the commandments that I have written,
so that you can teach them to the people.”
[13] So Moses set out with his helper Joshua.
And Moses went up onto the mountain of God.
[14] He said to the elders,
“Wait here for us until we come back to you.
Look, Aaron and Hur are with you.
Anyone who has a quarrel can go to them.”

[15] Moses went up the mountain.
And the cloud covered the mountain.
[16] The glory of the LORD settled on Mount Sinai.
The cloud covered it for six days.
On the seventh day the LORD called to Moses from the middle of the cloud.
[17] To the people of Israel, the glory of the LORD looked like a fire that burns everything up, on the top of the mountain.
[18] Moses went into the middle of the cloud and went up the mountain.
And Moses stayed on the mountain for 40 days and 40 nights.

> **What does this mean?**
> God himself has written the commandments on stone tablets. Stone stands for something that lasts.
> Joshua is allowed to come part of the way. He is being prepared for his later task.
> Six days of waiting, and on the seventh day God calls – as at creation.
> From below, the mountain looks like a fire. And Moses walks right into it. He has great trust in God.
> 40 days and 40 nights – a long time. During this time, God gives Moses the instructions for the holy tent (Chapters 25–31). Down in the camp, the people become impatient. Chapter 32 tells what happens then.
