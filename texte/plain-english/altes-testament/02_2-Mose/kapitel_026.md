# Exodus – Chapter 26
### God’s home: curtains, boards and veil

---

## The inner curtains of linen (Verses 1–6)

[1] “Make the tabernacle with ten curtains.
They shall be made of fine twisted linen and of blue, purple and scarlet cloth.
Cherubim shall be woven into them by a skillful worker.
[2] Each curtain shall be about 42 feet long and about 6 feet wide.
All the curtains shall be the same size.
[3] Five curtains shall be joined to each other.
And the other five curtains shall be joined to each other.
[4] Make loops of blue cloth on the edge of the last curtain of the first set.
And do the same on the edge of the last curtain of the second set.
[5] Make 50 loops on the one curtain,
and make 50 loops on the edge of the curtain of the second set.
The loops shall be exactly opposite each other.
[6] Make 50 clasps of gold.
Join the curtains to each other with the clasps.
So the tabernacle shall be one whole.

> **What does this mean?**
> God’s home is a large tent. It is built in several layers.
> The innermost layer is the most beautiful: colored linen with pictures of cherubim woven into it. Anyone who stands inside sees a sky full of cherubim above them.
> In the Bible, the measurements are given in cubits: 28 cubits long, 4 cubits wide. A cubit is about 18 inches.
> “Twisted” means: Several threads are twisted together to make one strong thread.

---

## The curtains of goats’ hair (Verses 7–13)

[7] “Also make curtains of goats’ hair.
They shall be a covering over the tabernacle.
Make eleven of these curtains.
[8] Each curtain shall be about 45 feet long and about 6 feet wide.
All eleven curtains shall be the same size.
[9] Join five curtains together into one set,
and six curtains into another set.
Fold the sixth curtain double at the front of the tent.

[10] “Make 50 loops on the edge of the last curtain of the first set,
and 50 loops on the edge of the last curtain of the second set.
[11] Make 50 clasps of bronze.
Put the clasps into the loops and join the tent together, so that it becomes one whole.
[12] There is a part of the tent curtains that is left over: the half curtain that remains.
It shall hang down over the back of the tabernacle.
[13] On each side, the tent curtains are about 18 inches longer than needed.
This part shall hang down over the sides of the tabernacle, on this side and on that side, to cover it.

> **What does this mean?**
> In cubits: 30 cubits long, 4 cubits wide. The part that hangs over is one cubit.
> Over the beautiful linen curtains comes a covering of goats’ hair that protects them. Nomads in the desert still use coverings like this for their tents today. They keep out rain and sun.
> This covering is a little larger than the inner one, so that it covers everything well.

---

## The outer coverings (Verse 14)

[14] “Make a covering for the tent of rams’ skins dyed red.
And above that, make a covering of sea cow hides.

> **What does this mean?**
> All together, there are four layers. From the outside, the sanctuary looked very plain, like a simple tent of leather. All the splendor was hidden inside.

---

## The boards (Verses 15–25)

[15] “Make the boards for the tabernacle of acacia wood.
They shall stand upright.
[16] Each board shall be about 15 feet long and about 2 feet 3 inches wide.
[17] Each board shall have two tenons, joined to each other.
Do this for all the boards of the tabernacle.
[18] Make 20 boards for the tabernacle for the south side.
[19] Make 40 sockets of silver under the 20 boards:
two sockets under each board for its two tenons.

[20] “For the second side of the tabernacle, the north side, make 20 boards
[21] and their 40 sockets of silver:
two sockets under each board.
[22] For the back of the tabernacle, toward the west, make six boards.
[23] And make two boards for the corners of the tabernacle at the back.
[24] They shall be double at the bottom, and they shall be joined together at the top, up to the first ring.
It shall be like this for both of them.
They shall form the two corners.
[25] So there shall be eight boards with their sockets of silver, 16 sockets:
two sockets under each board.

> **What does this mean?**
> In cubits: 10 cubits long, one and a half cubits wide.
> Verse 17: A tenon is a peg at the end of a board that fits into a hole.
> The boards form the walls of the sanctuary: 20 on the south side and 20 on the north side, 6 boards and 2 corner boards on the west side. The east side is open. The entrance is there.
> The silver sockets stand on the ground. The tenons of the boards are put into them. So everything stands firm and can still be taken down quickly.
> The sanctuary was about 45 feet long and about 15 feet wide.

---

## The bars (Verses 26–30)

[26] “Make bars of acacia wood:
five for the boards on one side of the tabernacle,
[27] five bars for the boards on the other side of the tabernacle,
and five bars for the boards at the back, toward the west.
[28] The middle bar shall go through the middle of the boards from one end to the other.
[29] Cover the boards with gold.
Make gold rings on them for the bars to go through.
And cover the bars with gold too.
[30] Set up the tabernacle exactly the way it was shown to you on the mountain.

> **What does this mean?**
> The bars hold the boards together, so that the walls are strong.
> Inside, everything was covered with gold. When the light of the lamp stand fell on it, everything shone like gold.

---

## The veil in front of the Most Holy Place (Verses 31–35)

[31] “Make a veil of blue, purple and scarlet cloth and of fine twisted linen.
Cherubim shall be woven into it by a skillful worker.
[32] Hang it on four pillars of acacia wood covered with gold.
Their hooks shall be of gold.
And they shall stand on four sockets of silver.

[33] “Hang up the veil under the clasps.
Bring the ark of the covenant in there, behind the veil.
The veil shall separate the holy place from the most holy place for you.
[34] Put the mercy seat on the ark of the covenant in the most holy place.
[35] Put the table outside the veil.
And put the lamp stand across from the table, on the south side of the tabernacle.
Put the table on the north side.

> **What does this mean?**
> The sanctuary has two rooms: the “Holy Place” and the “Most Holy Place.” A precious veil hangs between them.
> Verse 31: A veil is a large curtain.
> Only the ark of the covenant stands in the Most Holy Place. God is present there in a special way. Only the high priest may go in, once a year (Leviticus 16).
> In the Holy Place in front of it stand the table with the bread, the lamp stand and, later, the altar of incense.
> The New Testament tells: When Jesus died on the cross, the veil in the temple was torn in two from top to bottom (Matthew 27:51). Christians understand it this way: Through Jesus, the way to God is open for everyone.

---

## The screen at the entrance (Verses 36–37)

[36] “Make a screen for the door of the Tent.
Make it of blue, purple and scarlet cloth and of fine twisted linen,
the work of an embroiderer.
[37] Make five pillars of acacia wood for the screen and cover them with gold.
Their hooks shall be of gold.
And cast five sockets of bronze for them.

> **What does this mean?**
> Verse 36: A screen is a hanging curtain. An embroiderer sews colorful patterns into cloth.
> The entrance is on the east side. It also has a colorful curtain, but without cherubim.
> The closer you come to the Most Holy Place, the more precious the materials become: outside bronze, inside silver and gold. So the way it is built shows: The closer to God, the holier.
