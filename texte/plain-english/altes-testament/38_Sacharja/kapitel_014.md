# Zechariah – Chapter 14
### The LORD will be King over the whole earth

---

## The day of the LORD is coming (Verses 1–5)

[1] Look, a day of the LORD is coming,
when the things taken from you will be divided up among you.
[2] For I will gather all nations against Jerusalem to fight.
The city will be taken,
the houses will be robbed,
and the women will be raped.
Half of the city will be taken away as captives,
but the rest of the people will not be cut off from the city.
[3] Then the LORD will go out
and fight against those nations,
as he fights on a day of battle.
[4] On that day his feet will stand on the Mount of Olives,
which lies to the east of Jerusalem.
And the Mount of Olives will split in two,
from east to west,
so that a very large valley is formed.
Half of the mountain will move toward the north,
and the other half toward the south.
[5] You will flee through the valley of my mountains,
for the valley of the mountains will reach to Azel.
Yes, you will flee,
just as you fled from the earthquake
in the days of Uzziah king of Judah.
And the LORD my God will come,
and all the holy ones with you.

> **What does this mean?**
> The last chapter describes the great “day of the LORD,” the end of history.
> Verse 2: First, terrible things happen: Jerusalem is conquered and robbed, and women are raped. The Bible does not keep silent about what happens in war, especially what is done to women. This is a grave wrong, and the guilt lies only with those who do it. If you have experienced sexual violence, you can find help: In the US, call or text 988. In the UK and Ireland, call Samaritans at 116 123. Elsewhere, find help at findahelpline.com. In the US: Childhelp 1-800-422-4453, RAINN 1-800-656-4673. For domestic violence, US: 1-800-799-7233.
> Verse 3: But then God himself steps in and fights against the attackers.
> Verse 4: God stands on the Mount of Olives, east of Jerusalem. The mountain splits, and a large valley is formed, a way of escape for the people of the city.
> The Mount of Olives also plays an important role in the New Testament: There Jesus prayed in the night before his death (in the garden of Gethsemane at the foot of the mountain), and from there he was taken up into heaven (Acts 1:9–12). There the angels say: “This Jesus … will come back in the same way.”
> Verse 5: The earthquake in the time of King Uzziah was so strong that people still remembered it centuries later (compare Amos 1:1). We do not know where Azel was.
> “All the holy ones” are probably the angels who come with God.

## Living water and one God (Verses 6–11)

[6] On that day
there will be no light,
no cold, and no frost.
[7] It will be a day like no other,
a day that the LORD knows,
neither day nor night.
But it will happen:
In the evening there will be light.
[8] On that day
living water will flow out from Jerusalem,
half of it toward the eastern sea
and half of it toward the western sea.
It will be like this in summer and in winter.
“Living water” means fresh, flowing water.
[9] The LORD will be King over the whole earth.
On that day the LORD will be one,
and his name one.
[10] The whole land will be made flat like the Arabah,
from Geba to Rimmon, south of Jerusalem.
The Arabah is the low, flat valley of the Jordan River.
But Jerusalem will be lifted up and will stay in her place,
from Benjamin’s Gate to the place of the First Gate,
to the Corner Gate,
and from the Tower of Hananel to the king’s winepresses.
[11] People will live in it,
and there will be no more curse.
Jerusalem will live in safety.
