# 2 Kings – Chapter 11
### Queen Athaliah and little Joash

---

## A child is saved (Verses 1–3)

[1] Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, saw that her son was dead.
Then she got up and killed all the descendants of the royal family.
[2] But Jehosheba, the daughter of King Joram and sister of Ahaziah,
took Joash the son of Ahaziah.
She secretly stole him away from among the king’s sons who were being killed,
him and his nurse,
and put them in the bedroom.
So they hid him from Athaliah,
and he was not killed.
[3] He was hidden with her in the LORD’s house for six years,
while Athaliah ruled over the land.

> **What does this mean?**
> Athaliah is the daughter of Ahab and Jezebel. After the death of her son she wants to rule herself. For this she kills her own grandchildren. That is a horrible crime.
> With this, David’s family is in the greatest danger. God had promised David that his “lamp” would not go out. Now everything depends on one single baby.
> A brave woman, Jehosheba, saves the child at the risk of her own life. She is the boy’s aunt. According to 2 Chronicles 22:11 she was married to the priest Jehoiada. That is why she could hide the child in the temple.
> Athaliah is the only woman who ruled over Judah as queen. She ruled from about 841 to 835 BC.

---

## The plan of Jehoiada the priest (Verses 4–12)

[4] In the seventh year Jehoiada sent for the captains over hundreds of the Carites and of the guard.
He had them come to him into the LORD’s house.
He made a covenant with them.
He made a covenant with them in the LORD’s house,
and he showed them the king’s son.
[5] He commanded them,
“This is what you must do:
a third of you, who come on duty on the Sabbath,
shall keep watch over the king’s house.
[6] A third of you shall be at the gate Sur,
and a third at the gate behind the guard.
So you shall keep watch over the house and be a barrier.
[7] And your two groups, all who go off duty on the Sabbath,
shall keep watch over the LORD’s house around the king.
[8] You shall surround the king, every man with his weapons in his hand.
Whoever comes inside the ranks must be killed.
Be with the king when he goes out and when he comes in.”
[9] The captains over hundreds did everything that Jehoiada the priest commanded.
Each one took his men,
those who were to come on duty on the Sabbath, together with those who were to go off duty on the Sabbath,
and they came to Jehoiada the priest.
[10] The priest gave the captains over hundreds the spears and shields
that had belonged to King David
and that were in the LORD’s house.
[11] The guards stood there, every man with his weapons in his hand,
from the right side of the house to the left side of the house,
along the altar and the house, all around the king.
[12] Then he brought out the king’s son,
put the crown on him,
and gave him the covenant.
They made him king and anointed him.
They clapped their hands and shouted,
“Long live the king!”

> **What does this mean?**
> For six years Jehoiada the priest waits. Then he plans everything carefully. He uses the Sabbath, because that is when the guards change. So twice as many soldiers are there, without anyone noticing.
> The “Carites” were probably foreign soldiers who were paid to serve as bodyguards.
> David’s weapons are used. That is a sign: this is about David’s rightful family.
> “Gave him the covenant”: probably a scroll with God’s law or with the words of the covenant. The king is to rule by God’s word (Deuteronomy 17:18–19).
> “Long live the king!” Seven-year-old Joash is crowned.

## The end of Athaliah (Verses 13–16)

[13] When Athaliah heard the noise of the guards and of the people,
she came to the people in the LORD’s house.
[14] She looked and saw the king standing by the pillar, as was the custom,
and the captains and the trumpeters were by the king.
And all the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing trumpets.
Then Athaliah tore her clothes and cried out,
“Treason! Treason!”
[15] Jehoiada the priest commanded the captains of hundreds who were in charge of the army,
“Bring her out between the ranks.
Kill with the sword anyone who follows her.”
For the priest said,
“Don’t let her be killed in the LORD’s house.”
[16] So they seized her.
She went along the way where the horses go into the king’s house,
and there she was killed.

> **What does this mean?**
> Athaliah hears the cheering and hurries there. She cries “Treason!”, even though she herself came to power through murder.
> Jehoiada makes sure that no blood is shed in the temple. The holy place must not be made unclean.

---

## A new covenant (Verses 17–21)

[17] Jehoiada made a covenant between the LORD and the king and the people,
that they would be the LORD’s people.
He also made a covenant between the king and the people.
[18] All the people of the land went to the house of Baal and tore it down.
They smashed his altars and his images completely to pieces,
and they killed Mattan the priest of Baal in front of the altars.
And the priest put officers in charge of the LORD’s house.
[19] He took the captains over hundreds, the Carites, the guards,
and all the people of the land.
They brought the king down from the LORD’s house
and came by the way of the gate of the guards to the king’s house.
And he sat on the throne of the kings.
[20] So all the people of the land rejoiced,
and the city was quiet.
They had killed Athaliah with the sword at the king’s house.
[21] Jehoash was seven years old when he became king.

> **What does this mean?**
> The covenant is renewed: God, king, and people belong together. And there is also a covenant between king and people. The king has duties toward the people.
> The temple of Baal in Jerusalem is destroyed. Unlike in Jehu’s time, only the priest of Baal is killed here. The city stays quiet.
> In some Bibles, verse 21 is already Chapter 12, verse 1. That is why the verse numbering in the next chapter is shifted by one.
