# Day 029 – Your judgment, not the thing

## Post

```text
"If thou art pained by any external thing, it is not this thing that disturbs thee, but thy own judgment about it."
— Marcus Aurelius

You can't always change the thing. You can always re-examine your judgment about it.
```
