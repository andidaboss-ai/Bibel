## Quick Finder

When a particular situation is weighing on you, use this list to find the sections that speak to it most directly, along with the matching days of the 30-Day Practice.

- **When you feel overwhelmed by things you cannot control** — Enchiridion, Chapter 1; Of the Things Which Are in Our Power and Not in Our Power · Days 1, 2
- **When things are not going the way you planned** — Enchiridion, Chapter 8; Of Contentment · Day 3
- **When you face a hard stretch of life** — How We Should Struggle with Circumstances; On the Same · Day 5
- **Before an exam, a trial or a big meeting** — Of Tranquillity (Freedom from Perturbation); Enchiridion, Chapter 32 · Day 6
- **When you feel nervous before performing or speaking** — On Anxiety (Solicitude) · Day 13
- **When someone insults you** — Enchiridion, Chapter 20; Enchiridion, Chapter 28 · Day 9
- **When you hear that someone has spoken badly of you** — Enchiridion, Chapter 33; Enchiridion, Chapter 42 · Days 12, 25
- **When you are angry** — How We Should Struggle Against Appearances; That We Ought Not to Be Angry with Men; and What Are the Small and the Great Things Among Men · Day 11
- **When someone has wronged or stolen from you** — That We Ought Not to Be Angry with the Errors (Faults) of Others; Enchiridion, Chapter 42 · Days 12, 20
- **When a family member is hard to live with** — Enchiridion, Chapter 30; Enchiridion, Chapter 43 · Days 14, 23
- **When you are afraid of someone with power over you** — How We Should Behave to Tyrants; On Freedom from Fear · Days 2, 21
- **When you worry about money** — To Those Who Fear Want; Enchiridion, Chapter 24 · Day 19
- **When you envy someone else's success** — Enchiridion, Chapter 19; Enchiridion, Chapter 25 · Day 7
- **When you are tempted by a pleasure you will regret** — Enchiridion, Chapter 34; About Exercise · Day 18
- **When you want more and more things** — Enchiridion, Chapter 39; Of Finery in Dress · Day 19
- **When you lose something you value** — Enchiridion, Chapter 11; That We Ought Not to Be Angry with the Errors (Faults) of Others · Days 20, 26
- **When you are grieving or afraid of losing someone** — That We Ought Not to Be Moved by a Desire of Those Things Which Are Not in Our Power; Enchiridion, Chapter 3 · Day 26
- **When a friend is grieving** — Enchiridion, Chapter 16 · Day 27
- **When you are thinking about death** — Enchiridion, Chapter 5; Enchiridion, Chapter 21 · Day 28
- **When you are ill** — In What Manner We Ought to Bear Sickness; Enchiridion, Chapter 9 · Day 4
- **When you feel lonely** — What Solitude Is, and What Kind of Person a Solitary Man Is · Day 24
- **When you are unsure of your role or duty** — Enchiridion, Chapter 17; How We May Discover the Duties of Life from Names · Days 22, 23
- **When people mock you for trying to change** — Enchiridion, Chapter 22; Enchiridion, Chapter 35 · Day 25
- **When you want to impress others** — Against Those Who Wish to Be Admired; Enchiridion, Chapter 46 · Day 7
- **When you keep putting things off or have given up** — Enchiridion, Chapter 50; On Attention; To Those Who Fall Off (Desist) from Their Purpose · Days 29, 30
