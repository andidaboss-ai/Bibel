## The 30-Day Practice

This practice is for anyone who wants to live with *The Imitation of Christ* for a month and not only read it. Each day offers one short passage taken word for word from the book, the place where it is found, and a small task for the day. It should take no more than a few minutes in the morning and a few moments of looking back at night.

The month follows a path. The first week is about humility and honest self-knowledge, the ground on which everything else is built. The second week turns to inner peace, silence and the struggle with temptation. The third week listens in on the conversation between Christ and the soul and invites you into it, with trust and love. The fourth week takes up the cross, patience, Holy Communion and gratitude. The last two days are for looking back and choosing what to carry forward.

You do not need to follow the days strictly. If a day falls short, simply pick up again the next morning; no one is keeping score, and Thomas would be the first to tell you that a fresh start is always allowed. If you want more, read the whole chapter named under each passage. If you keep a notebook, write one sentence each evening about what you noticed. A friend or a small group can take the month together and share a word about it once a week.

### Day 1 – Beginning to Follow

> Let it be our most earnest study, therefore, to dwell upon the life of Jesus Christ.

Source: Book One, Chapter 1

Choose one short scene from the Gospels, for example Jesus calling the fishermen. Read it slowly this morning. During the day, call it to mind three times and ask what it shows you about Him.

### Day 2 – Weak and Frail Together

> All of us are weak and frail; hold thou no man more frail than thyself.

Source: Book One, Chapter 2

Notice today the moment when you feel above someone, smarter, more careful or more correct. Quietly remind yourself that you share the same weakness. Then do that person one small kindness.

### Day 3 – Knowing Yourself

> A lowly knowledge of thyself is a surer way to God than the deep searching of man’s learning.

Source: Book One, Chapter 3

Take five quiet minutes tonight. Name one strength God has given you and one habit that keeps tripping you up. Thank Him for the first and ask for help with the second, without harshness toward yourself.

### Day 4 – Doing Your Part

> Do what lieth in thy power, and God will help thy good intent.

Source: Book One, Chapter 7

Pick one task you have been putting off because it feels too large. Do the first small piece of it today. Offer the rest to God and let go of the need to finish it all at once.

### Day 5 – Not Judging

> Look well unto thyself, and beware that thou judge not the doings of others.

Source: Book One, Chapter 14

Each time you catch yourself passing judgment on someone today, in your head or out loud, stop and turn the question around: "What is going on in me right now?" Keep a simple tally if it helps.

### Day 6 – Bearing with One Another

> Endeavour to be patient in bearing with other men’s faults and infirmities whatsoever they be, for thou thyself also hast many things which have need to be borne with by others.

Source: Book One, Chapter 16

Think of one person whose habits annoy you. Today, when that habit shows up, let it pass without comment. Remember someone who has patiently put up with you, and thank them if you can.

### Day 7 – Losing and Finding Yourself

> For verily, by loving myself amiss, I lost myself, and by seeking and sincerely loving Thee alone, I found both myself and Thee

Source: Book Three, Chapter 8

Humility is not self-hatred. Write down one way you have been "loving yourself amiss," perhaps by needing to look good or by refusing rest. Then write one sentence of thanks that God loves you as you are.

### Day 8 – A Single Heart

> Blessed are the single-hearted, for they shall have abundance of peace.

Source: Book One, Chapter 11

Today, give one ordinary task your whole attention: washing dishes, a phone call, a walk. Leave the other screens and worries aside for those minutes. Notice how peace grows when the heart is not divided.

### Day 9 – Words That Build Up

> If it be right and desirable for thee to speak, speak things which are to edification.

Source: Book One, Chapter 10

Before you speak in a conversation today, ask yourself whether your words will build up or tear down. Choose at least once to say something encouraging, and once to stay silent instead of repeating gossip.

### Day 10 – The Gift of Silence

> In silence and quiet the devout soul goeth forward and learneth the hidden things of the Scriptures.

Source: Book One, Chapter 20

Find ten minutes of real silence today: no music, no news, no phone. Read a short psalm, such as Psalm 23, and then simply sit with it. If your mind wanders, gently come back.

### Day 11 – Rich in Christ

> When thou hast Christ, thou art rich, and hast sufficient.

Source: Book Two, Chapter 1

Notice today when you feel that something is missing, a purchase, an approval, a success. Pause and say quietly, "With Christ, I have enough." Let the wish pass if it is not a real need.

### Day 12 – Peace Begins at Home

> First keep thyself in peace, and then shalt thou be able to be a peacemaker towards others.

Source: Book Two, Chapter 3

Before you walk into a tense situation today, at home or at work, take three slow breaths and a short prayer for calm. Go in to listen first. See if your own peace changes the room.

### Day 13 – What Temptation Reveals

> Oftentimes we know not what strength we have; but temptation revealeth to us what we are.

Source: Book One, Chapter 13

Name one temptation that returns to you often. Today, watch for its very first stirring and meet it early with a short prayer and a change of activity. If it is a heavy struggle, think of someone wise you could talk to.

### Day 14 – The Way of Peace

> Strive, My Son, to do another’s will rather than thine own.

Source: Book Three, Chapter 23

Today, when you have a choice that does not matter much, such as where to eat, which show to watch or who goes first, let someone else choose. Do it gladly, and notice the freedom of not needing your way.

### Day 15 – Listening Within

> Blessed is the soul which heareth the Lord speaking within it, and receiveth the word of consolation from His mouth.

Source: Book Three, Chapter 1

Spend five minutes in prayer today without asking for anything. Simply say, "Lord, I am listening," and wait. Write down any word, memory or Scripture verse that comes to mind, even if it seems small.

### Day 16 – Strength for the Journey

> They show the way, but Thou givest strength for the journey.

Source: Book Three, Chapter 2

Think of a good book, teacher or sermon that showed you the way. Thank God for it. Then ask Him directly for the strength to do one thing you already know you should do, and take that step today.

### Day 17 – The Power of Love

> Love is a great thing, a good above all others, which alone maketh every heavy burden light, and equaliseth every inequality.

Source: Book Three, Chapter 5

Choose one duty today that feels like a burden. Do it consciously for love of someone, of God or of the person it serves. Notice whether love changes the weight of it, even a little.

### Day 18 – Seeking Jesus in All Things

> If thou seekest Jesus in all things thou shalt verily find Jesus

Source: Book Two, Chapter 7

Set a reminder for three times today. Each time it rings, look around and ask, "Where is Christ here?" He may be in a face, a task, a kindness or a difficulty. Name one sign of Him each time.

### Day 19 – Jesus as Friend

> Be thou humble and peaceable and Jesus shall be with thee.

Source: Book Two, Chapter 8

Talk to Jesus today as you would to a trusted friend: on the drive, in the kitchen, on a walk. Tell Him plainly about your day. Close with a moment of thanks for His company.

### Day 20 – Casting Your Care

> Greater is Thy care for me than all the care which I am able to take for myself.

Source: Book Three, Chapter 17

Write down your three biggest worries on a piece of paper. Pray over each one, then fold the paper and put it away as a sign of handing it over. Do the next practical thing, and leave the rest with God.

### Day 21 – Hope in God

> to hope in Thee above all things is the strongest solace of Thy servants.

Source: Book Three, Chapter 59

Look back over the past three weeks. Name one moment when God carried you through something hard. Tell someone about it today, or write it down, so that it can strengthen your hope next time.

### Day 22 – Lovers and Bearers

> Jesus hath many lovers of His heavenly kingdom, but few bearers of His Cross.

Source: Book Two, Chapter 11

Ask yourself honestly where you love the comfort of faith more than its cost. Today, accept one small inconvenience for someone else without complaining, and offer it quietly to Christ.

### Day 23 – The Cross That Carries

> If thou willingly bear the Cross, it will bear thee

Source: Book Two, Chapter 12

Think of one hardship in your life right now that you cannot change. Today, instead of fighting it in your thoughts, pray: "Lord, help me carry this with You." If the burden is too heavy, reach out to a pastor, friend, doctor or counselor.

### Day 24 – Grace for Patience

> Make possible to me, O Lord, by grace what seemeth impossible to me by nature.

Source: Book Three, Chapter 19

Pray this line whenever patience runs thin today, in traffic, in a queue, with a child or a coworker. Notice that it is a request, not a demand on yourself. Let grace do the heavy lifting.

### Day 25 – Human, Not Angel

> Thou art man, not God; thou art flesh, not an angel.

Source: Book Three, Chapter 57

When you fall short today, as everyone does, resist the urge to scold yourself harshly. Admit the fault simply, ask forgiveness, make amends where needed, and begin again. Speak to yourself as you would to a friend.

### Day 26 – The Invitation

> Come unto Me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will refresh you

Source: Book Four, A Devout Exhortation

Read this invitation of Jesus slowly, three times. If you will receive Communion soon, let it be your preparation. If not, accept the invitation in prayer: bring Him what makes you weary and rest a few minutes in His presence.

### Day 27 – Give Me Yourself

> Give me Thyself and it sufficeth, for besides Thee no consolation availeth.

Source: Book Four, Chapter 3

Before your next time of worship or Communion, or simply before a meal today, pray this line. Ask for nothing else. Afterward, take a quiet moment to give thanks for His presence, however you sense it.

### Day 28 – Thankful for Small Things

> Be thankful, therefore, for the least benefit and thou shalt be worthy to receive greater.

Source: Book Two, Chapter 10

Write down ten small gifts from today: a warm cup, a kind word, a moment of light. Thank God for each one by name. Keep the list where you will see it tomorrow.

### Day 29 – Looking Back

> Do now what thou wouldst do then, and thou shalt be very secure.

Source: Book One, Chapter 25

Leaf back through the past four weeks. Which day touched you most, and which task do you want to keep? Write down one practice to continue, and one person to thank for walking with you.

### Day 30 – Going Forward

> I have begun; I may not return back nor leave it.

Source: Book Three, Chapter 56

Make a simple commitment for the coming month: a chapter a day, a weekly time of silence, or one of the practices above. Write it down, tell a friend, and ask Christ to walk the next stretch with you.
