## The 30-Day Practice

Shinn ends her book with a simple instruction: choose an affirmation and use it. This 30-day practice is one way to do that. Each day offers a single statement, quoted exactly from her text, along with a short prompt or task to help you live it out.

The month follows an arc. Week one lays the foundations: the power of your words, the nature of faith, and loosening the grip of fear. Week two turns to relationships and forgiveness, which Shinn treated as central to everything else. Week three addresses practical life: work, money and guidance. Week four focuses on peace of mind, gratitude and her idea of a Divine Design for each life. The final two days invite you to look back and choose the words you will carry forward.

A few suggestions:

- Set aside five to ten quiet minutes each morning. Read the day's affirmation aloud at least three times.
- Keep a notebook for the prompts. Your notes will be part of the review on Day 29.
- Return to the affirmation during the day, especially when you feel anxious or stuck.
- Pair the words with action. Shinn's faith was always active.
- If you miss a day, simply pick up where you left off. This is a practice, not a test.

These affirmations are a spiritual and emotional practice. They are not a substitute for medical, financial or professional advice.

### Day 1 – The Power of Your Word

> Give thanks for the sun!

Source: Your Word Is Your Wand

Today, notice how you talk about your life, both aloud and in your head. Write down three phrases you use often. For each one that drags you down, write a brighter version you can say instead.

### Day 2 – Laying Down Burdens

> I cast every burden on the Christ within and I go free!

Source: Faith

Make a list of everything weighing on you right now. Read the list once, then say the affirmation and imagine handing the whole list over. Notice which burden feels lightest afterward.

### Day 3 – Faith That Knows

> I have perfect confidence in God and God has perfect confidence in me.

Source: Faith

Shinn said hope looks forward, while faith knows. Think of one prayer you are holding. Write a short paragraph describing it as already answered, and how you feel now that it has happened.

### Day 4 – Facing the Lion

> I walk boldly up to the lion on my pathway and find it is a friendly airedale.

Source: Happiness

Name one thing you have been avoiding out of fear. Today, take one small step toward it: send the email, open the letter, make the call. Afterward, write down whether the "lion" was as fierce as you expected.

### Day 5 – Unmoved by Appearances

> I am undisturbed by appearances. I trust in God—and He now brings to me the desires of my heart.

Source: Miscellaneous

When something looks discouraging today, pause before reacting. Take three slow breaths and repeat the affirmation. Write down one situation where you chose calm over alarm.

### Day 6 – Digging Your Ditches

> I now dig my ditches deep with faith and understanding and my heart’s desire comes to pass in a surprising way.

Source: Faith

Choose one hope and do something practical to prepare for it, as if it were already on the way. Clear space, gather information or set aside resources. Keep your preparation quiet for now.

### Day 7 – Trusting the Dawn

> It is dark before the dawn but the dawn never fails. Trust in the dawn.

Source: Words of Wisdom

Look back over your first week. Where did you feel a shift, even a small one? Write down one area that still feels dark, and write one sentence of trust about it.

### Day 8 – Peace With Yourself

> I am at peace with myself and with the whole world.

Source: Love

Begin your week of relationships with yourself. Write down three things you appreciate about who you are. Say the affirmation in front of a mirror, looking yourself in the eye.

### Day 9 – Love That Clears the Way

> Divine Love, through me, now dissolves all seeming obstacles and makes clear, easy and successful my way.

Source: Love

Think of one relationship that feels tense or stuck. Today, offer a small act of kindness to that person without expecting anything back. Notice how it changes your own feelings.

### Day 10 – Forgiving Others

> I forgive everyone and everyone forgives me. The gates swing open for my good.

Source: Forgiveness

Write the name of someone you are ready to begin forgiving. You don't have to contact them or excuse what happened. Simply say the affirmation, picture them, and wish them well, even if only a little.

### Day 11 – Forgiving Yourself

> Though my mistakes be as scarlet, I shall be washed whiter than snow.

Source: Forgiveness

Write down one mistake you still carry guilt about. If there is something you can do to make amends, plan it. Then say the affirmation slowly and allow yourself to release the rest.

### Day 12 – Releasing Resentment

> The Light of the Christ within now wipes out all fear, doubt, anger and resentment.

Source: Love

Shinn believed resentment did great harm to body and home. Notice any flicker of irritation today. Each time, pause, picture light flooding the feeling, and repeat the affirmation.

### Day 13 – The Way of Non-Resistance

> Great peace have I who love thy law of nonresistance and nothing shall offend me.

Source: Miscellaneous

Remember Shinn's story of the woman who blessed "all the cousins in the Universe." Think of a pattern that keeps repeating in your relationships. Instead of resisting it, bless the people involved and let your irritation go.

### Day 14 – Golden Links

> Every man is a golden link in the chain of my good.

Source: Success

List five people who have helped you, even in small ways, and include one you find difficult. Write how each might be a "golden link." Send a note of thanks to at least one of them.

### Day 15 – Your Right Work

> I am a perfect idea in Divine Mind and I am always in my right place doing my right work at the right time for the right pay.

Source: Miscellaneous

Write a description of the work that would feel truly right for you: what you do, with whom, and how it serves others. Circle one element you already have, and one you could move toward this month.

### Day 16 – Open Doors

> Unexpected doors fly open, unexpected channels are free.

Source: Success

Today, say yes to one small opportunity you might normally pass by: a conversation, an invitation, a new idea. Keep your eyes open for doors you didn't expect.

### Day 17 – Money Without Fear

> I spend money under direct inspiration wisely and fearlessly, knowing my supply is endless and immediate.

Source: Prosperity

Look at your finances calmly today. Review one statement or bill without panic, bless it, and make one wise decision about it. Wisdom and fearlessness belong together.

### Day 18 – Giving First

> Giving precedes receiving and my gifts to others precede God’s gifts to me.

Source: Success

Give something today: time, attention, a small gift, a generous tip or a word of encouragement. Choose something within your means, and give it freely. Write down how giving felt.

### Day 19 – Facing Obligations

> All my obligations are now wiped out, under grace in a perfect way.

Source: Debt

Shinn's statement is about releasing fear and shame, not ignoring bills. Write a simple list of what you owe, say the affirmation, and take one practical step: a payment, a plan or a call for reputable advice.

### Day 20 – Following Hunches

> Hunches are my hounds of Heaven—they lead me in the perfect way.

Source: Miscellaneous

Pay attention to quiet nudges today. Write down any hunch that comes, and act on one small, safe one. At the end of the day, note what happened.

### Day 21 – Asking for Wisdom

> Infinite Spirit, give me wisdom to make the most of my opportunities.

Source: Guidance

Write down one decision you are facing. Say the affirmation, then list your options without judging them. Sleep on it, and tomorrow morning notice which option feels clearest.

### Day 22 – A Calm Mind

> My poise is built upon a rock. I see clearly and act quickly.

Source: Success

Begin your final week with stillness. Sit quietly for five minutes, breathing slowly. Then say the affirmation and return to it whenever you feel rushed or rattled today.

### Day 23 – Clearing Out the Old

> I now let go of worn-out conditions and worn-out things.

Source: Success

Clear out one drawer, shelf or closet. As you let go of things you no longer need, name one old habit of thought you are ready to release as well.

### Day 24 – Joy and Interest

> I live in the Kingdom of eternal joy and absorbing interests.

Source: Health

Do one thing today purely because it delights or absorbs you. Care for your body as well: rest, move, eat well. Remember that affirmations go alongside medical care, never in place of it.

### Day 25 – Gratitude

> My barns are full, my cup flows over with joy.

Source: Happiness

Write down twenty things you are thankful for, big and small. Read the list aloud, then say the affirmation. Keep the list somewhere you will see it this week.

### Day 26 – Expecting Wonders

> Nothing is too good to be true

Source: Miscellaneous

Notice where you brace for disappointment. Today, practice expecting good instead. Write down any small surprise or kindness that comes your way, however ordinary.

### Day 27 – The Divine Pattern

> My mind, body and affairs are now molded according to the Divine pattern within.

Source: Divine Design

Remember Shinn's image of the oak inside the acorn. Write about the person you sense you are meant to become. What qualities, work or relationships belong to that pattern?

### Day 28 – Your Unique Place

> I now fill the place that I can fill and no one else can fill.

Source: Divine Design

List three gifts or qualities that are uniquely yours. Think of one way to offer one of them this week, in your family, work or community. Stop comparing your path with anyone else's.

### Day 29 – Looking Back

> I am fully equipped for the Divine Plan of my life; I am more than equal to the situation.

Source: Divine Design

Read through your notebook from the past 28 days. Underline moments of change, insight or courage. Write down the two or three affirmations that meant the most to you, and why.

### Day 30 – Commitment

> I will not weary of well-doing, for when I least expect it I shall reap.

Source: Success

Choose one affirmation to carry with you from now on. Write it on a card and place it where you will see it daily. Then write a short promise to yourself about how you will keep practicing, in words and in action.
