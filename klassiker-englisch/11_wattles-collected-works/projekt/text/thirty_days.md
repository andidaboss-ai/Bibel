## The 30-Day Practice

Wattles believed that ideas change a life only when they become habits, and that habits are built one ordinary day at a time. This practice gives you thirty such days.

Each day has a short passage from Wattles, a note telling you where it comes from, and a simple task. Read the passage slowly, perhaps twice. Then do the task and write a few lines on the ruled page. What did you notice? What was hard? What, if anything, changed? Fifteen minutes is enough. If you miss a day, just carry on. You are not taking a test.

The four weeks follow the shape of Wattles' teaching. Week one is about vision and the Certain Way: knowing what you want and why. Week two turns to gratitude, faith and the will. Week three is about action: doing today's work well, giving more than you take, and living what Wattles called the advancing life. Week four widens the circle to health habits, greatness of character, service and love, and the last two days are for looking back and looking ahead.

Two notes before you start. None of these tasks asks you to fast, skip meals or change your diet. If a health question comes up, talk to a doctor. And if a day touches something heavy, such as money worries, illness or grief, it is fine to go gently, to talk with someone you trust, or to seek professional help. The practice exists to serve you.

### Day 1 – The Desire to Live More

> The desire for riches is really the desire for a richer, fuller, and more abundant life; and that desire is praiseworthy.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, The Right to Be Rich

Wattles says we live for the body, the mind and the soul. Draw three columns with those headings and write down what "more life" would mean in each. Leave money out of it for now. Notice which column is easiest to fill and which is hardest.

### Day 2 – A Certain Way

> From all these things, we must come to the conclusion that getting rich is the result of doing things in a Certain Way.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, There is a Science of Getting Rich

Wattles thought the way you do things mattered more than talent or circumstance. Circumstances matter too, but think about what is in your hands. List three ways of working, speaking or deciding that you would like to make your own "certain way." Choose one and practice it today.

### Day 3 – First in Thought

> All the forms that man fashions with his hands must first exist in his thought; he cannot shape a thing until he has thought that thing.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, The First Principle in the Science of Getting Rich

Think of one thing you have made or achieved: a meal, a garden, a finished project. Trace it back to the moment it was only an idea. Then write down one idea you are carrying right now that has not yet taken shape.

### Day 4 – Create, Don't Compete

> You are to create, not to compete for what is already created.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, Increasing Life

Notice today when you feel you are in a race against someone, for attention, credit, a sale or a place in line. Each time, ask: what could I add here instead of trying to win? Write down one moment where the question changed what you did.

### Day 5 – A Clear Picture

> You must form a clear and definite mental picture of what you want; you cannot transmit an idea unless you have it yourself.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, Thinking in the Certain Way

Write one paragraph describing something you want as if it were already part of your life. Make it concrete: where you are, what you are doing, who is with you. Leave out vague words like "better" or "more." Read it once tonight.

### Day 6 – What Your Desire Tells You

> The fact that you want to be is proof that you can be.

Source: Making the Man Who Can, What You Desire

You may see Wattles' claim as a promise or as a clue. Name one thing you have long wanted to be or do. Ask what ability or interest that wish points to, and write one small step that would let that ability grow this week.

### Day 7 – Keep Your Face Toward It

> That clear mental picture you must have continually in mind, as the sailor has in mind the port toward which he is sailing the ship; you must keep your face toward it all the time.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, Thinking in the Certain Way

Look back over the week. Reread your picture from Day 5 and refine it if you like. Then choose a small reminder that will bring it to mind during the day, such as a note in your wallet or a word on your phone's lock screen.

### Day 8 – The Habit of Thanks

> It is necessary, then, to cultivate the habit of being grateful for every good thing that comes to you; and to give thanks continuously.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, Gratitude

Tonight write down ten things from today that you are grateful for, however small: a kind word, a hot drink, a bus that came on time. Try to name each one specifically. Notice how you feel when you finish.

### Day 9 – Looking at the Best

> The grateful mind is constantly fixed upon the best; therefore it tends to become the best; it takes the form or character of the best, and will receive the best.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, Gratitude

Choose one person you find difficult. Write down three genuine good qualities they have, or three things you have received through them. Gratitude does not excuse real harm, so if the person has hurt you, choose someone else.

### Day 10 – Faith Grows from Gratitude

> Gratitude has a twofold effect; it strengthens your own faith, and it brings you into close and harmonious relations with the Supreme.

Source: The Science of Being Well, Faith

Whatever your beliefs, take five quiet minutes to give thanks, whether in prayer, in words spoken aloud, or in a letter you do not send. Afterward write down whether your confidence in the day ahead feels any different.

### Day 11 – The Will Turned Inward

> That is the legitimate use of the will in getting what you want—to use it in holding yourself to the right course.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, How to Use the Will

Wattles warns against using willpower to push other people. Today use yours only on yourself. Pick one commitment you made earlier this week and keep it, especially at the moment you would rather not. Write down what that moment felt like.

### Day 12 – Choosing Your Attention

> The sole legitimate use of the will is in determining to what you shall give your attention, and what you shall think about the things to which your attention is given.

Source: The Science of Being Well, Use of the Will

Pay attention to what pulls at your attention today: news, feeds, worries, conversations. Three times, deliberately turn your attention to something you have chosen. Note what you turned away from and what you turned toward.

### Day 13 – A Little Stillness

> Get by yourself in the quietest place you can find, and where you may be free from interruption for a little while.

Source: The New Science of Living and Healing, Nature and Development of Will Power

Find ten minutes of quiet, even if that means a parked car or a closed bathroom door. Sit, breathe and do nothing else. Don't try to solve anything. Afterward write a line or two about how you feel.

### Day 14 – The Habit of Thought

> It is the habitual, not the periodical thought that decides your destiny.

Source: The Science of Being Great, Hurry and Habit

Look back over week two. Which thought about yourself do you repeat most often? Write it down honestly. Then write a fairer and more hopeful version that is still true, and repeat it to yourself at least five times today.

### Day 15 – Act Where You Are

> You cannot act where you are not; you cannot act where you have been, and you cannot act where you are going to be; you can act only where you are.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, Acting in the Certain Way

Make a short list of what is actually in front of you today: tasks, people, choices. Choose one thing you have been putting off until conditions are better, and do the first part of it now.

### Day 16 – Efficient Action

> Do, every day, all that you can do that day, and do each act in an efficient manner.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, Efficient Action

Wattles also says you are "not to overwork." So choose three tasks for today and no more, and do each one with your full attention and as well as you can. In the evening, note which of them felt strongest and why.

### Day 17 – Use What You Have

> Do not waste any time considering how you would use certain things if you had them; consider, simply, how to use what you have.

Source: How to Get What You Want, The Right Way to Get What You Want

Make an honest list of the resources you already have, such as skills, time, contacts, tools and experience. Circle one that you are underusing. Write down one way to put it to work this week.

### Day 18 – More Than You Take

> Give every man more in use value than you take from him in cash value; then you are adding to the life of the world by every business transaction.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, How Riches Come to You

Think of one person you serve, whether a customer, a colleague, a client or a family member. Ask what would make what you give them more useful in their life, not just more impressive. Do one such thing today.

### Day 19 – Never Hurry

> Go as fast as you can, but never hurry.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, Getting into the Right Business

Each time you catch yourself rushing today, stop for one breath. Bring your goal to mind and think of one thing you are thankful for. Then go on at a steady pace. Count how many times you caught it.

### Day 20 – The Impression of Increase

> Convey the impression of advancement with everything you do, so that all people shall receive the impression that you are an Advancing Man, and that you advance all who deal with you.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, The Impression of Increase

In each conversation today, try to leave the other person a little better off: an encouraging word, a useful idea, real interest in their plans. Don't boast and don't pretend. Write down one exchange that felt different.

### Day 21 – Take the Next Step

> Do not wait for an opportunity to be all that you want to be; when an opportunity to be more than you are now is presented and you feel impelled toward it, take it.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, The Advancing Man

Look back over week three. Is there an opportunity in front of you right now that is smaller than your dream but larger than where you are? Write it down, along with what is holding you back and the first step you could take.

### Day 22 – Breathe

> Straighten up; fill your lungs FULL; stretch your chest and straighten your spine, and exhale easily.

Source: The Science of Being Well, Breathing

Three times today, stop and do what this line describes, comfortably and without strain. If possible, do it once outdoors. If you have a lung or heart condition, keep it gentle. Notice how your mood changes, if it does.

### Day 23 – Rest

> It is in sleep that the brain of man is recharged with vital energy, and the Principle of Health within him is given new strength.

Source: The Science of Being Well, Sleep

Tonight, give yourself a calmer last half-hour before bed. Put screens away, let in some fresh air if that is comfortable and safe, and choose a peaceful thought to fall asleep with. In the morning, note how you slept. If sleep is a long-standing struggle, consider talking to a doctor.

### Day 24 – One Unhurried Meal

> Do not anticipate the taste of the next thing; keep your mind centered on the taste of what you have in your mouth.

Source: The Science of Being Well, How to Eat

Eat one of your normal meals today slowly and with attention, without changing what or how much you eat. Put your phone away and notice the taste. Give thanks before you begin, if you like. Write down what you noticed.

### Day 25 – No Common People

> There are undreamed of possibilities in the common lives all around us; in a large sense, there are no “common” people.

Source: The Science of Being Great, Any Person May Become Great

Choose someone you see often but rarely think about, such as a neighbor, a cashier or a coworker. Today, pay real attention to them, ask a sincere question, and listen. Write down one thing you learned.

### Day 26 – Greatness at Home

> It is a great person who can always be poised, assured, calm, and perfectly kind and considerate at home.

Source: The Science of Being Great, Action at Home

Choose one moment at home today, at a meal, when you arrive or at bedtime, and be at your calmest and kindest in it. Wattles also warns against meddling, so go one day without correcting anyone's habits. Note what happens.

### Day 27 – A Great Servant

> Lincoln was a great man because he knew how to be a great servant.

Source: The Science of Being Great, Jesus’ Idea of Greatness

Do one act of service today that makes use of your real strengths. Do it freely and without making yourself small, and without expecting thanks. Then write down what it gave you and what it gave the other person.

### Day 28 – Love Your Neighbor

> If I love her as myself, I will desire pie for her as intensely as for myself, and I will try as hard to get it for her as for myself.

Source: A New Christ, His Teachings About Wealth

Think of someone close to you and something good you want for yourself. Ask honestly whether you want that good for them just as much. Then take one concrete step toward it on their behalf.

### Day 29 – Looking Back

> Too much stress cannot be laid on the importance of frequent contemplation of the mental image, coupled with unwavering faith and devout gratitude.

Source: The Science of Getting Rich, Summary of the Science of Getting Rich

Read through your notes from the past four weeks. Mark the three days that meant the most to you and the one that was hardest. Write down what you have learned about your vision, your gratitude and your way of acting.

### Day 30 – Small Things, Right Course

> As he sees the true and right course in small things, he must take that course.

Source: The Science of Being Great, A Summary of the Science of Being Great

Choose three practices from this month to keep. Write each one down with a time and place it will happen. Then pick a date about a month from now to come back to these pages and see how you are doing.
