## The 30-Day Practice

Brother Lawrence said that a habit of remembering God is formed little by little: at first it takes some care, and later it becomes natural. This month-long practice is built on that promise. It asks for only a few minutes a day, and most of what it asks happens not in a quiet room but in the middle of your ordinary life.

Each day has three parts: a short passage from the book, the place where you can find it, and one small task. Read the passage in the morning, if you can, and read it twice. Then carry the task into your day. In the evening, take a minute to notice how it went. You may want to jot a line in the margin or in a notebook.

The month moves in four steps. The first week is about beginning: turning toward God and learning to pray in a few quiet words. The second week brings the practice into work, chores and the kitchen. The third week deals with the wandering mind and with faults, and how to meet both with peace rather than worry. The fourth week turns to trust in hard times and to love rather than fear. The last two days help you look back and decide how to go on.

If you miss a day, do not start over and do not scold yourself. Simply pick up where you are. That, as you will see, is the whole practice in miniature.

### Day 1 – A Tree in Winter

> seeing a tree stripped of its leaves, and considering that within a little time the leaves would be renewed and after that the flowers and fruit appear

Source: First Conversation

Today, find one bare or ordinary thing to look at for a full minute: a tree, a houseplant, the sky from a window. Let it remind you that God is at work even where nothing seems to be happening. Say a simple thank-you before you move on.

### Day 2 – Talking with God

> That we should establish ourselves in a sense of GOD'S Presence, by continually conversing with Him.

Source: First Conversation

Think of prayer today as conversation rather than performance. Three times during the day, tell God in a sentence what you are doing and how you feel about it. Keep it as plain as a text message to a friend.

### Day 3 – Plain Speech

> That we ought to act with GOD in the greatest simplicity, speaking to Him frankly and plainly, and imploring His assistance in our affairs, just as they happen.

Source: Second Conversation

Pick one task you are dreading today. Before you start it, ask God for help in your own everyday words, without any religious phrasing. Notice whether the task feels any different afterward.

### Day 4 – Little Adorations

> nothing is easier than to repeat often in the day these little internal adorations

Source: Third Letter

Choose a short phrase of love or praise, no more than five words. Repeat it silently whenever you pass through a doorway today. No one needs to know.

### Day 5 – No Great Matters

> He requires no great matters of us; a little remembrance of Him from time to time; a little adoration

Source: Seventh Letter

Let go of any idea that you must do something impressive for God today. Aim instead for small remembrances: a glance upward while the kettle boils, a quiet word at a red light. Count how many you manage, without judging the number.

### Day 6 – Not Alone

> He is always near you and with you: leave Him not alone.

Source: Tenth Letter

Imagine that a dear friend has come along with you for the whole day. Every so often, turn to that friend in your heart and share what you see. Before sleep, thank God for the company.

### Day 7 – Starting Again

> let us return with a full assurance to that FATHER of mercies, who is always ready to receive us affectionately

Source: Ninth Letter

Look back over this first week. Wherever you forgot, simply turn back now, without guilt, trusting that you are welcome. Write down one thing you want to carry into next week.

### Day 8 – A Straw from the Ground

> That he was pleased when he could take up a straw from the ground for the love of GOD, seeking Him only, and nothing else, not even His gifts.

Source: Second Conversation

Today, do one tiny unseen task on purpose: pick up something from the floor, wipe a counter, refill the paper tray. Offer it to God in a word. Let it be enough that he sees it.

### Day 9 – The Kitchen

> having accustomed himself to do everything there for the love of GOD, and with prayer, upon all occasions, for His grace to do his work well, he had found everything easy

Source: Second Conversation

When you prepare food today, even if it is only toast or coffee, ask for grace to do it well. Pay attention to the smells and textures as you work. Treat the counter as a place of prayer.

### Day 10 – Same Work, New Reason

> That our sanctification did not depend upon *changing* our works, but in doing that for GOD's sake, which we commonly do for our own.

Source: Fourth Conversation

Choose one routine part of your job or your household. Do it exactly as usual, but tell God beforehand that you are doing it for him. At the end of the day, ask yourself whether the change of reason changed anything else.

### Day 11 – United in Busy Hours

> That he was more united to GOD in his outward employments, than when he left them for devotion in retirement.

Source: Third Conversation

Pick the busiest hour of your day. Before it begins, decide on one signal, such as each new email or each time you stand up, that will remind you God is with you in the rush. Stay with it for that hour.

### Day 12 – A Prayer Before Work

> I beseech Thee to grant me the grace to continue in Thy Presence; and to this end do Thou prosper me with Thy assistance, receive all my works, and possess all my affections.

Source: Fourth Conversation

Write a one-sentence version of this prayer in your own words. Say it as you start work or your first chore today. Keep the note where you will see it tomorrow.

### Day 13 – Noise and Clatter

> in the noise and clatter of my kitchen, while several persons are at the same time calling for different things, I possess GOD in as great tranquility as if I were upon my knees at the blessed sacrament

Source: Fourth Conversation

When your surroundings get loud today, with children, coworkers, traffic or a crowded store, take one slow breath. Remind yourself that God is no farther away in the noise than in silence. Then carry on.

### Day 14 – At Meals and in Company

> Lift up your heart to Him, sometimes even at your meals, and when you are in company: the least little remembrance will always be acceptable to Him.

Source: Seventh Letter

At one meal today, with others or alone, silently turn your heart to God partway through, not only at the start. Do the same once in a conversation. Notice how small the gesture can be.

### Day 15 – You Are Not Alone in This

> you are not the only one that is troubled with wandering thoughts

Source: Eighth Letter

Today, simply notice how often your mind wanders, without trying to fix it. It may help to know that everyone has the same trouble. Be friendly toward your own restless mind.

### Day 16 – Bringing the Mind Back

> If it sometimes wander and withdraw itself from Him, do not much disquiet yourself for that: trouble and disquiet serve rather to distract the mind than to re-collect it: the will must bring it back in tranquility.

Source: Eighth Letter

Sit quietly for three minutes. Each time your thoughts drift, return gently to a single word, such as "God" or "here." Do not count the times you drift; count only that you came back.

### Day 17 – A Beggar at the Gate

> Hold yourself in prayer before GOD, like a dumb or paralytic beggar at a rich man's gate.

Source: Eighth Letter

"Dumb" here means unable to speak. Spend a few minutes in prayer today without words at all, just waiting before God like someone who has nothing to offer but need. Let that be enough.

### Day 18 – Mildly

> recalling our mind to GOD mildly, and with tranquility, as often as we find it wandering from Him

Source: Sixth Letter

Watch the tone of your inner voice today when you catch yourself distracted. If it sounds harsh, soften it on purpose, as you would speak to a child learning to walk. Then return to God.

### Day 19 – Faults Without Discouragement

> That he was very sensible of his faults, but not discouraged by them

Source: Second Conversation

When you make a mistake today, name it honestly to God in one sentence. Ask for help, and then let it go. Notice how much energy you save by not replaying it.

### Day 20 – After Forgetting

> That when sometimes he had not thought of GOD for a good while, he did not disquiet himself for it

Source: Third Conversation

You will probably forget God for long stretches today. When you notice, do not go back over the lost hours. Simply turn to him now, and let the fact that you needed him draw you closer.

### Day 21 – Inward Peace

> felt a profound inward peace, as if she were in her centre and place of rest

Source: Second Letter

"She" here is the soul. Lawrence found this peace only after long years of struggle, so do not demand it of yourself. Today, spend five quiet minutes simply resting in God, asking for nothing, and receive whatever peace is given.

### Day 22 – Trust

> That the trust we put in GOD, honors Him much, and draws down great graces.

Source: Third Conversation

Name one worry that keeps coming back. Each time it returns today, hand it over to God in a sentence. You may still need to act on it, but you do not have to carry it alone.

### Day 23 – Strength When It Is Needed

> GOD would not fail to give him the strength to bear it

Source: Second Conversation

Think of something hard that may be ahead of you. Instead of rehearsing it, ask God for strength for today only. Lawrence did not borrow tomorrow's trouble; try to follow him in that.

### Day 24 – A Faithful Friend

> We cannot have too much in so good and faithful a Friend, who will never fail us in this world nor in the next.

Source: Tenth Letter

Recall one time when you were helped through a hard season. Thank God for it in some concrete way: a written note, a call to a person who helped, a moment of silence. Let that memory strengthen your trust now.

### Day 25 – The Storm

> If the vessel of our soul is still tossed with winds and storms, let us awake the LORD, who reposes in it, and He will quickly calm the sea.

Source: Fourth Letter

The image recalls the Gospel story of Jesus asleep in the boat during a storm. When you feel tossed about today, picture that boat and call on him plainly. Then take one practical step toward calm, such as a walk, a glass of water or a talk with a friend.

### Day 26 – Offering Our Troubles

> sometimes to offer Him your sufferings, and sometimes to return Him thanks for the favors He has given you, and still gives you, in the midst of your troubles

Source: Seventh Letter

If something hurts today, in body or heart, offer it to God in a simple word, and then look for one gift in the same day to thank him for. You do not have to call pain good. You only have to keep God company in it. If you are ill, please take care of yourself and seek the help you need.

### Day 27 – When God Seems Hidden

> He sometimes hides Himself from us, but *faith* alone, which will not fail us in time of need, ought to be our support

Source: Twelfth Letter

If prayer feels empty or God seems far away, do not take that as a sign you have failed. Keep your small practice anyway, as an act of quiet faith. Tell God honestly how it feels.

### Day 28 – Loved More Than We Imagine

> He loves us infinitely more than we imagine.

Source: Thirteenth Letter

Say this sentence slowly three times today. Whenever you feel fear or shame, return to it. Let love, not fear, be the reason you turn to God.

### Day 29 – Looking Back

> That we should not wonder if, in the beginning, we often failed in our endeavors, but that at last we should gain a habit

Source: Fourth Conversation

Read back over your notes from this month. Mark the day or practice that helped you most, and the one that was hardest. Be glad of any small change, and do not worry about the failures.

### Day 30 – Within

> He is within us: seek Him not elsewhere.

Source: Fifteenth Letter

Choose one or two practices from this month to keep. Write them down where you will see them, and tell God simply that you mean to go on. Then begin again tomorrow, as Brother Lawrence did, every day.
