Some of these passages touch on grief, illness and death. Marcus's words can be a comfort, but they are not a substitute for people. If you are struggling, or if you have thoughts of ending your life, please reach out to someone you trust or to a professional. In an emergency in the US, call or text 988; in the UK, call Samaritans on 116 123.
